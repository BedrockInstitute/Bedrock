# Archived decisions: the D series

**The whole `D` series was ARCHIVED on 2026-08-09**, when the owner opened the
route-adjustment phase, ruled the two-tower bridge route, and then ruled that
the decision list be rebuilt on it. The live list is `dev/PLAN.md` section 3
and it uses a NEW series, `DD`.

**Nothing here is live. Read it for history, and for what a citation means.**

Why a new series rather than a renumbering: the `D` codes are cited in
hundreds of places across `dev/JOURNAL.md`, the memos, the briefs and the git
history. Renumbering them would falsify every one of those citations.
Archiving the series whole and starting a new one keeps every old citation
true to what it meant when it was written. **A `D` number is never reused, and
`scripts/check-rule-ids.py` resolves `D` citations against THIS file.**

Each live `DD` row names the `D` row it descends from, so the map runs both
ways. A `D` row with no `DD` descendant was dropped, either because it
contradicts the two-tower bridge ruling or because it was spent; the survey
that decided each one is `dev/memos/L3.32-route-adjustment-conflicts.md`.

The text below is the section as it stood at archival, unedited.
