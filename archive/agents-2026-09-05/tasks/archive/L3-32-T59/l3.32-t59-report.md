## 1. D-10 check (the arms' premises against the delivered tree)

The check was run before building the arms. Three of the four premises T57
names are not delivered in the cited shape; one (the negative control's)
is delivered but through an ingredient T57 did not cite.

### 1.1 `⟪ 𝒟ₒ A ⟫ = Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1` "definitionally" is FALSE (empirically)

T5's gate and T57's successor arm both rest on the claim that members of
`𝒟ₒ A = sett (Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1) defSet` ARE the formulas. In the installed
cubical library `⟪ sett X ix ⟫` is NOT `X`:

- `⟪ X ⟫ = V-repr X .fst .fst`
  (`Cubical/HITs/CumulativeHierarchy/Properties.agda:220-224`), and
  `V-repr (sett X ix)` is `sett-repr X ix` (same file, :146-153), whose
  index type is `X / Kernel` with `Kernel x y = ix x ≊ ix y` (bisimulation,
  equivalent to path equality of the values).
- Hence `⟪ 𝒟ₒ A ⟫ ≅ Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1 / (defSet φ ≡ defSet ψ)`, and
  `defSet` is not injective (e.g. `defSet ⊤̇ ≡ defSet (¬̇ ⊥̇)`), so the
  quotient is nontrivial. The composed map "member → formula → count" is
  not well-defined, let alone injective.
- Empirical check in the probe: `D10test : (A : S) → ⟪ 𝒟ₒ A ⟫ →
  Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1` with body `D10test A x = x` fails with `UnequalTerms`
  (the member type reduces to `sett-repr X ix .fst .fst`, the quotient,
  not `Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1`). T5's "definitional" reading was against an older
  library shape of `⟪_⟫`; it is false here.

Consequence: the successor arm `⟪ Lset (sucV ω) ⟫ ↪ ⟪ ω ⟫` (via
`Lset-suc`, `src/L/Axioms/Basic.lagda.md:196`, and `Def`/`defSet`,
`src/L/Definability.lagda.md:110-113`) requires an injection from the
quotient `Formula ⟪ Lset ω ⟫ 1 / (defSet ≡)` into `⟪ ω ⟫`: a choice of
representative per definable subset, i.e. the least-of extraction wall
(T31's class, `_build/l3.32-t31-report.md:115`), NOT a free composition.
The corrected target beside the original (D-10): the arm is an injection
`⟪ 𝒟ₒ (Lset ω) ⟫ ↪ ⟪ ω ⟫` whose honest route is
least-code-per-class over the count's image; the count alone gives only
`Formula ⟪ Lset ω ⟫ 1 ↪ ⟪ ω ⟫`-conditional-on-the-IH, which is not the
arm.

### 1.2 `SquareLaw.Initial ω iω` does not exist: `Init ω` is uninhabited

- `Init α` requires `⟨ ω ∈ˢ α ⟩` (`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:947-953`),
  so `Init ω` would need `⟨ ω ∈ˢ ω ⟩`, refuted by `∈-irrefl`
  (`src/V/Hierarchy.lagda.md:155-156`). The probe proves this positively:
  `Initω-false : Init ω → Empty.⊥`.
- The honest bound at `ω` IS constructible from delivered pieces: the
  `InitialCore` hypotheses at `α = ω` are discharged by `CoreAtω`'s
  `ω-limit` (:795-801), `ω∉β` (:802-807, the basis of the square-form
  no-injection clause), and `finite-excl-ω` (:856-859). But the chapter
  never performs that instantiation and exports no `sq ω`, `bound-ω` or
  `initial-bound ω`: `rg "CoreAtω"` finds only the module definition at
  :793, and the top-level exports `initial-bound`/`initial-bound-inj`/
  `initial-square-law` (:1271-1279) are all parameterized by `Init α` with
  `ω ∈ α`. This is a T47 delivery-shape finding: the pieces are there, the
  instantiation at `ω` is not.

### 1.3 `CardinalCount.Bound` at `β = ω` is uninstantiable

`module Bound (β : S) (oβ : IsOrd β) (ω∈β : ⟨ ω ∈ˢ β ⟩) (pairing : sq β)`
(`src/L/CardinalCount.lagda.md:155-162`): at `β = ω` the `ω∈β` argument
would be `⟨ ω ∈ˢ ω ⟩`, refuted by the same `∈-irrefl`. The count at `ω`
needs a custom numeral route (`#∈ω`, `src/L/Ordinal.lagda.md:248-249`)
plus the re-derived pairing at `ω`.

### 1.4 The limit arm's "truncated square law" cannot supply an injection

`initial-square-law α iα : ∥ sq α ∥₁` is a proposition
(`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:1278-1279`); a truncated witness cannot
be eliminated into the function type `⟪ ω ⟫ × ⟪ ω ⟫ → ⟪ ω ⟫` that the union
injection needs, and `Init ω` (hence the truncated law at `ω`) does not
exist anyway (1.2). The union's members are pairs
`(stage n, x ∈ 𝒟ₒ (Lset n))` modulo value equality: `⋃ S` is a `sett` over
`Σ r ∈ ⟪ S ⟫ , ⟪ ⟪ S ⟫↪ r ⟫` (`Cubical/HITs/CumulativeHierarchy/
Constructions.agda:100-113`), so `⟪ Lset ω ⟫` is a double quotient, and an
injection into `ω` needs stage-and-code extraction plus the finite-stage
induction: the 1.1(vii) skeleton itself, not a single "union read at the
index level".

### 1.5 The negative control's ingredients ARE delivered, but not the ones cited

T57 cited `Lset-mono`, `Lset-in`/`Lset-out`
(`src/L/Axioms/Basic.lagda.md:52-54`, the import list). The arm's actual
near-free route uses `ord∈Lset-suc` (`src/L/Ordinal/Stages.lagda.md:434`,
"an ordinal appears at the stage after itself") with `Lset-mono`
(`src/L/Constructible.lagda.md:355-356`) and `#∈ω`; T57 did not name the
ordinal-stage fact, which is the load-bearing ingredient.

## 8. The next move

On the evidence measured, the next candidate technique for the exact
obligation that defeated this gate is a **least-of extraction over the
count's image, stated as a generic quotient-choice lemma**: for a countable
`X` and an injective `count : X ↪ ℕ`, an injection
`X / (ix ≡) ↪ ℕ` by least-code-per-class, where the class's count-fiber is
a proposition (count-injectivity), so the truncation eliminates into a
Sigma. That lemma, priced once, is the missing bridge for both the
successor arm (1.1) and the limit arm (1.4), and its price (roughly 80-150
lines on the delivered `leastOf`/`natSWO` machinery,
`src/L/WellOrder/Base.lagda.md:165-168`) should be probed before the
tower-induction row is re-booked. Two smaller fixes feed it: re-export the
`InitialCore`-at-`ω` instantiation from `SquareLaw` (or accept `Init` with
`ω ∈ α` replaced by `ω ⊆ α`, which makes `Init ω` inhabited), and give
`CardinalCount.Bound` a numeral-route parameter so the count runs at `ω`
without `ω ∈ ω`.
