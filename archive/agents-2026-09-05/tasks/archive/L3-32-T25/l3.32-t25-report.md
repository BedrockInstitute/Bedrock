## 2. D-10: what the face + switch supply, and what the successor step still needs

The gate's question is about the **witnesses**, not the statement. The probe

recorded ([l3.31-r2lever-report.md:8](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-r2lever-report.md)).

**What they cannot supply, and the mechanized boundary.** The successor step
needs the *definable power* `𝒟ₒ C = Lset (sucV ζ)` as a member of the next
block, one stage beyond the carrier's reach. The face's slice is bounded in
the carrier ([ProbeSatRel.agda:87-88](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/ProbeSatRel.agda),
`bound x h = U.defSet⊆A σ x h`), and `Lset (sucV ζ) ∉ Jset δ limδ` at the
refuted instance `ζ = ω`, `δ = ω·2` ([p2-fork-recon.md:32-33, :120-138](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/p2-fork-recon.md);
T4 §3). The initial-segment story is a range-read (second components of
pairs in the tower segments), so its slice collects reachable **stages**, not
the definable subsets of the stage; its union is the carrier's own stage, not
`𝒟ₒ C`. Devlin's successor formula is a different object, one that
internalizes satisfaction over the stage
(`x ∈ L_α ∧ ∃φ,δ [Fml(φ) ∧ δ ∈ L_α ∧ ∀z ∈ L_α (z ∈ x ↔ Sat(L_α, Sub(φ,v,z,δ)))]`,
[dev6.txt:1665-1678](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev6.txt)):
it needs codes over `C` and an internalized satisfaction, which the face does

forces `K = 𝒟ₒ C` by the two `Covers` directions (extensionality), i.e.
the conclusion; any other code map is the retiring coding machinery, whose
membership in the level is discharged only by CodePred's description
([CodePred.lagda.md:1748-1749](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/CodePred.lagda.md),
`At.codeSet∈J`; the per-code half at
[CodeSet.lagda.md:349-372](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/CodeSet.lagda.md)).

**D-10 verdict.** The face plus the unconditional switch supply the limit
case's membership; they do **not** supply the successor hypothesis. The
re-stated object's statement is code-free and its consumer assembly works,
but its witnesses (the relation `R ∈ Sset γ` with the right slices, and the
covering code set `K ∈ Sset γ`) are not constructible from the face + switch:
the slicing needs internal satisfaction over the constructible stage, the
covering needs a code set with its level membership. Both are the content the
coded machinery was built to supply.
