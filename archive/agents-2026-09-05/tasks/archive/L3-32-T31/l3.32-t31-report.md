## 3. The classical route, and the flagged risk

The route is the initial-segment route of Jech and Enderton that T21 measured
green: the canonical (Godel) well-ordering of `α × α` compares the maximum
first, then the first coordinate, then the second (Devlin's `<*` and Jech's
canonical well-ordering of `Ord × Ord`; the classical statement followed is
Jech's Theorem 3.5, that `κ · κ = κ` for every infinite cardinal, with the
reduction to the initial ordinal, `_build/literature/jech13.txt:1105-1106`
for the well-ordering). The chapter proves the order core conditional on the
four hypotheses and then discharges them where it can. The successor case of
the classical induction disappears, exactly as T21 predicted: the reduction
lands on an initial ordinal, where the order runs on a limit.

**The flagged risk bits.** T21's §4 priced "the transfer from `κ = |α|` back
to `α` (straight bijection plumbing `⟪ α ⟫² ≅ ⟪ κ ⟫²` via `α ≅ κ`)". The
least-of search (`Card.least`) returns the ordinal `κ` honestly and the
minimality honestly, but its equinumerosity witness is `∥ ⟪ α ⟫ ≃ ⟪ κ ⟫ ∥₁`,
a truncation. To transfer the pairing one needs an honest bijection
`⟪ α ⟫ ≃ ⟪ κ ⟫`; the truncation cannot be eliminated into the equivalence
type (the recursion principle for propositional truncation requires a
propositional codomain, and `A ≃ B` is not one), and no canonical bijection
exists to reconstruct (e.g. `⟪ ω ⟫ ≃ ⟪ ω + 1 ⟫` admits the shift and many
others). The two routes T21 named for a non-truncated witness both fail here:
an `∈`-induction carrying the bijection cannot construct it at the step
(the least-of data contains no honest witness to carry), and Schroeder-
Bernstein requires a second honest injection `α ↪ κ`, which is exactly the
missing object. The transfer therefore cannot be written, and the square law
at every non-initial limit ordinal (`ω + ω`, `ω · ω`, ...) and at every
initial ordinal containing one remains named. This is reported precisely
rather than worked around: the chapter does not postulate the bijection, does
not silently assume it, and names it as the remaining piece.
