## 1. The one lever: recover the witness from a strengthened induction, and run one segment engine on both towers

### 1.1 The mathematics, from first principles

The bridge carries two residues, and they are mirror images. `Q-lim` says
the L-stage at a limit is a member of the S-level above it
(`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:983-986`). `below-lim` says the S-level at a
limit is a member of the L-stage above it
(`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:992-994`). Each is proved the same way: carve
the target as a definable set over the carrier at the limit, where the
defining formula says "x lies in a value of a story witness". A story
witness is an initial segment of the other tower's recursion, held as a
set of pairs inside the carrier.

The witness family is the expensive object. The current below-lim build
constructs it by an inner recursion (`Rec` at
`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:2138-2149`, `rec` at `:3438-3439`). The
recursion's limit case rebuilds the ENTIRE formula stack at the nested
carrier `Wξ = Lset ξ`: the graph layer, the story formula, the rename
decode, and a fresh `defSet` for the pair family
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:2337-2604`). That stack is T240's measured
miniature: 589 lines at 0.297 s per line, the instantiation class
(`dev/ledger.toml:1406`). T241 profiled it and found the cost concentrated
in exactly these decode families (`dev/ledger.toml:1438`).

The brief's question 1 asks: can the witness be RECOVERED rather than
carried? The answer is yes at every nested limit, and no at the top.
Strengthen the theorem's conclusion. Instead of

> `P γ = ⟨ isLimit γ ⟩ → ⟨ Sset γ ∈ˢ Lset (sucV γ) ⟩`

(the current motive, `src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:144-145`), the induction
proves

> `P γ = ⟨ isLimit γ ⟩ → ⟨ Sset γ ∈ˢ Lset (sucV γ) ⟩ ×
> Σ[ P ∈ S ] (decode: p ∈ P ⟷ ∥Σ ζ ∈ γ, p ≡ pr ζ (Sset ζ)∥) × ⟨ P ∈ˢ Lset (sucV γ) ⟩`

The extra components are the pair family at γ, its meta-level decode, and
its placement. At the top the STEP must construct them anyway: the family
is `defSet` of the pair-family formula over `W = Lset γ`, placed by
`𝒟ₒ-intro` and `𝒟ₒ⊆Lsuc` (the delivered shape at
`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:2717-2718`). At every NESTED limit `ξ ∈ γ`,
the inner recursion then reads the family from the strengthened induction
hypothesis instead of rebuilding it. The whole nested formula stack dies:
no `StepGraph.Layer` at `Wξ`, no `storyFormWξ`, no rename, no `defSet` at
`Wξ`, no `belowAt` plumbing
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:2337-2346`, `:2356-2441`, `:2447-2604`,
`:3369-3421`). What survives of the limit case is meta-level content: the
generic `Segment` instantiation, the limit-clause proof, and the
placements (`:2617-2713`, `:2717-2857`).

This is P-n's one admissible move, executed: need less instantiation, by a
change of mathematical shape (`dev/LESSONS.md:2272-2295`). It is the same
move T185 made one level up, when the T182 wall became an application of
the theorem instead of separate content (`dev/ledger.toml:394`, the T185
entry). The lever applies T185's trick to the witness family itself.

### 1.4 The risks that kill it

1. **The Σ-valued motive at the theorem level.** The current theorem is an
   hProp; the strengthened motive is not. The precedent is `Rec'`, which
   already runs a non-truncated Σ through `∈-induction`
   (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:3358-3359`, `:3439`). The risk is a
   truncation escape: if any step must extract untruncated Σ data from
   under a `PT.rec`, the shape dies. Gate 1 decides this.
2. **The L-side clause telescope.** The L-story's successor atom concludes
   `b = 𝒟ₒ c` over the S-carrier. Its internal form is the blockPowLim
   relation's shape, which the route owes anyway
   (`dev/ledger.toml:445-455`). If the atom cannot be stated over
   `Sset (U l)` with a two-way decode, component (ii) dies and only
   component (i) survives, which is under the naive floor. This is the
   widest risk. Gate 2 decides it.
3. **P-r at the engine boundary.** The engine must keep hand-written
   decodes in checked positions; a clause-list fold costs 2.9x
   (`dev/LESSONS.md:2322-2356`). The delivered bundle already pays 18.4 s
   at the miniature. The engine's contract must be the telescope, not a
   fold the consumers unfold.
4. **D-10 truth check, done here.** The strengthened conclusion is true at
   the intended generality: the top construction is the delivered
   `defSet` shape, the decode's hard direction is the delivered chain
   (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:1988-2094`), and the recursion the decode
   needs is `rec`, which the landing owes regardless. No new mathematical
   obligation appears. The check found one asymmetry worth naming: the
   strengthened IH must place the family at a stage the nested consumer
   can climb from, `Lset (sucV ξ)`, and the climb to `Lset σ` is the
   delivered suc-six geometry (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:2819-2828`).

## 2. The partial refutation: there is no second 500-line lever

The brief names the two big rows as the only possible hosts. I searched
both, and the rest, from first principles. Everything except the lever
above is closed, and closed by measurement rather than by my judgment:

- **The carried sequence's own content, in place.** T230 measured the
  floor and stated it did not compress by contortion
  (`dev/ledger.toml:1216-1240`). T238 checked the three escapes (a
  five-clause story, a carrier move, a one-way decode) and all three fail
  (`dev/ledger.toml:1327-1336`). T241 profiled the seconds and returned
  NO-GO with every candidate law checked at the site
  (`dev/ledger.toml:1407-1438`). The lever above is the one shape T241
  itself names as the remaining alternative: a change of mathematical
  shape, not a cure at the site.
- **The blockpowlim row.** The covering half's ideal form is a measured
  wash: 1,120-1,275 against 1,379 delivered (T209, `dev/ledger.toml:454`).
  The member half is measured green and small: 243-303 (T214, same
  line). The seconds face fits at every caliber (T249,
  `dev/ledger.toml:1586-1606`). I examined the one first-principles
  question the gates did not ask: can a SUB-FAMILY of codes serve? No.
  `𝒟ₒ` quantifies over all arity-1 formulas over the carrier, so the
  covering set must cover the whole code type; a sub-family changes the
  theorem. The row is priced at its floor.
- **The three small rows.** w3-ac-slice 336-556, choice-rehome measured
  188-248 for the row scope, retirement-surgery 180-310
  (`dev/ledger.toml:309-319`, `:1554-1585`, `:614-624`). None can hold a
  500-line lever; the brief says so and the bands confirm it. The slice's
  shell and carve can consume the lever's engine as a third instance, for
  perhaps 50-150, and that riding is inside the engine's amortization,
  not a separate lever.
- **The base, Bridge, StepGraph.** All measured NO-GO at their own
  profiles: Bridge floor 24.3 s, StepGraph ceiling 30.2 s, the base cut
  NOT THERE (`dev/ledger.toml:1520-1553`, `:1439-1466`). Lines in
  delivered green modules buy nothing: standing is measured, not
  projected.

## 3. Considered and rejected

1. **Re-express the carve's story content as object-language codes**
   (the brief's prompt 3, taken literally). The carve must produce a
   `Formula ⟪ W ⟫ 1`, because `𝒟ₒ` is defined over meta formulas and
   `Lset (sucV γ)` IS `𝒟ₒ (Lset γ)`. Putting satisfaction over `W` inside
   that formula is the internalized twelve-clause table, the exact wall
   T130 named and T132/T140/T142 measured at delivered size, the cone D18
   retired (`dev/ledger.toml:479-502`, `:532-547`). The delivered Sat
   engine lives at the block carrier, not at a variable `W`. The
   exploitable content of the P-m contrast is FEWER instantiations
   (section 1), not a code re-expression. REJECTED, and this closes the
   brief's strongest hint in the direction the brief did not expect.
2. **A code-to-formula compiler, so story formulas are stated once as
   sets.** This is the excluded stepform-rider (`dev/ledger.toml:686-690`)
   plus a fold-shaped interpreter. The decode still elaborates at the
   carrier (P-n), and the compiler is a deeper fold than the one P-r
   measured at 2.9x. REJECTED.
3. **Derive Q-lim without any L-side story**, for example from `Q` at
   successors plus tower arithmetic. The reduction I checked: `Lset l` is
   the union of the family `{Lset δ : δ ∈ l}`, every member lies in
   `Sset (U l)` by `Q` below plus `Sset-⊆-mono`
   (`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:798`), and the block absorbs the finitely
   many rud steps above. The family as a MEMBER still needs internal
   collection, a stage has no replacement, and `defSet` is the only
   collection instrument at a stage. The story returns. What the check
   yields is the lever's joint-induction shape, not an escape. REJECTED
   as an independent lever; absorbed into section 1.
4. **Recover the top-level family too, so no formula stack remains
   anywhere.** The top family at γ must be a member of `Lset (sucV γ)`,
   and nothing below γ supplies it: the strengthened IH speaks only below.
   One instantiation per residue is the floor. REJECTED, and this bounds
   the lever's band from above.
5. **Identify `Sset γ` with `Lset γ` at matched levels and carve by
   comparison.** The per-level identification is FALSE in general,
   machine-checked by the witness `U ω ∈ γ ω` minus omega (T194,
   `dev/ledger.toml:394`), and the first instance's omega dodge is exactly
   what T182 found unsound at general limits. REJECTED; the campaign
   already paid for this answer once.
6. **Share the tower ENGINE itself across the two towers.** My own T213
   section 3 finding stands: `L.Rud.Hierarchy` is already the shared
   one-tower engine, and what is unshared is precisely the asymmetry
   (the definable power needs formula relativization; D-26 records why
   no syntax-free key exists on the Def side,
   `dev/LESSONS.md:1660-1682`). The lever shares the SEGMENT machinery,
   which sits above the asymmetry. Sharing below it is REJECTED again.
7. **An adequacy-combinator DSL for the ∃-prefix walks.** T213 rejected
   it at 250-400 carrier lines for too few sites; P-r has since measured
   the fold class at 2.9x. The rejection strengthens. REJECTED.
8. **Unify the three segment story proofs (zero, extension, limit) into
   one decode-driven module.** They inherit differently: `succRec` proves
   its clauses by inheritance from the sub-segment, which is cheaper than
   any decode-driven rederivation, and the zero case is 100 lines of
   `Empty.rec`. T213 section 3's verdict holds: the assembly unifies, the
   clause proofs are each their own mathematics. The engine of section 1
   parameterizes them by TOWER, which is orthogonal to this rejected
   unification by CASE. REJECTED.

## 5. Where my reading contradicts the brief

1. The brief's prompt 1 hopes the carried sequence might vanish whole.
   It cannot: at the top carrier the witness family is a `defSet` or it
   is not a member, because a stage owns no replacement. The honest
   target is ONE instantiation per residue, and that is what the lever
   delivers. The brief's floor of 500 naive lines is met by the lever
   only in its band's upper half at the naive caliber; at the calibrated
   caliber it is met everywhere. I state this rather than shaping the
   band upward.
2. The brief's prompt 3 suggests object-language re-expression makes the
   tree cheaper in both dimensions at once. At this route's sites the
   arrow points the other way (section 3, items 1 and 2). The P-m/T249
   contrast is real, and its usable content here is "instantiate less",
   not "encode more".
3. Nothing else in the brief conflicts with what I read in the tree.
