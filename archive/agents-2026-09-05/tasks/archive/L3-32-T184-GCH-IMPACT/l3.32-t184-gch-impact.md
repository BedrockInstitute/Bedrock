### 1.3 F3: the W3 slice as the carried order-story

**Verdict: HELPS the wing. It is a shared foundation, and it attacks the exact

internalized satisfaction, which is the `crossing-rebuild` cone at 5,000-5,100

- Wall 2, the unbounded producer descent. Each argument comparison becomes one

The internalized SATISFACTION never enters. So F3 severs W3's general level
from the crossing rebuild.

## 4. The net verdict

**The AC compression does not tax the wing. It pays the wing.** Measured on
the compression alone, the F series moves the GCH endpoint down by 3,109 to
3,599 naive and 3,435 to 4,669 calibrated; the wing's booked endpoint did rise

mis-measurement: by pulling `L.Rud.StepGraph` into the AC closure, F2 moved
1,912 lines that the wing's own W3 row names as its supplier out of the GCH
column, so the board now understates the wing by between 460 and 1,912 lines,
