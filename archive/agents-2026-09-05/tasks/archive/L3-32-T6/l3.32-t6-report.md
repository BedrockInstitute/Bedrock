## 0. Method, calibration, and verification

Read-only, zero Agda, no git, no master touched. Every size claim is measured
on today's tree with `awk` over ` ```agda ` fences (non-blank in-fence lines,
the pinned convention); every structural claim carries `file:line`. Probes are
designed and named, never run.

**Calibration convention (stated once, applied everywhere):** two calibers per
candidate, three numbers each (rewrite cost, immediate saving, continuation
saving).

- *Naive*: best-guess cost and saving from today's measured lines.
- *Calibrated*: cost x 1.3 for a candidate whose saving is **probed** (a
  measured probe of the same content exists: C1 via R5p, C3/C4 via measured
  occurrence counts), cost x 3 for **unprobed** (C2, C5, C7; LESSONS D-6,
  R5p section 3.3's split). Continuation savings are estimates, so they are
  divided by the same factor; immediate savings are measured line deltas and
  are kept constant.
- *Blast radius*: number of masters importing the edited module
  (`Everything.lagda.md` excluded, probes excluded; counted by grep), plus a
  wave-1 collision note. Radius has a floor of 1 for scoring.

**Territory verification:** the brief's 3,383 total and every per-module count
re-measure exactly (sum FOL 1,050; V 614; Base 213; the ZF interface layer
1,506). The layer is 29 masters in `src/Everything.lagda.md` (`:117-375`,
`:1097-1123`) plus consumers below it.

**Wave-1 collision check:** `L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md` (T1's in-flight edit)
imports `V.Hierarchy`, `V.Model`, `V.Coding`, `L.Definability`,
`L.Constructible`, `L.Ordinal`, `FOL.ZFStructure/Syntax/LevyHierarchy/
Semantics` (`Bridge.lagda.md:25-55`) but **no** `FOL.Manipulation.*` and **no**
`L.Axioms.*`. So C1 (Basic internals + additive `DefOf` exports), C3, C4, C5

*retrofit* is a separate question answered no for now (perf law P-series:
