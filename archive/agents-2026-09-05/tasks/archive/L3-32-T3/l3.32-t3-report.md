## 1. The bridge-step hypothesis: exact shape, and why

The miniature's single module hypothesis (`ProbeRehome.agda:58-60`):

```agda
bridgeL→J : (x : V ℓ) → ⟨ isL x ⟩
          → ∥ Σ[ γ ∈ V ℓ ] Σ[ limγ ∈ ⟨ isLimit γ ⟩ ] ⟨ x ∈ Jset γ limγ ⟩ ∥₁
```

This is the class-level isL→isJ step with `isJ` unfolded (`isJ x` is the
truncated ⋁ of "x sits in some limit level", ClassJ:57-58, :76-77); it is the
type the retired `Bridged.bridge-isL→isJ` used to carry, and the current tree
no longer derives it from `Matching` (next bullet), so the probe takes it as
the module hypothesis.  Why this shape is the natural one for the re-home:

- **It is the shape the current tree actually supports.**  The naive bridge
  identification `Matching` (limits: `Lset γ ≡ Jset γ limγ`) is recorded
  **false** in the current tree (Devlin VI.2.4's `ω·2` counterexample; Bridge
  prose :440-468), and `Bridged`, the module that derived isL→isJ from it, is
  **retired** (Bridge:456-468): `bridge-isL→isJ` is not in the tree.  What
  survives is the stage-level L→J clause `Reduce.p2` (`Lset β ∈ˢ Sset γ` for
  `β ∈ γ`, `γ` limit; Bridge:752) under `Reduce`'s four-hypothesis telescope
  (Bridge:697-701); the class-level step follows from `p2` + `+ω`
  (`+ω-limit`/`+ω-mem`, OrdBlocks) + `Sset-trans`, with `Jset γ _ = Sset γ`
  definitionally (Hierarchy:544-545).  The bridge's export-design question is
  therefore settled: surface the **class-level corollary of `p2`** (or `p2`
  itself with the `+ω` push), not `Matching`/`Bridged`, because the choice
  function consumes it exactly once, at the family.
- **One application bounds everything.**  `rawRehome` applies
  `bridgeL→J a ha` once; `Sset-trans` then bounds every cell and every member
  in the same level (Step:285-286, the probe's `bound`, 4 lines).  No
  per-cell or per-member hypothesis is needed: the census's "the J-side bound
  is freer" claim (§3.1, 0.01-0.03k) is confirmed and slightly strengthened
  (it is two lines, not a re-proof).
- **The truncation is inherent and honest.**  Both `isL` and `isJ` are
  truncated joins; taking a witness-level hypothesis would consume an
  unavailable strengthening.  The probe eliminates the truncation only into
  propositional targets (`PT.rec PT.squash₁`), so no honest loss.

Consumed-hypothesis ledger (all delivered, verified by the green check):

```agda

  → (inh : (x : S) → ⟨ x ∈ˢ a ⟩ → ∥ Σ[ y ∈ S ] ⟨ y ∈ˢ x ⟩ ∥₁)
```
