**Why a graph complete only at pairs would not have sufficed.** The adequacy
of the both-new arm runs through the minimal triple in both directions. The
encode direction (meta order implies formula satisfaction) must witness the
shape at the true minimal triple, which can carry a non-pair `v` for
k in {11..14} (nothing in the delivered tree forces the minimal triple of a
new member to be pair-valued; forcing it is itself a closure theorem about
`left`/`right`/`pr`, which is projection content). The decode direction
(formula satisfaction implies meta order) must turn the formula's
shape-based minimality into true minimality; for k in {11..14} at a non-pair
`v'`, the shape is unsatisfiable while `x = F_k(u', v')` can hold, so a
one-way graph leaves smaller true representations unrulled-out without a

The correction makes the question moot: StepInL's graphs are two-way at every
