## 2. The module's telescope

levels. The outer level, `L.Rud.StepStory` (`StepStory.lagda.md:30`):

- `(A : V ℓ)` — the rud universe's relativization slot, `Step`'s third
  parameter; the graph layer's `Fof` and `step` are built over it. It never
  appears in the clause content (P-h: the parameters stay abstract through

  every scale of the below-lim induction (the story formula is evaluated at

  parameter (`LevelKit C Ctr`) and the step atom's argument placement
  (a member of an argument drawn from `u` or equal to `u` lands in `C` by

The graph layer is the inner `Clause` telescope (`StepStory.lagda.md:79`):

  disjunction and its two-way decode (the archived `Slot` exports).

Why the layer is parameters rather than imports: the op graphs and the
frame live only in the frozen archive, and nothing may import across the

the two levels: the outer telescope's names (`isTransV`) are
level-polymorphic and importable before the module header, while the layer
types mention the structure's `∈ˢ`/`SM`/`⊨ᵐ`, which are only in scope once
the carrier is fixed and the kit opened. The archived `Slot` is nested
inside `StepInL` for the same reason; `Clause` is the same shape.

Everything else is content, not parameters: `sucAt`/`sucAt-ok` (the bounded
successor atom, carrier-generic ordinal content), `stepMem`/`stepMem-ok`
(the step atom and its decode through the embedded graph disjunction),
