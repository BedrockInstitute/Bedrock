## 2. D-10: does the clause admit a bounded rewrite at a general carrier?

**Yes, and the reason is the collapse, at every carrier.** The clause is read
at `f : SM`, a member of the carrier (`SM = Σ x, x ∈ˢ u`,
[Definability.lagda.md:77-88](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Definability.lagda.md)).
At that reading `DefStep u (fst f)` is unconditionally true, as shown above,
so `⊤̇` is a correct bounded rewrite with the two-way reading
([ProbeDefStep.agda:33-54](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/ProbeDefStep.agda)).
The brief's assumption that the collapse is "legitimate where the Def-step
collapses, at a level or at the class, but not at an arbitrary transitive set
carrier" does not survive the delivered `defSet⊤≡A`: the collapse needs no
level theorem and no transitivity. The level chapter's own `collapse`
([LevelFormula.lagda.md:798-800](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/LevelFormula.lagda.md))
runs through the stage theorem, but the direct witness `w = g`, `φ = ⊤̇` is
two lines at any carrier, which is evidence the collapse is a universal fact,
not a level fact. At the class carrier the same witness works for **every**
set, not only the constructible ones, so even the class-side framing in T64
(D4) was narrower than the truth.

**The non-collapsed form is not expressible, but it is not needed.** The
honest quantifier structure "there is `w ∈ u` with `f ∈ Def w`" cannot be
written as a Delta-0 formula over `⟪ u ⟫`. No term of the object language
denotes `u` itself: constants are members of `u`, variables range over members
of `u`, and every bounded quantifier ranges over members of a member of `u`
(the restricted carrier is the members of `u`), while `u ∉ u` by
`∈-irrefl` ([V/Hierarchy.lagda.md:155](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/V/Hierarchy.lagda.md)).
And "`f ∈ Def w`" with `w` free would have to range over
`Formula ⟪ w ⟫ 1`, the formulas of the inner world, which are not members of
`u` in general. So the quantification over definable subsets that the brief
worried about cannot be bounded by the carrier's own members. The clause never
needs that form at the reading: since every member of the carrier is definable
in itself, the quantification is vacuous, and `⊤̇` is the correct rewrite.

**Consequence: T64's premise is refuted.** T64 claimed that at an arbitrary
transitive set carrier `M`, "`DefStep M f` is real content of the
satisfaction-recursion class". The face's Def-step read collapses everywhere.
The satisfaction-recursion content T64 named (`StepAt`, `DefAt`,
`satGraphAt`, ...) is real, but it lives in the delivered description's
object-level combinators ([Sequence.lagda.md:119-120,281-282](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/Sequence.lagda.md),
[Powerset.lagda.md:437-443](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/Powerset.lagda.md)),
which the face's `Cl` does not consume: the equivalence row (c) does. The
ivprobe's original gate pointed at exactly that object-level unit, "one
bounded rewriting of `DefAt` plus its equivalence"
([ivprobe-report.md:630-634](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ivprobe-report.md));
that unit remains real and unmeasured, and it belongs to row (c), not to the
face's clause.
