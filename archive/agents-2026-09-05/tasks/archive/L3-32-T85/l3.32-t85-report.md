## Verdict (one paragraph)

**The re-designed gate is GREEN, and it measures the widest term's successor
half at a generic live instance.** There IS a cheap inhabited instance: not a
concrete ordinal (the next inhabited `Init` after the refuted `ω` is
uncountable and nothing about it is concrete), but the generic `Init α`
itself, with the successor and lower-injection steps written schematically at

`⟪ Lset α ⟫ ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` at every `Init α`) builds from delivered pieces only:
the count at a variable `β = α` (`Bound.formula-bound`), the opaque delivered
square law (`Initial.square`), the delivered membership characterization of
the definable operator (`𝒟ₒ-inv`), and the delivered least-of machinery
(`ordSWO` + `leastOf`). No concrete carrier, no numeral, no quotient
identification, no `Init ω`, and no pairing at `ω` appears anywhere: all
three of T59's walls are sidestepped, and the two refuted premises are
verified (D-10) but carry no weight in the design. The probe
(`src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda`) checks GREEN in 136 non-blank lines, 12.8 s
whole-file wall on the first check (2.6 s warm), one process,
`GHCRTS=-M8g`; the generic lower injection
(negative control, `α ⊆ Lset α` at every `Init α`) is included at 18 lines.
The limit step is NOT measured: its honest route (stage-and-code extraction
through the union's double quotient, with cross-stage transport stability of
the count) remains the recorded wall class (T59 section 1.4), priced in
section 6. Ledger: the tower-induction row's successor half and lower
injection move from x3 to x1.3 (composition of delivered machinery, measured);
the limit half keeps x3. The row does not collapse to the probe's 136 lines.

## 1. Is there a cheap inhabited instance?

**Yes, and it is the generic `Init α` instance, not a concrete ordinal.**

The first gate chose `ω` for concreteness and died because `Init ω` is
uninhabited (verified below, section 2). The next ordinal where `Init` holds
is uncountable and the tree carries no uncountable ordinal construction
(T62 section 1 records the classical reading: every uncountable initial
ordinal satisfies `Init`; the tree never asserts one). So the cheap instance
has to be the hypothesis `Init α` itself, and the probe proves the step is
written without any concrete carrier:

- **Successor step**, the gate's target: for every `α : S`, every
  `iα : Init α`, and every IH `g : ⟪ Lset α ⟫ ↪ ⟪ α ⟫`, an injection
  `⟪ Lset (sucV α) ⟫ ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` (`successor-step`,
  `src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:155-159`). The proof is a composition of
  delivered pieces at the variable `α`; no numeral, ordinal, or quotient
  relation is named anywhere in the module (`Successor`,
  `src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:99-149`).
- **Lower injection** (the negative control, generalized from T59's `ω`
  arm): for every `Init α`, `⟪ α ⟫ ↪ ⟪ Lset α ⟫` (`lower-inj`,
  `src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:70-94`), 18 non-blank lines.

The instance is inhabited in the classical reading (every uncountable
initial ordinal), and the step is built conditional on the hypothesis, so
the probe never needs a concrete witness. This is exactly the D29-shaped
trade: concreteness is exchanged for genericity, and the measurement is the
rate of the mechanism, not the cost of one numeral.

The weakened-at-`ω` candidate (section 3) is subsumed: the generic step is
strictly stronger than anything that holds at `ω` alone, and it avoids the
concrete pairing-injectivity wall that killed the `ω` instantiation.

## 2. D-10 verification of both refuted premises

Both premises were verified empirically in this dispatch before any design
work, at the delivered tree and the installed cubical library:

**Premise 1: `⟪ 𝒟ₒ A ⟫` is NOT definitionally `Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1`.**
Confirmed two ways. (a) Negative: the identity
`(A : S) → ⟪ 𝒟ₒ A ⟫ → Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1`, body `x = x`, fails with
`UnequalTerms`; the checked message shows the member type normalizing to
`sett-repr X ix .fst .fst` (the set quotient), not to `Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1`
(scratch probe `src/ProbeT85neg.agda`, deleted after the verdict). (b)
Positive: at the transparent level, `⟪ DefOf.Def A ⟫` IS definitionally
`Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1 / (λ φ ψ → defSet A φ ≊ defSet A ψ)`: the identity
`D10-good A x = x` typechecks as `refl` (`src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:61-63`).
The library shape matches the report: `⟪ X ⟫ = V-repr X .fst .fst`
(`Cubical/HITs/CumulativeHierarchy/Properties.agda:220-224`) and
`sett-repr X ix` has index `X / Kernel` with `Kernel x y = ix x ≊ ix y`
(same file :146-153). The opacity of `𝒟ₒ`
(`src/L/Constructible.lagda.md:202-205`) hides the quotient from consumers;
the design below therefore never names it, and instead uses the delivered
membership characterization `𝒟ₒ-inv`
(`src/L/Constructible.lagda.md:306-308`).

**Premise 2: `Init ω` is UNINHABITED.** Confirmed by a positive proof:
`Initω-false : Init ω → Empty.⊥` with `Initω-false i = ∈-irrefl ω (i .snd .fst)`
(`src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:49-50`), GREEN. `Init`'s second conjunct is the
strict membership `⟨ ω ∈ˢ α ⟩` (`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:956-963`),
so `Init ω` needs `ω ∈ ω`, refuted by `∈-irrefl`
(`src/V/Hierarchy.lagda.md:155-156`).

Neither premise is load-bearing for the re-designed gate: the successor step
needs `sq α` (delivered opaque at every `Init α` via `Initial.square`), the
count at a variable `β = α` (delivered `Bound`), and the membership
characterization (delivered `𝒟ₒ-inv`), and no identification between members
and formulas anywhere.

## 4. The re-designed gate, and what it measures

The gate is one dispatch, one probe, stop-line 250 non-blank lines, with
three pieces: the D-10 checks (section 2), the generic lower injection, and
the generic successor step. All three are GREEN in
`src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda` (136 non-blank lines total).

**Why the successor step is buildable at a variable `α` when the `ω`
instance died.** T59's successor arm died on three independent walls:
`Bound` at `β = ω` needs `ω ∈ ω` (T59 section 1.3); the re-derived pairing's
injectivity at the concrete `ω` shape walled at check time (T59 section 3,
R-35/R-38 class); and the count-to-formula bridge needs a representative
choice per `defSet`-class because `⟪ 𝒟ₒ A ⟫` is the quotient
(T59 section 1.1). The re-designed step sidesteps all three:

1. **The count runs at `β = α`, a variable.** `Bound` is delivered as a
   module parameterized by `β` (`module Bound (β : S) ...`,
   `src/L/CardinalCount.lagda.md:155-162`); at `β = α` its hypotheses are
   discharged by `Init α` (`oα = iα .fst`, `ω∈α = iα .snd .fst`) and the
   square law is `Initial.square α iα` (the opaque delivered `sq α`,
   `src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:1251, :1271`). `Bound.formula-bound`
   (`src/L/CardinalCount.lagda.md:257-265`) then yields
   `Formula ⟪ Lset α ⟫ 1 ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` from the IH `g`. No `ω ∈ ω`, no concrete
   pairing normalization: the opaque seal hides the pairing's body.
2. **The quotient is never named.** Members of `⟪ 𝒟ₒ (Lset α) ⟫` are
   handled at the membership level: `member (𝒟ₒ (Lset α)) m` gives
   `⟨ ⟪ 𝒟ₒ (Lset α) ⟫↪ m ∈ˢ 𝒟ₒ (Lset α) ⟩`, and the delivered
   characterization `𝒟ₒ-inv`
   (`src/L/Constructible.lagda.md:306-308`, opened in the `unfolding 𝒟ₒ`
   block) turns it into `∥ Σ[ φ ∈ Formula ⟪ Lset α ⟫ 1 ]
   (defSet (Lset α) φ ≡ x) ∥₁`. This is the project's established pattern
   (`src/L/Ordinal/Stages.lagda.md:222` uses `𝒟ₒ-inv` the same way).
3. **The representative choice is the delivered least-of.** For each member
   `m`, the class predicate
   `P y = ∥ Σ[ φ ] (defSet φ ≡ x × count φ ≡ y) ∥₁`
   (`class-pred`, `src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:109-113`) is nonempty by
   `𝒟ₒ-inv`, and `leastOf` over the delivered well-order on `⟪ α ⟫`
   (`ordSWO α oα`, `src/L/Ordinal/Pairing.lagda.md:106-107`; `leastOf`,
   `src/L/WellOrder/Base.lagda.md:165-167`) picks the least count value in
   the class. Injectivity (`h-inj`, `src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:124-148`) uses
   only count-injectivity (to identify the representatives) and `↪-inj`
   (to identify the members), so no quotient eliminator and no transport
   through `𝒟ₒ`'s structure is needed.
4. **The tower transport is one line.** `Lset-suc : Lset (sucV σ) ≡
   𝒟ₒ (Lset σ)` (`src/L/Axioms/Basic.lagda.md:196`), and
   `successor-step` transports the operator-level injection along
   `sym (Lset-suc α)` (`src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:157-159`).

**The generic lower injection** (`lower-inj`, `src/ProbeTowerInd2.agda:70-94`)
generalizes T59's `ω` control: `ord∈Lset-suc` places each member at the stage
after itself (`src/L/Ordinal/Stages.lagda.md:434`), the successor-closure
clause of `Init` (`iα .snd .snd .fst`) puts that stage inside `α`, and
`Lset-mono` (`src/L/Constructible.lagda.md:355-356`) lifts it into `Lset α`.

**One wall class was hit and cured during the build, and it is a recorded
law, not a new one.** The first version of `lower-inj` did not terminate
(killed at >60 s for `lower-inj` alone, >3.5 min for the whole probe), on an
unpinned implicit of `mem-ord` (`mem-ord : ∀ {A} → IsOrd A → ...`,
`src/L/Ordinal.lagda.md:221-224`): unification must invert `A` through the
defined function `IsOrd`, the I-4 trap (`dev/LESSONS.md:1178-1184`). Pinning
`{A = α}` at the call site drops the whole file to 12.8 s. The project's own
style already pins this argument at the one delivered call site
(`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:1101-1102`); this probe re-confirms I-4 at
a second site, it does not mint a new entry.

## 6. The limit step: honest route and price

The limit step is `⟪ Lset α ⟫ ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` at a limit `Init α`, from the IH over
members. It was NOT built: its honest route is the stage-and-code extraction
through the union's double quotient that T59 recorded as the 1.1(vii)
skeleton's wall class (T59 section 1.4). The membership-level route that made
the successor step cheap still applies on the way in: `Lset-out`
(`src/L/Constructible.lagda.md:336-338`) gives
`∥ Σ[ δ ] (⟨ δ ∈ˢ α ⟩ × ⟨ x ∈ˢ 𝒟ₒ (Lset δ) ⟩) ∥₁`, and `𝒟ₒ-inv` at
`(Lset δ)` names the definability witness. The wall is the injectivity
half, which needs three new pieces:

1. A per-stage count uniform in `δ`: `Bound.formula-bound` at `β = α` with
   the composed IH `⟪ Lset δ ⟫ ↪ ⟪ δ ⟫ ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` (the second injection from
   `oα`'s transitivity plus `fiber`). ~40 lines of instantiation; the
   machinery is delivered.
2. A **disjoint-union count** `Σ[ m ∈ ⟪ α ⟫ ] Formula ⟪ Lset (⟪ α ⟫↪ m) ⟫ 1
   ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` (pack stage and code through the delivered pairing), whose
   injectivity needs the count and `defSet` to be stable under transport
   along stage paths `δ₁ ≡ δ₂`. That stability is the genuinely new raw
   material: transported memberships and presentations in compared statement
   positions are the R-35/R-37 wall class (`dev/LESSONS.md:768-793, :871-888`),
   and the union representation is meta-poisoned (R-35). ~80-150 lines,
   wall-class risk, probe before booking.
3. The union-level least-of over the class predicate, reusing the successor
   module's machinery. ~30 lines.

Naive price: ~150-220 lines; at wall-class x3, ~450-660 calibrated. The
recommended next dispatch is a probe aimed at piece 2 alone (the
transport-stability lemma at a variable stage path), since that is the
widest unmeasured term of the limit half. The `ω` instance of the limit step
(`⟪ Lset ω ⟫ ↪ ⟪ ω ⟫`) additionally needs finite-stage counts
(`⟪ Lset (# n) ⟫ ↪ ℕ`) that are not delivered, so the concrete instance is
not cheaper than the schematic one here.
