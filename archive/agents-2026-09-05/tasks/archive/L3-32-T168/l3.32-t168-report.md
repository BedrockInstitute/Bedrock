### 2.3 Library re-proofs

No candidate. The tree imports the standard lemmas it needs. It uses
`znots`, `snotz`, `injSuc`, `+-comm`, `·-comm`, `inj-m+`, `inj-toℕ`,
`toℕ<n` and `toFromId'` directly (`src/FOL/Count.lagda.md:31-33`,
`src/L/WellOrder/Combinators.lagda.md:31`, `src/L/Rud/HF.lagda.md:68-70`).
The local `isProp*` and `isSet*` lemmas are domain-specific. They prove
properties of local records and predicates (`isPropIsOrd`,
`isPropHostEq`, `isPropPowRel`). The Cubical library exports no equivalent
for them.

The `SWO` bundle (`src/L/WellOrder/Base.lagda.md:50-56`) is a local record.
The Cubical library exports no matching well-order record. `natSWO`
(`:197`), `unitSWO`, `prodSWO`, `sumSWO` and `listSWO` instantiate that
record. They are not re-proofs.
