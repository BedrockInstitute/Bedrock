## 1. What is delivered

The chapter is classical (module telescope `(lem : LEM (ℓ-suc ℓ))`, matching
`L.Ordinal.Linear` and `L.Ordinal.Stages`), and its main content is
parameterized by any ordinal `α` with `oα : IsOrd α`; the infinitude
hypothesis `⟨ ω ∈ˢ α ⟩` of the probe is not needed anywhere in the delivered
part, which is pure order theory. In detail:

1. **The order of an ordinal's members.** `_≺₁_` orders the index `⟪ α ⟫` by
   the membership of the sets the indices name. `ordSWO : SWO ⟪ α ⟫` bundles
   trichotomy (from `ord-tri`, transported along the injective embedding),
   irreflexivity (`∈-irrefl`), transitivity (the transitivity half of
   ordinality) and well-foundedness (regularity pulled back). This is an
   instance of the delivered `L.WellOrder.Base` bundle.
2. **The maximum.** `maxOrd` picks the larger coordinate by trichotomy;
   `max-spec` certifies the name (`m ≤₁ maxOrd m n`, `n ≤₁ maxOrd m n`).
3. **The canonical well-ordering of the product.** `_≺_` on `Pair =
   ⟪ α ⟫ × ⟪ α ⟫` compares the maximum first, then the first coordinate, then
   the second, exactly Devlin's `<*` and Jech's canonical well-ordering of
   `Ord × Ord` (citations in §5). `godSWO : SWO Pair` certifies all four laws.
   Well-foundedness reuses the bundle's lexicographic product (`prodSWO`,
   `prodSWO ordSWO` twice) pulled back along the injection
   `(a , b) ↦ (maxOrd a b , (a , b))`, with `subrel` proving the direct order
   implies the pulled-back lex order. The order is decided (`≺-dec`, via the
   trichotomy-based `≺₁-dec`/`≡₁-dec`); this is the filter the collapse needs.
4. **The collapse.** `col : Pair → S` is the well-founded recursion on `≺`
   whose step unions, over all pairs, the successor of the recursive value
   where the pair lies below the current one and the empty set elsewhere
   (`colStep`, with the small-index filter `colPick`). It is sealed `opaque`
   at birth with `col-compute` inside the seal, the rank chapter's pattern.
   `col-ord` says every collapse is an ordinal; `col-mono` says the collapse
   increases strictly along the order; `col-inj` says it is injective (two
   equal collapses would force an ordinal to belong to itself).
5. **The order type, exactly.** `τ` is the union of the successors of the
   collapses, an ordinal (`τ-ord`); every collapse lies in it (`col∈τ`), so
   `col→τ : Pair → ⟪ τ ⟫` injects (`col→τ-inj`). The surjectivity half,
   `col-img` (a member of a collapse is itself a collapse, by descent along
   the well-order) and `col-surj` (every member of `τ` is a collapse), give
   `col→τ-surj`, so the product's index **bijects onto `⟪ τ ⟫`**: `τ` is the
   order type of the canonical well-ordering, certified rather than asserted.
6. **The pairing, conditional on the bound.** `Pairing.bound : ⟪ τ ⟫ → ⟪ α ⟫`
   with `bound-inj` is the one remaining hypothesis; given it, `Pairing.pair`
   and `Pairing.pair-inj` have exactly the probe's `TupleCode` shape.

once the bound theorem lands in a later chapter.

## 4. The missing bound, priced in both calibers

The order type of the canonical well-ordering of `α × α` is, classically,
`α · α` (this is why the collapse alone cannot land inside `α`; for example at
`α = ω·2` the collapse of `(ω , ω)` is `ω·3`, outside `α`). The theorem that
completes the pairing is the classical square law: **for every infinite
ordinal `α`, `α × α` is equinumerous to `α`** (equivalently, `κ·κ = κ` for
every infinite cardinal `κ`, Jech Theorem 3.5, Hessenberg 1906). Its textbook
proof needs ordinal arithmetic the tree does not carry:

- ordinal addition and multiplication as set recursions on the well-founded
  member order (the chapter's collapse machinery is exactly the recursion
  pattern these need, but the operations and their laws are absent);
- the doubling lemma for infinite ordinals (an injection `β ⊎ β ↪ β`), and the
  base case on `ω` (Cantor's enumeration of `ℕ × ℕ`);
- the reduction of an arbitrary infinite ordinal to its cardinal (the least
  equinumerous initial ordinal), to apply the cardinal theorem at `κ = |α|`.

**Pricing (naive):** addition and multiplication with the laws the proof
needs, 90-130 lines; the doubling lemma and the `ω` base case, 130-200 lines;
the initial-ordinal/cardinality reduction, 80-130 lines. Total **300-460 naive
lines** beyond this chapter. **Calibrated:** the recursion-and-collapse parts
are anchored by this chapter's delivered pattern (1.3x); the initial-ordinal
theory is survey-class (3x). Calibrated band roughly **500-950 lines**. The
estimate is survey-class throughout (no probe has priced the arithmetic), and
per the two-caliber discipline the wide band is the honest price of that
