## THE OWNER'S QUESTION, and it is now the centre of this task

The orchestrator wrote that the interleaving is "paid at every limit,
TWICE". The owner asked the question that phrase deserves:

> **Is that mathematically necessary, or is it an engineering problem?**

**Answer it.** The orchestrator's own reading follows, stated so you can
refute it rather than inherit it. It is reasoning, not measurement.

**"At every limit" looks like mathematics.** At a limit the level is a
union, and definability over that level needs its parameters IN the
level. The recursion's history is not otherwise present there, so a
witness for the family below must exist at the level. That is why the
witness family exists at all.

**But "rebuilt at every limit" is not the same claim as "needed at
every limit", and the difference is where `[T250]` found its lever:**
the family can be RECOVERED from a strengthened induction hypothesis
instead of reconstructed. So part of what looks necessary is already
known to be engineering.

**"Twice" looks like engineering and the orchestrator suspects it is.**
Q-lim and below-lim are mirror images of one fact about how the two
towers interleave. The classical treatments prove such interleavings by
ONE induction that carries both directions together. This project
proves them as two residues, each paying its own full formula-stack
instantiation. `[T250]` noticed the same thing and proposed stating
them jointly over one induction on limits.

**So the question to settle is precise:** how much of the 894-899 s is
forced by the mathematics of definability at a limit, and how much is
this project having chosen two inductions, two witnesses and two
instantiations where one would serve?

**Say which, with reasons.** If the answer is "the twice is
