## THE FIVE CLAIMS, WITH THE EVIDENCE FOR EACH

Attack each one. State your verdict as **HOLDS**, **WEAK** or **BROKEN**, and

### 1. The profile-to-wall ratio is 1.0

The ledger said 0.6 to 0.7 for months. The orchestrator projected 11.3 minutes
for a tree that measures 18.0, and then measured both ways back to back:
un-instrumented 1,080.6 s, instrumented 1,074.3 s with a profile total of
1,072.2 s. Evidence: `dev/ledger.toml`, the `[timing]` block, key
`wall_vs_profile`.

**Attack:** two runs on one machine on one day. Could load, caching or the
order of the runs produce that? Does any other measurement in the repository
contradict a ratio of 1.0? The old 0.615 came from somewhere; find where and
say whether that source was wrong or was measuring a different thing.

### 2. The re-home IS the internalization cone

`[T129]` stopped rather than build, and refuted `[T67]`'s band. It measured
2,105 in-fence lines in the cone and 6,381 as the cluster bound, against a
500-line stop. Evidence: `_build/l3.32-t129-report.md`.

**Attack:** T129 re-measured T67's own slice at 243 against T67's 242, so the
slice agrees. The claim is that the slice is not the whole. **Is the cone it
walked the true dependency cone, or did it over-collect?** A cone that follows
every import edge, rather than the edges the consumer needs, is too big. Check
what `L.Condensation` actually uses.

### 3. The rud side cannot serve the crossings

`[T130]` refuted the cheapest escape, read-only, on two grounds: the index
question, and that `StepStory`'s clause ends at the S-tower step and never the
L-tower step. Evidence: `_build/l3.32-t130-report.md` section 1.

**Attack:** this is the one that saved the most money if wrong. **Is the
S-versus-L mismatch real at every carrier, or only at the class carrier?** If
some carrier makes them agree, the escape reopens.

### 4. The adequacy term is 1,630 to 1,940, not 700 to 900

`[T132]` closed one arm green at 174 lines and then found the estimate in the
wrong class, because `graphAt-holds` and `graphAt-unique` are proved through
the table, slot, sound and unique machinery, which is 1,866 delivered lines.
Evidence: `_build/l3.32-t132-report.md` sections 1 and 4.

**Attack:** the delivered proof goes through that tail. **Must a FRESH proof
go through it too?** D17 says price the ideal form, not the port. If a fresh
proof of those two readings avoids the tail, the whole re-price collapses and
D31 gets cheaper.

### 5. The AC trophy is 10,026 lines

`[T135]` computed it from the import graph and the owner corrected it from
22,680 by excluding the retirement set. Evidence: `dev/ledger.toml`, the
`[[trophy_split]]` block, and `scripts/ledger.py --trophy-split`.

**Attack:** ten rud modules are ambiguous and the rule puts them in the shared
part. **Does that rule flatter the AC number or inflate it?** Also check the
GCH root rule: GCH has no delivered statement, so the closure starts from
modules the route assigns. Is that circular?
