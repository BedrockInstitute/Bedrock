# [L3.32-T48] Is W3's internal order reachable at all, and at what price?

**Verdict in one paragraph.** W3 is reachable, and the cheapest route is not
the one the recorded verdict implies. The general-level order formula is
blocked on three expressibility gaps, but the fresh generic face closes one of
them (the level predicate) in a way that did not exist when W3 was scoped, and
the classical uniform-Σ₁ formulation (SZ 1.11(2)) flattens the producer-lex
recursion that T44 recorded as needing the retired satisfaction cone, at the
price of the family-table-as-set and the unmeasured op-leastness decode. So the
honest answer has three parts. (1) The face route is real but is W3's booked
band with the corrected placement, not a discount: roughly 1.0-2.7k naive /
3.0-7.9k calibrated, with the op-leastness content still unmeasured at ×3. (2)
The revival route costs the cone (6,254 lines, L/Coding minus Base) plus the
Choice order-side (about 5,860), about 12.1k lines back into a configuration
whose endpoint arithmetic (standing 12,633, endpoint 19.85-26.87k naive) passes
only because that code retires; keeping it moves the endpoint to roughly 32-39k
naive, a 7-14k breach of the owner's 25k line. (3) The consumers' need is
genuine for exactly one GCH-critical object, the hull's full elementarity at
hull parameters; the smallestness residue, the condensation part (iii), and
the counting/enumeration consumers can each be served by weaker or different
machinery, and T40's second obstruction (deconstantification) is outdated
because FOL.Manipulation.Parameters delivers exactly the constants-to-variables
face. **Recommendation:** dispatch the gate no batch has run, the
tower-of-relations (sequence-witness) formulation of the uniform order at the
first non-vacuous level `sucV (sucV ∅)`, before any revival or reformulation
is ruled; the runner-up is the scope ruling that deletes everything but the
hull elementarity from W3's consumer list.

## 1. Q1: the obstruction, precisely

### 1.1 What is not expressible

The recorded targets and their corrections are T44's D-10 record
([l3.32-t44-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t44-report.md:104-147)),
and the chapter's own record restates them
([OrderFormula.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/OrderFormula.lagda.md:69-119)).
In this development's vocabulary the obstruction is three gaps, not one:

1. **No internalized level predicate and no function symbols (B1).** The
   uniform formula `σ< : Formula ⟪ Sset α ⟫ 2` at a variable level needs
   stage-quantified clauses ("x lies in the level at `δ`", "x is fresh at
   `δ`"), which need the term `Sset δ` with `δ` bound. The object language is
   `Formula K n` with constants and variables only, no function symbols
   ([Syntax.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Syntax.lagda.md:150-161)),
   and no level predicate exists in the tree. The same gap is recorded for the
   code set: "a formula of the object language whose definable set is the code
   set", with the cofinality argument showing why no finite offset can hold it
   ([CodeSet.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/CodeSet.lagda.md:22-31,
   :380-420)).
2. **The producer lex is not finitely expressible (B2).** The same-stage
   clause compares the least producers lexicographically, by the operation
   index and then by the arguments, and the argument comparison descends
   through the producer trees without bound; the descent is well-founded but
   of unbounded depth, so a fixed finite formula cannot express it directly.
   The recorded correction: "a finite formula cannot express the descent
   without the internalized satisfaction, which the tree does not carry and
   which the retiring cone paid its whole cost for"
   ([OrderFormula.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/OrderFormula.lagda.md:85-93)).
3. **The successor-offset placement is false (B3).** The interface's first
   field `⟨ w ∈ˢ Sset (sucV α) ⟩` is the same-index discharge the bridge
   already records as false

   :141-142)); the tower absorbs definable subsets only at limit levels. The
   corrected placement is a limit level, delivered as a pattern by the carve
   ([OrderFormula.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/OrderFormula.lagda.md:95-106,
   :372-386)).

What survives the check, built and green in T44, is the expressible instance at

## 2. Q2: the face route

### 2.1 What the face supplies, exactly

The initial-segment face is the campaign's fresh internalized level story:
`σ(x) := ∃f ∈ K(u)[Ap(f) ∧ Cl(f,x) ∧ Rg(f,x)]` with the approximation entry
left in the adequacy telescope
([InitialSegment.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/InitialSegment.lagda.md:120-152,
:184-231)). Its two delivered instantiations are the level formula at the real
tower, `read-off : ⟨ ⟪ u ⟫↪ m ∈ˢ defSet σ ⟩ ⟷ Elem m` for `u = Lset α`
([LevelFormula.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/LevelFormula.lagda.md:872-877)),
and the level sigma at a rud carrier for `u = Jset α`
([LevelSigma.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:489-495)).

Two things follow for the order formula.

First, the face closes gap B1's level-predicate half. "x is a constructible
level" is delivered with two-way adequacy. The membership "x ∈ Lset δ" is a
short step further: pin the witness's domain to `δ` in the delivered `domForm`
and read the value. The ingredient formulas are all delivered (functionhood,
the zero clause, `domForm`, `limitForm`, `isOrdAt`
([LevelFormula.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/LevelFormula.lagda.md:194-273))),
but the relation "x ∈ Lset δ" itself and its two-way decode are new work,
anchored on the delivered domain-bound decode
([LevelFormula.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/LevelFormula.lagda.md:530-640)).
At the rud carrier the same relation needs the two clauses LevelSigma measured
as omitted, the limit clause and the ordinal domain bound, reported as such
with their prices carried over from the tower chapter
([LevelSigma.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:22-26,
:511-515)). At the S-tower, the carrier the delivered order lives on, "x ∈
Sset δ" needs in addition the operation-graph formulas (below). Estimate for
the relation plus decode: 100-250 naive at the L-tower, plus 100-200 for the
rud/S-tower instances, all ×3 unmeasured (the tower chapter's decode prices
carry over per LevelSigma's own report).

Second, the face does **not** supply gap B2's satisfaction half. The face's
adequacy is meta-level: a satisfied object clause is interchangeable with a
meta-level reading
([InitialSegment.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/InitialSegment.lagda.md:184-231)).
There is no satisfaction predicate the object language can quantify over, so
the direct producer-lex descent is exactly as unexpressible as T44 recorded.
The only way the face route survives B2 is the flattening of section 1.2(b):
write the order as "there is a correct tower of relations ending at β with
(x,y) in the last entry". Its prerequisites, each named with a price:

1. **The operation-graph formulas "x = Fof i u v" in variables.** What is
   delivered is the constant-arguments form: for the described operations a Δ₀
   formula over `⟪ A ⟫` whose satisfaction characterizes membership in the
   image, closed by `defSet Φ ≡ Fof i a b`
   ([Describe.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Describe.lagda.md:1-15,
   :390-423), [Switch.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Switch.lagda.md:394-465)).
   The variable form is a per-operation Δ₀ relation, new work, estimated 5-15
   lines per operation, 80-240 naive total, ×3.
2. **The correct-tower clause and the sequence witness.** The formula "f is a
   sequence of relations, `r_0 = ∅`, `r_{γ+1} = clause(r_γ)`, `r_λ = union`"
   reuses the face's functionhood and ordinal-domain machinery but is a new
   object with its own two-way decode: 100-250 naive, ×3.
3. **The family table as a set.** The existential over `f` must be witnessed
   in the model, and the sequence of order elements is SZ 1.11(1), whose

   ([l3.32-t42-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t42-report.md:120-141)).
   The limit-level carve pattern is delivered
   ([OrderFormula.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/OrderFormula.lagda.md:372-386)),
   so the discharge is a build on delivered machinery, 50-150 naive, ×3.
4. **The adequacy, both directions.** Formula satisfaction ⟷ delivered
   `Sset-below`. The soundness direction walks the correct tower into the
   delivered order facts (`order-agrees`, `old-before-new`,
   [Order.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Order.lagda.md:861-876,
   :995-1006)); the completeness direction needs the decode of the
   least-producer conjuncts against the key, which is the op-leastness content

   [l3.32-t19-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t19-report.md:189-193)).
   Op-leastness: 60-150 naive at ×3 (T22's row, unchanged).
