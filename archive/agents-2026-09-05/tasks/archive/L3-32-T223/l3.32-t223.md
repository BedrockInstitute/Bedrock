   rebuild? **The tree must never be left red**, and the master is not

   deliberately rather than by accident.

   general STEP is parameterized content; the a₀ instance is
   instantiation content. They are different classes and belong in
   different dispatches with different seconds budgets. Say where the
   line falls and what each block's stop-line should be.
3. **What survives from block 2 verbatim.** `[T195]` built the carve
   landing, the placements, the six-clause story assembly and the pair
   family at a₀. Some of that is the general STEP's content written
   concretely and lifts to a variable; some is genuinely the instance.
   **Sort it, name by name at `file:line`**, because that sort is the
   difference between a rewrite and a lift.
4. **The seconds budget per block**, written from `[T219]`'s rates and
   revised by `[T222]` if its report has landed. Each block's brief
   must carry a stop that fires before the tree's margin is spent.
5. **The hazards**, law by law, with the site each would bite: R-40 at
   the iterated limits, P-n if instantiation creeps back in, P-l at the
   presentations, C-23 if a name looks unreached.
6. **The verification.** After the reshape the theorem must still close
   at the first limit, and every export the Bridge landing will consume
   must keep its type. Name the checkable claims a return must carry.
