# [L3.32-T56] The variable-form op-graph frame, W3's confirmed prerequisite

**Verdict in one paragraph.** The frame is delivered and green: the
constants-to-variables transfer (`VarSat`), the extensional graph closure
(`GraphFrame`) and five instantiations (F0, F1, F5, F2, F10), 285 non-blank
in-fence lines, cold check 6.6 s, warm 1.5 s, no postulate, no hole, no
`TERMINATING`. The D-10 check found one structural obstruction: the four
tuple operations F11-F14 have no full two-way variable-form graph at the
delivered syntax, because their total definitions route through the left and
right projections, whose behavior on non-pair arguments has no delivered
object-language characterization; the expressible pair-decomposition shape is
one-way (sound, not complete at non-pair `v`). Per D-10 the build stops on
those four rather than working around them. The frame is therefore enough for
the sequence-witness formula's per-op clauses for the twelve expressible
operations, at a measured ~29 lines each, but the F11-F14 clauses need either
a projection-formula chapter or a ruling. T48's 80-240 naive band is
overrun: the frame plus five ops is 285, and the twelve expressible ops price
at about 490 naive. The measured frame converts the direct-op portion of the
row from x3 to x1.3; the F11-F14 portion stays x3 and is the row's named
wall.

## 2. The D-10 record

Checked first, before building the batch:

**Representative: F0.** The target's truth at the intended generality is
confirmed green: `F0Graph` with both adequacy directions
([OpGraph.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/OpGraph.lagda.md:293-331)).
The description is transparent, its two constants are exactly the two
arguments, and the placement is two cases mapping the occurrences to the `u`
and `v` slots.

**Hardest: F11 (with F12-F14 by the same shape).** The variable form is not
expressible at the delivered syntax with full two-way adequacy. The
obstruction, with the evidence:

1. F11 is total through the projections: `F11 x y = pr (left y) (pr x (right y))`
   ([Images.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Images.lagda.md:367-368)),
   with `left z = ⋃ (⋂ z)` (:159-160) and `right z = ⋃ (rightSlice z)` (:229).
2. The delivered description of F11 is pairhood-conditional: `F11-spec`
   requires `y ≡ pr a b` as a hypothesis
   ([Images.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Images.lagda.md:388-389)),
   and the description chapter's `F11Desc` takes `y≡ : y ≡ pr a b` as a
   module parameter ([Describe.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Describe.lagda.md:1421-1425)).
3. The expressible shape is the pair decomposition
   `∃ a b. (v ≡ pr a b) ∧ (x ≡ pr a (pr u b))`. Its soundness direction
   (satisfaction implies `x = F11 u v`) is provable from `F11-spec`, but the
   completeness direction needs `v = pr (left v) (right v)`, which is
   equivalent to `v` being a pair and fails at every non-pair `v` (e.g.
   `v = ∅`: `∅` is not `pr a b`, since a Kuratowski pair is a two-element
   set). The junk values `F11 u v = pr (left v) (pr u (right v))` at non-pair
   `v` are therefore not characterized by the expressible shape.
4. The junk behavior is meta-characterized only: `right-nonpair` gives
   `right b ≡ ∅` off pairs, and the `⋂` lemmas collapse `left b`
   ([Images.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Images.lagda.md:533-534,
   :557-571)). There is no object-language formula with a two-way decode for
   `w = ⋂ v` or `w = rightSlice v` at the delivered syntax; turning them into
   formula content is a new projection-formula chapter, not a detail.

**Correction recorded beside the original target.** The brief's D-10 asked
whether the variable form is expressible for some operation; the answer is
that twelve of the sixteen are expressible through the delivered frame, and
the four tuple operations are not. The brief's own stop instruction is
followed: no workaround is built for F11-F14 (in particular, no
projection-formula machinery, and no one-way "soundness-only" graph is
presented as the two-way adequacy the brief demands).

## 4. Is the frame enough for the sequence-witness formula?

**Partly.** The frame removes the per-op content wall for the twelve
expressible operations: each op's clause "x = Fof i u v" now has a formula
with a two-way decode at the carrier, built on the delivered descriptions via
the parameter chapter's transfer. The five built ops cover the structural
variants the other seven share: transparent two-argument (F0), negated
(F1), single-argument with the second slot unused (F5, same pattern as F6 and
F7), sealed-specification through the switch hops (F2, same pattern as F3 and
F4), and the image half (F10, same pattern as F8). F9's graph is the pair
atom `x = pr u v`, whose outer-world singleton/pair decodes are new but small
(the pair atom's decode is delivered only at the inner world via `PairKit`);
F15's graph is `x = P ∩ u` with the predicate as a constant, directly from
`F15-spec`.

What the sequence-witness formula still needs, named:

1. **F11-F14's clauses**: a projection-formula chapter (object-language
   `left`/`right` with two-way decodes, priced as new machinery, roughly
   200-400 lines naive) or a ruling that the one-way pair-decomposition form
   suffices for the decode direction (a weakening of the two-way adequacy the
   brief demands).
2. **The 16-op lex**: the op-graph frame does not deliver the op-order, which
   is the formula-level lex of T50's wall
   ([l3.32-t50-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t50-report.md:178-190)).
   The two-image clause needs "i < j" and the leastness over the op indices
   as formula content; that row's second half (the op-order) is untouched by
   this chapter.
3. **The graph is Π₁-shaped, not Δ₀.** The second conjunct of `graph` is an
   unbounded `∀̇`, so the frame delivers the honest equality reading of
   `x = Fof i u v`, not a Δ₀ formula. T48's "per-operation Δ₀ relation" is
   corrected on the record. This matches the sequence-witness formula's
   uniform Σ₁ face, where the step clause's per-op conjuncts sit under the
   tower existential; the adequacy directions here are stated at full
   generality and do not depend on any level.

## 5. Walls, by LESSONS class

1. **New wall (LESSONS candidate, R-40): a total operation whose defining
   equation routes through a projection is not variable-form expressible
   where the projection's off-domain behavior is not object-language
   characterized.** F11-F14's `left`/`right` are total, the pair
   decomposition is one-way, and the meta junk lemmas
   ([Images.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Images.lagda.md:533-534))
   do not transfer to formula content. Stated as a rule: "when an operation's
   spec is pairhood-conditional but its definition is total, the variable
   form's adequacy is two-way only on the condition; price the off-condition
   characterization before booking the row." The D-10 protocol caught it
   before the batch.
2. **P-h, followed**: the frame is module-parameterized with abstract `Op`,
   `body`, `u`, `v`; nothing unfolds.
3. **R-35, followed**: memberships are at small indices (`⟪ A ⟫`-valued), the
   adequacy travels through the fibre paths.
4. **P-d, followed**: every adequacy is a direction pair, never an `iff`.
5. **I-4/I-5, followed**: no implicit is inverted through a truth projection;
   the transfers are stated over the carrier.
6. **The empty-domain identification (measured, not new)**: the two
   interpretations of `⊥*` are not definitionally equal (a conversion hazard
   in the `refl`-closing of the `⊨-place` hypothesis), bridged by the
   structural `sat⊥-irrel`; the Name chapter's `sameReading` is the same
   identification at the function level
   ([Name.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Choice/Name.lagda.md:406-407)).
   Not a new law; the structural variant is recorded here as the shape a
   consumer of `⊨-place` inside a satisfaction needs.

The measured per-op cost (about 29 lines) is above T48's 5-15-line-per-op
guess even with the frame shared; the row's whole 80-240 band is overrun by
the frame plus five ops alone. The ledger effect is therefore: the direct-op
content of the row moves from the unmeasured x3 class to the measured x1.3
class, priced at about 490 naive for the twelve expressible ops (285
delivered plus seven at the measured ~29 each), and the row's remaining
content (F11-F14, the op-order) stays x3 with the F11-F14 obstruction named
as the piece the sequence-witness formula needs ruled or funded.
