# The rud route's `src/`, archived 2026-08-09

**What this is.** Every `src/` file that differed from `main` when the owner
ruled the two-tower bridge route and ordered `src/` restored to the
internalization tree. 72 files: 36 that existed only on the rud route, and 36
that the rud route had modified. `rud-route-src.patch` is the whole diff
against `main` in one piece.

**Nothing here is checked and nothing imports it.** It sits outside `src/` so
every gate is blind to it by structure, which is what keeps the no-postulate
claim literally true of the whole checked tree.

**READ IT BEFORE YOU REBUILD ANY OF IT.** This is not a graveyard, it is the
measured output of a year of work, and the owner ruled on 2026-08-09 that
consulting it is a MECHANISM rather than an encouragement: every brief lists
the archived material that may bear on its task, and every report names what
it actually used and what it took from each item.

**The four things worth knowing before you open a file here.**

1. **`L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md` is the expensive one.** 2,632 in-fence lines,
   569.94 s cold, exit 0. It closes `Sset γ ∈ Lset (γ+1)` at a general limit.
   Its seconds-per-line ratio is the worst in the tree and the reasons were
   measured, not guessed: a depth-six carrier chain at `segγ∈C`, and
   instantiation at a concrete carrier, which `dev/LESSONS.md` P-n calls a
   payable floor.
2. **The Story and Bridge modules carry the generic machinery.**
   `L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md` holds a carrier-generic `Limit` module and
   `L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md` holds the two-directional identification with its
   classical gate. DD2 keeps the two-directional bridge, so this is the
   closest thing here to directly reusable content.
3. **What the route could NOT do is as valuable as what it did.** The
   crossing rebuild, the carried sequence, the seconds search that closed
   with only one lever left: all of it is priced in `archive/dev/TASKS-archived.md`
   and `archive/dev/JOURNAL-archived.md`. `dev/JOURNAL.md` was archived and
   reopened empty on 2026-08-09, so it holds none of this. A new brief that
   re-asks a question already answered there has wasted a dispatch.
4. **A measured cure does not transfer by analogy** (`dev/LESSONS.md` P-l).
   Everything here was measured at ITS site. Re-measure at yours.

**Where the rest of the record lives.** `archive/dev/TASKS-archived.md` for what each
of the 265 dispatches found; `archive/dev/DECISIONS-archived.md` for the rulings the
route ran on; `dev/ARCHIVE.md` for the modules retired DURING the route, which
are under `archive/src/` rather than here; and `dev/LESSONS.md`, which is NOT
archived and still binds.
