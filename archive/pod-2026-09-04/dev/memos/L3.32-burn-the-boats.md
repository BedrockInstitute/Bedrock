## 8. The ruling that section 3's red branch would trigger, stated concretely

An earlier draft of this memo said only that the opaque stage interface would
be "a separate ruling", which is too vague to act on. This section says what
would be ruled and when.

**WHAT WOULD BE RULED: whether to seal the presentation functor `⟪_⟫`
tree-wide behind a new interface.** `[T98]` section 3 specifies it: a sealed
index-type former `Idx : S → Type ℓ`, which is `⟪_⟫` under `opaque`, with
sealed `idx↪`, `idx-inj`, `fiber`, `member` and the membership conversion all
proved INSIDE the seal where `⟪_⟫` still unfolds, plus sealed position lemmas
per construction (the `sucV` membership trio restated at `Idx`, the
omega-membership-to-numeral reading, the numeral count). Every theorem in the
tree would then state at `Idx a` and nothing would ever normalize a stage.

**WHY IT IS AN ARCHITECTURE FORK AND NOT AN OPTIMIZATION.** `⟪_⟫` comes from
the cubical library's cumulative hierarchy and is mentioned at **2,668 sites
across 77 of the tree's 124 masters**. Sealing it re-founds how every chapter
in the development names a member. AGENTS.md requires an architecture fork to
be surfaced to the owner with a recommendation rather than charged ahead on,
and this is the largest one available.

**STATUS 2026-08-06: THIS FORK IS CLOSED.** `[T102]` came back GREEN, so the local cure reaches and the tree-wide re-founding is not needed. The section is kept because the condition could recur at another module, and because the closing is itself the answer to the owner's question: the tower's presentation did NOT have to be re-founded.

**WHEN IT WOULD BE RULED: only if a local cure fails, and not before.** The

1. `[T102]` comes back RED, meaning the `h₀` chain cannot be stated without
   the tower presentation by a change local to `SquareLaw`; and
