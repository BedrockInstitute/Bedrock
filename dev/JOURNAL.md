dispatched, what came back, what was measured, what was refuted, and in what

was ruled, when, by whom, and where things stand; its rows stay short and point
here. This journal is the record of HOW each goal went. `dev/LESSONS.md` is the
law book: the measured engineering laws distilled out of these narratives,
which BIND new code. A fact belongs in exactly one of the three: a ruling in
PLAN, an episode here, a law in LESSONS.

**Reading order.** Sections are by goal code, oldest first. Within a section
the text runs chronologically, with rulings and measurements marked in bold.
Reconnaissance reports, probe reports and batch reports are cited by path under
`_build/`; the agent briefs that produced them are archived in `_build/briefs/`.
