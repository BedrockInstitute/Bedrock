Ruling memo for the `[L3.29]` route, 2026-08-01. Developer doc, English only.

was delivered on `godel-route`, the three audits' verdicts, the closure fork and

## 3. The closure fork

With the delivered route priced at 13.1-13.7k and the 10k band out of reach,
the fork was: keep the delivered certificate architecture (option A), or
replace the certificate cluster with a kinded closure tower (option B), whose
kinded-invariant induction replaces the per-entry certificate and its honesty
induction. Two probes were run 2026-08-01, each against the working tree with no
`src/` file touched: the cut probe (the closure's values-cut and the internal
tower) and the order probe (the order's internal side under the kinded
closure).

### 3.1 The cut probe: NO-GO on the fork as specified, with a priced partial salvage

`_build/cut-probe-report.md`. **Verdict: NO-GO on the fork as specified, with a
priced partial salvage.** The naive values-cut over an unkinded closure is
unsound: the seed-cut junk `values (A ∩ ⟦t⟧)` escapes `𝒟ₒ A` for non-transitive
carriers, so the closure must carry arity kinding in its level structure. The
flat name order cannot stay flat either (its arguments are closure members, and
a total order on the closure carrier is only internal if the levels carry
per-member construction records, which is the certificate returning). The
four junk cases, copied from the report §2.2:

| case | closure member | values-cut | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. mixed-arity intersection | `allTuples A 1 ∩ allTuples A 2` | `∅` | lands inside | graphs of length-1 and length-2 assignments are disjoint; `∅ = defSet ⊥̇` |
| 2. same-arity intersection | `allTuples A 1 ∩ allTuples A 1` | `defSet (⊤̇ ∧̇ ⊤̇)` | lands inside | banked `sat-⊤`, `sat-∧`, `sat-defSet` |
| 3. mismatched complement | `allTuples A 2 ∖ allTuples A 1` | `A` | lands inside | disjointness makes the difference the whole family; `values (allTuples A 2) ≡ A` |
| 4. seed cut | `A ∩ allTuples A 1` | `{ v : ⟨ {pr ∅ v} ∈ A ⟩ }` | **escapes in general** | membership in `Def A` needs "{pr ∅ v} ∈ A" first-order definable in `(A, ∈)`, which fails for non-transitive `A` (automorphism argument; enters the probe as one flagged postulate) |

The escape is a non-transitive phenomenon; the internal step cannot assume
transitivity without adding a conjunct, and the kinded design eliminates the
junk by construction (no clause applies an operation across slices). The salvage
is the closure-tower half, at a structural price:

| new chapter | estimate (Agda lines) |
|---|---:|
| the three meta lemmas (selections and family-extension on satisfaction sets) | 80 to 140 |
| the singleton-family slice/operation | 60 to 120 |
| the kinded-invariant induction | 150 to 250 |
| the terms-to-levels lemma | 80 to 150 |
| the internal closure chapter (level table, 10-clause step, recursion assembly) | 700 to 1,100 |
| the Def-equivalence against the internal description | 150 to 300 |
| **total new** | **1,220 to 2,060** |

The closure-tower half is salvageable: a kinded arity-indexed level table with
kinded op clauses (Unknown 3 assembles from the delivered descriptions, green
at about 1.5 s), whose meta-level kinded-invariant induction replaces the
delivered per-entry certificate, priced at roughly 40 to 60 percent of the
delivered `Codes`+`Table`+`Tower` cost. The flat order should be dropped and
the delivered skeleton/parameter order kept only if the order side cannot be
re-cut (the order probe then replaced it).

### 3.2 The order probe: GO, with candidate 1 (the stratified producer order)

`_build/order-probe-report.md`. **Verdict: the hole closes, with candidate 1.**
The paper argument holds: the level-indexed induction is honest because the
kinded step's producers always draw their arguments from the previous level,
the least producer triple exists under LEM (a lexicographic strict well-order),
and the triple determines its member, so trichotomy follows by pullback. The
crux formula (the least-producer condition for the intersection clause) and its
out-reader typecheck against the delivered `interAt-out` in about 1.5 to 2.3 s.

The two stress points, answered (report §2.1):

- **(s1) Arguments are always previous-level members.** The kinded step's
  clauses, validated by the prior probe's `LayerAt`, apply each operation to
  members of the previous level only, so a member first appearing at level
  `n+1` has all producer arguments at level `≤ n`, and the recursion is
  honestly stratified. The worry that "an argument's own producers tie into the
  same level again" is about the argument's producers, which draw from one
  level below, not about the comparison, which uses `ordₙ` by definition.
- **(s2) Equal least triples give equal members.** The producer triple
  `(tag, params, arg₁, arg₂)` determines the member `x = op_tag(params, arg₁,
  arg₂)`, so equal least triples give equal members and trichotomy holds. Two
  small gaps surfaced: level-0 (seed) members have no producers, solved by the
  seed-tag carrying the carrier member as a parameter (the delivered Codes
  pattern), which makes the seed map injective; and unary/nullary shapes need a
  canonical filler, a fixed closure member present at every level by
  cumulativity (priced at 20-50 lines).

The internal side needs **no certificate**: the successor clause of the
order-family table is a direct formula over the closure level tables, the
previous-level order set, and the two compared members; junk inner tables are
excluded because the successor clause is a definite description, so
functionality plus the base and successor clauses determine the table uniquely
by meta-induction on the level number.

**Candidate 2 (the skeleton-directed evaluation table) is NO-GO: it converges
back to option A's certificate** (report §3). Junk skeletons are not excluded
by pair projections: a non-skeleton set satisfies every finite-depth
projection reading, and "s is a bona fide skeleton" is not first-order
definable without a finiteness or rank predicate. Excluding junk requires
either a definable skeleton code set (the deleted `Codes` chapter, unpinnable
under route C) or a decode certificate, which is precisely the Tower honesty
machinery. There is no honest cheap version of "the formula reads s's
structure".

## 4. The ruled decision: option B

**Ruled 2026-08-01: build option B, the kinded closure tower with the
stratified producer order (candidate 1), alongside the delivered route on this
branch.** The order probe's final recommendation is adopted in full: B's
closure swap nets roughly -1,000 to -2,000 lines against the delivered
certificate cluster, candidate 1's order side is a wash against A's M5b-d band,
and it retires A's one unpriced risk, the skeleton order's own internalization,
entirely, because it never mentions skeletons.

The ruling's conditions, binding:

1. **Coexistence, not deletion.** The delivered route (the certificate
   architecture and the M5a skeleton order as they stand on this branch) stays
   green and untouched while B is built. The kinded tower and the stratified
   order land as new chapters beside it. Nothing retires at this ruling.
2. **Final measurement, then a fresh ruling.** At the end of the B build, one
   precise landing measurement is taken against the B tree, and a fresh owner
   ruling decides what retires (the certificate cluster, the skeleton order,
   the M5a re-cut) and what stays. The M7 rewire of the original plan is
   superseded by this fresh ruling; the original M6 pedagogy gate (the owner
   reads the new Part side by side before anything is deleted) is preserved as
   part of that ruling.
3. **Honest accounting.** The 9.5-10.5k band stays out of reach under both
   options (B lands about 13.4-14.8k); the primary yield is unchanged from the
   memo's (the satisfaction-internalization cluster retires, the build stays in
   the seconds-per-chapter class). These facts are recorded, not papered over.
4. **The probe discipline continues.** Each B chapter lands behind a
   before-and-after bisect per the route's method, with the recorded
   performance laws (P-c seals, P-d direction pairs, rules 1-16, rule 20)
   carried from the first line, per `dev/LESSONS.md`.

The M5a re-cut already landed on this branch (committed `9539088`, retired by
this ruling's build plan; its lessons are P-g and the graft entry in
`dev/LESSONS.md`).

## 6. Open risks carried into the build

The decision stands or falls on the items below; each is priced but not
measured, and each is the subject of the route's probe discipline before or
during its chapter:

1. **The full 10-clause kinded step and its Sequence-class assembly are
   unmeasured.** Unknown 3 validated three clauses and the inter out-reader
   (about 1.5 to 3.5 s); the remaining seven clauses and the recursion assembly
   are not written. The interaction of the arity-indexed recursion value with
   the `funct` contractibility (the P2 design fact, the domain must not be a
   stage) is the largest remaining performance question
   (`_build/cut-probe-report.md` §5, `_build/order-probe-report.md` §5).
2. **The successor clause's unbounded least-conjuncts are the P-d hazard.** The
   order-family table's successor clause carries unbounded forall-quantified
   least-producer conjuncts; satisfactions of concrete formulas with such
   conjuncts are exactly the measured P-d wall class (386 s as a path, 1.9 s as
   direction pairs, memo §9). The adequacy proofs will hit this shape, and the
   direction-pair formulation is the expected cure but must be measured
   (`_build/order-probe-report.md` §5).
3. **The fill directions and the other clauses' out-readers are unproved.**
   Only the inter-clause out-reader is green; the fill direction, the other
   clauses' out-readers, and the `orderL-fill`/`orderL-rep` assembly at M5d are
   priced, not proved. The empty-stage canonical-filler lemma is assessed
   meta-theoretically at 20-50 lines, not proved.
4. **The three new meta lemmas and the singleton-family description are priced
   but unproved.** The singleton-family is a genuinely new `At`-description and
   constructibility proof; the kinded-invariant induction runs against internal
   clauses conditional on numeral-ness, and the conditional shapes may inflate
   it beyond the 150-250 estimate (`_build/cut-probe-report.md` §5).
5. **The landing arithmetic carries two unmeasured tails.** The skeleton order's
   internal-side cost and the final landing measurement of the delivered route
   are branch work in progress, not probe questions; the B landing band
   (about 13,400-14,800, honest range 13,040-15,190) assumes the closure anchor
   and the order drift hold their estimates.

The shape of the mathematics, on the record: the naive cut fails on junk (case
4 escapes), the kinded level table makes the values-cut sound and complete with
the banked lemmas plus three small new ones, the flat order cannot stay flat,
the stratified producer order closes the order side without a certificate and
without mentioning skeletons, candidate 2 converges back to the certificate,
and the B decision is carried by the closure swap, which favors B by 1,000 to
2,000 lines with the order side a wash.
