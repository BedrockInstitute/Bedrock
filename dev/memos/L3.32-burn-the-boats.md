## 3. Dispatch 2: the `h₀`/`comp₀` chain, 360.6 s

The disease is **statement-bound**, proven by `[T88]`'s gutted-body
experiment: a hot body reduced to a one-line application of a free generic
lemma still paid its 190 s. So no consumer-side or body-side cure applies, and
the seal made it 1.9x worse.

**This is gated before it is built, exactly as the chase was.** The chase's
green gate cost one scratch file and 1.28 s and it converted a survey-class
term into a measured one; the same discipline applies here and D22 requires it.

**The probe:** can `h₀` be stated at a variable index, so that no statement in
the chain carries `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫`? Build the restated `h₀` and `h₀-inj`
alone in a scratch copy and measure. Do not build `comp₀`, `exclude` or the
consumers.

**The branches.**

- **Green.** The chain reshape is funded and dispatch 2b builds it in place.
  `SquareLaw` lands near its benchmark and the tree reaches roughly 1,266 s.
- **Red, and the reason is that `h₀`'s type genuinely needs the concrete
  stage.** Then the root cause **flips to mathematics for this one family**,
  which is a real and publishable answer to the owner's question. `SquareLaw`
  floors at about 507 s, the 360.6 s becomes a standing tax with a written
  reason, and the only remaining route is `[T98]`'s opaque stage interface,
  which re-founds the tower's presentation and is survey-class. **That is a
  separate ruling, not an escalation to be taken inside this dispatch.**
- **Red, heap.** Same rule as dispatch 1 branch B: ask what leaked into the
  telescope before asking anything else.

## 7. The standing rule this route is built on

**A cure does not transfer by analogy.** Four transplants were tried on
2026-08-06 and the three guided by resemblance all failed, while the one
redirected by a diagnosed root cause turned a 26-minute heap wall into 1.28
seconds. Every dispatch above therefore gates before it funds, and every
expected figure is a hypothesis until it is re-measured at its own site. P-l
carries the table.
