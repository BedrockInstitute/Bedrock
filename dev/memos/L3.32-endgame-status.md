## 1. What is proved today

The theorem is `(γ : S) → ⟨ isLimit γ ⟩ → ⟨ Sset γ ∈ˢ Lset (sucV γ) ⟩`
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:109`). It is the `∈-induction` closure of the
STEP (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:126-127`). The STEP at a general limit is
the module parameter `stepHyp` (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:120-125`).

The theorem closes at the first limit in a real master. `Step.firstStep`
proves `⟨ Sset a₀ ∈ˢ Lset (sucV a₀) ⟩`
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:1783-1784`). Its body is the carve landing
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:1590`). The carve clause states
`DefOf.defSet (Lset a₀) ψ ≡ Sset a₀` (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:1583`;
`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:95-101`). The master imports no HF and no
Finite (`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:103-106`). It uses no
`Ssetω≡Lsetω` identification (`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:107-111`).

The general STEP is a parameter. The theorem does not close at a general
limit today (`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:62-66`). Block 3 owes the
general-limit STEP (`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:73-81`). The general STEP
prices at 1,011-1,261 naive and 1,314-1,639 calibrated
(`_build/l3.32-t217-report.md:3-4`).

What this means: the first limit is proved in a master that Agda checks
(`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:60-70`). What it does not mean: the general
theorem is proved. The first-limit landing is the STEP's first use, not its
total (`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:73-81`).

The wiring contract is fixed. After the general STEP lands, the orchestrator
instantiates `Assembly` with `stepHyp`. It passes `below-lim-old` to the
