## 6. The D29 question, asked

This recon is D29's moment one for the re-home. The answer follows.

Where the re-home is structure-generic:

- The pick is carrier-level. All level-memberships in statement positions
  are variable hypotheses (R-37). [T24] re-parameterized the pick by the
  order bundle, so it is generic over the order, not fixed to one.
- The carve machinery is carrier-level. The family sits in
  `Ju = Jset γ limγ`. The carved set lands in `Jset (+ω γ)`. The level
  `γ` and the limit proof are parameters. The description `φ` is a module
  hypothesis.
- The bridge step is consumed as a class-level module hypothesis. Its
  delivery is the standing `Reduce` telescope at `Bridge:1154-1165`.
- The description wiring consumes the precedes atom at arity 3. The
  object language has no weakening or substitution
  (`FOL/Syntax.lagda.md:157`). W3 must deliver the formula in the
  consumption shape, or the build inlines a shifted copy.

What re-instantiation costs:

- The pick re-parameterized by the order bundle measures 22 non-blank
  lines ([T24] section 3).
- The carve machinery re-instantiates at each limit level by its
  parameters. The delivered pieces (`defSet-mem`, `full-switch-⊇`,
  `leastOf`, `+ω`) hold at every level.
- The sibling datum: the clause machinery re-instantiates in about 10
  lines against 353 content, and `StepStory` instantiated at `Lset ω`
  unchanged ([T126], [T128], the below-lim memo).

The answer is to write generic. A stop-line is not a reason to write
fixed. If the generic shape does not fit the budget, the agent says so.
The row is then re-priced.
