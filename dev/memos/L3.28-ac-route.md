# [L3.28] Route memo: three ways to L ⊨ AC, priced

Deliverable requested by the owner 2026-07-31 under the widened `[L3.28]` (best-effort
target: `src/` under 10,000 Agda lines). Developer doc, English only. Consumes the
`[L3.28]` survey recorded in PLAN §6.1, the import-graph measurements of 2026-07-31, the
`[L3.2]` rejection record, and the `[L2.4]` delivered route. **The charter rule that no
delivered statement may weaken is bypassed in this memo by owner instruction, for the
purpose of comparison only; adopting any option is a separate ruling and nothing here is
one.**

Answers one question: **is there an architecture, rather than a refactor, under which
`src/` lands at or below 10,000 Agda lines, and what does it cost in mathematics, prose,
risk, and book content?**

Short answer: the question turns out to be about **one axiom**. Cutting the single import
edge `L.Model → L.Choice.Transversal` splits the tree into a ZF-only cone of **4,180**
Agda lines and an AC-only radius of **13,217** (75.6% of 17,492): everything in
`L/Coding/` and `L/Choice/` exists to prove choice, via the internal well-order, via
internalized satisfaction. Option A (stay) lands 16.0k to 16.7k by the survey and never
reaches 10k. Option B (redefine `Def` by Gödel operations everywhere) is dominated and
should not be taken. Option C (a hybrid: `Def` stays satisfaction-defined; the internal
tower and the internal well-order rebuild over a **binder-free** operations calculus)
prices to a new tree of **8.3k to 10.5k**: it moves the 10k target from unreachable to
plausible-but-not-guaranteed, at the cost of retiring the satisfaction-internalization
chapters and their prose. Three bounded probes decide the two open risks. §8 states the
decision rule this memo asks of the owner.

## 2. Why the current route is expensive, in one paragraph

The survey traced the mass and the compression resistance to the same source. The
object language has **binders**, so internalizing satisfaction forces: environments as
sets, de Bruijn bookkeeping, a 12-clause satisfaction recursion whose soundness (801)
and uniqueness (630) quantify over environments, and a description language whose ∃/∀
reading pairs are 90 to 100% distinct because one side is a truncated Σ and the other a
Π. The measured conversion walls of this layer (statements containing satisfactions,
binder packing, non-local recursion values) are all satisfaction-shaped or
binder-shaped. The order then inherits all of it: naming a set means naming a formula,
so the order's internalization drags the whole coding cone behind it.

## 3. Option A: stay

The survey's verdict stands: levers sum to −755 conservative, −1,486 optimistic; landing
16.0k to 16.7k. The 10k target is formally abandoned. No probes, no risk, no content
change. This is the default if the ruling declines C or the probes fail.

## 4. Option C: the hybrid, in full

### 4.1 The mathematics

Keep `𝒟ₒ` (definable powerset via satisfaction) as the definition and keep every
delivered ZF-cone statement. Add the classical finite basis and use it only where
internalization was needed:

1. **Operations.** About ten set operations (pair, product, difference, membership
   graph, domain, permutations) as functions on `V`, with their elementary laws. Meta
   level only; ordinary set algebra on the HIT hierarchy.
2. **Terms.** A **binder-free** inductive syntax of operation terms with parameter
   leaves, and its denotation by structural recursion. No environments, no de Bruijn,
   no renaming, no relabelling: the three measured hazard classes of the current route
   have no habitat here.
3. **Normal form** (the one place the ×12 induction survives, once, at the meta level):
   for every `φ : Formula S n`, a term whose denotation at a transitive carrier is the
   inner-satisfaction set of `φ`. The quantifier case is domain-of plus permutation;
   the tuple algebra this needs is the genuinely new mathematics and the largest
   unknown (probe P1).
4. **Definability of the operations.** Each operation's graph is a small object-language
   description (atoms and pairs, no satisfaction inside), with `extAt`-class readings.
5. **Closure.** `cl(A ∪ {A})` as an internal ω-recursion whose step applies the
   operations: a `Sequence`-class chapter with a 10-clause step of set equations.
6. **Def-equivalence.** `𝒟ₒ A` is the subset-cut of the closure (both directions spend
   3, 4, 5). This is the bridge that lets the *internal* tower step be described by
   closure instead of by `DefAt` over codes, which is what releases the coding cone.
7. **The tower, re-based.** `L.Hierarchy`'s pattern re-proved against the closure step.
8. **The order, re-cut.** Meta side: order by (birth stage, term, parameters
   lexicographically by the earlier order), by ordinal recursion from `∅`; no separate
   finite base, no earliest-disagreement family, limits by end extension (already
   proved for a birth-primary family). Internal side: the description mentions the
   term-denotation graph (a binder-free internal recursion, `Sequence`-class) and a
   lexicographic comparison; the family-as-set is a `Table`-class chapter with the
   step condition swapped.
9. **Transversal.** Survives as delivered; the swap surface of §1 is re-supplied.

**Landing: 4,180 (ZF cone) + ~1,270 (kept) + 2,700 to 4,900 (new) + ~95 (index) =
8.3k to 10.5k.** The surviving-part compression levers add perhaps −0.2k to −0.4k. The
band straddles 10k honestly: the target becomes plausible, not guaranteed.

### 4.3 Build-speed expectation

Grounded in the 22 measured laws, not in optimism. What vanishes with the binders: the
satisfaction-in-statement wall class (rules 1, 2 and their kin fire on keys and
satisfactions inside types; term-denotation statements are set equations), and the
binder-packing wall (the ∃ⁿ frames exist for descriptions this route mostly does not
write). What persists: locality of recursion values (rule 13), so `approx-val`-class
theorems stay in the chapter that builds their recursion, and every description still
seals where it is built. Where new walls could appear: the tuple algebra (permutation
operations on Kuratowski pairs may normalize badly inside goals) and the 10-clause
closure step at concrete slots. Probes P2 and P3 are aimed exactly there.

### 4.4 What the book loses and gains

Loses: the internalized-satisfaction development, which is landmark-adjacent content
(the model reading its own syntax is Tarski-theorem territory and the current Parts
teach it end to end), and the trilingual prose of every dying chapter (file cost tracks
Agda cost at roughly 2.1 to 1). Gains: the order chapters become finite combinatorics
readable with set algebra alone; the beginner's path to `L⊨ZFC` shortens by roughly
half; the famous pedagogical objection to Gödel operations (an unmotivated finite
basis) is softened in this hybrid, because `Def` keeps its honest definition and the
basis appears only as a *theorem about* definability, which is its actual mathematical
status. Exact price is expected unchanged (`LEM (ℓ-suc ℓ)` and nothing else); the new
mathematics is more constructive than what it replaces, and a probe failure on this
would be an abort criterion, not a surprise to absorb.

## 5. Option B: full switch, and why it is dominated

Redefining `𝒟ₒ`/`Lset` by closure changes the definitional base of every delivered
ZF-cone statement, including the `𝒮ʟ` in the trophy signature: 4.2k lines of churn
(re-prove or transport), a changed meaning for every landmark that names `L`, and the
loss of the "definable subsets" motivation, in exchange for nothing option C does not
already take. Every cost of C, plus churn, minus the pedagogy. Not priced further.

## 6. The probes

All three run in a scratch branch against the existing tree; none touches `src/` on
`main`; each has an abort criterion fixed now, per the probe doctrine (these are
departures from the delivered route, so probes are warranted and feasibility is
genuinely open).

- **P3 (first, cheapest): one operation end to end.** Define two operations (pair,
  difference), their graphs as object-language descriptions, readings through `extAt`,
  and one closure-step clause. Abort if any reading exceeds 30 s cold or the
  descriptions demand a new wall class.
- **P2: the term-denotation internal recursion, statement test.** Write the binder-free
  graph (10 clauses) and one `approx-val`-class reading at variable slots; measure
  cold. This tests the claim that removing binders removes the wall class. Abort at
  60 s cold for the chapter.
- **P1 (the mathematical risk): tuple algebra plus the ∃ case of normal form.** The
  permutation and domain algebra on Kuratowski tuples, and the quantifier case of the
  simulation, against the existing inner semantics. Abort if the projected full
  normal-form chapter exceeds 800 lines or any module exceeds 60 s.

Expected total probe cost: two to three sessions. P3 and P2 together answer the
performance question; P1 answers the size question. If all three pass, §4.2's band
tightens from the top.

## 7. Consequence for the compression pass while unruled

Levers **(b)**, **(c)**, **(d)**, **(f)** operate on chapters option C deletes: they are
**gated** until the ruling (compressing a chapter that may die is spent work). Lever
**(a)** proceeds restricted to the ZF cone, lever **(e)** proceeds on the four
`Manipulation` chapters in the ZF cone (`Parameters` is AC-only and waits), and
candidate (1) (`𝒟ₒ→isL`, ZF cone) proceeds. If the ruling is A, the gates lift and the
survey's execution order resumes unchanged.

## 8. The ruling this memo asks for

Three choices, one decision rule.

- **Rule for C** if: the probes are worth two to three sessions to you, **and** the
  content loss of §4.4 is acceptable in principle (the satisfaction internalization
  leaves the book as delivered mathematics; its ideas survive only in prose). Then run
  P3, P2, P1 in that order and rule again on their numbers.
- **Rule for A** if: the internalized-satisfaction Parts are content you want the book
  to teach at full formal depth, regardless of length. Then 10k is formally abandoned,
  16.0k to 16.7k is the honest target, and the survey's execution order resumes now.
- **B is not offered.**

The recommendation, held loosely: **run the probes.** The measured facts (75.6% of the
tree serves one axiom; the swap surface is one import list; the walls are binder-shaped
and the replacement has no binders) are unusually favorable to C, and the probes are
cheap relative to the 12k-line question they decide. The pedagogical trade is real and
is the owner's call, not this memo's.

## 9. Probe results (2026-07-31), the two new laws, and the ruling

All three probes ran the same day, each in an isolated worktree against the warm tree,
each as one new module importing only generic infrastructure (no satisfaction
internalization anywhere). **All three PASS.** The numbers, and the two wall classes
they discovered, follow.

**P3 (ops descriptions and one closure step): PASS at ~20x margin.** 171 non-blank
lines: meta `diffV` from `V.Smallness`'s free tier, `diffAt` with both readings at
variable slots and full set-identity strength, and a closure-step description in
`Sequence`'s idiom with three readings. Cold **1.39 to 1.46 s** against the 30 s abort
line; **zero walls**, no `opaque` seal ever needed. Two extra findings: the ops-route
step needs **no `PowOK`-class side condition** (the described member is itself the pair
or difference, so nothing is conjured), and clause growth is **flat**: a 9-clause
variant of the step at ~180 lines cost ≈0 ms more. Extrapolation: the full
ops-definability chapter ≈600 to 850 lines, 5 to 10 s cold.

**P2 (binder-free internal recursion, the statement-shape test): PASS at 17x margin,
and it confirms the memo's central claim directly.** 665 non-blank lines: toy two-op
term syntax, tag-and-pair codes over `prʟ`/`numeralL`, `Sequence`-style approximation
and graph, assembly through `L.Recursion.mereFunct`, and the value pinned to the meta
denotation at a variable member. Cold **3.4 to 3.6 s** against the 60 s abort line.
The two decisive control measurements: the seal-at-build-site law, worth **99x** in
`Before` (376 s to 3.8 s), is **no longer needed** here (unsealed 3.7 s vs sealed
3.5 s, noise), and the concrete-key reading shape that ran past **400 s** in `[L3.23]`
costs **0.3 s**, because a term code is a small tag-pair tower over sealed numerals
with no satisfaction inside. Structural bonuses: uniqueness is a plain structural
induction (no well-founded descent, no single-valuedness hypothesis), and the
approximation is exhibited as a finite set with no formula spent. One design fact for
the real chapter: the recursion's domain must be the finite subterm-code set, not a
stage (a stage carries junk at which the graph is unsatisfiable and `funct`'s
contractibility fails); this is `Before`'s `ωʟ` pattern. Extrapolation with the
`Fin`-indexed single node constructor: ≈700 to 1,000 lines, 8 to 25 s cold.

**P1 (tuple algebra and two normal-form cases): PASS, at the boundary.** 621 non-blank
lines (tuple algebra 442, the two cases 111), cold **30.0 s** against the 60 s line,
worst single definition 14.4 s. The mathematical headline: **the classical permutation
algebra dissolved.** Tuples are graph-coded exactly as the tree's `env` already codes
them (a set of key-value Kuratowski pairs, keys as data), so the atom at arbitrary
indices is one fixed selection operation taking two numeral **parameters**, `i = j`
included, no case split; the residue of the whole permutation apparatus is one 12-line
successor-injectivity lemma. The existential is a re-keying shift (the binder takes
position 0 and every key renumbers by `sucV`), not a plain domain restriction.
Projections: normal-form chapter ≈**750** lines against the 800 abort line (a 6%
margin), ops chapter ≈**560** against the memo's 300 to 500 band (a 12% overrun);
combined ≈1,310 against the memo's combined 700 to 1,300. **Consequence: §4.2's
landing band tightens from 8.3k-10.5k to 9.5k-10.5k.** The 10k figure is a
boundary fight that the surviving-cone compression levers will have to win; the
primary yield (≈17.5k to ≈10k, the hardest 40% of the book gone) is unaffected.

**The two new laws**, probe-measured, to be confirmed in the first production chapters
and carried as `-- perf:` markers there:

- **Law P-a (from P2): discriminate tags through helpers whose numeral indices are
  explicit data; never let unification meet a literal numeral under `#`.** An absurd
  clause headed `snotz ∘ injSuc` with `#-inj′`'s implicits left to inference ran
  **252 s and failed with unsolved metas** (constraint-order luck: the `znots`-headed
  siblings happened to solve); through generic `tagNe`/`payNe` helpers with explicit
  indices, the same 16-clause induction sits in a 3.5 s module.
- **Law P-b (from P1): the operations calculus must be union-free at the definition
  level.** Each operation is **one direct `sett`** (sum-typed index); `∪`/`⋃` may
  appear in composition terms but never under a membership proof obligation. Written
  as singleton-∪-shift, one lemma did not finish in six minutes and drove the checker
  past 30 GB; as a direct `sett`, 7 s. This law binds the closure-step chapter too.
- **Law P-c (found 2026-07-31 building the tuple chapter, by a four-way bisect;
  mechanism corrected the same day at the satisfaction chapter): an operation
  whose index carries a `⋃`-tower is sealed `opaque` at birth.** The measurement
  arc, in order: the shift equation at bare hierarchy values ran **446 s** with the
  operation's head open, and neither locality (a verbatim same-module transplant,
  446 s) nor law P-a's helpers changed it; the identical proof at carrier-valued
  assignments checked in **1.1 s**, which first suggested the statement's value
  domain as the mechanism; then the existential satisfaction case, carrier-valued
  throughout, ran **250 s** against the same open head, refuting that reading; and
  with `tailGraph` sealed at its birth site, both the existential case (**3 s**)
  and the bare-hierarchy shift equation (**1.0 s**) collapsed at once. So the
  mechanism is the `⋃`-tower in the operation's index re-normalizing at every
  concrete set former it meets downstream, and the seal is the cure; the
  carrier-valued statement form is kept as the API (every consumer arrives
  carrier-valued) but is not load-bearing for performance. This sharpens law P-b
  one level up: union-freedom is a discipline for definitions, and where an index
  cannot avoid a `⋃`-tower, the seal replaces it.
- **Law P-d (found 2026-08-01 at the constant-atom reductions, by an eight-step
  bisect): a pointwise semantic reduction travels as a pair of directions, never
  as an hProp path.** Building the `⇔toPath` path between the satisfactions of
  two **fully concrete** formulas ran **386 s** for one atom shape, while its two
  directions cost under 2 s each; with the respect lemma's hypotheses restated as
  the two directions, the whole batch (the exceptional atom, five constant-atom
  reductions, both bounded-quantifier reductions, the classical cases re-adapted)
  checks in **1.9 s**. Semi-concrete formulas with variable leaves never paid
  (the classical cases were 17 ms as paths), so the hazard is the path former at
  concrete leaves, the same concreteness boundary laws 1 and 2 drew on the other
  side of the turnstile. Bisect eliminations along the way, recorded because
  each was a plausible mechanism: the renaming chapter's module application
  (removed entirely: the bounded quantifiers' weakening is definitional on both
  term constructors, so both directions are the identity), the extension and
  selection operations' open heads (sealed anyway, consistent with P-c), and the
  inline case lambda (named continuations with spelled payloads, the recorded
  law, remain necessary but were not sufficient).
- **Law P-e (found 2026-08-01 at the normal-form chapter, by the internal
  profile): a data type whose constructors mention a module parameter's
  presentation type is declared at an abstract type parameter and applied,
  never inside the parameterized module.** The composition-term syntax with
  `param : ⟪ A ⟫ → GT` inside the carrier module spent **345.5 s in the
  positivity checker alone** (the definition-level profile showed every
  definition under 200 ms and the internal profile attributed the whole cost
  to Positivity, which normalizes the presentation); declared as
  `GT (P : Type ℓ)` at top level and applied at `⟪ A ⟫`, the whole chapter
  checks in **1.4 s**, a 250x collapse from moving one declaration.

**THE RULING, 2026-07-31, by the owner: option C is adopted**, on the two-step branch

branch instead.
