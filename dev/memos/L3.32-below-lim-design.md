## 7. The structure-generic question, asked and answered

[D29] asks the question at three moments. This recon is moment one. The
answer follows.

Where the design is carrier-generic: `StepStory` is generic in two
telescopes. The clause machinery of [T128] is generic in effect. Nothing
in it names a concrete carrier. The segment should be written generic in
the index, once, and instantiated at a numeral and at a limit.

What re-instantiation costs: [T126] instantiated `StepStory` at `Lset ω`
unchanged. [T128] priced re-instantiation at about 10 lines against 353
content. The story formula is evaluated at each `Lset δ`. The generic
form is reused at every scale.

Where a fixed choice would be cheaper now: writing the clause or the
segment fixed to `Lset ω` saves the generic shell now. Each further
scale would re-derive it. [T126] section 5 measured the trade. The first
instance is about 60 naive more expensive at the center. Each further
scale is cheaper.

The answer is to write generic. A stop-line is not a reason to write
fixed. The attitude clause of [D29] binds. If the generic shape does not
fit the budget, the agent says so. The row is then re-priced. No fixed
shape is delivered silently inside a cap.
