# Route adjustment: what the existing documents contradict

The owner opened a route-adjustment phase on 2026-08-09 and ruled: build
the L tower and the J tower (through rud) and the bridge between them,
the TWO-TOWER BRIDGE route, and prove `L ⊨ AC` and `L ⊨ GCH` on it. The
core constraint is to follow the textbooks to the best route and
architecture, MAXIMIZING the code the two proofs share, and so reduce
the total. The quantitative constraint is that the two-tower bridge
route's DOUBLE-trophy total must not exceed the internalization route's
double-trophy total, and that number is to be made clear first if it is
not.

This memo lists what in the tree contradicts that. It rules nothing.

## 1. Direct contradictions, which the new ruling supersedes

**D26, the campaign route.** It settles the route as R2' and says a line
count "may never put the campaign route back on the table". The new
ruling makes a line count a HARD CONSTRAINT on the route's endpoint.
These cannot both stand.

**D37, the standing dispatch order.** Its terminus is "until `L ⊨ AC` is
proved inside every threshold". The new goal is BOTH trophies. The
terminus is wrong as written.

**D38, the two main lines.** Its line two is "close the double-trophy
line gap", framed as an economic comparison. Under the new ruling the
gap is a CONSTRAINT to be met, not a difference to be narrowed.

**D36, the AC trophy budget.** It caps the pure AC closure, first at
16,000 and now at 20,000, and its whole apparatus is AC-only: the
tripwire, the deletion test, the `[[gch_assign]]` mechanism. The new
quantitative constraint is on the DOUBLE total. A per-trophy cap can
now conflict with the joint one: content moved into the shared part to
protect the AC cap raises nothing jointly, and content split to protect
it may LOWER reuse, which the core constraint forbids.

**D33, the trophy split caliber.** It defines the measure as the ruled
AC route's closure. The new ruling needs a DOUBLE-trophy measure as its
primary figure. The tool already prints one, `endpoint`, but no ruling
makes it the caliber.

## 2. Tensions, which need the owner's word rather than a repair

**D18, the trophy configuration.** "The trophy stays on the Def tower;
there is no re-founding onto the rud/J tower." The new ruling keeps the
trophies in L, so the trophy does not move. But D18 also retires the
internalization route, and the new ruling makes that route the
BENCHMARK. A retired route whose total is the binding constraint is a
strange object, and the retirement's own arithmetic is now load-bearing.

**D32, the deferred rebuild.** It defers the internalization rebuild to
the GCH resume and binds a revival to "reproduce the factorization".
That is exactly what `[T259]`'s S-tower design does NOT do, and the new
ruling orders the BEST architecture. D32's revival condition now stands
against the core constraint.

**D30, the craft freeze and the wall gate.** The new ruling names lines
and reuse. It does not mention seconds. D30's exit condition (4) is a
600 s wall and the freeze stands on it, with `bridge-landing` released
by name. **Whether the wall survives the adjustment is unstated and the
orchestrator will not assume either way.**

**D31, arm B is the ideal-form rewrite.** Compatible in method, but it
was written for a retirement whose shape the new ruling may change.

## 3. What the new ruling leaves standing

**D19, the bridge is two-directional.** The new ruling names the bridge
and this is its content.

**D17, retirement priced from the rewrite side**, and **D22, gate every
block**, and **D29, generic writing at three named moments**. All three
serve the core constraint directly: D29 in particular IS the
maximize-reuse discipline, already ruled.

**D20, archive never delete.** Untouched.

## 4. The number the constraint depends on

`[T257]` priced the internalization route's double trophy at
**25,485-28,258 naive**, 29,909-37,275 calibrated, against the rud
route's 34,940-39,823 and 42,284-54,396. **On those figures the rud
route currently EXCEEDS the constraint by 9,455-11,565 naive.**

That figure is one pass by one agent and it carries a declared weak
point: it prices `carried-sequence` and `blockpowlim-instance` at zero
for the wing, worth 4,238-5,218. `[T260]` then supported that pricing on
import-graph facts, though its own probe never ran.

The ledger's `reference_line = 25000` is a best-effort target with no
ruling behind it. It sits inside `[T257]`'s internalization band, which
is a coincidence worth noticing and not evidence.
