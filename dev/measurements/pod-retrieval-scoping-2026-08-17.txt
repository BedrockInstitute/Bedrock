RETRIEVAL SCOPING MEASUREMENT, 2026-08-17
Cited by dev/memos/L9-pod-program-design.md section 7.4, Parts 1a and 1b.

METHOD. BM25 over whole files, k1=1.5, b=0.75, standard library only.
The tokenizer drops a stop list and words under two characters, lower-cases
the rest and keeps CJK characters singly. The query is the GOAL section of
the brief named in each row. The gold file is the one the detour episode
should have found, taken from agents/tasks/LJ-1-376/lj-1.376-report.md.
The brief own directory and agents/tasks/LJ-1-376/ are excluded from every
ranking, so a brief cannot retrieve itself.

CORPUS. agents/tasks/**.md + archive/dev/**.md + archive/src/**.lagda.md
        + dev/**.md + src/**.lagda.md
  full corpus:  1706 documents
  archive/src:  97
  archive/dev:  6
  agents/tasks: 1456

DILUTION, the measured cause. Composition of the documents that outrank the
gold file in the full corpus:
  ep 7: 443 above the gold, of which 418 agents/tasks, 15 dev, 6 archive, 4 src
  ep 5: 294 above the gold, of which 280 agents/tasks,  9 dev, 3 archive, 2 src

EPISODE 1, the one case no lexical method reaches. The query and the gold
passage of dev/PLAN.md share exactly two tokens:
  step  document frequency 1072 of 1706
  lj    document frequency  886 of 1706
Both are noise at corpus scale. At name level, "Step 6" and "the leaf supply"
share no token at all, which is what dev/JOURNAL.md:1026 says in words.

REPRODUCIBILITY. The archive corpus last changed 2026-08-13 and the briefs are
frozen records, so the scoped column reproduces. The full-corpus column does
NOT: agents/tasks grows with every dispatch, and it was 1456 documents on the
measurement date. That growth is the finding, not a defect in it.

SCRIPT. The experiment is preserved beside this file as
