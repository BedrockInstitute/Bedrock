# Terminology review, 2026-09-07

The owner requested a literature-first review of all 145 existing entries before
chapter authoring. This is the coordinator's accepted review, not the dispatch
proposals. Sources were searched online for the intended meaning. The references include conceptual background and search leads, not only direct lexical attestations. A university domain or an English textbook alone does not establish a CJK rendering.
An English source establishes a concept, not its Chinese or Japanese wording.
A composed or contextual rendering is not advertised as a directly attested term.
The table records every existing entry and the editorial decision；「retain」is not a claim that all three labels have been found verbatim in literature. Where exact wording has not been verified, retaining it is a contextual editorial choice, subject to stronger later evidence; lexical authority remains `../glossary.toml`.

Five existing entries change: generic absoluteness uses Japanese 強制法での絶対性，Japanese canonical becomes 正準 (正準的 in adjective
position), and the English surname in Lévy hierarchy keeps its accent.
The Chinese constant-domain and relabelling entries now share the 常元 stem
attested in first-order model theory. The
reflection entry concerned proof engineering; set-theoretic reflection now has
a separate entry using Japanese 反映. Fourteen missing entries fix vocabulary used
by the chapter framework. Their notes contain sources, searched questions and
scope limits. Unverified replacement proposals were rejected, including changing
successor 後者 merely because the word also means「the latter」，changing the
editorial metaphor face into a geometric face, and treating reification as Java
reflection. Ordinary project metaphors are retained for the later scaffold prose;
they are not recommended as headings for mathematical lessons.
