# The terminology dossier: fourteen renderings for the owner's ruling

Deliverable of [L3.32-T38], `tier: codex (default)`. This dossier is the
evidence for the owner's ruling, not a ruling itself. Nothing here writes to
`dev/glossary.toml`; the orchestrator writes the entries after the ruling.
Written incrementally: skeleton first, each term filled as its findings
landed.

## Method

Per `dev/ORCHESTRATION.md` section 6: search the Chinese literature for the
established rendering first; only where none exists, draft two or three
candidates by analogy, present the reasoning, and let the owner rule. Each
term reports (a) the established Chinese rendering with its source, if one
exists; (b) otherwise two or three candidates with the analogy each rests on
and the collision each avoids; (c) a recommended `avoid` list; (d) the
Japanese rendering if a source gives one. A term where the literature is
silent is said so plainly, and the final section lists every term where the
recommendation is a guess rather than a finding.

The in-use renderings are quoted from the zh prose of the delivered chapters
(evidence as `file:line`). Where the chapters already drift between two
renderings, the drift is reported, because ending it is exactly what the
glossary entry is for.

## 1. face

**Sense.** The side an object presents to a consumer: "the satisfaction face
of Def", "the initial-segment face", "the crossing's working face"
(`src/L/InitialSegment.lagda.md:60,96,102`; `src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:95,141,301`).
The idiom "on the face of it" is a different
sense and already renders 表面 / 乍看 (`src/L/Axioms/Numerals.lagda.md:123`，`src/Base/Choice.lagda.md:101`)；the glossary term must not cover it.

**In use.** 面孔，seven zh sites: `src/L/InitialSegment.lagda.md:98,116`;
`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:303`; `src/L/PairAtoms.lagda.md:24`;
`src/L/LevelFormula.lagda.md:29`; `src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:28`;
`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:902`.

**(a) Established rendering.** None found. The searches returned only the
literal face (「面孔」as face recognition, polyhedron face = 多面体的面). No
Chinese mathematical writing found that uses face as a metaphor for the side
an object presents. The literature is silent.

**(b) Candidates** (no established rendering, so by analogy):
- 面孔 (in use): the concrete metaphor, an object shows its face to the
  consumer. Avoids 面 (polytope face, and the overused standalone character)
  and 接口 (interface, already used for the adequacy interface,
  `src/L/InitialSegment.lagda.md:157`).
- 门面 (storefront): the front one presents; more commercial, and 门面 is not
  otherwise a math word.
- 面貌 (appearance): reads as static looks rather than an interface.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:面` (polytope face; overloaded), `zh:接口` (interface),
`zh:界面` (interface / UI), `zh:表面` (surface; also the idiom's rendering).
The idiom sense is out of scope, not on the avoid list.

**(d) Japanese.** No source. Candidates 顔 (literal face) and 表面 (collides).
Tentative：顔，pending a literature check or owner ruling.

**Recommendation.** Keep 面孔：consistent across seven zh sites, no collision,
no established competitor. Guess: the literature is silent.

## 2. initial segment

**Sense.** An initial segment of the L-tower: a function whose domain is an
ordinal in the carrier, starting at the empty set, taking Def steps at
successors and unions at limits (`src/L/InitialSegment.lagda.md:8-10`); also
the collapse read as an initial segment in the square-law argument
(`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:245-247`).

**In use.** Drift confirmed. 初始段 in `src/L/InitialSegment.lagda.md:23,73,180`，`src/L/PairAtoms.lagda.md:22`，`src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:28,485`，`src/L/LevelFormula.lagda.md:29,868`；初段 in
`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:13,256` and `src/Everything.lagda.md:327`.
The provisional suggestion 初段 is exactly the drift minority.

**(a) Established rendering.** 初始段. Sources:
- 数学辞海第一卷 (山西教育出版社)，index p. 624 lists 初始段 and 初始节；the
  transfinite-induction entry defines it：「只要初始段
  X₁ = {β ∈ X | β < α} ⊆ E 就有 α ∈ E」. (Search snippet of the book text;
  the book itself is a published printed encyclopedia, not paywalled but not
  online in full.)
- 实变函数论 (И.П.那坦松，高等教育出版社，Chinese translation)：「W 与其一初始段 W_γ 相似」.
- 集合论基础 (Shen & Vereshchagin，陈光还译，高等教育出版社)：序数 α 与序数 β
  的一个真初始段序同构当且仅当 α ∈ β.
- The 初始节 entry of 数学辞海 is a variant found in the same index；前段
  (banana-space.org 集合论讲义 defines 前段 as the initial segment of a
  well-ordered set, the set of its predecessors) and 初片段 (zhihu notes)
  also circulate. None of these displace 初始段.

**(b)** Not needed: an established rendering exists.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:初段` (the drift form; also the established word for the
lowest Go rank，「初段」= first dan, which reads as a grade, not a segment),
`zh:前段` (attested but minority; also means "front section" in ordinary
prose)，`zh:初始节` (数学辞海 variant), `zh:初片段` (zhihu variant).

**(d) Japanese.** 始切片. Sources: a Mie University repository paper speaks of「E-始切片」for initial segments of a well-order (mie-u.repo.nii.ac.jp,
record 1198); Tsukuba logic lecture notes define it: "I ⊂ X が
y ∈ x ∈ I → y ∈ I が成立するとき，始切片という" (math.tsukuba.ac.jp,
tsuboi/und/14logic3.pdf).

**Recommendation.** 初始段：the established textbook and encyclopedia
rendering, and the majority in the delivered prose. Refutes the provisional
初段 on both counts (literature and collision with the Go rank).

## 3. presentation

**Sense.** The HIT-flavoured sense: a set given as an index type plus an
indexing map, "the small member type `⟪ a ⟫` presents the set `a` through the
embedding `⟪ a ⟫↪`" (`src/V/Presentation.lagda.md:11`); "a set is presented
by its own members" (`src/L/Coding/CodeSet.lagda.md:379`).

**In use.** 呈现，thirteen files, e.g. `src/V/Presentation.lagda.md:11`
(小呈现，呈现集合)，`src/L/Coding/Shape.lagda.md:397` (一个集合由其诸成员的呈现)，`src/L/Coding/CodeSet.lagda.md:379`，plus the Recursion / Choice / Rud /
Axioms chapters.

**(a) Established rendering.** None found for this sense. Chinese algebra
renders "presentation" (of a group) as 表示 or periphrastically as
由生成元与关系给出 (e.g. the 清华大学出版社 abstract-algebra textbook sample：「G 是由元 a 所生成的」)；the HIT sense "a set presented by a type" has no
settled Chinese word in the sources searched (HoTT Chinese materials do not
fix it). The literature is silent on this sense.

**(b) Candidates**:
- 呈现 (in use): the verb-noun "presenting"; reads naturally as「集合由类型呈现」. Avoids 表示，which collides with representation, already a glossary
  entry (`dev/glossary.toml:349`).
- 给出 (give): generic and weak; collides with ordinary "given by".
- 展示 (display): suggests exhibition, not a mathematical giving.
- 呈示 (Japanese-flavoured variant of 呈现；not found in Chinese math).

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:表示` (representation, glossary entry), `zh:表现`
(renders representation in ja thinking and "behaviour" in zh), `zh:展示`.

**(d) Japanese.** No source for the HIT sense. Japanese algebra's 群の表示
(group presentation) suggests 表示，which does not collide in ja because the
glossary fixes representation as ja 表現. Tentative：表示，pending a check.

**Recommendation.** Keep 呈现：consistent across thirteen files, and the
collision analysis is exactly the one the provisional suggestion named. Guess:
the literature is silent for this sense.

## 4. crossing

**Sense.** Moving a statement between an ambient reading and an inner one:
the condensation "crosses from that inner reading" to the ambient one
(`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:11-12`), "the crossing obligation"
(`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:139`), "the first reader to cross"
(`src/L/Coding/Model.lagda.md:15`), "a witness crossing between the languages"
(`src/L/Choice/Limit.lagda.md:780`).

**In use.** 跨越，ten files, e.g. `src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:303`
(整条跨越，跨越义务)，`src/L/Coding/Bridge.lagda.md:112` (跨越层次)，plus the
PairAtoms / LevelFormula / Rud / InitialSegment files.

**(a) Established rendering.** None found. Searches for 跨越 / 跨层 in
mathematical and logical terminology returned only ordinary prose (a school
book title「跨越从算术到代数」) and unrelated usages (跨层连接 in neural
networks). The literature is silent.

**(b) Candidates**:
- 跨越 (in use): "to stride across", the natural verb-noun for moving between
  readings; already the condensation chapter's word.
- 跨层 (cross-level): more precise about the ambient/inner levels, but
  collides with the neural-network term 跨层连接 and reads as network jargon.
- 穿越 (pass through): wrong flavour, suggests time travel or paths.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:交叉` (crossing in the graph-theoretic sense，交叉数 =
crossing number), `zh:过渡` (transition, a different motion), `zh:跨层`
(jargon collision), `ja:横断` (Japanese transversal).

**(d) Japanese.** No source. 横断 collides with transversal；越境 is literary.
Tentative: none offered; leave to the owner.

**Recommendation.** Keep 跨越：consistent, no collision, no competitor. Guess:
the literature is silent.

## 5. decode

**Sense.** Reading a code back into the thing it codes: the shared decode that
reads the object-language clauses back into meta-level readings
(`src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:143-146`), the decode that walks a shape with a
constant list (`src/FOL/Count.lagda.md:461`).

**In use.** 解码，seventeen files, e.g. `src/FOL/Count.lagda.md:461`,
`src/L/PairAtoms.lagda.md:24`, `src/L/Coding/CodeSet.lagda.md:379`. No drift:
no chapter uses 译码.

**(a) Established rendering.** Both 解码 and 译码 are attested; the literature
is split rather than silent:
- 译码 / 译出 is the coding-theory and computability rendering：可计算性引论 (王元元，东南大学出版社) decodes by「译出语句」；coding theory books (e.g.代数编码导引，樊恽、刘宏伟，科学出版社) treat 编码 and its inverse; Huffman
  work in Chinese data-structure texts is usually 哈夫曼编码与译码.
- 解码 is the general CS and school rendering: a published primary-school
  math unit on codes teaches「编码和解码能力」；video/network decoding is 解码.
Neither is wrong for this project's sense; the two coexist in Chinese.

**(b)** Not needed in the strict sense, but the choice is a judgment：译码 is
the more "mathematical logic" flavoured option，解码 the more general one.

**(c) Avoid.** None strictly required. If the owner wants one rendering，译码
could be added to avoid to kill any future drift, but no chapter uses it today.

**(d) Japanese.** No math-logic source found. General Japanese for decoding is
復号 (coding theory, cryptography). Tentative：復号.

**Recommendation.** Keep 解码：used in all seventeen sites, pairs naturally
with the prose's 编码 (encoding) in the coding chapters, and is the common
rendering. Note 译码 as the coding-theory alternative if the owner prefers
the more traditional form. Partial guess: the choice between two attested
renderings is a judgment, not a literature finding.

canonical well-ordering", "its order type is τ" (`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:244`;

**(a) Established rendering.** 序型. Sources:

**(b)** Not needed, but see the 序类型 note.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:序类型` only if the owner wants the short form canonical

**(d) Japanese.** 順序型. Sources: Tsukuba logic lecture notes，「整列順序の順序型を順序数 (ordinal) という」(math.tsukuba.ac.jp, tsuboi/gra/logic09.pdf);

**Recommendation.** 序型：the encyclopedia and textbook rendering, and the

## 7. canonical well-ordering

**Sense.** The well-ordering of the product of ordinal members: Gödel's
ordering of Ord × Ord, "the canonical well-ordering of the product"
(`src/L/Ordinal/Pairing.lagda.md:155,170,586`).

**In use.** 典范良序，three files: `src/L/Ordinal/Pairing.lagda.md:18,155,586`;
`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:256`. One site writes 典范 (Godel) 良序
(`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:13`).

**(a) Established rendering.** 典范良序. Sources:
- 李文威代数学方法 (第一卷：基础架构)(高等教育出版社)：「称作 α×α 上的典范良序」. (Confirmed via the book's searchable text; the book is also open
  source at github.com/wenweili/AlJabr-1, though the master LaTeX does not
  carry this exact phrase.)
- banana-space.org 集合论讲义，基数 chapter：「诱导的上的良序称为它的典范良序」for the Ord × Ord ordering.
The compound reads naturally and the glossary already fixes 典范 (canonical)
and 良序 (well-order) as separate entries.

**(b)** Not needed.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:典型良序` (a dictionary rendering，有道，「typical」rather
than「canonical」)，`zh:正则良序` (正则 collides with regular cardinals and
regular open sets), `zh:自然良序` (not found; would suggest "natural").

**(d) Japanese.** No source. Candidates：正準整列順序 (正準 is the Japanese
math word for canonical，整列順序 is the glossary's well-order)，標準的整列順序.
Tentative：正準整列順序.

**Recommendation.** 典范良序：two published/lecture sources and consistency
with the existing glossary entries.

## 8. square law

**Sense.** The cardinal identity: the product of the members of an infinite
ordinal injects into the ordinal itself, "the classical square law for
infinite ordinals", cited as a bound, not proved as a theorem
(`src/L/Ordinal/Pairing.lagda.md:18,586,619`; `src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:13,256`).

**In use.** 平方律，three files: `src/L/Ordinal/Pairing.lagda.md:18,586`;
`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:13,256,758`. 平方定理 appears nowhere in the
prose.

**(a) Established rendering.** None found under the name "square law". The
Chinese literature names the fact by its statement rather than a short name:
the Googology wiki article「无穷基数的平方等于自身」states and proves
ℵα × ℵα = ℵα via the Ord² well-ordering. No Chinese source found uses 平方定理
for this identity (the only 平方定理 hits are unrelated, e.g. Fermat's
two-square theorem). The literature is silent on the name.

**(b) Candidates**:
- 平方律 (in use): "square law", matches the English name and the chapter
  name SquareLaw; the brief's point that it is cited as a bound rather than
  proved as a theorem supports 律 over 定理.
- 平方定理：would upgrade a cited bound to a proved theorem, which the
  chapter explicitly does not do; no Chinese source uses it here.
- 平方等势：descriptive but clunky and not attested.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:平方定理` (wrong status: a bound, not a proved theorem, and
unattested for this fact), `zh:平方反比律` (physics, inverse square law, a
different law).

**(d) Japanese.** No source. 平方律 is the generic Japanese for "square law"
(physics usage). Tentative：平方律.

**Recommendation.** 平方律：the in-use rendering, matches the English term and
the chapter name, and the bound-versus-theorem distinction the brief asks
about comes out in its favour (no source calls it 平方定理). Guess: the
literature is silent on the name.

## 9. equinumerous

**Sense.** Two sets with a bijection between them: "the least ordinal
equinumerous with α", "no smaller member equinumerous with it"
(`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:571-572`; `src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md:558`).

**In use.** Drift confirmed. 等势 in `src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:572,758`
and `src/L/Ordinal/Pairing.lagda.md:586`；等数 in
`src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md:23,322,543,558` (including the heading
等数、基数、后继基数).

**(a) Established rendering.** 等势. Sources:
- 数学辞海第四卷：「将集合的基数 (势) 定义为在等势关系下该集合所在的等价类」.
- 百度百科，等势：「指两个集合之间存在双射 (一一映射)，从而具有相同的基数」.
- 集合论、拓扑与代数初步：「任何一个无限集，必然包含一个与 N 等势的子集」.
等数 is attested only as an older form：汉典 gives 等数 =「相等的数」，and a
translation of Menne's 方法论导论 (王路译) uses「两个集合叫作等数的」for
equinumerous sets. It is not the modern standard.

**(b)** Not needed.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:等数` (the drift form in CardinalPredicates; older
rendering; also the ordinary word for "equal number"), `zh:均势` (equilibrium
in politics), `zh:对等` (Chinese rendering of reciprocity; and the Japanese
word, see below).

**(d) Japanese.** 対等. Sources: Osaka Metropolitan University lecture notes
(田丸，24yoron-a / 25yoron-a，「集合 A と B が対等であるとは … 全単射」)；Tohoku
University OCW cardinality notes list 対等関係 among its keywords.

**Recommendation.** 等势：the established modern rendering and the majority
form in the prose; the CardinalPredicates 等数 drift is exactly what the
avoid list is for.

## 10. initial ordinal

**Sense.** An ordinal not equinumerous with any smaller ordinal: "the
reduction lands on initial ordinals" (`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:13`),
"cardinal is an ordinal equinumerous with no smaller one, that is, the
initial-ordinal notion" (`src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md:558`).

**In use.** 初始序数：`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:13`；`src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md:558`.

**(a) Established rendering.** 初始序数. Sources:
- 数学辞海第一卷，index p. 626：初始序数.
- 百度百科初始序数：「若序数 α 不与任何序数 β < α 等势，则 α 称为初始序数」.
- 科普中国初始序数：same definition.
- Chinese Wikipedia (冯·诺伊曼基数指派)：「有给定基数作为它的势的最小的序数被叫做这个基数的初始序数」.

**(b)** Not needed.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:起始序数` (a rendering found on the Bohrium sciencepedia
page，「唯一的起始序数」，not established), `zh:初始基数` (mixes the ordinal
and cardinal notions).

**(d) Japanese.** 始順序数. Source: the Japanese Wikipedia label on the
DBpedia entry for Von Neumann cardinal assignment gives 始順序数
(しじゅんじょすう，「initial ordinal」)；the Japanese article explains the
cardinal as the least ordinal of its cardinality.

**Recommendation.** 初始序数：established, in use, no collision.

(`src/FOL/Count.lagda.md:77`).

**(a) Established rendering.** None for this specific scheme. The generic
notion is established as 配对函数 (百度百科 and 科普中国：「在数学中，配对函数是唯一编码两个自然数到一个单一的自然数的过程」)，and Cantor's version is 康托尔配对函数.
This square variant is not Cantor's, and no Chinese name for it was found.
Literature silent on the name.

**(b) Candidates**:
- 平方配对 (in use): "square pairing", compact and distinct from 平方律.
- 平方配对函数：fuller；配对函数 is the established generic, so adding 平方
  reads naturally, but the English term here is "square pairing", not "square
  pairing function".
- 平方方案配对：clunky; the prose already uses 平方方案 for the scheme.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:康托尔配对` / `zh:康托尔配对函数` (a different, non-square
scheme), `zh:平方律` (item 8, the cardinal law), `zh:配对律` (unattested).

**(d) Japanese.** No source. Japanese for pairing function is 対関数
(Cantor's is カントールの対関数). Tentative：平方対関数.

**Recommendation.** 平方配对：in use, distinct from 平方律 by one character,
and the generic 配对函数 gives it a clear reading. Guess: the literature is
silent for the scheme.

## 14. pair atom

**Sense.** The atomic formulas of the pairing vocabulary: "this variable is
the singleton of that one", "the unordered pair of those two", "the
Kuratowski pair of those two" (`src/L/PairAtoms.lagda.md:11-14`), written as
object-language atoms under a bounded existential (`src/L/LevelFormula.lagda.md:173`).

**In use.** Drift confirmed. 对原子 in `src/L/PairAtoms.lagda.md:24,118`,
`src/L/LevelFormula.lagda.md:100,184`, `src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:146`;
有序对原子 (ordered-pair atom) in `src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md:382`;
对子原子 in `src/Everything.lagda.md:326` only. The provisional suggestion
对子原子 is exactly the catalog-only form.

**(a) Established rendering.** None found for the compound. The base word is
established：原子公式 (atomic formula，百度百科). No source for 对原子 or
对子原子. Literature silent.

**(b) Candidates**:
- 对原子 (in use in all chapters): the atom about pairs，对 as a modifier.
  Matches the book's own 对 + noun pattern：对编码 (pair coding,
  `src/L/PairAtoms.lagda.md:73`)，对套件 (pair kit)，对证书 (pair certificate,
  `src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md:382`).
- 对子原子 (catalog only)：对子 as a noun "a pair/couple"; reads as
  "pair-atom" but 对子 is colloquial (a pair of things, a couple), and it
  breaks the book's 对 + noun pattern.
- 配对原子：「pairing atom」，but 配对 is already the verb for pairing
  functions and blurs into item 13's territory.

**(c) Avoid.** `zh:对子原子` (drift form; colloquial 对子)，`zh:配对原子` (blurs with pairing), `zh:原子对` (atomic pair, the wrong order: an atom that
is a pair, not an atom about pairs).

**(d) Japanese.** No source. Tentative：対原子，by kanji correspondence, pending
a check.

**Recommendation.** 对原子：the unanimous chapter rendering, fits the book's
对 + noun pattern, and 原子公式 gives the reading "atom(ic formula) about
pairs". Refutes the provisional 对子原子. Guess: the literature is silent.
