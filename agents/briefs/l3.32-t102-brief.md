## THE THING MOST LIKELY TO KILL THIS, AND YOU SHOULD LOOK FOR IT FIRST

**There may be TWO presentations here, not one.** `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫` is the
obvious one. But `γp p` is itself `⟪ α ⟫↪ (max' ...)`, so `⟪ α ⟫` appears too,
and `h₀-inj`'s proof uses `↪-inj {a = α}` (`:404`, `:408`).

**Abstracting only the codomain may leave `⟪ α ⟫` in the telescope and buy
nothing, or reproduce `[T88]`'s heap wall.** Establish early how many
presentations the statement actually carries, and say so: that fact alone is a
publishable finding and it decides the shape of any cure.
