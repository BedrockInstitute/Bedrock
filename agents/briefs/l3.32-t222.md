# [L3.32-T222] The deciding rate: what does a generic STEP cost per line?

tier: codex (default; a D-1 timing probe. It MEASURES SECONDS and the
number it returns decides the wall, so it needs a quiet machine and the
orchestrator holds the second slot open.)

## GOAL

`[T221]` showed the below-lim master is RESHAPEABLE: the general STEP
at a variable limit subsumes the first-limit construction, saving
898-933 lines. Its seconds verdict turns on **one number nobody has
measured**, and this probe measures it.

**The question.** `[T219]` established two rates at this site:
parameterized content checks near **0.010 s per line**, instantiation
content near **0.22 s per line** (laws P-m and P-n). The generic STEP
is a THIRD shape that no measured episode covers: it **states
object-language formulas and proves their two-way decodes at a
BOUND-VARIABLE carrier**, under a module telescope, with the induction
hypothesis as a parameter.

Which rate does that shape check at?

- At 0.010-0.013 s per line, the tree lands **409-423 s** against 498:
  the gate clears with 75-89 s.
- At `[T215]`'s cured rate, 0.063, it lands **462-486**: clears by
  12-36.
- At 0.22, it lands **630-694**: the gate FAILS by 132-196.

## WHAT TO BUILD

**A representative slice of the generic STEP, not the STEP.** Pick the
piece whose shape is most characteristic: an object-language formula
stated at a variable limit `γ` with a bound-variable carrier, plus its
two-way decode. `[T221]` section 3 names the STEP's content; the
witness layer at `Lset γ` or the bounded formula with its decode are
the natural candidates. State the carrier as a module parameter, take
the induction hypothesis as a parameter, and prove the decode.

**Then time it, and compute the rate.** The rate is the deliverable.
The lines are only its denominator.

## THE STOP LINES

- **150 non-blank in-fence lines.** Over that, stop and report the rate
  from what you built.
- **If the slice exceeds 30 s cold**, stop: the answer is already the
  instantiation rate and the campaign needs to know immediately.
- **No postulate, no hole, no `{-# TERMINATING #-}`**: a postulate
  makes the timing meaningless. Module parameters are the point of the
  shape; list each with its reason.
- **R-40, R-35, I-5, P-l** bind. **P-n binds hardest**: if the cost
  shows up anyway at a bound-variable carrier, that is the finding, and
  it means the reshape does not buy seconds.

## MACHINE DISCIPLINE, and the timing is the deliverable

- Before EVERY timed run, poll `pgrep -x agda` and wait until empty.
  Label every figure CLEAN or CONTENDED. A contended figure is not a
  measurement and must not enter the rate.
- `GHCRTS=-M8g agda <file>`, one process, cold with dependencies warm.
  Three runs; report all three and the mean.
- Report a heap exhaustion as a wall. Never raise the cap.
