## WHY A REWRITE, AND THE ROOT CAUSE YOU MUST ADDRESS

Three defects of one kind were found in this chapter's structural story in one
day, all by different agents, and **every one of them typechecked perfectly**:

1. `sndIn` read a FIRST component while its name, its comment and all three use
   sites said second, so the range clause `Rg` read a **domain**. Fixed at
   `src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:386-387`.
2. The corrected `snd∈Snd` asserts **equality** of second components where the
   limit clause needs **membership**, so `limOut` still does not read the
   delivered inclusion.
3. `limIn` has its own binder error: its second binder ranges over the
   singleton member rather than the pair member, making the value inclusion
   **vacuous**, machine-checked unsatisfiable at a concrete pair.

**The root cause is not carelessness, it is what the proofs certify.** The
delivered obligations are `Δ₀` and `Σ₁` witnesses, which are claims about a
formula's SHAPE. A wrong de Bruijn index changes a formula's MEANING while
leaving its shape untouched, so nothing in the pipeline could have caught any
of these: they passed every linter, every gate, and several full typechecks.
`σᴹ`, `σL`, both transports and the Levy certificates were all built on top of
the wrong content, and stayed green.

**So the rewrite's defining requirement is this: every atom carries a two-way
decode against a delivered meta-level reading, and no atom may be used before
its decode exists.** That is the discipline the rest of the campaign already
uses (the face's `read-off`, `LevelSigma`'s decodes, `L.Rud.OpGraph`'s
two-way graphs); the structural story is where it was skipped. Shape
certificates stay, but they stop being the only thing proved.
