  birth, and never `with` on a transparent least-witness term, measured 60x),
  **R-36** (opaque-unfolding read lemmas), **R-35** (state at the small index).
  A chapter that skipped them is the likely hot spot.

## THE MEASUREMENT COMES FIRST, AND GUESSING IS THE FAILURE MODE

**Do not propose a sealing plan from reading the source.** Where Agda spends
time is famously counter-intuitive, and this project's own lessons were all
won by measurement.

Agda 2.8.0 supports `--profile`. Establish the per-module breakdown, by
whatever method you can actually make work, and **report the method you used**:

- `agda --profile=modules src/Everything.lagda.md` if that option exists in
  this build (check `agda --help`), which gives the whole breakdown in one run;
- otherwise per-module timing, with the caveat that checking one master cold
  pulls its dependencies in and the numbers overlap;
- `-v profile:7` or the RTS statistics if they add anything.

**A cold run costs about half an hour of wall clock and demands a quiet tree**
(no agent may write a master while it runs, or it reads half-written files).
Two Agda agents are live now; the orchestrator will tell you if that changes.
**Plan for at most ONE full cold run**, and get everything you can from it.

## THE QUESTIONS

1. **What is the honest steady-state cold time**, on an unchanged tree, today?
2. **Where does it go?** Rank the modules by their own cost. Name the top few
   and say what they have in common.
3. **Is the owner's comparison sound?** The retiring internalization tree is
   still in the repository (`src/L/Coding/`, `src/L/Godel/`, `src/L/Choice/`,
   about 30,000 lines of it). **Measure it**: how long does that subtree cost
   today, and how does its per-line cost compare with the surviving trunk's?
   If the retiring tree really is three to four minutes for 30,000 lines while
   the survivors take much longer for 18,000, the difference is the finding and
   the answer is in what those chapters did differently.
4. **What would sealing buy, specifically?** Per hot module: what is
   unsealed, what would sealing it cost in lines and in risk, and what would it
   save in seconds. **Measured, not estimated**, at least for the top one: seal
   it in a scratch copy and time it.
5. **Is five minutes reachable at all?** Answer plainly. If it is not, say what
   is reachable and what the residual is made of. A number nobody can hit is
   worse than an honest one.
