## WHAT THIS IS

**Look at the mathematics.** With the rud route and the thresholds
fixed, find NEW mathematics and NEW method that make the proof shorter.
Two named directions, and anything else you see:

1. **Generalize a lemma so one proof serves many sites.** The tree
   proves many things several times at different instantiations. A
   statement general enough to cover them all is one proof instead of
   n, and the campaign has already measured this working once:
   `L.Rud.StepGraph`'s frames carry the shared form of sixteen
   operations, and block 2 landed at rate x0.79, UNDER delivered size,
   because the frames absorbed the repetition. **Find the other
   n-fold structures.**
2. **Restructure the modules.** Ask whether the current decomposition
   is the one the mathematics wants. A boundary in the wrong place
   forces content to be restated on both sides; a telescope that should
   be a record, or a record that should be a telescope, taxes every
   use site. `[T178]`'s extraction of `L.TowerKit` is the small
   version of this; ask whether a larger one exists.

## THREADS WORTH PULLING, none of them prescriptions

You are the mathematician here; these are only where the orchestrator
would start.

- **Two-way decodes are everywhere.** The membership formulas, the
  story clauses, the frames, the adequacy proofs: each states a
  formula and proves both directions against a delivered spec. Is
  there a general adequacy combinator that takes the spec and yields
  the decode, so each site writes only what is specific to it?
- **The two towers.** Many facts are proved for `Sset` and again for
  `Lset`. The towers differ in their step (sixteen operations against
  the definable power) but share their shape (zero, successor, limit,
  cumulativity, transitivity). Is there a general tower over an
  abstract step whose instances are both? `L.Rud.Hierarchy` is the
  S-side engine; ask what a two-tower engine would cost and save.
- **The inductions.** `Q`, `below-lim`, `rudBelow`, the story's
  clauses, and now the carried order-story all run inductions over
  ordinals with limit cases. `[T185]` found that stating one of them
  as a limit induction dissolved a whole term. Is there a general
  induction principle for "a property that holds at zero, transfers at
  successors, and is carried at limits by a sequence", proved once?
- **Carrier genericity (P-h).** Where does content still name a
  concrete carrier that a parameter would generalize? The law is old;
  the corpus may still violate it in places the line counts hide.
- **The story clauses.** Six clauses, each with a formula and a decode.
  Is the sixth (the limit clause) an instance of a general shape the
  other five share, or genuinely different?

## THE RULES OF EVIDENCE

- **Every candidate names its mechanism** so a builder could execute
  it, the SITES it would collapse at `file:line`, and a band with a
  basis. A generalization with no named sites is an aesthetic
  preference, not a saving.
- **Price the generalization's own cost.** A general lemma has a
  telescope, and every instantiation pays for it. `[T169]` measured a
  dedup at 27 lines against a 60-140 survey precisely because the
  carrier cost most of the saving. Say what each candidate's carrier
  costs.
- **Seconds are a threshold too.** A generalization that saves lines
  and costs seconds may be inadmissible: the wall margin is 102.8 s and
  `[T211]` just measured a term that may consume most of it. Say what
  each candidate does to check time, or say that it is unmeasured.
- **P-l binds.** A rate measured at one site is a hypothesis at
  another.
