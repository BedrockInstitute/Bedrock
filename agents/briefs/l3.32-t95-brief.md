## WHAT TO ATTACK, IN PRIORITY ORDER

### 1. The parser, `scripts/obligations.py`

Read `scan()` and try to break it. Specific things worth testing, and you
should invent more:

- **Multi-line type signatures.** The tool dropped continuation tracking on the
  argument that "Agda's layout rule makes a continuation line indent past the
  declaration it continues, so a column-0 line is always a new declaration."
  **Is that argument sound, and does it hold for INDENTED signatures too?** An
  indented signature's continuation is indented further, and if such a line
  begins with something matching `name : `, it is double counted. Find a real
  instance in `src/` or prove none can exist.
- **The name character class** is `[^\s:;{}()@"]+`. What legitimate Agda
  construct does that wrongly admit or wrongly reject? Consider `⦃ ⦄`
  instance brackets, `{ }` implicit binders at line start, `[ ]`, `.` dotted
  patterns, `@` as-patterns, `_` sections, and operators containing a colon.
- **Record fields** are counted (the `field` line is skipped, its indented
  members are not). Is that right? Argue it either way, but notice it is a
  CHOICE the docstring does not defend.
- **`where`-clause names, `with`-abstractions, pattern-matching lambdas,
  copatterns.** Are they counted, and should they be?
- **Definitions without a signature** (`foo x = ...`) are invisible. **Measure
  how many exist** in each tree. If the two trees differ in this habit, the
  ratio is biased and nobody has checked. This is the single most likely place
  for a fourth defect.
