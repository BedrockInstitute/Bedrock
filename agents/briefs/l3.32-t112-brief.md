The owner asked whether section 11's MASTER status table and the new Task index
need to be two tables. Measured:

| | rows | median | max | total chars |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MASTER status | 96 | 224 | **6,966** | 50,639 |
| Task index | 111 | 92 | 117 | 10,231 |

**51 of 96 goal rows exceed the task index's 200-character cap.** The four
worst are `L3.32-F5` (1,119 words in its status cell), `L3.32-F2` (780),
`L3.32-F6.0` (566) and `L3.32-F0` (469), all written by the orchestrator today.

**This is the same defect `[T108]` fixed hours ago at a larger scale**, where
the `L3.32` cell had reached 12,633 words of episode content in a register.
The taxonomy that condemns it is in `AGENTS.md`: a ruling is a PLAN row, an
episode is a JOURNAL entry.

**And the gate that should have caught it does not.** `[T110]`'s
`plan-cell-size` check caps a cell at 1,600 words, derived from the 12,633-word
failure; `L3.32-F5` at 1,119 words passes green. A threshold derived only from
the last accident does not catch the next one.

## WHAT TO DO

### 1. Slim every goal row, moving the narrative to JOURNAL

Each row in section 11's MASTER status table keeps only what a register row
needs: **the ruling and its authority, the CURRENT status, what it is blocked
or gated on, and a pointer to the detail.** Everything whose tense is past and
whose subject is a dispatch moves to `dev/JOURNAL.md`.

`dev/JOURNAL.md` is already organised by goal (`## [L3.29]`, `## [L3.30]`,
`## [L3.31]`, `## [L3.32]`), so a row's narrative goes into its goal's section.
The `L3.32-F*` rows are sub-goals of `[L3.32]` and their narrative belongs in
that section, which `[T108]` has already populated.

**Judgement matters more than mechanism here.** A row that is one sentence of
status needs nothing done to it. Do not churn the 45 rows that are already
short: touching them costs review attention and buys nothing.

### 2. Re-derive the cap, and argue it

Set a character cap for a goal row and **argue it from what a register row
actually needs**, not from the failure. Inputs you have: the current median is
224 characters, the task index cap is 200 for a pure pointer row, and a goal
row legitimately carries more than a task row because it holds a ruling and its
authority.

**State what a row must be able to say at your cap, and show a real row that
fits.** A cap nobody can write a legitimate row under will be raised the first
week, which makes it worse than none.

### 3. Tighten the gate so it catches this class

`scripts/check-dev-docs.py`'s `plan-cell-size` currently caps a cell at 1,600
words and misses `L3.32-F5` at 1,119. Bring it in line with your cap, keep
`[T110]`'s regression tests passing, and add the current worst rows as new
fixtures so the check would have fired on today's tree.

`scripts/tests/test_dev_docs.py` is `[T110]`'s, 23 cases, all currently
passing. **Extend it, do not rewrite it.**
