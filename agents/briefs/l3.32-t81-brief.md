## WHAT TO SEARCH FOR

At least these, and follow what you find:

- CSB in **cubical Agda** or the `agda/cubical` library (issues, pull requests,
  `Cubical.Foundations`, contributed files, gists, blog posts);
- CSB in the **Agda standard library** (`Function.Bijection`, `Relation.Binary`)
  and in **Coq**, **Lean's mathlib**, **Isabelle** and **Rocq**, for technique
  rather than transcription;
- the **constructive/HoTT literature**: the Escardo-style treatments, the
  HoTT book's treatment, Pradic and Brown's constructive CSB work, and anything
  on CSB for **sets rather than types** (which is our case, and is often
  simpler);
- proofs that avoid the usual back-and-forth chain construction, since that
  chain is usually where the lines go.
