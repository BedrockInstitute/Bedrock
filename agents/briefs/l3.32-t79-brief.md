## THE BAR A LEVER MUST CLEAR

A proposal is only useful if it is measured. For each lever:

1. **Name the lines.** Which files, which regions, how many lines are in scope.
   Count them (non-blank inside ` ```agda ` fences) rather than estimating.
2. **Say what replaces them**, concretely enough that someone could brief it.
3. **Price the replacement from the rewrite side** (D17), in both calibers with
   its class. **D-19 prices PORTS and must not be quoted at a rewrite
   question.**
4. **Give the NET**: lines removed minus lines written, and say plainly whether
   it is positive. A lever that saves 300 and costs 280 is not a lever.
5. **Name the risk.** What breaks, what has to be re-proved, and which
   consumers move. A lever whose risk you cannot name has not been thought
   through.

**Rank by net lines saved per unit of risk, and say which single lever you
would do first.**
