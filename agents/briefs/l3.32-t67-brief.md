For each of the nine crossings, answer four things:

1. **What is actually imported?** The exact names, not the module. A crossing

2. **Is the content itself retiring, or only its current home?** Three
   outcomes, and say which: the name is dead with its chapter; the name is
   alive and must move to a surviving chapter (say which, and whether that
   chapter exists); or the name is alive and already has a surviving twin (say
   where, and the crossing is then a one-line re-point).
3. **What does re-homing cost**, in both calibers with its class? Follow D17:
   price the IDEAL FORM of the content the consumer actually needs, written
   fresh today, not the cost of copying what is there.
4. **When must it happen?** Some crossings can be re-homed now, independently;
   others must wait for a build that has not landed. Say which, because that
   ordering is what makes the surgery schedulable at all.

Then give the **total**, in both calibers, as a paste-ready replacement for the
