## THE RULE THAT MAKES THIS WORK, AND IT IS NOT THE ONE FROM DISPATCH 1

Dispatch 1's rule was "keep the presentation out of the generic telescope".
**This one is different and sharper**, and it is what `[T102]` discovered:

**The cost is in the BODIES' concrete applications, not in the statements.**
The four concrete memberships `fst∈sucmax {a , b} {p} pr` at
`β = sucV (γp p)` force the normal form of `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫` wherever they
appear, **including when they are merely passed as arguments**. Moving them
into module parameters means they are checked once at a neutral position.

**So the test after you build is not "is the body short". It is: does any
concrete application of `fiber`, `fst∈sucmax` or `snd∈sucmax` at the tower
position remain anywhere inside `h₀` or `h₀-inj`, including inside their
arguments?** If one does, you have rebuilt the thing that cost 374 seconds.
This is P-l's corrected text; read it before you start.
