# [L3.32-T205] Where did the 16,000 compression floor come from?

tier: codex (default). A read-only provenance audit. It runs no Agda.

## GOAL

Ruling D36 caps the pure-AC closure at 16,000, and the owner gave TWO
reasons (`dev/PLAN.md`, the D36 cell): beat the internalization route's
delivered 17,496, and beat the 16,000 the campaign's own compression
work said that route could reach. **The first is measured. Audit the
second.**

`[T203]` has just made the audit urgent: the rud route's AC landing
projects 17,810-19,430 naive against the delivered route's measured
17,496, which is a WASH. If the 16,000 floor was itself a survey
figure, then no route has ever been measured under it, and the owner is
holding both routes to a bar that measurement has never supported. If
the floor WAS measured, the campaign needs to know exactly what it
measured and whether that content still exists.

## WHAT TO ESTABLISH

1. **The origin.** Find the 16,000 figure's first appearance. Search
   `dev/`, `_build/`, and `git log` on both branches (`main` carries
   the internalization route). Quote it at `file:line` with its date
   and its author task.
2. **Its basis.** Was it measured, projected from measured comparables,
   or survey? Name the calibration it used. This project's own rule:
   about 1.3x where a probe or comparable reaches, 3x where only a
   survey does.
3. **What it counted.** The caliber matters more than the number: did
   it count the same thing D36 counts (non-blank in-fence lines, the AC
   closure of the ruled route, surviving tree)? A figure computed on a
   different caliber is not a bar, it is a coincidence.
4. **What it assumed would be removed.** A compression floor is a
   claim that specific content goes. List that content and check its
   fate: still present, archived, refuted, or never identified.
5. **The verdict**: the floor is SUPPORTED at its own caliber,
   SUPPORTED-BUT-INCOMPARABLE (measured, but not the same caliber D36
   uses), or UNSUPPORTED (a survey figure that measurement has since
   passed by).

## RETURN

Write `_build/l3.32-t205-report.md` incrementally (C-22):

1. **First line: the verdict**, with the figure's origin at
   `file:line` and its date.
2. The provenance chain, quote by quote, from first appearance to the
   D36 cell.
3. The caliber comparison, stated as a checkable claim.
4. The assumed removals and their fate, one line each.
5. **What the honest bar would be** if the floor is unsupported: what
   number does the evidence support, and on what basis? State it in
   both calibers and name what would have to be true for 16,000.
6. What you could not decide (D-10).

ruled this cap and may keep it whatever you find; your job is the
evidence, not the recommendation.
