tier: codex (default), `--agda`. **The tree is RED and this repairs it.**

## THE DEFECT

`src/L/Rud/HF.lagda.md` fails, exit 42, at `HF.lagda.md:502.28-50`,
`[CannotApply]`. Verified by the orchestrator, 3 s, and independently
by `[T233]`, whose A/B proved the lift is not the cause.

The cause is `[T226]`'s story assembly. Its fold ends every clause list
with a unit:

```agda
  Conj List.[] f = Unit.Unit*
  Conj (c List.∷ cs) f = c f × Conj cs f
```

So a five-clause story now has the type
`c₁ × (c₂ × (c₃ × (c₄ × (c₅ × Unit*))))`, where the hand-written story
had `c₁ × (c₂ × (c₃ × (c₄ × c₅)))`. Every consumer that destructures
POSITIONALLY sees a different shape. At `HF:502` the projection
`st .snd .snd .snd .snd` used to be the clause function `c₅`; it is now
the pair `(c₅ , tt*)`, so Agda reports an expression used as a function
that does not have function type.

**`[T226]` verified that the exported SIGNATURES were byte-identical
and concluded the exports were unchanged. Signature-identical is not
type-identical.** The signatures never mention the conjunction's
internal shape, and the shape is what a positional consumer reads.

## THE FIX

Give the fold a one-element case, so a non-empty list ends BARE:

```agda
  Conj List.[] f = Unit.Unit*
  Conj (c List.∷ List.[]) f = c f
  Conj (c List.∷ cs) f = c f × Conj cs f
```

and make the SAME change to `ConjForm` and to both decode walks,
`conj-out` and `conj-in`, so all four agree
(`src/L/LevelKit.lagda.md`, the story assembly section).

**Why this and not a consumer repair.** Repairing `HF:502` treats the
symptom, leaves every other positional consumer exposed, and keeps the
generalization's types different from the hand form it replaced. This
fix makes the assembly genuinely export-identical for every non-empty
clause list, which is what `[T226]` claimed and what block A needs.

**Check the empty case still works.** `Conj [] f = Unit*` must stay:
the decode walk needs it, and P-o's companion trap says it must be the
universe-polymorphic `Unit.Unit*`, never the cubical `Unit`.
