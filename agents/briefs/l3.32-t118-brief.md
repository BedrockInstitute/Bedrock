## WHAT THIS ARTEFACT ACTUALLY IS, SO THE DESIGN READ IS HONEST

A private engineering board for ONE reader, the project owner, opened from disk
several times a day to answer three questions: where is the work, what is it
costing, and what is the orchestrator about to do. It is not a landing page and
has nothing to sell. It carries a route graph, two pie charts, a goal
hierarchy of about 96 rows, and hand-written notes.

The subject matter is a formal mathematics project: a machine-checked
construction of Goedel's L. Restraint and density suit it. **Dark, by the
owner's standing request.**

## THE HARD CONSTRAINTS, WHICH NO AESTHETIC OVERRIDES

- **Self-contained, offline, static.** Verify and paste both counts:
  `grep -cE 'https?://|src=|@import|fetch\(' _build/dashboard.html` must be 0
  and `grep -c '<script' _build/dashboard.html` must be 0. **This forbids web
  fonts, icon sets, CSS frameworks and any CDN.** System font stacks only.
- **No JavaScript.** The page has zero and keeps zero. That rules out
  scroll-driven animation, GSAP, and every motion technique some of these
  skills teach. **If a skill's section assumes JS, skip that section and say
  you did.**
- **Every colour stays a variable in `:root`.** A literal left in a rule below
  is a light patch nobody sees until the page is opened at night.
- **Every panel keeps its source line and mtime.** That is not decoration: it
  is what makes a stale panel visible instead of silently wrong. You may style
  it down; you may not remove it.
- **The no-data states stay.** They are how the page stays honest when a source
  is missing.
- Prose in the page: **no em dash in any language.**

## WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE HERE

The reader is looking for signal, fast. Consider, without treating this as a
checklist: a real type scale rather than four sizes that nearly match;
alignment that makes the eye travel down one edge; whitespace used as
structure; the two charts sharing one visual language; the route graph reading
as a graph rather than as boxes that happen to be near each other; numbers
that are the loudest thing on the page, because they are the point.

**Anti-defaults**, from the skill's own section 0.D: no AI-purple gradients, no
glassmorphism on everything, no three equal cards, no Inter-plus-slate-900
reflex. Reach past the first instinct deliberately.
