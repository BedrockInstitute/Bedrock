## THE HYPOTHESIS TO TEST, STATED SO YOU CAN KILL IT

Every cheap module in this repository works at an **abstract carrier**:
`module _ (A : V ℓ) where`, with the carrier's properties as hypotheses and
nothing concrete ever named (`src/L/Godel/Closure.lagda.md:135`,
`src/L/Godel/Levels.lagda.md:133`; `[T93]`). Every expensive module names a
**concrete tower position**: `SquareLaw`'s hot statements carry
`⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫` where `γp p = ⟪ α ⟫↪ (max' (fst p) (snd p))`
(`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:372-375`), and `⟪ a ⟫` unfolds to
`Σ[ x ∈ S ] (x ∈ₛ a)` (`src/V/Presentation.lagda.md:31-38`), so the type itself
drags the `sucV` union tower through normalization.

**The hypothesis: these three theorems are expensive precisely because their
CONTENT is about the tower's structure, so an abstract carrier is unavailable
to them by definition rather than by neglect.** You cannot state "the square law
holds at stage `γp p`" over an abstract carrier, because the statement is about
that stage.

**Test it, and try hard to break it.** The strongest counter-evidence to look
for: does any cheap module in the tree prove something genuinely about tower
structure? If one does, the hypothesis is dead and the cost is a presentation
choice after all, which is the finding that funds the rewrite.

## THE QUESTION THE HYPOTHESIS DOES NOT SETTLE, AND IT IS THE REAL ONE

Even if a theorem must be ABOUT a concrete stage, it does not follow that the
typechecker must NORMALIZE that stage. A stage could be an opaque atom carrying
named properties, with every theorem about the tower stated against that
interface and nothing ever unfolding.

**`[T87]` did exactly this once, locally, and it worked and then stopped
working.** Sealing `γ-compute` took `Bridge` from 939 s to 199 s, a real 4.7x,
and then it **plateaued at 14x the benchmark**. So:

1. **WHY did it plateau?** What is the residual 199 s made of, and what is
   still normalizing after the seal? This is the single most informative
   unanswered question in the campaign, because `Bridge` is the one controlled
   experiment where a seal demonstrably worked.
2. **Could the tower carry a full opaque stage interface**, rather than one
   sealed lemma? Name what the interface would have to expose for
   `SquareLaw`, `Condensation` and `Bridge` to be stated against it. If some
   theorem needs to see inside a stage, name which and why: **that theorem is
   then the actual root cause, and it is a specific, falsifiable answer.**
3. **What does `[T88]`'s heap exhaustion mean?** The abstract-carrier form of
   `SquareLaw`'s counting chase exhausted 8 GB twice, once even without the
   instantiations. A generic form that is more expensive than the concrete one
   is unusual and diagnostic. What is being elaborated there?
