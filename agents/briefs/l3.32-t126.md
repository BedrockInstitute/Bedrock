## THE DESIGN IS FIXED BEFORE YOU START, AND HERE IS WHY

Two constraints meet, and together they leave exactly one shape.

**1. D29 and the rule that has cost this project most.** Write content
structure-generic rather than fixed to a carrier. `[T125]` built O4's
neighbours at the concrete carrier `Lset ω` because it was a throwaway probe.
A master may not: the general `below-lim` instantiates at every limit, so a
carrier-fixed O4 would be rewritten the second time it is used.

**2. The graph layer no longer exists in `src/`.** O4's decode runs against
the op graphs `graphOf`, `graph-out`, `graph-in` and the `eqFrame` they are
built from. Those live ONLY in `archive/src/L/Rud/StepInL.lagda.md:2139-2160`,
archived by `[T122]` under D18, and **nothing may import across the archive
boundary**. Verified by `rg` over `src/`: `eqFrame` does not exist there at
all, and the `graph-in`/`graph-out` in `src/L/Coding/Graph.lagda.md` are a
DIFFERENT pair (satisfaction graphs, in a chapter that is itself retiring).
Do not use them and do not be misled by the name collision.

**So O4 takes the graph layer as MODULE PARAMETERS.** That is not a
workaround, it is the correct shape: it is what makes the module generic, and
it is what lets it be built today instead of waiting on the frozen StepInL
rewrite. The rewrite supplies the instance later.

**What survives and may be imported normally:** `Op16` and `Fof`
(`src/L/Rud/Step.lagda.md`, `src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md`), `step` and
`stepSet`, and `L.LevelKit`. Check each yourself rather than trusting this
list.

## THE CLAUSE

The one-way successor clause, in the classical form `[T65]` established after
refuting the two-way version:

    pr a c ∈ f  →  pr (sucV a) b ∈ f  →  b ≡ step c

with the exact domain bound. `[T65]` is load bearing here: **the delivered
two-way successor-value clause is too strong** and forces an infinite chain of
distinct pairs inside a finite HF member, so the family equality is false as
set up with it. Build the one-way clause. If you find yourself needing the
two-way form, stop and report it, because that is `[T65]`'s refutation
returning and it changes the route rather than the price.
