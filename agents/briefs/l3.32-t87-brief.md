## THE FIX, AS MEASURED

`[T86]` profiled Bridge at 939 s and found **95 percent of it is the module's
own gamma index-tower family**, not its consumers. The cause: `γ-compute`'s
stated type ends at the tower body, so each of its ten `subst` consumers must
convert `towerStep α (λ δ _ → γ δ)` to `+ω (U α)`, re-normalizing the union
tower inside `towerStep` **about eight times, once per definition**.

The fix is P-c and R-36 applied one layer up: restate the read lemma to end
where every consumer actually uses it, and absorb the normalization once inside
the seal. Keep the existing lemma under a new name, and add:

```agda
opaque
  towerStep≡+ωU : (α : S) → towerStep α (λ δ _ → γ δ) ≡ +ω (U α)
  towerStep≡+ωU α = refl

opaque
  γ-compute : (α : S) → γ α ≡ +ω (U α)
  γ-compute α = γ-compute-full α ∙ towerStep≡+ωU α
```

**Change nothing else.** T86 reports all ten `subst` uses have endpoints
matching the new type exactly (`:728`, `:743`, `:781`, `:783`, `:854`, `:856`,
`:891`, `:969`, `:1108`), so no consumer edit is needed, and the one external
consumer is `L.Rud.HF` (`:46`, used at `:616`), whose probe copy checked green
in 5.7 s against the sealed module.

**Measured result to reproduce: Bridge's own check falls from 939 s to 170 s.**
