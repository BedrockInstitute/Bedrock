## 3. Arm B: measured, and one mechanism correction to T52's route

The family `F = {Lset n : n < ω}` is a subset of HF: `stage∈HF`
(ProbeCarried.agda:144) delivers each stage below `ω` as an HF member, and
HF is transitive. The landing is delivered: `full-switch-⊇` at
`(α = γ ω, β = ω)` (with `ω ∈ γ ω`, ProbeCarried.agda:122) puts any
`defSet (Sset ω) ψ` into `Sset (γ ω)`. What is missing is the carve formula
`ψ` with `defSet (Sset ω) ψ = F`, and here the probe found a mechanism
correction to T52's Arm B route:

1. **The face's K-collection is impossible at the carrier HF.** The face
   bounds the witness by a carrier member `K : ⟪ u ⟫`
   (InitialSegment:120, :131-132). To carve all of `F`, `K` must contain the
   honest segments of every length below `ω`, infinitely many finite
   functions; but every HF member is finite (`sTally`, BaseBlock:318), so no
   such `K ∈ HF` exists. This is the sharp form of T52's "binding-set
   collection in the carrier" (T52 section 2 item 1): at the first limit the
   question is not just open, it is answered NO at the carrier T52 proposed
   (`Sset ω`). T52's own Devlin-shape formulation (T52 section 3b) avoids `K`
   by quantifying over the carrier itself, and that is the only viable route.
2. **The Devlin-shape formula needs the tower story at HF, including the
   successor-value clause.** Honesty at HF is not the delivered three-clause
   fragment (LevelSigma:128-130): without "`f (suc a) = Def (f a)`" and the
   strengthened bound `dom f < ω`, an honest-ish witness may map numerals to
   arbitrary HF members, so the range carve overshoots. The successor-value
   clause is T52's item 4 (priced 0-100, ×3); the strengthened bound is
   discharged by the hard half (`ord∈HF→∈ω`), which is the one piece of
   Arm B the probe actually built and measured. The limit clause is vacuous
   at `ω` (no limit ordinals below `ω`), so the port is cheaper at the first
   limit than at general `l`, but it is still the LevelSigma-style story
   build, not the "finite L-tower is definable from `baseStage∈J`" T52's
   route phrasing suggested.

So Arm B's verdict is RED within this dispatch: the statement layer
(face + full-switch) and the per-stage memberships are delivered, but the
honest-segment carve at HF is a new build of about 150-300 lines
(successor-value clause and its decode, strengthened-bound discharge, the
`F = defSet (Sset ω) ψ` equality, plus the union assembly for Arm C), which
does not fit inside the 350-line stop-line alongside Arm A. The route is not
dead: the pieces are individually priced and mostly small; the first-instance
truth of `F ∈ Sset (γ ω)` is classical (rank `F = ω`, so `F ∈ Lset (ω+1) ⊆
Lset (U ω)`), and the obstruction is the formula, not the mathematics.
