**Contradictions with the brief.** The measured rate matches none of the
three named projections; the measurement wins, per the brief. The

cold figure is 10.7 s, so the run continues. The 150-line stop line

**The P-n finding.** The decode machinery costs at the bound-variable
carrier: 7.5 s of the 10.7 s check sits in `storyW-out` and `storyW-in`,
both named with written types. The bound carrier reduces the cost to
about 0.085 s per line from the concrete carrier's 0.22, but it does not
remove the class. The reshape buys seconds against the instantiation
class and still fails to buy the parameterized-class seconds.

**Slice-composition caveat.** The measured slice is the witness layer.
The generic STEP's segments, carve and placements are the same
satisfaction class, so the full STEP should price at or above 0.085; the
memberships (piece 1) are the cheaper part. Applying the rate to the
whole 1,011-1,261 band is the brief's instruction; the band straddles
the gate either way.
