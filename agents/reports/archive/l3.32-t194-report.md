## 3. The structural obstruction, at file:line

The first-limit carve-⊇ reads the exact bound through the carrier's
ordinal content. The load-bearing step is `δ∈ω = ord∈HF→∈ω δ ordδ δ∈u`
(`HF:556`), then transitivity gives `a ∈ ω` (`HF:557-558`). `ord∈HF→∈ω`
(`HF:224-225`) is proved by finiteness and tally (`HF:100-224`). It holds
only at `Sset ω`, the first limit, where the carrier index IS the limit.

At a general limit `l`, the design's carrier is `Sset (U l)`
(`Bridge:763-775`): every stage below `l` lies in
`Sset (γ δ) ⊆ Sset (U l)` by the tower hypothesis, `γδ⊆U` (`Bridge:785`)
and `Sset-⊆-mono` (`Bridge:798`). The carrier index `U l` is the running
sup of the tower values below `l`. It is strictly above every `γ δ` for
`δ < l`, because `γ δ ∈ γ (sucV δ) ⊆ U l` (`γ-suc`, `+ω-mem`,
`limit-succ-mem`). The exact bound `δ₀` of a story witness at the carrier
is an ordinal member of `Sset (U l)`. The delivered facts bound it only by
the carrier's ordinal content, and that content is not in the tree at
general levels.

The bridge the carve needs, `a ∈ U l → a < l`, is FALSE. The probe proves
the witness at the first limit's own configuration: `ω ∈ U ω`
(`ProbeT194.agda:158-159`) and `ω ∉ ω` (`:162-163`). `U ω` is the running
sup of the tower values, an ordinal far above `ω`. `ω` is a member of it
through `γ 1 = +ω ω` (`γ-suc ∅ ∅-ord`, `γ∅≡ω`, `+ω-in`). So an ordinal in
`U l` need not be below `l`.

Consequence: the carve-⊇ conclusion `a < l` is not derivable from the
delivered exact-domain clause at a general carrier. The delivered clause
bounds the index `a` only by an ordinal member of the carrier. Nothing
delivered bounds that member below `l`. The bridge that would, `a ∈ U l →
a < l`, is false. The recorded family-equality target, `{Lset δ : δ < l}`,
is not derivable as set up, and its natural formulation is refuted in
premise, in the T65 class (`dev/ledger.toml` carried-sequence gate text).
Whether the target is outright false, or true only for a modified family
formula, is open; both readings need the repair priced in section 4. A
repair must bound the witness's index below `l` inside the family formula,
and it still needs the S-side ordinal content for the witnesses' exact
bounds to be carrier members. Both are unnamed content.
