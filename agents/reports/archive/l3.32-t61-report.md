## 3. The carve question

**Can `{Lset δ : δ < l}` now be carved? At `l = ω`: yes, structurally

At the first limit, the carve's formula layer is now complete at the carrier

exactly `strong-discharge` with that half. What still blocks the equality
`defSet (Sset ω) ψ = F` is consumer-side, not story-side:

1. **The over-HF identification**: the carrier-internal powerset of a finite
   L-stage is its next stage, `powRel (Lset a) = Lset (suc a)` over HF. This
   is true (every subset of a finite set is in HF, `BaseBlock:437-441`, and
   definable over the stage by the equality disjunction over its finite
   members), and it is what turns the successor-value clause's values into
   the L-stages; it is not in-tree as a theorem and is priced as part of the
   carve build (T53's roadmap item 3).
2. **The equality assembly and landing**: `F = defSet (Sset ω) ψ`, then

   Arm C's `Lset ω = ⋃ F ∈ Sset (γ ω)` via the delivered union halves
   (`Bridge:994-1010`) and `Jset-rud` (`Step:954`). T53 priced items 3-4 at
   about 40-100 lines past the story.

At general `l` two further seams separate the story from the family:

3. **The strengthened bound's object form**: the bound `l` names a carrier
   constant only above the first limit (Arm A: `ω ∈ Sset (U ω)`, built at
   `ProbeCarried.agda:371`; classically `l ∈ Sset (U l)` for every limit

4. **The successor-value clause's reading at general `l`**: the internal

Two small development datums, stated as rules for the owner to file or
reject (LESSONS is outside this task's write scope):

- **Rule (candidate): never name a decode direction `in`; it is a reserved
  word in Agda 2.8.0.** The natural `out`/`in` pairing parses as a
  `[ParseError]` at the definition; renaming the reverse direction to `bwd`
  (the DefOf convention) cured it. The same rename was needed in the
  successor decode's internal direction.
- **Rule (candidate): write an object-language "`z = a`" conjunct with `≐`,
  not with the two-way subset encoding.** The satisfaction of `≐` at `𝒮ᵥ` is
  path equality by definition (`_≈ˢ_ = λ x y → (x ≡ y) , setIsSet x y`,
  `src/V/Hierarchy.lagda.md:82`), so the conjunct builds and destroys as a
  path. The first version of `sucAt` used `z ⊆ a ∧ a ⊆ z` (two error cycles);
  switching to `≐` cut the successor atom's decode by about a third. This is
  load-bearing for any future successor-shaped or equality-shaped atom.

Three ordinary error cycles, all cured, no lesson: de Bruijn index shifts
inside nested bounded quantifiers (the `sucAt` atom), `step`/`A`/`K` name
clashes with imported or module-scope names, and `ext-⊆` argument direction
against a `subst` motive.
