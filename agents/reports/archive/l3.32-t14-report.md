### 1.2 The three consumption points, exactly

**1. The order as a formula inside the level's satisfaction (Devlin 5.3's hull).**
The hull's defining formula is ψ(v₀) = φ(v₀) ∧ ∀v₁ (v₁ <_L v₀ → ¬φ(v₁))
([dev2.txt:1349-1351](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt)), and
the hull is the set of elements of L_α definable from X by such formulas
([dev2.txt:1329-1332](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt)). The
order is **named inside the extended language and inside the satisfaction of
L_α**; a meta-bundle cannot be named by a formula, so the external face cannot
serve this point. (T5's gate B meta-pick formulation
([l3.32-t5-report.md:37-39](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t5-report.md))
removes this specific dependency at the price of changing the classical
statement's shape; it does not help the next point.)

**2. The order as the least-witness engine inside condensation itself (Devlin
5.2(iii)).** This is "condensation's consumer end" in the brief, and it is
stronger than the hull point. Condensation's part (iii) is "π(x) ≤_L x for all
x ∈ X". The proof: "Suppose that π(x) >_L x for some x ∈ X. Let x₀ be the
`<_L`-least such x... But `<_L` is uniformly Σ₁^λ for limit λ > ω and
π⁻¹: L_β → L_α (and moreover α and β are limit ordinals), so the above
inequality yields x₁ <_L x₀"
([dev2.txt:1301-1310](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt)). The
step consumes **(i)** the `<_L`-least element of a class (least-witness via the
order), **(ii)** 3.4(i) ("if x <_L y and y ∈ L_α then x ∈ L_α",
[dev2.txt:955-974](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt)) to pull
x₀ back into L_β, and **(iii)** the **uniform Σ₁ face** of `<_L` to transfer
the comparison through π⁻¹ by elementarity. None of the three is stateable from
a meta-bundle; the meta-pick fork does not help because the argument quantifies
over the class X inside the structure.

**3. The order as the enumeration's spine (the GCH counting).** SZ 1.17's Σ₁
enumeration runs along the order: "let Φ : otp(<^A_α) → J_α^A denote the
enumeration of J_α^A according to <^A_α", with "if α is closed under the Gödel
pairing function then otp(<^A_α) = α"
([j-hierarchy.md:146-149](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/dev/literature/j-hierarchy.md));
Devlin 3.6's Enum(α, x) is "x is the α-th member of L under the well-ordering
`<_L`" ([dev2.txt:987-1005](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt)).
The counting chapter (W7's cardinal row, |L_γ| = |γ|) needs the order's
order-type and least-producer structure as an internal object the enumeration
can quantify over, not merely the existence of a well-order at each level.

Additionally, in the fine-structure route W4 (Σ₁ Skolem functions, off the
Devlin-GCH critical path) consumes W3 directly: SZ 1.15's h^M picks "the first
component" of a minimal witness "using N = S_β^A and R = <^A_β"
([j-hierarchy.md:80-84](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/dev/literature/j-hierarchy.md);
gch-projection recon W4 row cites the delivered `leastOf`/`pick` at
[Order.lagda.md:783, :1057-1058](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Order.lagda.md)).
W5 (condensation) and W7 (hull + counting) are the Devlin-critical consumers
([gch-projection-recon.md:25, :27](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/gch-projection-recon.md)).

**Why the external face gives none of this:** it is not an element of any level,
no formula defines it, and its least is a meta-search. All three arguments

the concrete comparison data at every level, coherently (`order-coherent`/
