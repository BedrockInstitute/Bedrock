## 1. THE ANSWER

**The cost is presentation, not mathematics.** The claim, stated so someone
could disprove it: the 1,258 s sits in the *types* of the hot statements
carrying the transparent presentation functor `⟪_⟫` at tower-built positions
(`⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫`, `⟪ ω ⟫`, `⟪ # n ⟫`), where `⟪ a ⟫` unfolds to a
kernel-quotient tower and `sucV`/`ω`/`#`/`⋃` are transparent constructions;
every theorem in the three modules can be stated against an opaque stage
interface (a sealed index type with named position lemmas), exactly as the
cheap tree already states tower-structure content at opaque stage values;
and the only content that genuinely sees inside a presentation, the count of
`⟪ # n ⟫`, is already cheap. The falsifier is named in section 4 and 6: if
the counting chases cannot be restated against an abstract finite-enumeration
interface without a heap wall, the chase's content is presentation-bound and
the answer flips to mathematics for SquareLaw.

Evidence, in the order that forces the verdict:

1. **The counter-example the brief asked me to look for exists, and it kills
   the hypothesis as stated.** HF proves content genuinely about the tower at
   concrete positions and is cheap. `ord∈HF→∈ω : (x : S) → IsOrd x →
   ⟨ x ∈ˢ Sset ω ⟩ → ⟨ x ∈ˢ ω ⟩` (src/L/Rud/HF.lagda.md:224) and
   `stage∈HF : (ζ : S) → ⟨ ζ ∈ˢ ω ⟩ → ⟨ Lset ζ ∈ˢ Sset ω ⟩`
   (HF.lagda.md:263) are statements about the first limit stage, with
   `Sset (sucV δ)` positions inside the proofs (HF.lagda.md:228, :235-242).
   HF checks at 20.0 s for 664 lines, 0.030 s/line (T86 profile rows,
   `/tmp/t86-profile.txt`, cited in T93 section 1 item 3), and contains zero
   `opaque` of its own (rg finds none in the file). It is cheap because the
   stage values are opaque upstream: `opaque Sset = ∈-induction Sset-step`
   (src/L/Rud/Hierarchy.lagda.md:219-220) and
   `opaque Lset = ∈-induction LsetStep` (src/L/Constructible.lagda.md:221-223).
   So `Sset ω` is an atom in the statement and nothing unfolds. "You cannot
   state the square law at stage `γp p` over an abstract carrier, because the
   statement is about that stage" is true and irrelevant: being about a stage
   does not force the stage's *presentation* into the type. The cheap tree
   proves stage-aboutness all over HF.

2. **The cheap tree is not uniformly abstract-carrier.** `L.Godel.Closure`
   and `L.Godel.Levels` are abstract-carrier throughout
   (`module _ (A : V ℓ) where`, Closure.lagda.md:135, Levels.lagda.md:133),
   but HF is the counter-example: concrete positions, cheap, because the
   values are opaque. The correct generalization is narrower and sharp: no
   cheap module forces the *transparent presentation* of a tower-built
   position into a statement type. Sealing is not the point either (Closure
   and Levels use zero `opaque`, T93 section 4); the point is that nothing
   in a statement unfolds to a concrete presentation.

3. **The mechanism, verified at the library (D-10 correction to the brief's
   premise).** The brief's "`⟪ a ⟫` unfolds to `Σ[ x ∈ S ] (x ∈ₛ a)`
   (src/V/Presentation.lagda.md:31-38)" is wrong as a definition and right as
   a mechanism. The definition is `⟪ a ⟫ = V-repr a .fst .fst`
   (Cubical CumulativeHierarchy Properties, Properties.agda:220-222), the
   monic presentation: for `a = sett X ix`, `sett-repr` builds the kernel
   quotient `X / Kernel` with `Kernel x y = ix x ≊ ix y`
   (Properties.agda:147-158). `Σ[ v ∈ V ℓ ] ⟨ v ∈ₛ a ⟩ ≃ ⟪ a ⟫` is a
   delivered *equivalence* (`presentation`, Properties.agda:275-282), not an
   unfolding. And `⟨ x ∈ₛ a ⟩ = repFiber ⟪ a ⟫↪ x` (Properties.agda:239-247),
   so membership propositions unfold `⟪ a ⟫` too. The tower-built positions
   then force large normal forms: `sucV N = N ∪ ⁅ N ⁆s`,
   `# suc n = sucV (# n)`, `ω = sett (Lift ℕ) (# ∘ lower)`
   (Constructions.agda:160-172), so the presentation of `sucV (γp p)`, `ω`
   and `# (suc (n · n))` is a tower of kernel quotients, one per construction
   layer, and every elaboration that forms, checks, or compares a statement
   type mentioning it rebuilds that normal form.

4. **The hot statements really do carry the transparent positions (D-10).**
   `h₀ : (p : PairA) → (r : PairA) → r ≺' p → ⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫ ×
   ⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫` and `h₀-inj`'s `h₀ p r pr ≡ h₀ p r' pr' → r ≡ r'`, with
   `γp p = ⟪ α ⟫↪ (max' (fst p) (snd p))`
   (src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:370-375); `no-inj-finite : (n : ℕ) →
   (f : ⟪ α ⟫ → ⟪ # n ⟫ × ⟪ # n ⟫) → ...` (SquareLaw.lagda.md:166-167); and
   `Xx : ⟪ ω ⟫ = fiber ω (...)` inside the ω chase
   (SquareLaw.lagda.md:835-838). Condensation's hot transports state the
   concrete formula's satisfaction: `level-abs₀ : (δ : Sᴹ ^ 2) →
   (δ AbsM.⊨ᵐ σᴹ) ≡ ((map fst δ) AbsM.⊨ᵛ σᴹ)` with `σᴹ = embed levelStory`
   (src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:770-784), and the mirror at the class carrier
   (Condensation.lagda.md:825-850). Bridge's pre-fix read lemma stated the
   tower body: `γ α ≡ towerStep α (λ δ _ → γ δ)` (T86 section 4; the post-fix
   rename is Bridge.lagda.md:697).

5. **The controlled comparison inside the tree.** Bridge's γ family is the
   same content (tower values, limits, successor, monotonicity) and is cheap
   after the seal: the consumers drop to milliseconds once `γ` and
   `γ-compute` are opaque (T87 section 4: `γ-lim` 265.4 s to 21 ms,
   `ordStep` 159.6 s to 20 ms, `γ-mono` 105.8 s to 60 ms). The seal is
   exactly the opaque-stage mechanism HF inherits from upstream. So the same
   mathematics, the same aboutness, costs 939 s or 199 s depending on whether
   the statements carry a transparent tower body or an opaque value. That is
   the definition of a presentation choice.

The strongest counter-reading I could construct and why it fails: "the four
hot proofs need the presentation's *injectivity* (`↪-inj`, `fiber`), which is
presentation content." It fails because `↪-inj`, `fiber` and `member` are
already generic, delivered, and cheap (Presentation.lagda.md:31-38, all
stated at any `a`); the cost is that the statements name `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫`
transparently, not that the content needs the presentation. The one genuine
presentation-content piece, the count of `⟪ # n ⟫` (SquareLaw.lagda.md:96-140,
`toFin`/`fromFin`/`factor`), is already in the cheap tail (T88 section 2:
every non-hot definition under 8 s).

## 2. Why Bridge's seal plateaued

The residual 199.5 s is two parts, both measured:

1. **149.5 s inside the two sealed read lemmas: the one-time price of the
   tower's computation rule.** Post-fix per-definition profile (T87 section
   4, `--profile=definitions`, total 199,484 ms): `γ-compute-full` 94,863 ms
   and `towerStep≡+ωU` 54,642 ms, summing to 149,505 ms, 75% of the residual.
   `γ-compute-full = ∈-induction-compute towerStep` (Bridge.lagda.md:697-698):
   its stated type `γ α ≡ towerStep α (λ δ _ → γ δ)` carries the tower body
   `+ω (⋃ (sett ⟪ α ⟫ (λ m → γ (⟪ α ⟫↪ m) (mem m))))`
   (Bridge.lagda.md:685-686), and elaborating the instance normalizes the
   union's presentation. `towerStep≡+ωU α = refl` (Bridge.lagda.md:708-710):
   checking `refl : towerStep α (λ δ _ → γ δ) ≡ +ω (U α)` is a conversion
   check between the tower body and the closed form, and because `U` and
   `fam` are separate definitions (Bridge.lagda.md:701-706) the two sides are
   definitionally equal only through the presentation machinery. This is the
   P-k mechanism precisely: the seal was in the right place, and the
   statements still carry the union; the seal's own elaboration pays the
   tower once.

2. **About 50 s of the module's remaining content.** The pre-fix
   "everything else" was about 47 s of the 939.2 s total (T86 section 4: the
   listed γ-family rows sum to 892.3 s); T87's table itemizes no row below
   the two sealed lemmas, so the ~50 s is the un-itemized remainder. It is
   the R-38 class T93 ranked: transparent kit invocations at concrete
   composite arguments, measured at 25.7 s per unsealed `right-spec`
   invocation (r5a-report.md:468-481; T93 section 1 item 1).

Why it plateaued at 14x the benchmark: the seal converted an about-eightfold
repeated consumer-side normalization (ten `subst` consumers each re-normalizing
the union tower, T86 section 4) into a once normalization inside the seal. It
cannot remove the once: the tower's computation rule must, at least once,
relate `γ α` to its body, and the body is a union over the presentation. So
199 s is "once" where 939 s was "eight times", and the residual is not
reachable by more sealing. Below 199 s requires either the ~50 s tail
(sealed consumer aliases, cheap, section 5) or a different tower formulation
whose computation rule avoids the union conversion (unmeasured,
survey-class, and the heap-risk class).

## 3. The opaque stage interface

What it would have to expose, named per module:

- **Shared:** a sealed index-type former `Idx : S → Type ℓ` (the presentation
  `⟪_⟫` under `opaque`), with sealed `idx↪ : (a : S) → Idx a → S`, `idx-inj`,
  `fiber`, `member`, and the membership conversion, all proved inside the
  seal where `⟪_⟫` unfolds; plus sealed *position lemmas* per construction:
  sucV membership in/elim/self (the content of V.Model's `∈sucV-inl`,
  `∈sucV-elim`, `self∈sucV`, Model.lagda.md:218-236, restated at `Idx`),
  ω-membership-to-numeral (SquareLaw.lagda.md:77-82, already cheap), and the
  numeral count (`toFin`/`fromFin`/`factor`, SquareLaw.lagda.md:96-140,
  already cheap).
- **SquareLaw:** the counting chases (`no-inj-finite`, `no-inj-finite-ω` and
  their `g-inj`/`pair-eq` interiors) restated against an abstract enumeration
  `E : ℕ → Type` with `toFinE : (n : ℕ) → E n → Fin n`, `fromFinE`, `factorE`
  and injectivity hypotheses, instantiated once at `E n := ⟪ # n ⟫` using the
  delivered count; the `h₀`/`comp₀` chain stated at `Idx (sucV (γp p))` with
  `fst∈sucmax`/`snd∈sucmax` (SquareLaw.lagda.md:287-305) proved once inside
  the seal.
- **Condensation:** `σᴹ`/`σL` and their witnesses sealed
  (`opaque σᴹ = embed levelStory`, Condensation.lagda.md:770-778 and
  :825-840), with the five transports (`level-abs₀`, `level-transfer`,
  `level-abs₀L`, `level-transfer-up/down`, Condensation.lagda.md:783-850)
  proved inside the seal so the exported statements carry the opaque formula;
  consumers (`CrossOut` at Condensation.lagda.md:170-171, `TransferM` at
  :293-294, `TransferL` and the Crossing assembly at :888-928) use them by
  name.
- **Bridge:** nothing further; `γ`, `γ-compute-full`, `towerStep≡+ωU` and
  `γ-compute` are already sealed (Bridge.lagda.md:691-714).

Which theorem must see inside a stage: **none of the four hot SquareLaw
theorems, and none in Condensation or Bridge.** The only content anywhere in
the three modules that is about the presentation itself is the count of
`⟪ # n ⟫` (toFin/fromFin/factor/no-inj-Fin), and it is already cheap (T88
section 2). If the restated chase proves to need the numeral presentation in
its statement, the named root-cause theorem is `pair-eq` (the only object
whose closest generic restatement walled, T88 probe 2/2a); that theorem is
then the specific, falsifiable answer, and this report's verdict for
SquareLaw flips to mathematics.

## 4. What [T88]'s heap exhaustion is telling us

The two heap events (probe 2: 10:26 with instantiations; probe 2a: 26:10
with the generic module alone, both at `-M8g`, T88 section 4b) mean: **the
partially abstract restatement kept the concrete numeral presentation in the
telescope.** `NoInj (A : Type ℓ) (into : (m : ℕ) → ⟪ # m ⟫ → A) (into-inj :
...)` abstracts the carrier A but leaves `⟪ # m ⟫`, at symbolic depth (m a
module parameter; `# (suc n) = sucV (# n)`, Constructions.agda:164-165).
What is established is the direction: the concrete module pays 177-208 s per
chase and never walls (T88 section 2), while the generic module alone walls
at the cap even before any instantiation (T88 probe 2a), so abstraction made
the elaboration strictly worse. The consistent reading of the direction,
which the section 6 probe tests: the chase instantiates the enumeration at
`suc (n · n)`, a concrete layer over the stuck `# (n · n)`, so the generic
elaboration compares a symbolic-depth presentation tower against concrete
layers, and the unification domain is the variable-depth kernel-quotient
tower, unbounded where the finite-depth concrete version was only expensive.
Whatever the exact mechanism, the wall is the recorded class of this chapter:
T31's heap exhaustion at the Core-at-ω instantiation
(l3.32-t31-report.md:115-116) and T47's five heap events building SquareLaw
(l3.32-t47-report.md:123, :172-173), classed C-12/P-i
(dev/LESSONS.md:1778-1805, :203-283).

It does NOT establish that the chase's content is presentation-bound. The
probe stopped one layer short of full abstraction: it abstracted the carrier
but left `⟪ # m ⟫` in the `into` telescope. The fully abstract version
removes `⟪ # m ⟫` entirely (E, toFinE, fromFinE, factorE as module
parameters; the concrete count discharged once at FiniteBase's delivered
lemmas). T85's 12.8 s probe is no substitute: it measured the lower injection
at variable α (l3.32-t85-report.md section 1), a different theorem with no
chase and no numeral position in the statement, and T88 explicitly records
that it does not transfer to the counting chase (T88 section 4b).
