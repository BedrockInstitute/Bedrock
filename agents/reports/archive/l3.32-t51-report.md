## 2. Is the unbounded-quantifier count the obstruction (Q2)

**No, the count is a symptom; the missing Levy certification is the
obstruction; and an isomorphism-invariance of satisfaction does not exist in
the tree, would cost about 0.08-0.14k naive to build, and would not discharge
the two transfers even if it did.**

The machine-checked fact is precise: `LsetGraphAt zero (suc zero)` carries
208,565 nodes, 1,688 constant occurrences, 2,287 unbounded `∃̇` and 2,159
unbounded `∀̇`, re-verified exactly against R2p
([ivprobe-report.md:230-245](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ivprobe-report.md)).
The quantifiers live deep in the formula: the graph is one existential over an
approximation ([Sequence.lagda.md:361-362](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/Sequence.lagda.md)),
the approximation carries two unbounded universals
([Sequence.lagda.md:281-282](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/Sequence.lagda.md)),
and the step is an extension over three existentials over the definable-power
description ([Sequence.lagda.md:119-120](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/Sequence.lagda.md),
[Powerset.lagda.md:442-444](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/Powerset.lagda.md)),
whose leaves are the satisfaction-recursion formulas
([Graph.lagda.md:104-112](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/Graph.lagda.md),
[CodeSet.lagda.md:264-266](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/CodeSet.lagda.md)).
The three delivered transfer theorems are conditional on Levy witnesses: `abs₀`
needs `Δ₀`, `σ₁-up` needs `Σ₁`, `π₁-down` needs `Π₁`
([Absoluteness.lagda.md:122-123,182-190](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Absoluteness.lagda.md)),
and the formula carries none of the three classes as delivered, so none of the
three theorems applies (T33's machine check,
[t33-report.md:118-124](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t33-report.md)).

The D-10 correction goes one step further than "no witness applies". The
raw-form obligations are not a sound target at arbitrary carriers at all. An
inner satisfaction `⊨ᵐ` at a transitive carrier quantifies over the carrier's
members, while the ambient reading `⊨ᵛ` quantifies over the whole universe, so
inner truth of a formula with an unbounded universal carries no information
about ambient elements outside the carrier, and inner-to-ambient cannot be
derived for such a formula. `TransferM lifted` is exactly that shape, and
`TransferL` is its mirror (ambient-to-inner with unbounded existentials, where
an ambient witness need not lie in the carrier). The transferable object must
therefore be a Levy-equivalent form of the level story, proved equivalent to
the delivered description at each carrier. That is precisely what W1' prices:
the Σ₁ form with its bounded witness, the Levy certificates, and the
equivalence legs ([ivprobe-report.md:478-493](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ivprobe-report.md)).
The count is the measured distance between the delivered description and that
form, not the mechanism that blocks the transfer.

**Does the tree carry an isomorphism-invariance of satisfaction? No.** The
candidates, checked:

- `⊨-map` moves a formula between constant domains within ONE semantics and
  proves the meaning unchanged ([Relabelling.lagda.md:154-169](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Manipulation/Relabelling.lagda.md));
  it is not a cross-structure invariance.
- `abs₀`, `σ₁-up`, `π₁-down` compare inner and ambient readings at ONE carrier
  and are Levy-conditional ([Absoluteness.lagda.md:122-190](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Absoluteness.lagda.md)).
- `abs-defSet` is the same absoluteness aggregated at one transitive carrier
  ([Definability.lagda.md:282-288](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Definability.lagda.md)).
- `V.Collapse.iso` preserves membership both ways under the collapse, with no
  satisfaction statement anywhere in the chapter
  ([Collapse.lagda.md:335-338,342-349](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/V/Collapse.lagda.md)).
- The closest object, `L.Hull.TV→elem`, is a full-syntax induction proving that
  a membership-preserving, identity-on-values map `inL : SM → SL` with the
  Tarski-Vaught witness-closure hypothesis and excluded middle gives agreement
  of the two INNER readings on every formula
  ([Hull.lagda.md:120-128,175-249](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Hull.lagda.md)).
  It is the skeleton of an iso-invariance proof, but it is not stated as one:
  its `tm-agree` term lemma is `refl`-based precisely because `inL` keeps the
  underlying value ([Hull.lagda.md:160-163](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Hull.lagda.md)),
  its universal case spends excluded middle and the criterion
  ([Hull.lagda.md:196-206](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Hull.lagda.md)),
  its bounded cases spend transitivity
  ([Hull.lagda.md:207-246](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Hull.lagda.md)),
  and it compares two inner readings, never an inner reading with an ambient
  one. It also lives in `L.Hull`, wired after `L.Condensation`
  ([Everything.lagda.md:448-449](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/Everything.lagda.md)),
  so the crossing cannot consume it without rewiring.

**What a true iso-invariance theorem would cost.** Model it on `TV→elem`'s
`go` induction ([Hull.lagda.md:178-246](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Hull.lagda.md),
about 70 lines including blanks) plus its helpers `lookup-inL`, `tm-agree`,
`dne` ([Hull.lagda.md:155-167](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Hull.lagda.md)):
ten constructors, each a congruence,
the existential and universal cases run on the iso's surjectivity and
injectivity instead of the criterion and excluded middle, the bounded cases run
on membership preservation both ways instead of transitivity, and the term
lemma is no longer `refl` because the collapse changes underlying values (it
consumes the delivered membership iso, [Collapse.lagda.md:335-338](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/V/Collapse.lagda.md)).
Estimate: 0.08-0.14k naive non-blank in-fence lines, class x3 unprobed (the
induction skeleton is measured at Hull, but the statement and its hypothesis
plumbing differ), calibrated 0.24-0.42k. It is cheap, and it is the wrong leg
for W1': it moves satisfaction between two ISOMORPHIC carriers' inner readings
(the collapse leg X to M), while `TransferM` and `TransferL` are
inner-to-ambient and ambient-to-inner at a single carrier. Even in a
re-factorized collapse route through a substructure X with elementarity, the
final inner-to-ambient step at a level carrier is still a Levy-conditional
absoluteness step, so the Levy content of W1' does not disappear; it relocates.
That is a technique observation about a different architecture, not a
re-pricing of the delivered block, and under D26 it changes no band
([PLAN.md:298](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/dev/PLAN.md)).
