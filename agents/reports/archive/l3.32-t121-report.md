## 5. Contradictions with the brief

1. **"Four masters carry 175.9 s" does not reproduce.** The cold table's
   four rows sum to 329.4 s (56.7 + 52.1 + 27.1 + 193.5); the first three
   sum to 135.9 s. Neither is 175.9 s. My per-definition warm profiles sum
   to 64.1 + 53.0 + 27.7 + ~194 (Bridge own) = ~339 s. The brief's 175.9 s
   is not reachable from any grouping I tried; the cold table is the source
   of every "seconds now" figure above.
2. **"The four modules are the only place the remaining 152.1 s can come
   from" is false.** StepInL (85.7 s cold, outside the brief) loses ~49 s
   cold-equivalent to the same Images cure, measured (88.2 s to 37.8 s
   profile-instrumented). The four briefed modules alone remove ~104 s;
   the gap closes only when that spillover is credited.
3. **T93's Images mechanism was wrong.** T93 priced the three `right-spec`
   invocation sites inside Images (F10-spec:303/:311, rightAt:386) as the
   lever. The profile shows those sites are cheap; Images' 52 s is the
   DECLARATION of `rightSlice-pair`, and the lever is sealing `left`, which
   T93 did not name.
4. **T93's Step verdict was wrong for the invocation half.** T93 read
   Step's 26.6 s as the intrinsic cost of the sixteen transitivity proofs
   ("the dedup class, not a seal lever"). The profile shows 93% is
   `right-at-pair`, a seal lever, and it is Images-side (the seal removes
   it with zero Step edits; the transitivity cases were always ~10-20 ms).
5. **T93's Bridge floor claim is CONFIRMED, not contradicted.** The residue
   is exactly the once-pair it named (γ-compute-full 100.9 s,
   towerStep≡+ωU 54.9 s), and the gut shows nothing else is buyable.
   Bridge: NO-GO.

## Method

- Warm per-definition profiles: `GHCRTS=-M8g agda --profile=definitions <master>`
  run from a scratch mirror at `/tmp/t121-warm/`. Agda 2.8.0 stores
  interfaces under `<cwd>/_build/2.8.0/agda/`, not next to the sources, so
  the mirror's own cache was seeded from the repository's
  `_build/2.8.0/agda/src/` (read-only) and the profiled module's own
  `.agdai` was moved aside so it re-elaborates while dependencies stay warm
  (the `[T86]` appendix pattern).
- One Agda process at a time, `-M8g` heap cap on every invocation (C-12).
- Scratch copies under `/tmp` only; nothing under `src/` is written.
- Abort criterion fixed in advance (D-1): a cure experiment that does not
  typecheck, does not move time, or moves it to consumers is a red verdict and
  costs the attempt and nothing else.
- Postulate guts were run in renamed scratch copies with `--safe` dropped
  (scratch-only; the tree keeps `--safe`), so a body can be postulated to
  separate statement cost from body cost.
