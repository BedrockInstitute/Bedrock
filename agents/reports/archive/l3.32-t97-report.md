# [L3.32-T97] Archaeology: how did the internalization route get cheap?

Status: COMPLETE. Read-only history dig. No Agda, no `make check`, no `src/`
write, no git write; the only write is this report. Every load-bearing claim
carries a commit, a `file:line`, or a command that reproduces it.

## 0. The answer in one paragraph

The route was not born cheap and it did not get cheap later. It was slow at
more than a dozen measured points during its construction, and every one of
those walls was cured by a law that is now recorded in `dev/LESSONS.md`. The
final tree is cheap because those cures were then applied as construction
discipline: statements at abstract carriers and variable indices (P-h, Rule 1,
R-30), seals at the birth site of any tower or heavy construction (Rule 2,
P-c, R-36), and obligation types reshaped so no heavy operator is applied to a
deep term (P-i [E]). The three expensive theorems do not follow the statement
half of that discipline, and the measured reason is that their obligations'
TYPES mention the concrete tower, which is exactly the wall class P-i [E]
names as incurable by any seal: only an interface reshaping can move it.

The D-10 check on the premise: **the premise is true, with one nuance worth
stating first.** `L.Godel.Closure` itself was born fast: its earliest
committed form (all of 2026-08-02, commits `5cd66e2`, `97bb716`, `6e12593`,
`810d9cd`, `27f4206`) already checked at about 107 s cold
(`_build/b4e-report.md` §3), essentially today's 102.1 s (T86 profile). But
its uncommitted first formulations were slow, >4 min cold and never finishing
(`_build/b4d-report.md` §3, `_build/b4e-report.md` §1), and were cured
in-flight by the P-h restatement the same day, with one wall (the shift
image) left uncured at the end of the build. So "how did it get that way"
has a real answer: the cheapness is the accumulated wall-and-cure record of
the whole route, not a property the content was born with.

### 1.1 Statements at abstract carriers and variable indices (the statement discipline)

**What it is.** The single most load-bearing habit of the retiring tree:
theorems are stated over module parameters that stay abstract (`X : V ℓ`,
`A : V ℓ`), and every index is a variable over that carrier, so no conversion
ever normalizes a concrete presentation. In the tree today:
`module _ (A : V ℓ) where` at `src/L/Godel/Closure.lagda.md:135` and
`src/L/Godel/Levels.lagda.md:133`; the P-h walks at
`src/L/Godel/Closure.lagda.md:2101` (`ValsWalk (X : V ℓ)`), `:2573`
(`SelWalk (X Ka Kb τ : V ℓ)`), `:3448` (`ShiftWalk (X τ : V ℓ)`).

**Where it was introduced.** It is a discipline, not a commit, and its
measurements are the walls: Rule 1 in `dev/LESSONS.md` records [L3.21] decode
2,237 s to 1.5 s via variable restatement, [L2.4] Limit's level inlined at its
six sites 144.55 s to 1.76 s (82x), [L3.23] a value at a named key past 400 s
twice to a 4.6 s corollary of the variable-argument version. P-h names it for
walks: `dev/LESSONS.md:174` (B4d/B4e, 2026-08-02). The earliest recorded
instance is [L3.0.1] U5b, `L.Coding.Sound` 801: "the two atoms hit a
conversion wall, cured by flattening the nesting into readings at a variable
environment" (`dev/JOURNAL.md` dated records).

**What it bought.** The whole route's rate: 26,479 lines at 0.0134 s/line and
4,837 signatures at 0.0736 s/signature, both views of one 356 s measurement
(`dev/ledger.toml` `[caliber]`). The two largest files check with zero
`opaque` and zero `postulate`: `L.Godel.Closure` 3,490 code lines at 102.1 s,
`L.Godel.Levels` 2,775 at 60.3 s (T86 profile; `rg -c "opaque|postulate"`
returns zero on both files today).

**Can the three use it?** Partially, and the boundary is measured. The form is
present: `module Core (α : S) (oα : IsOrd α)` (SquareLaw.lagda.md:244),
`module L.Rud.Bridge {ℓ} (lem) (A : V ℓ)` (Bridge.lagda.md:23),
`module L.Condensation {ℓ} (lem)` (Condensation.lagda.md:40). What is missing
is that their statements name concrete tower positions inside those blocks:
`⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫ × ⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫` in `h₀`'s codomain
(SquareLaw.lagda.md:370-378), `M ≡ Lset β` (Condensation.lagda.md:172-189),
`⟨ Lset δ ∈ˢ Sset (γ δ) ⟩` in Bridge's tower family (Bridge.lagda.md:984).
T85 proved the variable-carrier
discipline transfers to this chapter's family at a variable `α` (the tower
induction successor step, probe green at 12.8 s, `_build/l3.32-t85-report.md`
verdict), and T88 proved it does NOT transfer to the counting chase:
the generic-module restatement of `pair-eq` heap-exhausted at `-M8g` twice
(`_build/l3.32-t88-report.md` §4b). So the discipline is usable for the
statements whose content is variable-indexed, and blocked where the
mathematics itself is about a concrete tower position (section 4).

### 1.3 Reshape the obligation so the heavy operator never appears in its type (P-i [E])

**What it is.** The playbook's [E]: when a heavy operator is welded into the
TYPE of a field or proof obligation and applied to a deep recursive term,
`opaque`/`abstract` cannot help, because the type still mentions it; the only
cure is changing the upstream interface so the obligation never applies the
heavy operator to the deep term. Imported into this repository with P-i,
`dev/LESSONS.md:203`, from `../fol-reification/docs/WORKLOG.md` §5.3 (case
11, the `hasInfinity` von Neumann restatement).

**Where it was introduced and what it bought.** The in-repo instances are the
statement reshapes the coding route measured: [L3.0.1] U6, "the `⊥̇` case
walled until stated with the formula recovered from the key rather than
substituted into it" (`dev/JOURNAL.md`); Rule 1/R-30's 400 s to 4.6 s
(`dev/LESSONS.md:710`); R-35's small-index restatement, "the original F8-spec
backward direction via fiber extraction over `⟪ y ⟫` was the OOM runaway that
crashed the machine... the small-index restatement checks the whole file in
54.6 s cold" (`dev/LESSONS.md:768`). P-i's own warning is the sharpest
transferable sentence: "merely rewriting the equation MOVES the explosion; the
replacement obligation must not mention the heavy operator at all"
(`dev/LESSONS.md` P-i, [E]).

**Can the three use it?** This is the one cure T88's measurements leave
standing for SquareLaw: "the cost sits in elaborating the statement itself,
whose type mentions the concrete tower `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫`, not in per-site body
normalization by consumers... What remains is an R-35 statement reshaping of

(`_build/l3.32-t88-report.md` §4a, §4c). For Condensation, T89 points at the
same class: the heat "is in the statements that sit at concrete tower
positions, the `σᴹ`/`σL` transports and the absoluteness applications at the

## 3. What `L.Rud.CodePred` does to reach 0.039 s/obligation

CodePred (archived today under D17/D20, `dev/ARCHIVE.md:70`) is the closest
thing to a controlled comparison in the repository: rud-route content, an

Five deliberate choices, each documented where it was made:

1. **A sealed shape vocabulary at birth.** `prS`, `numS`, `sucS`, `sglS`,
   `cupS` in one `opaque` block with one equation each
   (`archive/src/L/Rud/CodePred.lagda.md:113-150`). The prose measures it:
   three minutes with the library's pair, two seconds with the sealed one
   (CodePred.lagda.md:98-103). This is Rule 2/P-c/R-36 at the vocabulary
   level, on the `L.Rank` precedent (same reason, same seal;
   CodePred.lagda.md:103).
2. **Module-parameterized at a seven-fact telescope with no concrete stage
   anywhere in it.** `module Pred (W : S) (Wtr : Transitive ...) ...`
   (CodePred.lagda.md:238), the seven facts as K3 names them ("transitivity,
   the carrier is a member, closure under pair / numerals / singleton /
   binary union, the empty set, plus `codeIn`") discharged only in the single

   `opaque unfolding` block (CodePred.lagda.md:1707-1742). K3 records this as
   "P-i [A], third instance" (`_build/k3-report.md` §2).
3. **Inner-world reading: no `Δ₀`, no absoluteness.** The predicate is read

**What the expensive three can take from this list:** the sealed vocabulary

## 4. The techniques the expensive three CANNOT use, and what blocks each

The block is one mechanism with four measured faces: **the obligations'
statement types mention the concrete tower, so every technique that operates
on proof bodies or on consumer sites leaves the cost untouched, and the one
technique that operates on statement types (P-i [E]) is a build, not a seal.**

1. **Bridge's read-lemma seal (P-k): blocked for SquareLaw by the statement,
   and it is the one case where the playbook's own diagnostic predicts the
   failure.** T88 probe 1c (transparent generic lemma): neutral, `h₀-inj`
   still pays ~190 s with a body reduced to a one-line application
   (`_build/l3.32-t88-report.md` §4a). T88 probe 1 (sealed, the exact Bridge
   shape): at least 1.9x slower, past 26:06 clean. The playbook's diagnostic
   discriminates consumer-side from definition-side disease by whose names
   appear in the profile: Bridge's ten consumers showed other modules' names
   (case 17, seal the terminal exports); SquareLaw's four hot rows are its own
   definitions with the cost in elaborating their statements. The Bridge fix
   cannot transfer because Bridge's disease was consumer-side and SquareLaw's
   is definition-side.
2. **The variable-carrier restatement (Rule 1, P-h, the retiring route's
   signature shape): blocked for SquareLaw's counting chase by heap
   exhaustion.** T88 probes 2 and 2a, the generic module at a variable carrier,
   exhausted the heap at `-M8g` twice (10:26 with instantiations, 26:10 alone;
   `_build/l3.32-t88-report.md` §4b). T85's green 12.8 s probe proves the
   variable form is cheap for a DIFFERENT theorem of the same chapter (the
   tower-induction successor step at a variable `α`, `_build/l3.32-t85-report.md`
   verdict), so the block is specific to the counting chase, not to SquareLaw
   as a whole.
3. **Sealing (P-c/R-36/B′): blocked when the statement itself mentions the
   tower.** SquareLaw's hot statements carry `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫` in their own
   types (SquareLaw.lagda.md:370-378); P-i [E] states the general law: "when
   the heavy operator is welded into the TYPE of a field or proof obligation
   and applied to a deep term, `opaque`/`abstract` cannot help (the type
   still mentions it)" (`dev/LESSONS.md` P-i, [E], and the WORKLOG §5.3's
   parallel paragraph at `../fol-reification/docs/WORKLOG.md`). T88 confirmed
   the mechanism in place: the seal leaves the statement untouched and the
   cost is the statement.
4. **Condensation's variable-index discipline: already followed, and it does
   not cool the module.** T89 rewrote the structural-story clauses at variable
   indices (the exact P-h/I-4 shape) and measured the module at 203.3 s
   against 203.5 s, unchanged within noise: "the variable-index repair is
   therefore not a cooling lever for this module... Condensation's heat is
   not in the structural-story formulas but in the statements that sit at
   concrete tower positions, the `σᴹ`/`σL` transports and the absoluteness
   applications at the two carriers" (`_build/l3.32-t89-report.md` §4). The
   untested lever T89 names is the T86-style read-lemma restatement of those
   transports.
5. **The fused-clause dedup: priced and unfunded.** SquareLaw's four hot rows
   are two byte-mirror pairs; the shared generic form is the heap-poisoned
   probe, and the two cores' hypothesis lists differ, so the dedup is not a
   cheap change (`_build/l3.32-t88-report.md` §4c).

**What feeds the root-cause dispatch.** T88's own close: "The honest answer:
this 856 seconds is expensive to remove; no cheap fix exists within the
60-line seal stop-line... What remains is an R-35 statement reshaping of the
`h₀`/`comp₀` chain" (`_build/l3.32-t88-report.md` §4, §4c). The root cause,
stated as the playbook would: the obligations' types apply the `sucV`/`ω`
