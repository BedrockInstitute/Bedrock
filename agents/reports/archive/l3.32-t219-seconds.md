# L3.32-T219: the seconds doctrine

**Five diseases. The single best predictor: a transparent construction at a
concrete argument in a checked type or body.**

This report synthesizes ten measured episodes. Each episode lives in
`_build/`. The doctrine states what makes a master expensive here. It states
how a build brief writes content cheap the first time.

## 1. The taxonomy

The ten episodes reduce to five diseases. Each disease has one mechanism.
The LESSONS entries name P-k, P-l, R-35, R-38, I-4, I-5 and R-40. Five of
the names survive as diseases. Two do not.

| disease | mechanism | episodes | measurement | LESSONS ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the stopped-short read | a sealed read lemma ends one layer before the consumers' form. Each consumer re-normalizes the missing layer | T87 | -767 s for six lines | P-k |
| the transparent presentation at a concrete position | a transparent construction (union, `left`, fiber, tower body) sits in a checked type or body at a concrete argument. The elaborator normalizes it once per occurrence | T103, T121, T123, T124 | -406 s; -104 s; -37.8 s; -70.3 s | P-l |
| the untyped eliminator branch | an eliminator receives inline lambda branches without written types. Each branch re-elaborates the machinery per constraint | T143 | 595 s to 27 ms | I-5 |
| the deep successor chain in a premise | a membership premise at an iterated successor normalizes super-linearly in depth | T154 | 631 s to 59 s | R-40 |
| the content-intrinsic satisfaction content | the object-language formulas, decodes, per-operation descriptions, and placements at a concrete carrier are the cost. Named branches do not cure it | T144, T180, T195 | 150.2 to 11.5 s; -55 s; 6.3 to about 216 s | none yet |

R-35 is not a disease in these episodes. T143 ruled it out at its site
(`_build/l3.32-t143-report.md:132-134`). T154 ruled out its mechanism and
used its cure shape (`_build/l3.32-t154-report.md:157-161`). I-4 has no
measured episode in this set. No report among the ten attributes a wall to
I-4.

R-38 is a sub-case of the second disease. A consumer's alias of a
transparent imported operation is a birth site. T124 measured it in Step.
`right-at-pair` alone was 26.8 s of the module's 28.8 s. The Images seal
removed it with zero Step edits (`_build/l3.32-t124-report.md:11-12`).

P-l's finer split (consumer-bound, body-bound, withdrawn statement-bound)
is the anatomy of the second disease. The stopped-short read is its
sharpest consumer-bound form. It earns its own class. The fix is a
statement change, not a seal.

One could merge the second and the fifth diseases. They share the
concrete-position root. The cures differ. The second is curable: a seal,
a read lemma, or a parameter lift. The fifth is avoidable or payable:
deletion, a generic frame, or a priced floor. The doctrine keeps five
classes because the cure decides the build brief.

## 2. The predictor

The list ranks the signatures by the seconds each cost. It names shapes a
writer or a reviewer can check before elaboration.

| rank | signature | seconds | episode |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | a read lemma whose right side is a tower body, consumed by several `subst` sites | 767 | T87 |
| 2 | a premise with an iterated successor, depth three or more | 631 | T154 |
| 3 | an eliminator branch without a written type | 595 | T143 |
| 4 | a concrete membership application in a body, at a fiber, a union, or a `left` | 406 | T103 |
| 5 | satisfaction content at a concrete carrier: formulas, decodes, per-operation descriptions, placements into deep stages | 194 removed; about 210 added | T144, T180, T195 |

The single best predictor covers ranks one and four. It is a transparent
construction at a concrete argument in a checked type or body. Rank one
is the same mechanism at the statement level. The read lemma's right side
is the transparent tower body. Every consumer checks it at concrete
arguments. Together the two ranks removed about 1,170 seconds in this
campaign.

The checkable signatures, for a reviewer:

1. `subst` along a read lemma whose right side still contains the tower
   body (T87).
2. a transparent `⋃`, `left`, `⋂`, `fiber`, or `Lset ∘ sucV` layer at a
   concrete value in a body application or a theorem type (T103, T121,
   T123, T124).
3. two or more exposed `Lset ∘ sucV` layers in a module hypothesis type
   (P-i).
4. an eliminator with inline lambda branches over inner-world content and
   no written branch types (T143).
5. a membership premise with `+ω-iter n` or a `sucV` chain of depth three
   or more (T154).
6. object-language formulas, their decodes, per-operation descriptions,
   and placements into iterated stages at a concrete carrier (T144, T180,
   T195).

## 3. The block-1 against block-2 rate

The two figures come first. Block 1 of the below-lim master measures 604
in-fence lines at 6.30 s cold, profile total 5,840 ms, slowest definition
130 ms (`_build/l3.32-t191-report.md:78`, `:86`, `:197`). Block 2 adds
948 lines, and the whole master then measures about 216 s cold, profile
total 218,334 ms (`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:140-142`). Block 2 costs
about 210 s of that total.

The rates: block 1 checks at 0.010 s per line. Block 2 checks at about
0.22 s per line. The ratio is about 21 times on the whole figures. The
brief says thirty times. The exact factor depends on the denominator.

If block 1's definition content alone is the denominator, the ratio is
about 60 times. Its 3,658 ms Miscellaneous is 63 percent of the profile.
The mechanism does not depend on the denominator.

The two blocks are different content classes. That is the explanation.
Block 1 is parameterized content. Its definitions check at bound
variables. The story takes the nine-clause telescope as module parameters
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:205`). The carried sequence takes the limit
index, the pair family, and the decode as parameters (`:423-426`).

Nothing is instantiated. T191 did not port the carrier placements. Their
absence removed the probe's dominant 17.2 s term
(`_build/l3.32-t191-report.md:160`).

Block 2 is instantiation content. It builds the first limit instance. It
instantiates the graph layer at the witness carrier `W = Lset a₀`
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:879-880`). It states object-language
formulas: `storyFormW` (`:933`), `segForm` (`:1290`), `ψ` (`:1463`). It
proves decodes that walk the satisfaction relation: `segForm-ok`
(`:1315`), `P∈` (`:1412`), `ψ-out` (`:1466`), `ψ-in` (`:1501`). It places
concrete sets into deep stages, at `δ₀ = sucV (sucV (sucV a₀))`
(`:1594-1595`) and at stages to `sucV` depth six (`:805`, `:1692`).

Each such definition makes the elaborator normalize a concrete
presentation.

The profile confirms the class. The hot rows are the decode and placement
definitions. `seg∈C` takes 37.2 s. `ψ-in` takes 27.0 s. Its `go` takes
17.9 s.

The `out`, `bwd`, and `δNum` rows take about 30 s together. `segForm-ok`,
the `sat` reads, and `storyW-in/out` take about 33 s together. About 66
percent of the profile sits in ten definitions
(`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:149-155`). Every hot branch is a named
helper with a written type. The cost is the machinery, not a missing type.

The ratio is therefore the class ratio, not a measurement artifact. The
two blocks share one master, one author, and the same cited laws. Block 1
wrote the parameterized frame. Block 2 wrote the first instantiation. Line
count never predicted either figure. The class predicted both.

The ledger gives the same class gap at tree scale. The retiring subtree,
which states at abstract carriers and variable indices, checks at 0.013 s
per line over 26,483 lines. The surviving trunk checks at 0.104 s per
line over 22,761 lines (`dev/ledger.toml:1345-1357`). The caliber block
measures 0.0134 against 0.0702, a ratio of 5.2 (`dev/ledger.toml:759-764`).
Block 1 matches the abstract class. Block 2 is two to three times the
trunk average.

Block 2 sits at the extreme end of the concrete class. It combines
satisfaction decodes with deep placements. Depth compounds the rate. T154
measured 0.7 s at depth 1, 18.9 s at depth 3, and over 600 s at depth 6
(`_build/l3.32-t154-report.md:73-79`).

Is there a defensible seconds-per-line figure? Yes, per class. The
parameterized class checks at about 0.010 to 0.013 s per line. The
concrete instantiation class checks at about 0.22 s per line at this
site. The surviving-trunk average sits between them.

Use the rate as a class certificate. Do not use it as a line metric. A
block whose rate exceeds the parameterized rate by about twenty times is
instantiation content. Re-price it or split it before funding.

## 4. The writing rules

Each rule carries the episode that measured it. A build brief can carry
these rules verbatim.

**Rule 1. State a read lemma at the consumers' form.** Check the right
side against the consumers' `subst` targets before you seal it. The
proof's endpoint is not the consumers' form. Measurement: T87. Six lines
removed 767 s. Bridge went from 966,562 ms to 199,484 ms
(`_build/l3.32-t87-report.md:34-35`). The gate `scripts/check-timing.py`
enforces this class (`dev/LESSONS.md:2193`).

**Rule 2. Keep transparent constructions out of concrete positions.**
Seal them at birth (P-c, R-36). Move the concrete applications into
module parameters, where they check once at a neutral position (P-h).
Measurement: T103 removed 406 s for a net one line
(`_build/l3.32-t103-report.md:24-28`). T123 removed 37.8 s for seven
lines (`_build/l3.32-t123-report.md:5`). T124 removed 70.3 s for seven
lines, and Step fell with it for zero edits
(`_build/l3.32-t124-report.md:11-12`).

**Rule 3. Name every eliminator branch and write its type.** An inline
lambda branch over inner-world content re-elaborates the machinery per
constraint. Measurement: T143. Two inline branches at one eliminator took
595 s. The named branches with written types took 27 ms
(`_build/l3.32-t143-report.md:9-12`).

**Rule 4. State membership witnesses at shallow indices.** Climb by the
limit-ordinal successor closure, one step per line (R-40). Measurement:
T154. A depth-six witness did not finish in 600 s. A nine-line climb
checked in 59 s (`_build/l3.32-t154-report.md:99-100`).

**Rule 5. Price satisfaction content as its own class.** If the content
must state formulas, decodes, or placements at a concrete carrier, expect
the instantiation rate. Delete it or replace it with a generic frame where
the content can be avoided. Measurement: T144 removed 138.7 s by deletion
(`_build/l3.32-t144-report.md:113`). T180 removed 55 s by replacing
the per-operation descriptions with a generic frame
(`_build/l3.32-t180-report.md:62-64`). T195 paid about 210 s for 948
lines of instantiation content (`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:140-142`).

**Rule 6. Write the generic frame before the first instantiation.** The
frame is the same mathematics at bound variables. It checks at the
parameterized rate. The instantiation checks at the concrete rate.
Measurement: T191's frame at 0.010 s per line, against T195's first
instantiation at 0.22 s per line.

## 5. The proposed laws

The orchestrator assigns the IDs. The doctrine proposes two entries.

**Law A (P-series candidate). The check-cost rate is a content-class
certificate.** A block's seconds per line identifies its content class
before the profile. Parameterized content at bound variables checks near
0.01 s per line. Instantiated satisfaction and placement content checks
near 0.22 s per line at this site. Line count alone predicts nothing.
Evidence: `_build/l3.32-t191-report.md:78`, `:86`;
`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:149-155`; `dev/ledger.toml:759-764` and
`:1345-1357`.

**Law B (P-series candidate). Satisfaction content at a concrete carrier
is a payable floor.** Named, typed branches do not remove the cost of
object-language satisfaction content at a concrete carrier. The cost
concentrates in the decode and placement definitions. Deletion or a
generic frame is the only measured removal. Evidence:
`_build/l3.32-t195-report.md:149-155` (every hot branch is a named
helper); `_build/l3.32-t144-report.md:113`;
`_build/l3.32-t180-report.md:62-64`.

## 6. What I could not decide

**T144's class boundary.** The archive records the cost as P-l
(`dev/ARCHIVE.md:89`). The doctrine reads it as content-intrinsic, because
deletion was the only measured cure. A re-homed ambient form with a
profile can settle the boundary. No such form exists.

**The exact ratio factor.** The whole figures give about 21 times. The
definition-content figures give about 60 times. The brief says thirty
times. The mechanism is robust to the denominator. Block 1's 63 percent
Miscellaneous makes the definition-content denominator small and noisy.
The honest figure is a band, not a point.

**T180's attribution.** The dispatch took no per-definition profile. The
per-operation-description reading rests on the import diff and the
module-level delta (`_build/l3.32-t180-report.md:62-64`, `:78`). No one
measured a seal-only variant of the descriptions.

**R-35's status.** T143 and T154 ruled it out as a disease. They are the
only two sites in this set that tested it. Its own measured entry still
stands (`dev/LESSONS.md:768`). The doctrine cannot generalize further from
two refusals.

**Block 3's rate.** The general-limit STEP may cost less at the second
instantiation. The induction frame re-instantiated at 78 lines and the
Q-lim frame at 62 lines (`dev/ledger.toml:394`). No one measured a second
STEP instantiation. The doctrine names the probe. Measure the second
instantiation before funding block 3.

**I-4's status.** No episode in this set measured an I-4 wall. The
doctrine can neither confirm nor refute it here. It drops out of the
taxonomy by absence, not by refutation.
