### 3.2 The successor clause, proved

`ProbeCondense.agda:123-136`, 12 code lines:

```agda
succ-step : HasLevels φ → SucClosed
          → (δ : S) → ⟨ δ ∈ˢ M ⟩ → IsOrd δ
          → (x : S) → ⟨ x ∈ˢ 𝒟ₒ (Lset δ) ⟩ → ⟨ x ∈ˢ M ⟩
```

"If M's internal story supplies a level at `δ+1`, then every definable subset of the `δ`-th

delivered facts and one hypothesis.

## 4. PIECE 3 — the residue: the crossing, reduced to one obligation at two carriers

### 4.1 The reduction, proved

Everything the assembly needs is `CrossOut φ`:

```agda
CrossOut φ = (v b : Sᴹ) → IsOrd (fst b) → ⟨ (v ∷ b ∷ []) AbsM.⊨ᵐ φ ⟩
           → fst v ≡ Lset (fst b)
```

and the probe proves (`ProbeCondense.agda:221-266`) that `CrossOut` follows from exactly two
things:

1. **`TransferM`** — M's inner reading implies the ambient reading of the transported formula
   (what `σ₁-up` would give, at the set carrier);
2. **`AmbientOnly`** — the **ambient-reading form of `Lset-only`**:
   `(v b : S) → IsOrd b → ⟨ (v ∷ b ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ LsetGraph ⟩ → v ≡ Lset b`,

which in turn factors (`ambientOnly-from`, 6 lines) into **`TransferL`** (the ambient reading
implies L's inner reading — what `π₁-down` would give, at the class carrier) plus
**`ValueIsL`** (the value named by an ambient satisfaction is constructible).

**This is the probe's sharpest result. The condensation crossing is ONE absoluteness theorem
about ONE formula, instantiated at TWO transitive carriers (`M` and `L`), plus one
constructibility side condition. It is not a family of obligations; it is one obligation
priced twice.** The delivered `Lset-only` ([Hierarchy.lagda.md:333]) is stated at `AbsL`'s
*inner* reading only, so it does not reach either instance on its own.
