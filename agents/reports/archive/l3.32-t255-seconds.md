# [L3.32-T255] The seconds question: why 570 s, and is there a formulation that does not pay it?

**Headline: the largest new seconds lever is the stack-shape family, minus 30
to 70 s per surviving formula stack, hypothesis, two gates named. It is not
of the gap's size. The 294-299 s gap does NOT close on this route from
anything in the record or in this analysis. Section 6 gives the arithmetic.
Section 5 names the widest unmeasured term: 389 of the master's 570 s have
never been profiled, and one warm profile run decides where the truth sits.**

## 1. The owner's question: mathematics or engineering, settled in three parts

The phrase under test is "paid at every limit, TWICE". It bundles three
claims. They separate cleanly, and they get different verdicts.

### 1.1 "At every limit" is mathematics, and it costs ONCE at check time

At a limit γ the next stage is the definable power:
`Lset (sucV γ) = 𝒟ₒ (Lset γ)`. Its members are exactly the definable
subsets. So `Sset γ` enters only as a carve: a formula over `⟪ W ⟫` with
its witnesses ranging over members of `W = Lset γ`
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:2733-2738`). The recursion's history is not
otherwise present in `W`. A stage owns no replacement, so `defSet` is the
only collection instrument (`src/L/Definability.lagda.md:110-111`), and the
witness must be a set of pairs inside the carrier. [T250] section 3 items 3
and 4 already closed the escapes, and I re-checked both. This half is
mathematics.

But the seconds meter does not pay it per limit. The STEP is written once,
at a variable γ (`Stepγ`, `src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:799`), and
`∈-induction` closes it (`:136-137`). The elaborator checks one module. So
"at every limit" multiplies proofs at run time, never seconds at check
time. The check-time count is the count of WRITTEN stacks.

### 1.2 "Rebuilt at every limit" is engineering, already found

The written count today is two stacks in this master: the top stack at `W`
(`:2429-2861`) and the nested stack at `Wξ = Lset ξ` (`:1252-1828`). The
nested one exists because the inner recursion rebuilds what the induction
hypothesis could carry. That is [T250]'s lever, minus 50 to 80 s, and its
Gate 1 runs as [T251]. Nothing here re-derives it.

### 1.3 "TWICE" is mathematics at the stack level. I refute the engineering reading

The orchestrator suspected the twice is engineering, because classical
treatments run ONE induction that carries both directions. The suspicion is
half right and the half that matters is wrong.

**The joint induction is real, and it buys lines, not seconds.** One
induction on limits can carry both residues and both witness families, and
the segment machinery is already carrier-generic and step-generic
(`module Story`, `src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:223-249`;
`src/L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md`). Sharing it is [T250] section 1.2. P-q says
what that is worth at the wall: almost nothing, because a shared
restatement is cheap and the use at a carrier is what costs.

**The two top stacks do not merge, for three reasons.**

1. **Two targets, two instruments.** Below-lim lands `Sset γ` inside
   `Lset (sucV γ)`: the only door is `𝒟ₒ`-intro over the L-carrier
   (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:2733-2738`). Q-lim lands `Lset l` inside
   `Sset (γ l)`: rud steps are finitary, an infinite new collection enters
   an S-block only through the definable-power covering
   (`BlockPowLim`, `src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:980-982`), which consumes a
   formula over the S-carrier. Each membership has exactly one instrument,
   and each instrument demands a formula at its own carrier.
2. **Formulas and satisfaction are carrier-indexed.** `Formula ⟪ A ⟫ n`
   and `⊨ᵐ` elaborate per carrier. Identical clause shapes stated at
   `Lset γ` and at `Sset (U l)` are two elaborations. A single joint story
   carrying both sequences makes it worse: one BIGGER tree, still walked
   once per carrier, so two walks of a larger formula.
3. **Neither residue derives from the other.** The sandwich inclusions
   give `⊆`, never `∈`. Deriving Q-lim from below-lim still needs the
   L-sequence collected over the S-carrier, which is the L-story stack.
   Deriving below-lim from Q-lim points the memberships the wrong way. The
   membership at every index is load-bearing: the successor case of
   `Below.rstep` consumes the stage placement of `Sset δ` through the
   self-arm (`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:1069-1107`), so no thinning to
   cofinally many limits exists.

**Verdict for the owner.** The interleaving's honest floor is one formula
stack per residue: two stacks total. The nested rebuilds, the fold
assembly, the rename plumbing and the alias rows on top of that floor are
engineering. The count "two stacks" is a fact about definability and about
finitary rud generation, not about this project's choices, and no
formulation removes it while the route needs both membership directions.

## 2. Prompt 2: what makes the carried sequence instantiation-class at a variable carrier

**The class certificate follows the FORMULA, not the carrier.** The carrier
`Lset ξ` is sealed and atomic; [T241] checked P-l at the site and it does
not bite. What the four hot families share is a TYPE that applies the
transparent satisfaction recursion `⊨ᵐ` to a concrete formula tree:

- `storyFormWξ`, itself a transparent fold over the six-clause list
  (`KWξ.storyForm storyWClausesξ`, `src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:1338-1339`;
  the fold is `ConjForm`, `src/L/LevelKit.lagda.md:793-820`);
- its rename `storyRenξ = renameFo embStoryξ storyFormWξ` (`:1376-1377`);
- `segFormξ`, which embeds `storyRenξ` under three `∃̇` (`:1427-1435`);
- the `defSet` transports through `∈-asFiber` at `⟪ Wξ ⟫` (`:1439-1440`
  with `src/L/Definability.lagda.md:110-111`).

`⊨ᵐ` computes by recursion on the formula. Whenever a definition's type
states `⟨ δ ⊨ᵐ F ⟩` for a built tree `F`, conversion unfolds the
recursion over the whole tree, and the type's normal form has the tree's
size. The carrier variable never unfolds. The tree always does.

The evidence, all measured:

1. **Three one-line aliases cost 36.6 s** (`storyWξ-ok` 18.1,
   `storyWξ-in` 9.3, `storyWξ-out` 9.1; [T241] section 2). Their bodies
   are kit lemma applications. The cost is the elaboration of their stated
   types, which each contain the fold instance.
2. **[T222]'s slice at the same carrier class checked at 0.085.** It held
   one of the four families. The carrier was equally variable. The
   missing 52.8 percent of the check was the missing formula content
   ([T242], `dev/ledger.toml:1467-1491`).
3. **[T249] is cheap exactly where the walks are hypothesized.** Its bulk
   is parameterized content whose satisfaction facts arrive in a module
   telescope, at 0.0113 s per line. Its own hot rows are its adequacy
   decodes, 28.9 percent, the same P-n shape in miniature.
4. **P-r moved one site 3x by changing only the formula's presentation**
   (hand tree 7.5 s, fold 22.0 s, `dev/LESSONS.md:2322-2356`). The carrier
   did not move.

So "instantiation content" means: content whose types state satisfaction
or membership of concrete syntax trees. P-n's phrase "concrete carrier"
was a proxy, correct at every measured site so far because every stack so
far built its trees at a stage. The consequence is operational. The floor
of a stack is the number of distinct tree-applications the mathematics
must state, times the walk. Cutting the carrier free buys nothing further;
cutting the COUNT of stated applications is the remaining engineering.

Block 1 is the proof by exhibition: the same six clauses, the same
segment mathematics, at 0.010 s per line, because `graph-out`, `graph-in`
and `eqFrame-ok` are telescope hypotheses
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:223-249`) and no built tree is stated.

### Lever B: cut the count of stated tree-applications at a stack

**Mechanism.** Three interlocking shape moves at one stack site.

1. **Seal the built formulas.** Make `storyFormW`, `storyRen`, `segForm`

   `:2503-2575`, `:2584-2618`, `:2621-2692`).
2. **One `⟷` row, projections for free.** State `story-ok` once; derive

3. **Hand-write the assembly.** Replace the clause-list fold and its kit
   aliases with the hand conjunction. P-r measured this exact swap at

**Sites.** The top stack `:2429-2861`; the nested stack `:1252-1828`
while it exists; the future Q-lim stack at its own carrier.

**Seconds band.** Minus 30 to 70 per stack site, hypothesis. Anchors: the
nested site's four families sum to 127.7 s measured, the gut ceiling is

**The risk that kills it.** Two. First, the P-n boundary: if conversion
re-walks the sealed symbols anyway wherever the `∃̇`-prefix chains

Second, D36 naturalness: C4's fold was kept for reading, and the hand

`storyWξ→storyW` destructures the PREDICATE list, not the formula
(`:1356-1369`), so a formula seal does not break it; `defSet` consumes
the formula as data, so opacity is legal there.

**Why this is not a P-n violation, said plainly.** [T241]'s NO-GO
refuted ROW-level cures: postulate a hot row and downstream rows do not
move. It never tested the count of tree statements ACROSS rows, which is
a different quantity. The gut ceiling 109.8 still bounds everything, and

**Gate.** Re-shape [T240]'s miniature with the three moves; three clean

**So the answer to the brief's title question:** the 570 s is, in order,
one stack of mathematics (the top carve), one stack of engineering being
removed (the nested rebuild, [T251]), an unmeasured 389 s tranche that one
profile run attributes, and a fold-and-rename tax worth 45 to 95 s of
hypotheses. A formulation that does not pay 570 exists and is section 4's
composite. A formulation that pays under the 600 gate for the WHOLE
landing does not exist on this route on today's evidence. The owner's
decision is between moving the gate and re-shaping the route, and the two
instruments that would firm this verdict before that ruling are Gate 0
and [T251], both cheap, one already running.

## 6. Considered and rejected

1. **A joint story formula serving both carves.** One tree carrying both
   sequences, stated once. Rejected: satisfaction elaborates per carrier,
   so the pair still walks twice, over a BIGGER tree. Anti-lever.
2. **Deriving one residue from the other.** Both directions checked in
   section 1.3. The sandwich gives inclusions; memberships need their own
   carves. Rejected as mathematics, not preference.
3. **The carve consuming `P` as a formula constant.** `ψ` could name the
   pair family instead of restating the story, killing one story walk.
   Rejected: constants must be carrier members, and `P ∈ 𝒟ₒ W`, not
   `P ∈ W`. [T250] section 3 item 4 is the same wall from the other side.
4. **A syntax-free definition of `Sset γ` over `Lset γ`.** No history
   witness, no story. Rejected: the towers disagree per level (the ζ = ω
   counterexample), and D-26 says a definable-power stage carries no
   generation data, so the defining formula with a history witness is the
   only key. The classical uniform-Σ₁ definitions use the same witness.
5. **Thinning the induction to cofinally many limits.** Rejected: the
   self-arm of the successor case consumes the membership at EVERY index
   (`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:1104-1107`).
6. **Sealing at the row level, or `abstract` blocks.** Rejected by
   [T241]'s measured gut: downstream rows do not move, and the once is
   never bought. Lever B is the tree-level version and is gated, not
   assumed.
7. **Naming shared sub-trees as definitions to share the walk.**
   Rejected: conversion walks per application, transparent definitions do
   not stop it. Only `opaque` stops it, which is lever B.
8. **Re-opening the carried sequence, Bridge, StepGraph, or the base.**
   Not done. The brief's NO-GO record stands; nothing above touches those
   sites.

## 7. Where my reading contradicts the brief

1. **"Every large site is already measured NO-GO" is not quite true.**
   The delivered master itself, 569.94 s, was never profiled per
   definition; the record profiled its 174.62 s miniature and other
   modules. 389 s carry no attribution. The largest single unmeasured
   term in the campaign is inside the module the brief is about. My
   reading wins, and Gate 0 is the cheapest dispatch on the board.
2. **"Paid at every limit" is not a seconds statement.** At check time
   the limit case is paid once, at a variable γ. The honest multiplier is
   written stacks: two per residue today, one per residue at the floor.
3. **The 894-899 landing is biased low.** It carries the Q-lim residue at
   a 9.12 s shell floor while section 1.3 shows its content must include
   an instantiation-class stack. The 294-299 gap is a floor on the true
   gap.
4. **The orchestrator's "twice looks like engineering" is refuted at the
   level that carries the seconds**, and confirmed at the level that
   carries the lines. Section 1.3 is the reasoning; it is the answer the
   owner asked for, with the classical treatments on the mathematics'
   side: their one induction carries both directions, and it also states
   one history-witness definition per hierarchy, not one total.
