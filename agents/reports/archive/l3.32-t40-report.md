**Verdict in one paragraph.** The flagged risk is settled by building, and the
verdict is the one the scoping recon bet on: the delivered reflection shape
does **not** force a different formulation, because the direct definable-hull
(Devlin 5.3) goes through the Tarski-Vaught criterion induction and never
reaches for the reflection package. The chapter closes inside the stop-line
at 241 non-blank in-fence lines (row A, elementarity + criterion, 112 lines;
row B, the hull core, 92 lines; the import block 37; the two rows' sum 204),
under T5's 0.35-0.75k naive band for the two rows. The meta-pick formulation
delivers the hull, `X ⊆ M`, and the Tarski criterion at the hull's own
parameter source unconditionally; what the leastness encoding costs is
measured: the two remaining Devlin 5.3 consequences, the full elementary
reading at hull parameters and the smallestness, each reduce to the adequate
internal order formula `σ_<` at the carrier (W3's unprobed residual), and the
full reading has a second, independent obstruction, the constants-to-variables
face of the relabelling kit, which the delivered syntax lacks. Both are stated
precisely below, priced, and left standing.

## 3. The verdict on the flagged formulation risk

The recon's flagged question was: does the delivered reflection's shape (one
formula at a time, `φ` outside the quantifier, closure not inherited by
larger stages) force a different formulation of the hull, and what does it
cost? The build answers: **the direct formulation was already the right one,
and the reflection package is simply not reached for**; the reflection
machinery (`ClosedFor`, `Ladder`, `mkReflect`) is not imported anywhere in
the chapter; the order enters only through the delivered `orderAt`/`leastOf`
family ([L.Choice.Step](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Choice/Step.lagda.md:740),
[L.WellOrder.Base](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/WellOrder/Base.lagda.md)).
Elementarity is proved by the criterion induction, exactly the recon's
prescription; the hull is constructed by the meta-pick least-witness route
over the delivered `orderAt`/`leastOf`, exactly the recon's option (ii).

The measured cost of the meta-pick formulation is confined to the two
Devlin 5.3 consequences that the recon bundled into row B but that the
leastness encoding does not deliver unconditionally:

1. **The full elementary reading at hull parameters** (`M ≺ Lset α` for
   `M := Hull`, formulas with parameters from the hull, not just from `X`).
   The hull's criterion instance (`hull-closed`) is over `X`-parameters. The
   step from `X`-parameters to hull parameters is the parameter-relabelling
   argument: a hull member `a` is the least witness of some `θ`, and the
   witness set of `φ(x, ā)` equals that of `∃ȳ (φ(x, ȳ) ∧ ⋀ᵢ θᵢ(yᵢ))` once
   each `θᵢ` is replaced by "`yᵢ` is the least witness of `θᵢ`". Encoding
   leastness as a formula is exactly the internal order formula `σ_<` at the
   carrier (W3's unprobed residual, 0.30-0.70k unprobed in
   [l3.32-t5-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t5-report.md):500-510).
   There is a second, independent obstruction the recon did not price: the
   relabelling kit moves constants between domains
   ([Bounding.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Manipulation/Bounding.lagda.md:146))
   but has no constants-to-variables face, so a formula with hull constants
   cannot be rewritten over `X`-variables inside the delivered syntax.
   Stated as a residue: for `M := Hull`, `Elementary` follows from
   `hull-closed` together with (a) an adequate `σ_<` at the carrier and (b) a
   deconstantification operator `Formula SM n → Formula ⟪ X ⟫ (suc n)` for
   formulas whose constants are least witnesses. Piece (b) is syntax
   machinery the tree does not carry (Syntax's recap: "no substitution and no
   weakening operators anywhere",
   [Syntax.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Syntax.lagda.md)); the
   report prices it as part of the residue, not of this chapter.

2. **Smallestness** (`Hull ⊆ N` for every elementary `N` containing `X`,
   Devlin 5.3's last paragraph and 5.4's "unique smallest"). Given an
   adequate `σ_<`, each hull member `a` (least witness of `θ`) is the
   *unique* witness of `ψ = θ ∧ ∀v₁ (σ_<(v₁, v₀) → ¬θ(v₁))`, and Devlin's
   argument transfers `L_α ⊨ ∃ψ` into `N` by elementarity and reads the
   witness back. The reduction is proved on paper in section 5 below; the
   formal cost is the `σ_<` residue plus a variable-shifting (weakening)
   operator for the `θ` inside `ψ`, which the delivered syntax also lacks.
   Stated as a residue: `(x : S) → ⟨ x ∈ˢ Hull ⟩ → ⟨ x ∈ˢ N ⟩` for every
   transitive `N ⊆ Lset α` with `N ≺ Lset α` (full `Elementary` at `N`) and
   `X ⊆ N`, from `hull-closed` plus the adequate `σ_<` and the weakening
   operator.

Both residues were priced before building (D-10) and the build confirmed
them: the meta-pick formulation's unconditional core is the hull, `X ⊆ M`,
and the `X`-parameter criterion; the two "smallest/elementary" consequences
are the order-formula cost, precisely the cost the recon's option (ii)
flagged as "changes nothing in Steps 3-5" while leaving the smallestness
unexamined.

## 5. What remains of W7's hull side

The two residues of section 3, stated with their exact types:

- **Full elementarity of the hull**: `AtM Hull <hull-transitivity> <hull⊆L> .Elementary`, where `hull-transitivity` and `hull⊆L` are themselves open
  (the hull's transitivity and `Hull ⊆ Lset α` follow from the hull's
  membership shape, `leastVal φ w : S` with `fst (leastWit φ w)` inside the
  stage, but are not yet proved). Reduction: `Elementary ↔ TarskiVaught`
  (delivered, `TV-thm`) plus the `X`-parameter criterion (delivered,
  `hull-closed`) plus the two faces of section 3.1.
- **Smallestness**: `(x : S) → ⟨ x ∈ˢ Hull ⟩ → ⟨ x ∈ˢ N ⟩` under `N`
  transitive, `N ⊆ Lset α`, `AtM N Ntr N⊆L .Elementary`, and `X ⊆ N`, from
  the adequate `σ_<` and the weakening operator. The paper reduction: for
  `x ∈ Hull` with `x` the least witness of `θ`, `x` is the unique witness of
  `ψ` (uniqueness by trichotomy of the meta-order against `σ_<`'s adequacy);
  `L_α ⊨ ∃ψ`; `N ≺ L_α` gives `N ⊨ ∃ψ`; the `N`-witness `y` satisfies
  `L_α ⊨ ψ(y)`, hence `y = x`. Nothing in the delivered chapter contradicts
  this; the missing formal pieces are the order formula and the shift.

The cardinal chapter (Devlin 5.4's `|M| = max(|X|, ω)`) remains W7's long
pole and is untouched here; the hull's `sett` index,
`Σ[ φ ∈ Formula ⟪ X ⟫ 1 ] Witnessed-small φ`, is the count-friendly index T5
prescribed (formulas directly, not `Name`), so the cardinal chapter's count
runs on it without re-encoding.
