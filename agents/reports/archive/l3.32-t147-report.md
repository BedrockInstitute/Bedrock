## 1. Verdict, stated precisely

The word "alone" carries two readings. The split proves one and not the other.

**Reading A, the delivered closure. TRUE, and machine-verified.**
Delete all 22 masters. The AC endpoint `L ⊨ ZFC` still typechecks.
Round 1 reached `L.Model` and checked it green, then failed at `Bridge`.
The run log prints `Checking L.Model` two lines before the Bridge error.
Every module of the base part, the ac-only part and the shared-proper part
checked green in the same run. All 32 retiring masters checked green too.
The green set therefore CONTAINS the 5,949 lines that
`scripts/ledger.py:307` calls `ac_delivered`. Nothing in it needs the 22.

**Reading B, the tree. FALSE.**
Ten surviving L modules cannot be scope-checked once the 22 are gone.
They hold 4,077 lines. The rule at `scripts/ledger.py:293` puts all ten into
part 3, `shared`, and part 3 feeds `ac_total`. So the board's AC figure of
10,026 contains 4,077 lines that CANNOT COMPILE without the 9,197 lines the
board calls GCH-alone. The two numbers are not independent, and the matrix
presents them as if they were.

`[T139]` reached the same conclusion from the arithmetic side and rated the
single AC figure WEAK (`_build/l3.32-t139-report.md:151-173`). This report
adds the machine proof. The 4,077 lines are not merely unattributed. They are
structurally downstream of the GCH-only part.
