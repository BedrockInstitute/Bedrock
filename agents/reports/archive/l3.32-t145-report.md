**The slice does not separate from the full rewrite.** The full rewrite
serves W3: the sixteen-op lex and the full graph family
(`dev/ledger.toml:386-388`, T146's accounting at
`_build/l3.32-t146-report.md:177`). The three roles are exactly those two

The measured obligations total about **1,718 in-fence lines** (228 + 149 +

its top edge. T146 estimated the slice at about 85 percent of the row

estimate: the slice is about 99-100 percent of the band's top. The
minimal supply that rides the D30(B) exemption is not minimal. It is the
full graph layer at delivered size.

Of the 1,718 lines, about 223 close over survivors as fresh glue
(the stage dance 65, the empty-stage support 20, the `Values` shell 18,
