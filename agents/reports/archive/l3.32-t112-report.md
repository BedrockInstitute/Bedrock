## 2. The cap and its argument

**The cap is 1,200 characters per goal row**, measured the same way the task
index's cap is measured: the whole row, `len(line.rstrip())`, for the MASTER
table rows of section 11 (the `| L` rows between `## 11.` and `### Task
index`). The task index's 200-character cap (`CAP = 200` in
`scripts/check-task-index.py:44`) is for a pure pointer row: code, task name,
verdict, detail pointer. A goal row legitimately carries more, because it
holds a ruling and its authority.

What a row must be able to say at the cap: the status word with its date and
authority; the ruling in one or two clauses; the current status; what gates or
blocks the goal; and a pointer to the detail. The `L3.32` row
(`dev/PLAN.md:1179`) says exactly that, at 1,001 characters and 150 tokens:

> **RULED AND ACTIVE 2026-08-04** by D18. The Def tower keeps the trophy; the
> wing rides a fresh-generic Sigma-1 face; the reindexed bridge lands at wing
> tail as a corollary; choice re-homes through it; the internalization cone,
> the choice tree, the Goedel trees and the coded cluster retire. Wave 1 (...)
> has returned; wave 2 builds the face chapter, then W2, then W3 and W5 in
> parallel, then the bridge, the re-home, the retirement surgery and W7. The
> check-cost campaign (D30's freeze, and the work that lifts it) and the
> current gates run in the `[L3.32-F*]` rows below. (...) Execution record,
> with the wave-by-wave measurements: `dev/JOURNAL.md`.

That row is the anchor, and deliberately so: `[T108]` built it hours before
this dispatch as the register form, so the cap is derived from what the
register actually needs, not from the last accident. **1,200 = the exemplar
(1,001) plus one legitimate register edit** (a new gate sentence or a second
pointer is on the order of 150 to 200 characters), about 20 percent above the
exemplar. It is 5.8x below the failure the finding names (`L3.32-F5` at 6,966
characters), and every row that exceeded it on today's tree was at least
1.57x over (`L3.32-F3.1` at 1,883 characters), so the gate fires with room to
spare rather than at the boundary.

The cap is writable, which is the test the brief sets: after the slimming the
largest goal row is `L3.32-F0` at 1,022 characters (85 percent of the cap),
zero rows exceed it, and the total goal-row text fell from 50,639 to 27,589
characters. One row, `L3.32-F6.0`, initially landed at 1,247 characters with a
first, generous split of kept content, and the cap forced the re-judgement
described in section 3. A cap that cannot be written under gets raised the
first week; this one was tested against a real row on the first day.
