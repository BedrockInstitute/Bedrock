## 2. The engine gaps, named (D-10, D-15[B])

### 2.1 Gap A — `switch-⊇` is conditional, and the census named it as the engine

The census's §2.2 rests the D-bucket on `switch-⊇` (Switch:806-809) and
`realize-defSet` (Realize:1213-1215). Measured on the tree:

- `switch-⊇` sits inside `module Eval-J (Jimg : …)`, itself inside
  `module Closure`. Its packaged trunk face is
  `LimitSwitch.Up.definable→closure`, and `module Up` is parameterized by
  **`Jimg`, the rud image principle** ("the image of a composite family
  over a member of a closed level is again a member"). Switch's own
  recap names it: *"Two residues stand… The closure side takes the rud
  image principle as a hypothesis… it is a chapter of its own."*
- `grep -rn "LimitSwitch\|Jimg"` over `src/` returns **no instantiation
  anywhere**. The hypothesis is discharged nowhere.
- It additionally carries a **Δ₀ rider** (`(d : Δ₀ φ)`) that the L-side
  `defSet→isL` does not have.

**Retraction, recorded (C-6, C-16).** My first reading was that `Jimg` is
*unnameable* outside Switch, because Switch takes `Comp` from Realize's
`Basis` with a non-`public` `open` — a C-14[B] delivery defect.
**Perturbation refuted this.** Re-applying `Realize.Basis` at Switch's
own eighteen exported arguments (`F0 pairSpec F1 diffSpec interOp
interSpec F2 prodSpec memOp memSpec unionOp unionSpec colOp colSpec
chSepOp chSepSpec eqSepOp eqSepSpec`) reconstructs `Comp`, and
`LimitSwitch.Up` accepts a `Jimg` of the reconstructed type: the probe's
throwaway `ProbeR5X.agda` typechecked `U.definable→closure` green. The
gap is **mathematical, not a naming defect**, and costs 3 lines plus
imports to reach. Reported as unmeasured-and-wrong rather than
retro-fitted.

**Price:** not priced here (D-10: a named gap is a deliverable). Switch's
recap prices it as "a chapter of its own"; the only in-repo calibration
for a rud-images chapter is `L.Rud.Images` itself.

   evaporates.** `pr∈Lset-suc` climbs **two** successor stages to place a

   Basic: every downstream consumer that tracks which stage an ordered
   pair, a graph, a table or a sequence landed in stops tracking. The
   census books none of this, and it is the structural reason the
   fine-structure literature is J-only.
3. **R-37 does not fire on the J side, by construction.** R-37's trigger
   is a transported membership in a statement position *at a concrete
   index*; the L side's statements are at `Lset (sucV σ)` and
   `Lset (sucV (sucV σ))`, exactly that shape. Every J-side statement in
   the probe binds at `Jset α limα` with α a **variable** limit, which is
   R-37's own recorded cure applied by construction. A second unbooked
   reason the port is cheap, and a candidate extension to the R-37 entry.
4. **The tower-generic churn rate is exactly 1.0x, confirmed end to
   end.** P7's 39 L lines became 41 J lines with zero errors on the first

5. **A census's chapter classification is not its import graph.**
   SatSets was retired by adjacency in `Everything`'s import block. A
