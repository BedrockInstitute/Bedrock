# [L3.31-IF-Rp] D-1 probe report: Separation over isJ (churn vs proof), plus the Sset-Ord content family priced by statement

**Verdict, compressed. The third GO condition of R5p is discharged at the low
end of its own band, and the ordinal-content family is priced by statement,
not reduced.** The representative Separation-class target, an arbitrary
Δ₀-definable subclass carve at a fixed J level (`separateAtJ` in
`src/ProbeR5Sep.agda`, 300 non-blank lines, cold check 10.4 s at
`GHCRTS=-M8g`, zero wall events), ports from `L.Axioms.Separation` at
**1.0-2.0x per declaration (the core rows at 1.0-1.4x; every row above 1.3x
is a tax, not a re-proof)**, with the introduction half a genuine
deletion: `carve∈J` is one line through `SatSets.LimitFullSwitch
.full-switch-⊇`, unconditional and Δ₀-free, exactly as R5p's P6b predicted.
The hardest population therefore stays inside Separation's **0.32-0.53k naive
band at the low end (measured ≈ 0.32-0.44k)**, and R-ideal's R-3 D-bucket
holds at ≈ **0.83-1.35k naive** (ifrecon:515). The `Sset γ ∩ Ord ≡ γ` family
is written as seven statements with their consumers and mechanism map: the
family is **not a pure re-point** (two genuinely-new statements named,
`rank-Sset` and the successor-content shape), the J-native forward shape is
at `+ω` through the limit switch rather than at the successor, Infinity's
D-row under-prices its dependency on the carve form F4, and W7's `|J_γ| = |γ|`
cannot even be stated in the current language (the r5census:405
statement-shape trap is confirmed). The R-5 band (200-500 naive / 600-1,500
calibrated, ifrecon:517) stands, now anchored: re-pointable core ≈ 86-100,
genuinely-new statements ≈ 8-45, W7 reading ≈ 60-370 and unstateable today.
Neither result moves the fork: R-ideal naive tightens ≈ 0.1k at the high end
and still clears the tripwire; the calibrated R-ideal still breaches. The one
structural consequence is the reaffirmation of SatSets retention: with it the
family's forward half has its engine delivered, without it (the census's
retirement) the forward half loses its mechanism and reverts to an unpriced
`Jimg` chapter (r5probe:388-390).

## 2. Churn versus genuine proof

The split is sharpest as three populations, mirroring r5probe §1:

- **Ported one-for-one (S2, S4, S5; L 83 → J 88, 1.06x).** The five-step
  bridge, the transport, and the 46-line `separateAt` spec are character-level
  re-points: `isL` → `isJ` (absoluteness and member certificates),
  `Lset σ` → `Jset β limβ`, `layer-trans` → `Sset-trans`, `uniqueL` →
  `uniqueJ`. The **genuine** proof content of the bridge is one composite
  (the last link, `abs₀` at `isJ`); everything else is statement churn, as
  D-16 (carrier-generic absoluteness) predicted. `separateAt` → `separateAtJ`
  at 1.02x is the flagship: the hardest population (an arbitrary Δ₀ carve
  against an arbitrary subclass at a J level) is a re-point, not a new
  chapter.
- **Deleted or cheaper (S3's introduction half).** `carve∈𝒟ₒ` (three lines
  of `𝒟ₒ-intro` plus a formula witness) becomes `carve∈J` (three lines, one
  of which is the switch). The L-side introduction needed `𝒟ₒ` and its
  extensionality; the J side needs neither. The Δ₀ hypothesis is **deleted
  from the introduction half** on the J side: the statement is stronger, and
  the census's "the carve is `DefC.defSet ψ` plus `𝒟ₒ-intro` (one line), the
  J twin is `full-switch-⊇` (one line)" is confirmed verbatim.
- **Riding a named residue (S6, S7).** `separateΔ₀J` is proved green from the
  named `MkBoundedJ` (the 71-line formula recursion, pure churn at ~1.0x) and
  the delivered `leastOrd`. The 24 J lines of S6 are the assembly; the
  recursion is not written, exactly the P6/`DefClosureJ` pattern.

**What is NOT churn, and stays named.** Two things are genuinely new on the J
side and are not built: (i) the `𝒮ⱼ` uniqueness face (`extensionalJ` +
`uniqueJ`, 13 lines, the P7 shape) is delivered only in untracked `ProbeR5`,
so the probe re-derives it (S0); (ii) the J-side stage extraction (`jst` +
`leastOrd`, 5 lines) has no packaged export, so every D-bucket consumer
re-derives it until someone seals a `stageJ`.

   **≈ 0.83-1.35k**, and R-ideal's naive total (16.0-21.3k, ifrecon:532)
