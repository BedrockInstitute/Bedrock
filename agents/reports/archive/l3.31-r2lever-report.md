## [L3.31-R2L] Recon: compression levers for the R2' bridge theorem

**Verdict, compressed.** The wide term is not the crossing itself; it is the absence of a carrier-generic Σ₁ face. The composed best case — R2' at wing time, priced as the bridge's increment over a standing generic W1+W2 — lands at **0.60–1.50k naive / 1.3–3.5k calibrated (center), ex-P2**, against R2p's 2.25–4.3k / 6.8–12.9k (l3.31-r2probe-report.md:198–210). The compression rests on four machine-checked facts the R2p census did not have:

1. `LimitFullSwitch.full-switch-⊇ : (φ : Formula ⟪ Ju ⟫ 1) → ⟨ DefOf.defSet Ju φ ∈ˢ Jα ⟩` (SatSets.lagda.md:1650) is stated on `DefOf.defSet` directly, so the limit case's defSet-membership is free one ω-block up — deleting R2p's 0.4–0.8k "identifying T_φ with Sat A ψ" row (r2probe:201).
2. The tower's limit clause must be γ(λ) := +ω (⋃_{β<λ} γ(β)), closed form γ(β) = ω·(β+1) — Devlin VI.2.3's own index J_{β+1} (dev6.txt:1583, :1552–1565). The ω-fixed-point naming mine (r2probe:160–167) is a symptom of the ⋃-spec, not a theorem-level obstruction.
3. The limit case can be stated at the element level: Lset λ = defSet ψ with ψ(x) := "∃f ∈ carrier [f an L-tower initial segment ∧ x ∈ ran f]" — no ordinal named at all, so the 0.2–0.5k λ-naming split and its `Sset γ ∩ Ord` dependency are deleted.
4. The Σ₁ normal form is best built fresh as that initial-segment formula (Devlin's φ, dev6.txt:1665–1672) on the retained SatSets/FOL.Coding engine, not as a rewriting-plus-equivalence of the 169,683-node `LsetGraph` — the equivalence chapter and the 1,688-constant transport certificate become unnecessary.
