The whole probe (all three arms, all cures, the controls) checks in 2.11 s
wall / 1.22 s user at 420 MB, against a 60-150 s wall at 6.4-10.5 GB for a

**Mechanism, measured.** The wall is the with-construction, not the search:
the named helper's codomain is written (`KeyShape`, or the equation-carrying
`Σ[ s ∈ KeyShape ] keyBack m s`), so per-branch checking never re-elaborates
the least-element search; the case analysis happens at the helper's variable
parameter. Sealing (`opaque`) makes the scrutinee a stuck constant, so even
the `with` form checks. The re-home avoids the case entirely by eliminating
the truncation into a proposition. The with-case on a *variable trace* being
instant is the same fact read from the other side.

**Answer.** Arm 2's abstract hang IS curable; the three cures all move the
wall from "does not terminate, RSS climbing past 6-10 GB" to sub-second
checks. W3 is a design problem with a known, measured first-formulation
cure, not an irreducible cost. The C-6 statement is not vacuous: the
positive control proves genuine comparison content at `sucV ∅` through the
cured decomposition, and the walled shapes are reproduced as negative
controls.

## 5. The prescription (first-formulation rules for the W3 build brief)

1. **Never `with` on a transparent least-witness term.** Every case analysis
   over key-derived data goes through a named helper with a written type
   carrying the equation: `go : (p : Producer) → memberKey α ordα m .fst ≡ p
   → Goal` (I-5, measured here 60 s+ to 0.91 s). The search is never run.
2. **Seal every consumer alias of the transparent least-witness machinery
   at its birth site** (R-38): `opaque keyAt = memberKey` with the spec
   inside, read through `opaque unfolding keyAt` read lemmas (R-36). Only
   the sealed alias may be cased or compared in consumers (measured: the
   `with` on the sealed key checks).
3. **State memberships and comparisons at the small index** (R-35, the
   restricted carrier): formula and adequacy statements live at
   `Member α`/`⟪ u ⟫` with the presentation abstract or the key stuck; never
   form a concrete tower index (`⟪ Sset (sucV ∅) ⟫`-level fibers) inside a
   checked obligation. Pin every presentation implicit explicitly (I-4).
4. **Take ordinals as variables, ordinality as a separate hypothesis**
   (R-37): `(δ : S) (ordδ : IsOrd δ)`, never a concrete pair `(∅ , ∅-ord)`
   in a compared position until the final instantiation behind a seal.
5. **Re-home truncated search results into proposition-valued goals**: the
   `member-trace`/`trace-exists` truncations eliminate only into propositions
   (`keyStage`, `keyVal`, `stageAbsorb`), never into a case split (the
   re-home pattern, T21-reused).
6. **Write every branch type that reaches a comparison or a search**:
   `PT.rec`/`PT.map` branches and `subst`-into-comparison arguments carry
   written types (I-5; measured again here, the `inr` under the self-image
   comparison needed its written type to check).
