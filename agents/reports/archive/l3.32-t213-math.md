## 2. The single best restructuring: split BelowLim into the generic machine and the first instance

**The mathematics.** The below-lim theorem is an induction over limit
ordinals; T185 proved the assembly (GREEN at 139 lines) and the ledger binds
the build to the induction shape. An induction's step content belongs at the
generic carrier: a variable limit `l`, the IH as a hypothesis, nothing
concrete in any statement (P-h, P-k). The current module does the opposite.
`BelowLim.Step` hard-wires the first limit: `W = Lset a₀` at BelowLim:879,
`mA₀`, `qA₀` at :915-918, numerals at :819-828, and the whole segment engine
:1091-1405 is stated at that one carrier. The generic content that already
exists (Story at :205-404) stops before the segments.

**Why the current decomposition is not the one the mathematics wants.** The
same content is already written twice inside one file: `chainValue` at :288
against `chainValueW` at :958, `story` at :248 against `storyW` at :929. The
general-beta band (T182 section 4) then prices the segments and the six
clauses AGAIN from per-instance comparables. A boundary in the wrong place is
forcing content to be restated on each side of it, which is exactly the
defect class the brief names.

**The right decomposition.** Three parts. (1) `BelowLim.Generic`: the story
record, the segment-extension lemma, the chain-union lemma, the bounded
family formula and its carve, all at a variable limit with the IH as a module
hypothesis. (2) `BelowLim.First`: the a₀ instance, re-derived as an
instantiation (the omega identification the first instance uses, Bridge:475,
stays here, quarantined where it is true). (3) The induction shell, which is
T185's frame. The carried-sequence build then funds (1) once and pays
instantiation at every limit.

**The D-10 check on the two generic lemmas, done here at the intended
generality.** Chain-union: pairhood and the zero clause pass to unions
directly; single-valuedness needs two pairs to sit in one chain member, which
the chain property gives; the exact domain of the union is the union of exact
domains, an ordinal when the domains are cofinal; the one-way successor
clause quantifies over PRESENT pairs, so both antecedent pairs sit in one
chain member and its own clause concludes; the limit clause is the same
present-pair argument. One-way clauses are exactly the shape that survives
chain unions; the two-way clause that would NOT survive is the one T65
already refuted out of the story. Segment-extension: adding the top pair
pr (sucV a) (step c) preserves each clause by the same case split T138
measured at the first limit (152 lines for four clauses). I checked truth,
not price; the price is the gate's job.

**What this does not claim.** T182's structural blocker (the second
instance's top pair needs the row's own residue at the previous limit) is not
dissolved by the split; it is dissolved by T185's IH, which the generic
module takes as a hypothesis. The split's saving is the per-instance segment
and clause terms, not the discharge term T185 already removed.
