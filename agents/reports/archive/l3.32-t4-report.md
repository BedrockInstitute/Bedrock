## 1. Question 1 — the reshaped Q induction vs Reduce's four hypotheses

**The reshaped statement.** R2′ replaces `Matching` and `Reduce`'s `Joint` with the existential-in-γ statement (p2-fork-recon.md:203-207), carried over the index tower γ(0)=ω, γ(β+1)=+ω γ(β), γ(λ)=⋃, closed form γ(β)=ω·(β+1) (p2-fork-recon.md:259; l3.31-r2lever-report.md:7):

**Fate of Reduce's four current hypotheses (Bridge.lagda.md:687-696):**

1. `defStage∈J` (Bridge.lagda.md:688-689) — **DEAD as an obligation; superseded, not met, by `BlockPowLim`.** Both state "one Def stage stays a rud member", but `defStage∈J` is the same-index form (`Lset (sucV ζ) ∈ Sset γ` at the caller's γ) and `BlockPowLim` is the one-block-up form (`𝒟ₒ (Lset ζ) ∈ Sset (+ω δ)` with δ a limit). The same-index form is **false**: at ζ=ω, γ=ω·2 its premises hold and its conclusion `L_{ω+1} ∈ S_{ω·2}` is refuted (p2-fork-recon.md:32-33, :120-138). `BlockPowLim` is precisely D-10's recorded correction (l3.31-p1-report.md:208-212): Devlin VI.2.3's successor step, one hypothesis (`SatRelation`) away. The two do not meet; `BlockPowLim` supersedes `defStage∈J` entirely.
2. `stepSet∈L` (Bridge.lagda.md:690-693) — **NOT a Q obligation.** Q is L-into-S; `stepSet∈L` is S-into-L ("one rud step is a definable set over a constructible stage", Bridge.lagda.md:648). It survives only as the retained `p4` half's supplier (Bridge.lagda.md:809).
3. `values∈L` (Bridge.lagda.md:694-695) — same: not a Q obligation; survives only for `p4` (Bridge.lagda.md:800).
4. `slot∈L` (Bridge.lagda.md:696) — same: `p4`'s successor needs it (Bridge.lagda.md:773); at the trunk it is discharged by `slot-empty` (SatTable.lagda.md:418-419).

**Do `values∈L`/`stepSet∈L` meet `BlockPowLim`?** No, and neither supersedes the other: they live on opposite sides of the bridge. `values∈L`/`stepSet∈L` say rud-step values are definable over L-stages (StepInL.lagda.md:370, :2419); `BlockPowLim` says the Def-power of an L-stage lands one ω-block up in the S tower. Gate 6's claim "stepSet∈L/values∈L leave Reduce's telescope" (l3.31-lt-report.md:40) is true of the *reshaped induction*; it says nothing about the retained `p4` half, which consumes those exact two theorems.
