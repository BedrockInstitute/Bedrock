### 2a. The target at the intended generality

The statement is `Sset β ∈ Lset γ` for limit `β < γ`, γ a limit. The classical
content is Devlin VI.2.5's P(α): `S_v ∈ L_{ωα}` and `(Sτ | τ ≤ v) ∈ L_{ωα}`
for `v < ωα` (`_build/literature/dev6.txt:1725-1760`), with the sequence
uniformly Σ₁-definable over `L_{ωα}`. At a limit β, `β = ω·α` for some α
(every limit ordinal is a multiple of ω), so `S_β = J_α = L_{ω·α} = L_β`
classically (`J_{α+1} = S_{ωα+ω}`, `dev6.txt:1565`), and `S_β ∈ L_{β+1} ⊆
L_γ` because `β + 1 < γ` for a limit γ above β. The tree-level facts the
instances ride:

- `β = ω`, `γ = ω·2`: `Sset ω = ⋃_{n<ω} Sset (sucV n)` (`Sset-union-*`,
  `Bridge:1012-1028`); every `Sset (sucV n)` is finite (`sTally`,
  `Finite:767`) and every finite set is in `Lset ω` (the L-side finite-set
  closure, the one small piece the gate must build; its mirror `Lsetω⊆Ssetω`
  is delivered at `HF:654`); the family is definable over `Lset ω` (the
  S-story at ω, the gate's build); the family lands in `Lset (sucV ω)` (the
  L-side defSet landing, §3); `sucV ω < ω·2` (`limit-succ-mem`,
  `Hierarchy:455`); so `Sset ω ∈ Lset (ω·2)`. TRUE with a constructible route.
- `β = ω·2`, `γ = ω·3`: `Sset (ω·2) = ⋃_{δ<ω·2} Sset (sucV δ)`; members enter
  `Lset (ω·2)` at stages below `ω·2` (the entry induction of §3, each S-step
  costing finitely many L-stages through `stepSet∈L`); the family is definable
  over `Lset (ω·2)` by the story at scale `ω·2` (inductively, §3); the landing
  `Lset (sucV (ω·2)) ⊆ Lset (ω·3)` by the same slack. TRUE.
- The recorded false boundary beside the statement is T45's `S_3 ∉ L_4`
  (`_build/l3.32-t45-report.md` §1): a successor index, outside `below-lim`'s
  domain (β is a limit), recorded as a boundary, not a refutation. The naive
  "one level up" form `S_β ∈ L_{β+1}` is TRUE at limit β (S_β = L_β ∈ L_{β+1})
  and is in fact the inner landing of the corrected route; T45's false "one
  level up" example was the finite-successor case, which is exactly where the
  boundary lives.

### 2b. The route, and where T45's recorded route is wrong

T45 and T52 recorded the route as: the family `W = {Sset (sucV δ) : δ < β}` in
the target stage, `W ∈ Lset γ`, then `Sset β = ⋃ W ∈ Lset γ`. Two defects
against the intended generality:

1. **The family a carve over the target stage actually produces is not T45's
   W, and it lands one level up.** The honest-segment story's range read
   collects the VALUES of honest segments with domain `δ₀ < γ`: the value
   chain (zero clause + one-way successor clause + exact domain) forces
   `x ∈ ran f → x = Sset ξ` for `ξ ∈ δ₀`, and `ξ` ranges over ALL ordinals
   below `δ₀`, including limit indices. So the carve over the carrier `Lset γ`
   produces `{Sset ξ : ξ < γ}`, a strict superset of T45's W (which names only
   successor-index values), and its union would be `Sset γ`, which is not in
   `Lset γ` by rank. More sharply: `defSet (Lset γ) ψ ∈ Lset (sucV γ)` always
   (a definable subset of a stage is in the definable power, `𝒟ₒ-intro`
   `Constructible:301` + `Lset-suc` `Basic:196`), and `Lset (sucV γ)` is not
   below the target `Lset γ`. The carve-over-the-target route cannot land at
   the exact target.
2. **The family-collection route is not needed.** The union route's real
   content is `⋃ {Sset ξ : ξ < β} = Sset β`, and the corrected carve below
   delivers that union directly; the intermediate object is the full family
   `{Sset ξ : ξ < β}`, not T45's W.

The corrected route: **carve the family `{Sset ξ : ξ < β}` over the SMALLER
stage `Lset β`** (not the target stage), land at `Lset (sucV β)` by the L-side
defSet landing, and ride the delivered +1 slack `sucV β < γ`
(`limit-succ-mem`, `Hierarchy:455`) into `Lset γ`. The members `Sset ξ` and
the honest-segment witnesses sit in `Lset β` by the induction at smaller
indices (each S-step over L costs finitely many stages through `stepSet∈L`, so
`Sset ξ` enters `Lset β` for `ξ < β` by limit closure; the segments `f_ν` for
`ν < β` enter at `ν` plus a finite offset, also below `β`). The strengthened
bound is FREE on the L-side: the story's `exactDom` yields a domain `δ₀ ∈ Lset
β` with `IsOrd δ₀`, and `ord∈Lset→∈` (`Stages:265`) gives `δ₀ < β`, exactly
the discharge Q-lim's J-carrier could not get without a 100-200-line lemma.
This is the S-side's structural advantage over Q-lim: the target has one whole
successor of slack (`β + 1 < γ`), where Q-lim's landing was exact
(`Sset (γ l)`).

coherent alternative: the induction needs the story at every scale, so the
