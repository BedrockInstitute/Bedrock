# [L3.32-T130] Tower-graph crossing: can the rud-side story serve the two Condensation crossings?

**Status: COMPLETE.** Read-only recon; no `src/` write, no probe, no Agda
run, no git. Deliverable: this report only.

## 1. Verdict

**NO.** The rud-side route as hypothesized cannot serve `L.Condensation`'s
two crossings, and the decisive reason is the index question plus the tower
mismatch, in that order:

1. **The rud side delivers per-step and per-family content at a stage
   carrier, not a two-slot (value, index) formula at the class carrier.**
   `StepStory.succForm` is a formula whose free slots are (story set,
   carrier point) with the successor relation inside bound quantifiers
   (`src/L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md:408-410`); `LevelSigma.succValForm` is
   (witness, read member) (`src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:318-319`); the
   crossing needs `Φ : Formula Sʟ 2` whose slot 0 is the value and slot 1
   is the index, satisfied at the ambient reading (`AbsL.⊨ᵛ`) and the inner
   reading (`AbsL.⊨ᵐ`), as `LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero)` is
   (`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:885,890-891,895,902`). The two-slot wiring
   (`∃ f. story f ∧ pr b v ∈ f`) plus its class-level decode is new content
   that does not exist anywhere on the rud side.
2. **The story content the wiring would have to carry at the class level is
   the L-tower successor, and that is the internalized definable-powerset
   step, which the rud side does not have.** `StepStory`'s successor clause
   concludes `b ≡ step c`, the *S-tower* step (sixteen-operation closure),
   not the L-tower step `𝒟ₒ`; `LevelSigma`'s `powRel` is the
   carrier-internal powerset, which equals the definable-powerset relation
   only at a rud carrier, never at the class `L` (`src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:226-235`);
   and the delivered/probed chain concludes `x ≡ Sset a`, not
   `x ≡ Lset a` (`src/ProbeT127.agda`, `chainValue`; T127 report section 2).
   The S/L per-level identification is not a delivered theorem, and the
   bridge records a naive per-level statement as **false** (Devlin VI.2.4,
   `src/L/Rud/DefInJ.lagda.md:14-16`).
3. **The languages differ by more than a rename.** `LsetGraphAt` is
   `Formula Sʟ n` (constants are L-sets, and the cone genuinely contains
   `con (numeralL k)` code tags, `src/L/Coding/Model.lagda.md:586`);
   the rud-side formulas are `Formula ⟪ C ⟫ n` for a stage `C`. The class is
   not a stage, so the kit's carrier-generic decodes, proved once per stage,
   cannot instantiate at the class; the formulas can be mechanically
   re-indexed (they are slot-only) but every decode must be re-proved at the
   class structure.

**D-10, applied.** The hypothesis's target, priced before any route could be
built: "the condensation's two crossings can be served by `StepStory` plus
the limit clause plus the carve." The target is false at the intended
generality for the reasons in section 3 (the index question) and section 4
(the tower mismatch). The corrected target, if the owner wants the rud-side
route at all, is: "the condensation's determination theorem can reuse the
*shape* of the general-scale chain/limit-clause content, provided the
class-level two-slot description with the internalized successor is built
separately." That corrected target is priced in section 4.2 and is not
cheaper than T129's ideal form.

A clean no is the deliverable here: it converts the open question into a
decided one, and it removes the "below-lim spend pays twice" reading of arm B.

## 2. What `L.Condensation` actually requires of `LsetGraphAt` and `Lset-only`

The two names are consumed at exactly five sites, all in the crossing's
ambient-form and crossing sections:

| Site | Use |
|---|---|
| `src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:61-62` | the two imports, `L.Coding.Sequence.LsetGraphAt` and `L.Hierarchy.Lset-only`, both in the D18 retirement set (ledger rows: `prefix = src/L/Coding/`, `path = src/L/Hierarchy.lagda.md`) |
| `:885` | `AmbientOnly = (v b : S) → IsOrd b → ⟨ (v ∷ b ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero) ⟩ → v ≡ Lset b` |
| `:890-891` | `TransferL`, the ambient reading (`AbsL.⊨ᵛ`, env over `S`) implies the inner reading (`AbsL.⊨ᵐ`, env over `Sʟ`) |
| `:895` | `ValueIsL`, ambient satisfaction implies `isL v` |
| `:902` | `ambientOnly-from tl vl v b ob h = Lset-only zero (suc zero) (vL ∷ bL ∷ []) (tl vL bL h) ob` |

The crossing's generic machinery additionally consumes the formula as an
object of relabelling: `Transport` takes `Φ : Formula Sʟ 2` with a
`BoundedFo InM Φ` certificate, relabels it to `Sᴹ` via `Down.liftFo`, and
proves the ambient agreement (`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:280-289`). For
the delivered formula the certificate is not vacuous: the cone contains
`con (numeralL k)` constants (`src/L/Coding/Model.lagda.md:586`), so the
code numerals must each be shown in `M`.

The real requirement, distilled from the use sites:

1. **A closed two-slot object-level formula at the class carrier**
   (`Formula Sʟ 2`), with the value in slot 0 and the ordinal index in slot
   1, whose satisfaction at both readings of the absoluteness pair means "the
   value is the L-stage at the index". It is used as the specific instance of
   the crossing's statements and as the object of the class-carrier
   equivalence with the Levy story.
2. **The inner determination theorem** (`Lset-only`): inner satisfaction at
   an ordinal index implies the value equals `Lset` of the index
   (`src/L/Hierarchy.lagda.md:332-347`). This is the only genuinely deep
   content of the two crossings: it is the class-level induction, over the
   ordinals, that the description determines the tower.
3. The formula's constants must be relabelable to `M` (the `BoundedFo InM`
   certificate), and the crossing's own obligations (`TransferL`,
   `ValueIsL`, `TransferM`, the equivalence with the Levy story, the limit
   case) are stated generically over `Φ` and are not part of the two names'
   supply (they are the `W1p` row, 700-1720 naive, ledger.toml:160).

What the consumer does **not** require of the two names: the full
approximation machinery as such, the twelve satisfaction clauses, or the
code/table/slot/sound/unique cluster. It requires the formula and the
determination; the cone is what those two bottom out in (T129 section 1).

**One finding that reframes the hypothesis.** The crossing chapter itself
already carries a parameter-free class-level level story with a limit clause:
`levelStory : Formula (⊥*) 2` with the six clauses (pair, single, zero, dom,
limit, range), Δ₀-certified, Def-step entry collapsed to `⊤̇`
(`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:565-603`); the set-carrier and class-carrier
embeddings `σᴹ`/`σL` (`:770-771`, `:825-826`) with delivered `TransferM σᴹ`
(`level-transfer`, `:788-789`) and delivered both-direction transfers at `L`
(`level-transfer-up`/`level-transfer-down`, `:847-855`). So the "story at
the class carrier" that the hypothesis wants the rud side to provide already
exists inside the consumer, in Levy form, without any `L.Coding` import. What
it does not supply is (a) the two-slot (value, index) shape (its free slots
are witness and read member), and (b) the successor content (the Def-step
entry is `⊤̇`, `:594-603`; the successor determination is exactly what the
coding cone's `StepAt`/`DefAt` carry). This is why the two crossings are not
wiring: the formula part and the determination part are the internalized
successor step, which neither the rud side nor the chapter's own story
contains.

## 3. The shape comparison and the index question

### 3.1 Same syntax family, two instances, no delivered translation

`FOL.Syntax.Formula K n` is indexed by the constant type `K`
(`src/FOL/Semantics.lagda.md`, `At K ι`: `_⊨_ : ∀ {n} → S ^ n → Formula K n
→ Ω`; the environment is over the *structure's* carrier, the formula over
its *constants*). Consequently:

- `LsetGraphAt : ∀ {n} → Fin n → Fin n → Formula S n` at
  `src/L/Coding/Sequence.lagda.md:361-362` is over the `S` of the class
  structure `𝒮ʟ = 𝒮ᵥ ↾ isL`, whose carrier is `Sʟ = Σ x ∈ S, isL x`
  (`src/L/Constructible.lagda.md:410-411`; `src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:87-88`).
  Its two free slots are the value `y` and the index `x`; the approximation
  `f` is bound by the formula's one existential. Its cone contains constants
  (`con (numeralL k)`, `src/L/Coding/Model.lagda.md:586`).
- `StepStory.succForm : Formula ⟪ C ⟫ 2` at
  `src/L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md:408-410` is over the member type of a
  concrete stage `C : V ℓ` (the `Clause` telescope at `:79`). Its free slots
  are the story set and the carrier point `ι x`; the successor relation sits
  inside three bound quantifiers.
- `LevelSigma.succValForm : Formula ⟪ u ⟫ 2` at
  `src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:318-319` is over a rud carrier `u`, slots
  (witness, read member).

The class `L` is not a stage: there is no `C : V ℓ` with `⟪ C ⟫ ≡ Sʟ`. The
kit's decodes are proved once, inside modules parameterized by the stage and
its transitivity (`src/L/LevelKit.lagda.md:86`; `src/L/PairAtoms.lagda.md`
`PairKit`), and they speak about the *stage-restricted* satisfaction
(`⊨ᵐ` at `𝒮ᵥ ↾ C`). Re-indexing the slot-only formula text from `⟪ C ⟫` to
`Sʟ` is mechanically trivial (no `con` leaves to translate), but the
satisfaction semantics and every decode proof are structure-specific: the
quantifiers range over the stage's members in one reading and over all L-sets
in the other. There is no delivered translation `Formula ⟪ C ⟫ n → Formula
Sʟ n` carrying adequacy, and writing one is re-proving the decodes at the
class, not renaming.

### 3.2 The index question: per-step relation only, and the wrong carrier's tower

`LsetGraphAt y x` is a **two-slot graph**: slot `y` is the value, slot `x`
is the index, and the formula's satisfiability at `(v, b)` with `IsOrd b`
determines `v ≡ Lset b` (`src/L/Hierarchy.lagda.md:333-347`). The rud side
delivers:

- `StepStory.succClause`: a per-step relation, `pr a c ∈ f → pr (sucV a) b
  ∈ f → b ≡ step c` (`src/L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md:357-359`), with the step
  atom reading membership in the sixteen-operation closure. This constrains
  one successor value of one table; it is not a two-slot graph.
- The family story at a carrier (T127's `story`/`storyForm` at
  `src/ProbeT127.agda:93-107`): a whole-table predicate, slots (witness,
  member).
- The chain at the first limit (`chainValue`,
  `src/ProbeT127.agda:125`): a *meta-level* theorem, `story f → a ∈ ω →
  pr a x ∈ f → x ≡ Sset a`. It concludes the **S-tower**, at `ω` only.

To get a two-slot (value, index) formula from the story one must add
`∃ f. story f ∧ pr b v ∈ f` with a class-level decode, and at the class level
the successor content of that formula must say "v is the definable-powerset
step of the predecessor". That content is not expressible by the bounded
atoms the kit has (`prAt`, `sucAt`, `powAt`, `isOrdAt`, `limAt`): the
definable-powerset relation is not first-order expressible without
internalizing satisfaction (coding formulas as sets and stating the object
satisfaction predicate), which is precisely the retiring cone (T129 sections
1.1-1.2). The level-sigma trick of reading the successor through the
carrier-internal powerset works only at a carrier closed under definable
subsets; the class `L` is not such a carrier (a subset of `Lset ζ` lying in
`L` is not definably so, which is the substance of the false per-level
statement the bridge records, `src/L/Rud/DefInJ.lagda.md:14-16`).

So the answer to the brief's index question is: **the rud side gives only a
per-step relation (inside a clause) and a per-family story (whole table),
both at a stage carrier; it does not give a two-slot graph at the class
carrier, and the wiring that would turn it into one is not free, because the
successor content it must carry is the internalization cone.**

## 4. `Lset-only` on the rud side, and the price

### 4.1 The ordinal hypothesis is available; the determination is not

The brief asks whether the content of `Lset-only`, "the second argument is
an ordinal", is available on the rud side. Split it:

- The **ordinal hypothesis** itself is a survivor: `ord∈Lset→∈` at
  `src/L/Ordinal/Stages.lagda.md:265` (an ordinal in `Lset α` is a member of
  `α`), which T125 used for free. `IsOrd`, `isLimit`, `ord-tri`, `mem-ord`
  all live in surviving `L.Ordinal`/`L.Rud.OrdArith`.
- The **determination content** is the theorem `satisfaction at (v, b) +
  IsOrd b → v ≡ Lset b`. Its delivered proof is a class-level
  `∈-induction` over the ordinals (`approx-val`, `src/L/Hierarchy.lagda.md:273-304`,
  using `∈-induction` from `V.Hierarchy`), with the step read from the
  object-level `StepAt` back through `DefAt`'s adequacy, and completeness
  from `ApproxAt-value` (`Lset-only`, `:332-347`). Nothing on the rud side
  proves the class-level statement:
  - the delivered/probed chain concludes `x ≡ Sset a` (the S-tower) at `ω`
    only (`src/ProbeT127.agda:125`; T127 section 2);
  - the general-scale chain needs the limit clause (value at a limit index =
    union of values below) and the honest segments/carve, which exist only
    as probes: the limit clause is expressible and measured at 353 content
    lines in `src/ProbeT128.agda` (T128 sections 3-5), and the general
    carve/limit-index work is a priced *hypothesis* (T128 section 5: 80-120
    naive, x3 calibrated), not built;
  - the step from "story value = S-stage" to "story value = L-stage" needs
    the S/L identification, which is not delivered and whose naive per-level
    form is recorded false (`src/L/Rud/DefInJ.lagda.md:14-16`). The
    surviving L-side successor fact is `Lsuc≡Def : Lset (sucV ζ) ≡ 𝒟ₒ (Lset
    ζ)` (`DefInJ:79-80`), which is the L-tower's own equation and does not
    identify the towers.

### 4.2 The cheapest route that does exist, against T129's 1.1-1.5k

T129's ideal form (fresh write of only what Condensation needs) remains the
floor: formula definitions 250-350 + DefAt and graph adequacy 700-900 + the
step/approx/`Lset-only` region ~130 = **1.1-1.5k naive, 1.4-2.0k calibrated**
(T129 section 2). The rud-side content does not displace any of it:

- the class-level two-slot description still needs the internalized
  successor (the 700-900 adequacy piece);
- the determination still needs a class-level induction (the ~130 region);
- the rud side's genuinely overlapping pieces are the limit clause (353
  measured content lines, T128) and the general chain/carve shape (80-120
  priced hypothesis) - and those are themselves unbuilt in any master
  (probe-only at `ω`), so "wiring against work the campaign is funding
  anyway" is not available today and, when built, replaces at most the ~130
  region and part of the adequacy, not the internalization.

If the owner nevertheless wants the rud-side route pursued, the honest
price is the ideal form **plus** the rud-side general build (limit clause
master ~353 x1.3 = ~460, carve hypothesis 80-120 x3 = 240-360) minus the
overlap (~130): roughly **1.5-1.9k naive / 2.0-2.6k calibrated**, with the
false-premise risk of the S/L identification on top. That is dominated by
the ideal form and should not be chosen.

The crossing's own `σL` does deliver one real saving, but it is on the
`W1p` side, not the two names' side: if the face re-based on `σL`, both
`TransferL` and `TransferM` for the story are already delivered
(`level-transfer`, `level-transfer-down/up`,
`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:788-789,847-855`), which would shrink the
W1p row's transfer obligations. It does not remove the two crossings'
supply: `ValueIsL`, the determination, and the (value, index) description
remain.

## 5. The `defSet` re-statement option

`L.Definability.defSet : Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1 → S` is a host-level operation:
`defSet φ = sett (Σ[ m ∈ ⟪ A ⟫ ] ⟨ smallSat φ m ⟩) ...` with `smallSat`
compressing the inner satisfaction one universe down
(`src/L/Definability.lagda.md:107-111`, `:96-103`). `DefAt` is an object-level
formula in `Formula Sʟ n` with an adequacy theorem (`DefAt-in`/`DefAt-out`,
`src/L/Coding/Powerset.lagda.md:645-690`). The condensation needs the object
level because absoluteness is two instantiations of one satisfaction over one
formula: `AbsL.⊨ᵛ` and `AbsL.⊨ᵐ` are both readings of the same
`Formula Sʟ 2` object (`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:884-902`). A meta-level
`sett` has no satisfaction and no second reading; it cannot be the object of
`TransferL`, `ValueIsL`, or the relabeling `Down.liftFo`.

Is the gap bridgeable? In principle yes, in the only way such gaps are
bridged: write an object-level formula meaning "b is the definable powerset
of a", which requires coding formulas as sets and internalizing the
satisfaction predicate - that is the content of the retiring cone. There is
no shorter route: the object language's quantifiers range over sets, not
over the host-level `Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1` index family, so the "syntax as index
set" trick that makes `defSet` cheap at the meta level
(`src/L/Definability.lagda.md:96-111`) is unavailable inside an object
formula. **The gap is the whole cost.** The `defSet` re-statement is not a
cheaper alternative to T129's ideal form; it is the natural shape of that
ideal form's 700-900-line adequacy piece (re-state `DefAt`'s semantics
against the surviving `defSet`/`defSet-mem` instead of porting the
twelve-clause encoding), and it stays inside the 1.1-1.5k band.

## 7. Contradictions with this brief

1. **The brief's cluster size, 6,441, does not reproduce.** T129 measured
   6,254 in-fence lines for the retiring `L.Coding` cluster (19 masters
   minus Base) and 6,381 with the `L.Hierarchy` slice (`_build/l3.32-t129-report.md:151-153`,
   `:260`). The brief's 6,441 matches neither; the measured figures are the
   ledger-consistent ones.
2. **`StepStory` is not a story of the L-tower.** The brief reads "the
   campaign has spent two days building an object-level account of the very
   same tower on the RUD side." The object-level account is of the **S-tower**
   (rud step, `succClause` concluding `b ≡ step c`,
   `src/L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md:357-359`), and the S/L identification is
   not delivered, with a naive per-level form recorded false
   (`src/L/Rud/DefInJ.lagda.md:14-16`). The L-tower's per-scale story is
   `LevelSigma`'s (`powRel` successor, `src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:226-235`),
   which is also per-scale and carrier-relative, not class-level.
3. **The "below-lim spend pays twice" claim fails.** The funded campaign
   content (StepStory at any carrier, the limit clause, the carve) is
   per-scale and, where built, first-limit-only (probes). The condensation
   needs one class-level formula and one class-level determination; the
   overlap is the determination-induction shape plus the limit clause, a
   minority of the price, and the general-scale pieces are themselves
   unbuilt in any master.
4. **The hypothesis under-states what the consumer already has.** The
   condensation chapter itself carries a parameter-free class-level story
   with the limit clause, Δ₀-certified, and delivered transfers for `σᴹ`/`σL`
   (`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:565-609,770-789,825-855`). The two names'
   supply is not the story; it is the (value, index) description and the
   determination, whose successor content is the internalization cone. A
   route that re-supplies only the story would leave the crossing unable to
   determine successor values.
5. **The crossing's Def-step collapse is not a successor clause.** The
   chapter's `Cl = ⊤̇` ("every set is definable in itself", `defSet⊤≡A`,
   `src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:594-603`) records that the Def-step entry is
   trivially true as an object formula; the forward tower-facing read is
   carried separately in the decodes. This is the same convention as the
   kit's (`src/L/LevelKit.lagda.md:189`, `Cl = ⊤̇`), and it means neither the
   kit story nor the chapter's story constrains successor values by itself.
