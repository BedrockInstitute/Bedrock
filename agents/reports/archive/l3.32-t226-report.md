of gate margin". The measurement says the opposite at the current sites: the

per definition, and block A writes a 15-17 line clause list and five aliases

## 3. The generalization's shape, its home, and the carrier's measured cost

The assembly lives in `L.LevelKit`, inside `module LevelKit (u : V ℓ)
(utr : isTransV u)`, in the new section "The story assembly"
(`LevelKit:717-847`). The shape:

- `StoryClause` (`:745-750`): a right-nested `Σ` of the predicate
  `S → Type (ℓ-suc ℓ)`, the object formula `Formula ⟪ u ⟫ 2`, and the two
  decode directions at the witness environment `f ∷ ι x ∷ []`.
- `mkClause` (`:753-757`) and `clauseOk` (`:760-763`): the constructors,
  one for separate out/in lemmas, one for a delivered `-ok` lemma.
- `StoryClauses` (`:766-775`): `List.List StoryClause`, with the local
  operators `_∷₊_` and `end` for readable consumer lists.
- `Conj` (`:778-780`), `clProject` (`:783-785`), `ConjForm` (`:788-790`):
  the predicate fold, the predicate projection, and the formula fold.
- `conj-out` / `conj-in` (`:793-804`): the decode walk, both directions.
- `storyCl`, `storyForm`, `story-out`, `story-in`, `story-ok`
  (`:808-824`): the assembled story at a consumer's clause list.

Two measured constraints shaped the design. First, a record with a field of
type `Formula ⟪ u ⟫ 2` (or any `⟪ u ⟫`-typed field) hangs Agda 2.8.0's
elaborator: a minimal probe with only that field timed out at 25 s, while
the identical type as a plain definition checked instantly. The clause type
is therefore a nested `Σ`, the codebase's fast shape, at
`Type (ℓ-suc (ℓ-suc ℓ))` because the predicate component lives one universe
up. Second, the walk's empty-list case needs a universe-polymorphic unit
(`Unit.Unit*`); the cubical `Unit` at `Type ℓ-zero` fails the sort check.

The home argument: LevelKit is the only candidate every current and future
site already imports. LevelSigma (`module Kit = LevelKit u utr`, :131),
LevelFormula (`LevelFormula:92`), StepStory (`module Kit = LevelKit C Ctr;
open Kit public`, `StepStory:58-59`), and BelowLim (`module K = LevelKit C
Ctr; open K public`, `BelowLim:84-85`) all open the kit publicly, so the
assembly names arrive with zero new imports. StepStory additionally requires
the whole rud Step tower, and LevelFormula does not import StepStory at all;
hosting the assembly there would add a dependency that today does not exist.
The content is carrier-generic story machinery, exactly the kit's charter,
and T225's concurrent C3 build is landing the same class of content (the
bounded successor atom and the one-way clause) in the same file. The choice
is LevelKit.

The carrier's measured cost (the brief asks for it, not T213's estimate):

T213's "record intro 10-20 per consumer" is not what happens: because the

The prose changed by +2 lines. Both calibers: +72 naive measured,

## 5. How block A consumes it (spec for the reshape's brief)

Block A's "witness story at `Lset γ`" piece (T223 rows: witness story 90-110,
the six-clause story 70-150) becomes a clause list plus five aliases, about
15-17 lines. The Step module at `Lset γ` writes:

```agda
  storyWClauses : StoryClauses
  storyWClauses = mkClause KW.pairhood KW.pairForm KW.pairhood-out KW.pairhood-in ∷₊
                  mkClause KW.singleValued KW.singleForm KW.single-out KW.single-in ∷₊
                  mkClause KW.zeroClause KW.zeroForm KW.zero-out KW.zero-in ∷₊
                  mkClause KW.exactDom KW.exactDomForm KW.exactDom-out KW.exactDom-in ∷₊
                  clauseOk WCl.succClause WCl.succForm WCl.succ-ok ∷₊
                  clauseOk limitClause limitForm limit-out limit-in ∷₊ end

  storyW : S → Type (ℓ-suc ℓ)
  storyW = storyCl storyWClauses

  storyFormW : Formula ⟪ W ⟫ 2
  storyFormW = storyForm storyWClauses

  storyW-out : (f : KW.SM) (x : ⟪ W ⟫)
             → ⟨ (f ∷ KW.ι x ∷ []) KW.⊨ᵐ storyFormW ⟩ → storyW (fst f)
  storyW-out = story-out storyWClauses

  storyW-in : (f : KW.SM) (x : ⟪ W ⟫)
            → storyW (fst f) → ⟨ (f ∷ KW.ι x ∷ []) KW.⊨ᵐ storyFormW ⟩
  storyW-in = story-in storyWClauses

  storyW-ok : (f : KW.SM) (x : ⟪ W ⟫)
            → ⟨ (f ∷ KW.ι x ∷ []) KW.⊨ᵐ storyFormW ⟩ ⟷ storyW (fst f)
  storyW-ok = story-ok storyWClauses
```

The limit clause's formula and decode come from T128's gate (expressible
with a two-way decode) and exist in the level-formula shape already
(`LevelFormula:182-183`, :267+). Against today's hand assembly
(`BelowLim:929-957`, 24 in-fence lines) and against T223's 70-150 projection
for the six-clause piece, the list-plus-aliases form is the measured
consumer shape of this build: +2 lines over the current five-clause hand
assembly, and the six-clause variant adds only the sixth record.

The two BelowLim sites consume the generalization as follows, and neither is
edited here: `Step.storyW` is exactly the code above with its current
clauses; `Story.story` is the conjunction-only consumer, which uses `Conj`
over the six predicates until block A gives the limit clause its decode.

The block A brief must carry four obligations: (1) the aliases' types stay
byte-identical to today's `storyW`/`storyFormW`/`storyW-out/in/ok`;
(2) the alias specialization is timed at the bound-variable carrier (P-l),
with the LevelSigma +485 ms concrete-carrier figure as the worst case;
(3) the sixth clause's record uses `clauseOk limitClause limitForm
limit-out limit-in`, whose pieces block A supplies per T128's gate;
(4) no definition in the assembly re-proves clause content (P-n).
