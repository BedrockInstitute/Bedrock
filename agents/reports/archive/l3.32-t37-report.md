## 3. The rulings, confirmed

**Equinumerosity is the bijection form.** `eqFo a b = ∃̇ (bijFo zero (suc a)
(suc b))` ([CardinalPredicates.lagda.md:537](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md)):
there is a set `f` of Kuratowski ordered pairs relating `a` onto `b`,
single-valued, total, injective, surjective. No injection-in-each-direction
anywhere; `HostEq` is the truncation of "there is such an `f`" (:362). The
per-consumer Cantor-Bernstein obligation T18 ruled out never appears.

**Nothing is a GCH-specific shim.** The formulas are parameter-free and generic
in `K` and `n`, so the same `eqFo`/`cardFo`/`succCardFo` serve the W7 sentence,
fine structure's `|J_γ| = |γ|` with its Σ₁-cardinal strengthening, and
geology's internal `|·|`. The host notions `HostEq`/`IsCard`/`IsSuccCard` are
the classical definitions: equinumerosity by a bijection, cardinal as an
initial ordinal (`IsCard v = IsOrd v × ∀ y ∈ v, ¬ HostEq y v`, :565), successor
cardinal as the least cardinal above (`IsSuccCard`, :588). The classical
anchors are Jech 13.13's Π₁ "α is a cardinal" (jech13.txt:519-525) and 13.20's
"least cardinal in L greater than α" (jech13.txt:1128-1130); the internal
`cardFo` reads exactly as Jech's form, `Ord ∧ ∀ y ∈ v, ¬∃ f ...`
([CardinalPredicates.lagda.md:562](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md)).

## 4. What the later eras extend rather than rewrite

- **The ordered-pair substrate** (`sglFo`/`pairFo`/`oprFo`, Δ₀ with witnesses)
  is the reified "this is the Kuratowski pair of those" T18's §1 found absent
  in the tree; every later consumer that says "a set of ordered pairs" quotes
  it.
- **`eqFo` + `HostEq`** is the base of geology's internal `|·|` at a class
  carrier: "the least ordinal equinumerous to a" is stated on top of exactly
  these (T18 §5); the internal-cardinality function itself stays deferred.
- **`cardFo` + `IsCard`** is the base of fine structure's Σ₁-cardinal
  (surjection-shaped strengthening of the same initial-ordinal predicate, T18
  §5) and of the Jech 13.27(iii) transfer (a V-cardinal is an L-cardinal).
- **`succCardFo` + `IsSuccCard`** is the relation form Devlin's 5.5-5.6
  consume (dev2.txt:1373-1389); the aleph enumeration and general cardinal
  arithmetic remain deferred exactly as T18 named them.
- The Levy classification is left honest: the pair substrate is Δ₀ (its
  witnesses are delivered), while `bijFo`/`eqFo` carry unbounded component
  quantifiers by T18's shape, so no Δ₀ witness for the bijection is claimed;
  `cardFo`'s reading matches Jech 13.13's Π₁ classification conceptually.

## 5. The D-10 record: truth and expressibility of each predicate

D-10 (LESSONS.md:1271) ran first on each predicate; the targets and their
corrections:

1. **`Eq(x,y)`**: true and expressible at the intended generality: at `𝒮ᵥ`,
   satisfaction is `HostEq` on the nose (`eqFo-adequate`). The T18-wording
   target "satisfaction at `𝒮ʟ` equals the host notion" is FALSE as an iff: a
   bijection between constructible sets may exist only outside `L` (cardinal
   collapse, Jech 13.27, jech13.txt:1030-1040). Corrected target for the
   model layer, stated but deferred to the W7 proof layer (it needs the
   inner-semantics transfer of the five clauses, the `isL-trans` lifting of
   pair components):
   `eq-inner→outer : (a b : SL) → ⟨ (a ∷ b ∷ []) ⊨ʟ φ-eqS ⟩ → HostEq (fst a) (fst b)`,
   "a constructible bijection is a bijection", which is the one-way direction
   that holds.
2. **`Card(v)`**: true and expressible at `𝒮ᵥ` (`cardFo-adequate`). The
   model-layer correction, also deferred, is the Jech 13.27(iii) direction:
   `card-outer→inner : (v : SL) → IsCard (fst v) → ⟨ (v ∷ []) ⊨ʟ φ-cardS ⟩`,
   "a V-cardinal is an L-cardinal", which composes `eq-inner→outer` with the
   `ordFo` Δ₀ absoluteness. The reverse direction is false in general
   (collapse), so no iff is claimed at `𝒮ʟ`.
3. **`SuccCard(s,κ)`**: true and expressible at `𝒮ᵥ`
   (`succCardFo-adequate`). Neither direction transfers cleanly to `𝒮ʟ`
   (successor-cardinality is not absolute either way), so the model layer gets
   no transfer; the W7 proof works with the ambient certificate and the
   initial-ordinal facts Devlin's argument uses.

The correction is recorded here rather than hidden: the delivered layer's
certificates are the ambient full equivalences, and the model-level one-way
facts are precisely stated targets for the next layer, not silently dropped.
