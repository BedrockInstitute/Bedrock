so the satisfaction head is stuck at the check site, and the concrete exported

(`_build/l3.32-t96-report.md:112-114`); the tree check below confirms it. A
module signature change with zero consumers and zero instantiations costs
nothing. This is the cheapest unclaimed lever in the tree.

**Piece 3 (the ~54 s `Lset-only` application): YES, the position is liftable.**

the concrete-index application under a satisfaction head, which the telescope

**The dispatch's premise is confirmed in direction.** `[T96]` priced
restatements of the exported obligations and concluded "No export-preserving

## 2. Per piece: what is lifted, what the telescope holds, export impact, expected seconds

### Piece 1: the ~77 s environment conversion

**What is lifted.** The concrete formula `LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero)` out
of the conversion-check position. Today the conversion happens inside
`ambientOnly-from`'s body: `h : ⟨ (v ∷ b ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ LsetGraphAt {2} zero
(suc zero) ⟩` (the `AmbientOnly` premise, `src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:883-886`)
is checked against `tl`'s premise `⟨ (fst vL ∷ fst bL ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ
LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero) ⟩` (`TransferL`, `:888-891`). The two
environments are definitionally equal (the projections unfold), but the
conversion checker must whnf both satisfaction applications at the CONCRETE
formula, and that walk is the measured ~77 s (`_build/l3.32-t96-report.md:98-100`).

**What the telescope holds.** A generic helper whose body is checked once at a
variable formula:

```agda
module EnvProj (φ : Formula Sʟ 2) where
  proj : (u v : S) (wu : ⟨ isL u ⟩) (wv : ⟨ isL v ⟩)
       → ⟨ (u ∷ v ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ φ ⟩
       → ⟨ (fst (u , wu) ∷ fst (v , wv) ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ φ ⟩
  proj u v wu wv h = h
```

In the body, `φ` is a module parameter: `⊨ᵛ φ` is a stuck head, the checker
compares environments by projection reduction and congruence, and no concrete
satisfaction tree is ever normalized. The concrete use is
`EnvProj.proj (LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero)) v b (vl v b ob h) (ord-isL b ob) h`,
whose argument and result checks are identical-term matches (the formula
appears identically on both sides after instantiation), so the walk is not
re-paid at the call site either.

**Export impact.** None. `AmbientOnly` (`:883-886`), `TransferL` (`:888-891`),
`ValueIsL` (`:893-896`) and `ambientOnly-from` (`:900-902`) keep their exact
statements.

**Expected seconds.** The ~77 s leaves the module's profile. The mechanism is
the one `[T102]` measured at 34-48 ms per instantiation
(`_build/l3.32-t102-report.md:109`, the after row): identical-term checks at concrete
positions do not walk, and variable-position checks cannot walk. Unmeasured at
this site; the probe in section 5 prices it.

### Piece 2: the ~56 s `Crossing` piece

**What is lifted.** The concrete formula out of `Crossing`'s header and out of
the `Transport` instantiation inside it (`src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:911-922`).
`Transport` is already generic in `Φ` and has no profile rows
(`_build/l3.32-t96-report.md:63-65`); only the concrete instantiation inside
`crossOut-from` pays.

**What the telescope holds.** The formula and its boundedness certificate:

```agda
module Crossing (M : S) (Mtr : isTransV M)
                (Φ : Formula Sʟ 2) (h : BoundedFo (AtCarrier.InM M Mtr) Φ) where
  open AtCarrier M Mtr
  open Transport Φ h
  crossOut-from : TransferM lifted → AmbientOnly-Φ → CrossOut lifted
  crossOut-from tm ao v b ob hv =
    ao (fst v) (fst b) ob
      (subst ⟨_⟩ (amb-agree (fst v ∷ fst b ∷ [])) (tm v b hv))
```

where `AmbientOnly-Φ` is `AmbientOnly` with the concrete formula replaced by
`Φ`. (The statement must follow `Φ`: with `Φ` a module parameter, the `ao`
application's premise is `⟨ (fst v ∷ fst b ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ Φ ⟩`, which is not
definitionally the concrete formula.) The body is unchanged textually and
elaborates at neutral `Φ`, exactly like `Transport`'s own definitions, which
`[T96]` recorded as having no rows.

If the concrete crossing theorem must remain an elaborated definition, keep
the current concrete `Crossing` as a one-line wrapper around the generic
module: `crossOut-from tm ao v b ob hv = CrossingΦ.crossOut-from
(LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero)) h tm ao v b ob hv`. The instantiation's
checks are identical-term matches (`lifted`, `TransferM`, `AmbientOnly-Φ` at
the concrete formula are each the same term on both sides).

**Export impact.** The parameterized form changes `Crossing`'s header
(`:911-914`) and `crossOut-from`'s statement (`:918-922`); the wrapper form
changes neither. Either way, zero tree impact: nothing outside the file
references any of these names (section 4).

**Expected seconds.** Parameterized and uninstantiated, the 54.4 s row
vanishes from the module's profile (the concrete application is never
elaborated). Wrapped, the row drops to the instantiation cost, expected ms by
the identical-term mechanism. Unmeasured; the probe measures both variants.

### Piece 3: the ~54 s `Lset-only` application

**What is lifted.** The concrete indices `zero (suc zero)` out of the
`Lset-only` application. `Lset-only` is already generic in `w b : Fin n` and
`γ : S ^ n` (`src/L/Hierarchy.lagda.md:332-345`); the cost is paid at its
instantiation at the concrete indices, where `LsetGraphAt w b` is a concrete
formula under the satisfaction head. `[T96]` measured this cleanly: `loLset3`,
the delivered application with FREE-VARIABLE environment `vL bL`, still costs
54,134 ms (`/tmp/t96/ProbeT96Decomp.lagda.md:912-915`), so the environment is
not the driver; the concrete indices are (its own I-4/I-5 classification,
`_build/l3.32-t96-report.md:167`).

**What the telescope holds.** `w b`, the transfer and the value witnesses, so
the application sits at variable indices:

```agda
module AmbientOnlyFrom (w b : Fin 2)
                       (tl : (u v : Sʟ)
                           → ⟨ (fst u ∷ fst v ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ LsetGraphAt w b ⟩
                           → ⟨ (u ∷ v ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵐ LsetGraphAt w b ⟩)
                       (vl : (u v : S) → IsOrd v
                           → ⟨ (u ∷ v ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ LsetGraphAt w b ⟩ → ⟨ isL u ⟩) where
  from : (u v : Sʟ) → ⟨ (fst u ∷ fst v ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ LsetGraphAt w b ⟩
       → IsOrd (fst (lookup b (u ∷ v ∷ [])))
       → fst (lookup w (u ∷ v ∷ [])) ≡ Lset (fst (lookup b (u ∷ v ∷ [])))
  from u v h ob = Lset-only w b (u ∷ v ∷ []) (tl u v h) ob
```

Inside, `LsetGraphAt w b` unfolds to the fixed formula tree with neutral
`suc w`/`suc b` leaves (`StepAt v b f = extAt v (∃̇ (∃̇ (∃̇ (StepBody b f))))`,
`src/L/Coding/Sequence.lagda.md:119-120`; `ApproxAt f a = domAt f a ∧̇ ...`,
`:281-284`), so the satisfaction heads at every check position are stuck and
the checker never normalizes a concrete leaf. All body checks are
identical-term matches. The concrete exported statement is recovered by
instantiating at `zero (suc zero)` with the where-block pairs written inline,
so the concrete checks reduce to lookups and projections plus identical-term
matches (`fst (lookup zero ((v , ...) ∷ (b , ...) ∷ []))` reduces to `v`, and
`Lset (fst (lookup (suc zero) ...))` reduces to `Lset b`).

**Export impact.** None: `ambientOnly-from`'s statement (`:900-902`) and the
three type synonyms (`:883-896`) are byte-identical.

**Expected seconds.** The 54 s leaves the profile if the stuck-leaf mechanism
holds; the concrete instantiation is expected ms. Unmeasured; the probe prices
it. This is the piece where the transplant law bites hardest: `[T96]`'s
"no cheaper body of that type exists" (`_build/l3.32-t96-report.md:106-109`) is
true as a statement about proof content, but the measured 54 s is an
application-position cost at concrete indices, and `[T102]`'s lesson is that
such costs sit in the application, not the content.

The honest negative is not delivered here: the analysis says the levers exist,
and the measurements that would decide the seconds are exactly what `[T96]`
never ran. If the probe goes green, up to the full 182.9 s of hot rows
(128.5 s `ambientOnly-from` plus 54.4 s `crossOut-from`,

exported statement, against a measured floor of about 19 s
(`_build/l3.32-t96-report.md:133`). The 133 s the brief names is the
conservative two-piece count; the honest read of `[T96]`'s own decomposition
is that all three pieces are application-position costs, so the reachable
total is the full 183 s, subject to the probe.

If the probe goes red (the seconds travel to the instantiation or to the
generic rows), the priced refusal stands and this report's section 2 becomes
the record of the failed transplant, exactly the shape P-l's transplant table
asks for (`dev/LESSONS.md:1990-1997`). Note that the one prior transplant to
Condensation, the abstract-carrier discipline, moved nothing (203.3 s against
203.5 s, `dev/LESSONS.md:1994`); that transplant abstracted the CARRIER,
where `[T96]`'s decomposition showed the cost is not, whereas the design here
abstracts where the decomposition located the cost: the concrete-formula
satisfaction positions. That is the same distinction between "abstract by
analogy" and "abstract where the diagnosis says the cost is" that separated
`[T88]`'s heap wall from `[T99]`'s 1.28 s (`dev/LESSONS.md:1996-1997`).

The verdict to record for the ledger: `[T96]`'s "no export-preserving
combination exists" is withdrawn pending the probe; the module is not refused
