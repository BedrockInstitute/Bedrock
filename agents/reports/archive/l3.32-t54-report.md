## 3. The two predicted resisters

**Neither resisted bounding without a new carrier fact.**

1. **pairForm (unbounded pair existentials).** The delivered clause is
   `forall-in (var zero) (exists (exists (prAt ...)))`
   ([LevelFormula.lagda.md:194-196]) with two unbounded existentials over the
   pair components. The rewrite `pairForm'` ([ProbeLevy.agda:139-146])
   replaces "exists a b, z = pr a b" with the structural pair predicate
   `kpair` ([ProbeLevy.agda:40-53]): z has a singleton member x, every other
   member is a two-element set containing x's element, and z has at most two
   members. This is the classical Delta-0 pair characterization, it handles
   the degenerate `pr a a = {{a}}` (the zero clause's shape), and every
   quantifier is bounded by z itself: no union, no transitivity, no carrier
   fact. Certified in 4 local lines.
2. **domForm (unbounded ordinal witness).** The delivered clause is
   `exists (isOrdAt zero and ...)` ([LevelFormula.lagda.md:230-237]) with an
   unbounded existential over the ordinal delta. The rewrite `domForm'`
   ([ProbeLevy.agda:148-168]) never names delta: it reads "the set of first
   components of the pairs of f is an ordinal" as two bounded clauses, D
   transitive (a first component's members are first components) and D's
   members transitive, both quantified over the pairs of f and their
   members. `IsOrd` is exactly transitive-with-transitive-members in this
   hierarchy ([LevelFormula.lagda.md:453-455], `isOrd-out`), so the read is
   faithful, and the exactness `a in delta <-> exists b, pr a b in f` becomes
   definitional for the definable D. No carrier fact enters. Certified in 19
   local lines: the bounding was free, the RATE is what exceeds the survey.

**Finding not on T51's list:** limitForm is the heaviest clause (24 local
lines). Its rewrite ([ProbeLevy.agda:186-217]) must recover both components
of the limit point and of every comparison pair (fstLt, sndIn, sndInSnd), and
express the limit-ordinal premise bounded (isLimitAt', isLimitOf); that
component recovery is the same cost that makes domForm heavy, and it is
amortizable only inside one clause family, not across the cone.

## 4. Walls

**No performance wall.** The file checks green in ~1.5 s cold; the probe is
pure syntax (formulas and witnesses, no semantics), so the P-class conversion
machinery never fires. All walls were formulation errors, and every one was
caught by the typechecker or a manual de Bruijn audit:

1. **Unbalanced parens in witness chains** (domFormD0 twice, limInD0 once):
   parse errors whose cure was mechanical per-line paren counting. Class:
   craft (no LESSONS entry); the witness chains are the only place it fired.
2. **Fixity mismatch in delta-or nesting** (kpairD0's at-most-two
   disjunction): `_or_` is infixr, so the formula nests right and the witness
   must too; the left-nested witness failed unification. Cure: match the
   witness nesting to the fixity. Craft.
3. **Index-shift bugs in helper formulas** (fstLt, fstEq): original-index
   variables must shift inside a binder (`var l` to `var (suc l)`), and
   freshly bound variables sit at small fixed indices (var 0/1) independent
   of the parameters. Cure: state the fresh binders at the small fixed
   indices, R-35's spirit ([LESSONS.md:768-793]).
4. **A semantic binder bug the typechecker cannot catch** (domForm' conjunct
   1): the existential bound z2 over z1 instead of f (var 4) after four
   binders. The Delta-0 witness verifies structure but never the variable
   indices, so a green typecheck is not a truth certificate: every binder had
   to be re-derived by hand. Class: D-10-adjacent (the truth check requires
   reading what the formula says, not that it types
   ([LESSONS.md:1271-1336])); the audit is recorded here as the enforcement
   point for this class.
