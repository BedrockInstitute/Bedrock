## 1. The profile-to-wall ratio is 1.0. HOLDS.

The two back-to-back runs are recorded in dev/ledger.toml:1038-1058. The
un-instrumented run measured 1,080.64 s of wall. The instrumented run
measured 1,074.29 s. The profile total was 1,072.2 s. The ratio is about
0.99.

The instrumented run came second and was 6 s faster. Page-cache warmth
explains the direction. The bias favors the claim. The ratio is about 0.99
on either reading. No load artifact can produce 0.615 from these two runs.

The old 0.615 is recorded as unmeasured in dev/ledger.toml:1055. Its band,
0.6 to 0.7, came from [T86]. T86 compared a profiled run with a 27:54 wall
record taken on a different tree (l3.32-t86-report.md:33, :256-257). T86
itself flagged that record as load-affected and not steady-state. The
comparison crossed different trees, days and loads. It measured something
else, not a ratio.

Three independent checks agree with 1.0. The checkpoint predicted 722.4 s
and measured 677.4 s (dev/ledger.toml:986-987). The error is minus 6.2
percent, in the campaign's favor. [T129] measured 142.28 s of profile
against 143.64 s of wall (l3.32-t129-report.md:224-225). [T132] measured
21.36 s against 21.69 s (l3.32-t132-report.md:87-92). Each ratio is about
0.99. I found no measurement in the repository that contradicts 1.0.

The projected 11.3 minutes checks out as arithmetic: 1,099 profile seconds
times 0.615 equals 676 s. The tree measures 1,080.6 s, which is 18.0
minutes. Two guesses multiplied, exactly as the ledger says.

## 2. The re-home IS the internalization cone. HOLDS.

The consumer uses exactly the two names. The sites are
src/L/Condensation.lagda.md:61-62 and :885-902. The claim is not built on
import edges. It is built on the definitional and proof dependency chains
of the two names. I traced both chains at source.

The formula chain is real. `LsetGraphAt` unfolds through `ApproxAt`,
`StepAt`, `StepBody`, `DefAt` and `DefBody` (Sequence.lagda.md:361-362,
:281-283, :119-120, :113-117; Powerset.lagda.md:442-443, :437-440).
`DefBody` contains `satGraphAt`. `satGraphAt` is the twelve-clause table
(Graph.lagda.md:94-111). The twelve clauses live in Model.lagda.md:890-2000.
For the formula to typecheck, every definition in the chain must exist in
the home's dependency graph.

The proof chain is real. `Lset-only` uses `step-Lset` and `approx-val`
(Hierarchy.lagda.md:332-347, :273-304). `step-Lset` reads `StepAt` through
`DefAt-out` and `DefAt-in` (Sequence.lagda.md:181, :215). `DefAt-in` and
`DefAt-out` call `graphAt-holds` and `graphAt-unique` (Powerset.lagda.md:502,
:560). Those use `slotClosed`, `soundness`, `entry-in` and `Good.pinned`
(Powerset.lagda.md:359-400). Every home in that chain is in the retirement
set. A port cannot skip the chain.

I re-ran the FENCE parser over all 18 direct regions. The total is exactly
2,105. The cluster count is 6,254 over 19 masters. The Hierarchy slice adds
127. The bound is 6,381. Each figure reproduces exactly.

The cone is a dependency cone, not an import cone. The import closure would
be larger. The counted ranges are the clause, code and adequacy machinery
itself. Even a generous over-count cannot cross the 500-line stop. The
measured cone is 4.2 times the stop. The bound is 12.8 times it.

## 3. The rud side cannot serve the crossings. HOLDS.

`StepStory`'s successor clause concludes `b ≡ step c`
(StepStory.lagda.md:357-359). `step` is the rud closure, the union of the
sixteen operation images (Step.lagda.md:338, :446-447). The L-tower
successor is the definable powerset. The equation is
`Lsuc≡Def : Lset (sucV ζ) ≡ 𝒟ₒ (Lset ζ)` (DefInJ.lagda.md:79-80).

The per-level identification is recorded false (DefInJ.lagda.md:14-16,
Devlin VI.2.4). The delivered chain concludes `x ≡ Sset a`, never
`x ≡ Lset a` (ProbeT127.agda:125).

The mismatch is not class-carrier-only. The successor clause fixes the
operator, not the carrier. The story is parameterized by a stage, and the
clause concludes the rud step at every stage. The two operators differ
structurally. No carrier choice changes `step` into `𝒟ₒ`.

Even an agreeing carrier cannot serve the crossing. The crossing needs the
class carrier. The sites state a two-slot formula over `Sʟ`, satisfied at
both the ambient and the inner reading (Condensation.lagda.md:885-902).
The class is not a stage. No stage `C` gives `⟪ C ⟫ ≡ Sʟ`. The kit's
decodes are stage-restricted and do not instantiate at the class.

The index question holds as well. `succForm` is a whole-story formula at a
stage carrier (StepStory.lagda.md:408-410). The crossing needs a two-slot
value-and-index formula at the class. The two-slot wiring is new content
that does not exist on the rud side.

## 4. The adequacy term is 1,630 to 1,940. WEAK.

The direction is solid. The 700-to-900 estimate is refuted. The measured
arm is 174 non-blank lines (src/ProbeT132.agda, from line 62). The log
shows 21.69 s of wall and exit 0 (_build/t132-check.log). The estimate
implied about 90 lines per arm. The measured arm is nearly double that.

The counted regions add about 960 to 1,008 more lines. Both readings
exceed 900. The tail content is structurally required, not a design
choice. I traced the chain at source. `step-Lset` reads `StepAt` through
`DefAt-out` and `DefAt-in` (Sequence.lagda.md:181, :215). Those read
`graphAt-holds` and `graphAt-unique` (Powerset.lagda.md:502, :560). Those
use the table tail (Powerset.lagda.md:359-400). The probe itself imports
the delivered graph theorems as its counted frame (ProbeT132.agda:14-21,
:106, :156). It does not re-derive them.

The band is the weak part. The 1,630-to-1,940 figure adds an unmeasured
estimate. The estimate is a fresh-compressed tail at 620 to 930 lines
(l3.32-t132-report.md:124-131). T132 states the estimate is unmeasured and
names it as the next probe (l3.32-t132-report.md:131). The fresh cost of
the tail is the open term. If it lands at 300, the adequacy is about 1.3k.
If it lands at 900, the adequacy is about 1.9k. The direction of the
overage does not change on either reading.

Arithmetic note: T132's arm-table counts run about 5 percent high. My
FENCE counts of the same ranges give Sat 179, Bridge 49, Graph 66 and
Shape 28. T132 gives 187, 55, 72 and 32. The no-tail sum is about 960,
not 1,008. Under the same tail estimate, the band is about 1,580 to 1,890.
The verdict does not change.

## 5. The AC trophy is 10,026 lines. WEAK.

I ran `scripts/ledger.py --trophy-split`. It prints
`ac-total 10,026 | standing 19,085`. The number reproduces exactly. The
four parts sum to standing, and the code asserts the sum
(scripts/ledger.py:239-283).

The ambiguous rule inflates the AC number. The ten rud modules total 4,077
lines. I recomputed the split without them. The AC total falls to 5,949.
The rule adds 69 percent to the delivered-closure answer. The answer to
the attack's question is: the rule inflates, it does not flatter.

The GCH root rule is route-assigned. GCH has no delivered statement. Its
closure starts from 13 modules the route names. The AC total is sensitive
to that assignment through the ambiguous set. The ambiguous set is the
surviving L content in neither closure. Adding a GCH root that imports the
rud engine moves those 4,077 lines from ambiguous to gch-only. AC then
reads 5,949. GCH stays at 18,970. The split is a projection, not a
measurement.

The ledger discloses the rule. The dashboard does not show the
delivered-closure figure. A reader acting on 10,026 as the delivered need
would over-allocate by 4,077 lines.
