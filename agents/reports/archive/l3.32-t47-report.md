## 3. D-10 verification of T43's load-bearing claim

T43's claim: "every elimination of the Core's `ih` hypothesis lands in a
proposition (the `exclude` case at `src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:418-436`)".

**Verified TRUE.** `rg` for `ih` in the delivered chapter finds exactly three
mention sites: the hypothesis declaration (line 246) and the two projections
`fst (ih ...)` / `snd (ih ...)` inside `exclude` (lines 435-437). `exclude`
has type `(p : PairA) → colA p ≡ α → Empty.⊥`, so the single elimination
target is `Empty.⊥`, a proposition. The truncated `ih` can therefore be
eliminated there, exactly as T43 planned.

**The plan's second, unrecorded claim is FALSE as priced.** To *consume* the
truncated `ih` inside a new core one must still *provide* it as a total
function at every infinite member `β` of every cardinal `α`. Providing
`∥ sq β ∥₁` at an arbitrary infinite `β ∈ α` requires either the induction
hypothesis at `β` (not available: `β` need not be initial) or the transfer to
`|β|` (not available when `|β| = β`, i.e. when `β` is itself a least-of
cardinal): in that case `∥ sq |β| ∥₁` is exactly `∥ sq β ∥₁` and the
obligation is the no-injection-down at least-of cardinals, whose proof is the
Cantor-Bernstein composition T31 recorded as "standard-but-blocked-by-
elaboration" (`_build/l3.32-t31-report.md` §5) and which is not in the tree.
This is D-10's killer class applied to the *plan's* hidden totality
requirement, not to the recorded claim: the recorded claim is true, the
derived obligation is not fundable in this budget.

**The chapter's response is therefore a strengthening, not a workaround.**
The `exclude` case never needs the law at the member: it needs an injection
`⟪ α ⟫ → ⟪ β ⟫` refuted, and the honest composition `k ∘ comp₀` was only one
way to get it. Strengthening the no-injection hypothesis to the square form
(`noinj²`: no injection `⟪ α ⟫ → ⟪ β ⟫ × ⟪ β ⟫` for infinite `β ∈ α`) lets
`exclude` refute `comp₀` directly, with no `k` at all. Classically the two
hypotheses coincide at these ordinals (the diagonal injection gives
`noinj² →` no-injection-down, and the square law at members gives the
converse), so the chapter's `Init` is the classical initial-ordinal notion
restated at the index level, which is T31 §5's own recorded cure.
