## The rule I apply to a bad citation

The dossier recommends a rendering. The bibliography supports it. I separate the
two:

- The rendering has no verified attestation, or an attack lands on it: FAIL.
- One cited source fails, but a second cited source attests the rendering, and I
  verified that second source myself: PASS, with the bad citation recorded as a
  correction.

I state the rule because I apply it four times. A dossier that attests nothing
has not done the work. A dossier with one good source and one bad source has.

A 403 response is not a dead link. Three sites (Baidu Baike, Zhihu, Fandom)
block an automated fetch but serve a human reader. I do not fail a claim for a
403. I corroborate the claim from a reachable source instead, or I say that I
could not.
