## 2. The three clauses as delivered code, and what each consumes

The object is the set of pairs (code, tuple) satisfied in the block. The
probe defines it by recursion on a three-constructor code fragment. Each
clause is the pair-set `F2 (sgl (code φ)) (T n φ)` over the standing engine,
its closure membership, and both decode directions at a tuple.

```agda

  R (at i j) = F2 (sgl (code (var i ∈̇ var j))) (S.T _ (var i ∈̇ var j))
  R (bin c d) = F2 (sgl (code (dec (bin c d)))) (S.T _ (dec (bin c d)))
  R (qu c) = F2 (sgl (code (dec (qu c)))) (S.T _ (dec (qu c)))
```

The binary clause's rud combination is the delivered `capOp` of the two
sub-satisfaction sets; the quantifier clause is the delivered `ranOp` at the

membership follows the same recursion (`code∈Jf`), and the closure
membership is one `JF2` per clause (`hR`).

