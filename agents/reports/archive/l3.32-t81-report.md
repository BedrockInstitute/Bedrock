## 1. Verdict

**Keep ours.** No published proof is shorter than our 145 lines in a setting
this project can spend. The only proofs that measure below 145 lines are the
Isabelle/ZF Banach-decomposition proof (about 20 lines on top of Isabelle's
`lfp` toolkit) and the mathlib4 theorem (a few lines on top of the well-ordered
cardinal theory), and neither is comparable: both rest on theory that would
have to be built or imported here, which is exactly the "not shorter in any way
we can spend" case the brief names. The one technique that transfers to our
setting is Escardó's g-point predicate (TypeTopology, agda-unimath): it replaces
the Cₙ chain in our first fence (31 lines at `src/L/Cardinal.lagda.md:98-128`)
with a single ∀-predicate, at a naive saving of 10 to 25 lines. But the
surjectivity side of that proof is heavier and the cubical translation is
unprobed, so the calibrated band runs from a modest saving to a small net loss.
Keeping our proof is therefore the right outcome, plainly: the published
versions are not shorter for us, and 145 lines is a fair price for this setting
(a Cubical HIT set-theoretic universe, constructive base, LEM only as a module
parameter). If the owner wants the g-point idea spent anyway, the cheapest next
step is a probe that rewrites only `module CSB` and measures; until that probe
goes green, the naive 10 to 25 lines are not money.

## 3. Technique analysis: what would shorten OUR 145 lines

The 145 lines split into two fences: `module CSB` plus the `csb` wrapper at
`src/L/Cardinal.lagda.md:89` (82 non-blank lines) and `module Graph` plus
`csb-eq` at `:213` (63 non-blank lines). The literature only speaks to the
first fence: the graph lift is an orthogonal coding obligation that no source
touches.

**(a) Escardó's g-point predicate (rows 1-4).** The idea, from
`is-g-point x = (x₀ : X) (n : ℕ) → ((g ∘ f)^n) x₀ = x → fiber g x₀`, is to
replace the whole chain with one ∀-predicate: `h x = g⁻¹ x` when `x` is a
g-point, `h x = f x` otherwise. This is the only technique that transfers
cheaply, because our carriers are sets: injectivity is an embedding
(`injEmbedding`, section 4), so fibers are propositions and `g⁻¹` is well
defined, exactly the prop-ness Escardó's proof leans on. Where it lands in our
file: inside `module CSB` at `:89`, it deletes the block `C₀` at `:98` through
`C-view` at `:128` (31 lines: `C₀`, `C₊`, `Cₙ`, `C`, `c-in`, `gf-closed`,
`C-view`; `imG`, `fiberG`, `ginv` survive) and simplifies the mixed cases of
`h-inj` at `:151-158` and the `inl` branch of `h-surj` at `:160-165`, which
currently do `C-view`/`gf-closed` bookkeeping. The `csb` wrapper at `:183` and
the whole second fence stay as they are.

Estimated saving, in both calibers. Naive: 10 to 25 lines of the 145 (roughly
145 down to 120 to 135), because the replacement is a short predicate plus two
case proofs. Calibrated: the deletion is certain, but the replacement is
survey-reached rather than probe-anchored, so per the project convention it
carries 3x, and Escardó's surjectivity side (the f-point predicate and the
disjointness of the two image parts) is where the translation risk sits; the
calibrated band therefore runs from a modest saving to a small net loss. The
honest number is "0 to 25 naive, unprobed", and it does not change the item's
class either way. Recommendation: keep, unless an Agda probe on this exact
rewrite goes green.

**(b) Knaster-Tarski / Banach fixed point (row 7).** Replace the chain with the
least fixed point of the monotone operator `X ↦ A ∖ g (B ∖ f X)`, as in
Isabelle/ZF. On paper this is the shortest route, but only because Isabelle
ships an `lfp` theory for lattices; the cubical library has no Knaster-Tarski
and no least-fixed-point machinery (section 4), so we would build a fixpoint
theory over `ℙ A` first, which costs more than the 30 to 40 lines it would
save. It would land in the same spot, `:98-128`, but with new imports and a
mini-theory before it. Net negative for a one-theorem item.

**(c) mathlib well-ordering (row 6).** CSB becomes antisymmetry of cardinal
`≤`, but only because mathlib's cardinals are well-ordered by ordinals. Our
`Data.Cardinal` is a set-truncated hSet with only a preorder, so there is
nothing to import and the route is not spendable.

**(d) Decidable-orbit arguments (rows 8-10).** Shorter only when a carrier is
structurally decidable or enumerable. Our carriers are arbitrary sets in a HIT
hierarchy. LEM as a module parameter does yield `Dec` on paths in a few lines
(`Relation.Nullary`), but then the orbit case analysis is the same size as the
chain we already have. No win.

## 4. What the cubical library already has

Verified against the installed library at
`/opt/homebrew/Cellar/agda/2.8.0-r3/share/agda/cubical/Cubical`:

- **No CSB**: `rg` for `schroeder|bernstein` over the whole tree returns
  nothing. The only related artifact in the repository is PR #969, which adds
  cardinality order without CSB.
- **`Cubical.Functions.Embedding`**: `isEmbedding` (`:45`), `A ↪ B` (`:72`),
  `isEmbedding→hasPropFibers` (`:98`), `injEmbedding` (`:166`, the load-bearing
  piece: injectivity becomes an embedding when the codomain is a set, which is
  how our proof gets propositional fibers), `Embedding-into-isSet→isSet`
  (`:219`, already imported at `Cardinal.lagda.md:93`), and
  `Set-Embedding-into-Powerset` (`:442`, Cantor).
- **`Cubical.Data.Cardinal`**: `Card = ∥ hSet ℓ ∥₂` (`Base.agda:24-25`) and
  `_≲_` with `isPreorder≲` (`Properties.agda:155, 172`). There is no
  antisymmetry, so no CSB component.
- **`Cubical.Functions.Fixpoint`**: only `Fixpoint` (`:15`) and Kraus's
  `2-Constant→isPropFixpoint` (`:27`). No Knaster-Tarski and no `lfp`: a grep
  for `knaster|tarski|lfp|least.?fix` finds nothing relevant (the `monotone`
  hits are order and algebra lemmas; the containers file only mentions least
  fixed points of container types).
- **`Cubical.Axiom.Omniscience`**: `LLPO` (`:29`) and `WLPO`, the exact pieces
  agda-unimath uses to deconstruct the decidable-embeddings version (row 4);
  relevant only if we take that route.
- **`Cubical.Foundations.Powerset`**: `ℙ X` with `isSetℙ` (`:29`) and `_∈_`
  (`:34`), the substrate a Banach-style proof would need to build its own
  lattice theory.
- **`Cubical.Relation.Nullary`**: `Dec` and `Discrete` (`Base.agda:68-69`) with
  `sectionDiscrete` and `isoPresDiscrete` (`Properties.agda:31-38`), the
  decidable-equality machinery a decidable-orbit route would use.
- **`Cubical.HITs.CumulativeHierarchy`**: `⟪_⟫`, `⟪_⟫↪`, `isEmb⟪_⟫↪`,
  extensionality, `_⊆_`, already imported by the chapter; nothing new to take.
