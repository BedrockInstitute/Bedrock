`uniqueSetOf`/`mereSetOf→isContr` aliases) and the Numerals projection

   without completing, against a ~1 s baseline for the identical file with the
   original proofs (measured by restoring and re-checking). The re-expression
   changes definitional behaviour on the module's conversion-critical sealed
   chain.

Both edits were reverted in full (ZFModel and Numerals back to original,
rechecked green). The frame lemma itself is harmless and could return with a
different consumer set; the gate's immediate-value clause does not justify it
here.
