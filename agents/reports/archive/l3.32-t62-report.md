## 2. Was `⟨ ω ∈ˢ α ⟩` the intended clause?

**Recommendation to the owner: keep `⟨ ω ∈ˢ α ⟩`; it is a choice, not a
slip, and the alternatives cost more than they deliver.** Analysis:

- For an ordinal `α`, "at least `ω`" splits as `ω ⊆ α` iff
  `ω ∈ α ⊎ ω ≡ α`. The strict clause drops the `ω ≡ α` disjunct, which is
  the entire difference between "initial at or above `ω`" and "initial
  above `ω`".
- The dropped disjunct is exactly where the other `Init` clauses still
  hold: at `α = ω`, successor closure is true (finite successors stay in
  `ω`), the no-injection clause is vacuous (no infinite `β ∈ ω`), and
  `IsOrd ω` holds. So `Init` minus the membership clause is inhabited at
  `ω`, and the clause is the sole boundary keeper.
- The honest bound at `ω` IS constructible (T59 §3's `Bω = InitialCore ω ...`
  instantiation), but the pairing's injectivity at the concrete `ω` shape
  walls at check time (T59 §3, R-35/R-38 class), so a widened `Init`
  including `ω` would make the exports non-vacuous at `ω` only at a
  recorded check-time wall for every consumer that instantiates there.
- The counting's own interface already uses the strict form:
  `CardinalCount.Bound` takes `ω∈β : ⟨ ω ∈ˢ β ⟩`
  (`src/L/CardinalCount.lagda.md:155-162`), so widening `Init` alone would
  not unblock the count at `ω`; the interface change would have to run
  through `Bound` and its consumers as well.
- Cost of the alternative if ever wanted: change `Init`'s second conjunct
  to `⟨ ω ∈ˢ α ⟩ ⊎ ω ≡ α` (or `ω ⊆ α` plus an ordinal comparison lemma),
  re-check the whole chapter, and carry the same widening through
  `CardinalCount.Bound` and the counting's five call sites (T43 §2); the
  tower-induction gate's upper arms stay blocked at `ω` regardless, by the
  quotient premise and the pairing-injectivity wall (T59 §1.1, §3).

**Not changed in this dispatch**, per the brief: the predicate is an
interface and goes to the owner.
