### 2.2 The order formula at the general level (what forces cost)

The description's `σ<` must read the level's order back two-way. The order
is the pull-back of the producer-tree order along the least producer:
`Jset-order γ limγ = Sset-order γ (isLimit-ord γ limγ)` and
`Sset-below α ordα m n = memberKey α ordα m ⊰ memberKey α ordα n`
(`Order.lagda.md:835-838`, `:1026-1028`). The producer comparison is
stage-first, then self-vs-image, then operation index, then the two
arguments recursively:

```
prod-image δ i p q ≺ prod-image γ j r s =
  ⟨ δ ∈ γ ⟩ ⊎ ((δ ≡ γ) × ((i <∙ j) ⊎ ((i ≡ j) × ((p ≺ r) ⊎ ((p ≡ r) × (q ≺ s))))))
```

(`Order.lagda.md:329-332`). A finite object-language formula cannot express
this at an arbitrary level at the delivered face, for two measured reasons:

1. **The stage clause is not expressible.** "x is born at stage δ" needs the

   record of `src/L/OrderFormula.lagda.md` (B1).

   trees without bound; a finite formula cannot express the descent without
