## 2. Method, and its caliber

Every line count is the ledger caliber: non-blank lines inside ` ```agda `
fences, read at HEAD, through `scripts/ledger.py`'s own `count`. I ran no
Agda. I made no edit outside this file. Every figure below reproduces at
commit `ed7f949`, where `ledger.py --brief` reads "measured 15,409".

**One live caveat.** `src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md` carries an uncommitted
change in the working tree while I write, at 1,867 insertions and 988
deletions. It belongs to another slot, not to me. The ledger caliber reads
HEAD, so my 1,815 figure is the correct one to quote. The in-flight work
strengthens section 3's verdict: `BelowLim` is the AC chain's live build.

I extracted top-level definitions from each surviving master. I recursed
into a top-level `module ... where` block when it held more than two units,
so a whole-file wrapper module does not swallow the file. A definition's
span runs to the next unit at the same indentation. A definition's nameset
holds its head name plus every constructor and field name inside its span.

**C-23 binds and I applied it.** For each definition I first scanned the
file's own LATER bodies, then the cross-file bodies. A same-file
instantiation counts as a consumer. Nested bodies inside the definition's
own span do not count.

Cross-file consumers use transitive importers, not direct importers. A
re-export through an alias therefore still registers, which is the class
`[T155]` section 1 had to handle by hand.

**Two closures, and both are reported.** The consumer-only reading seeds
the AC set with the `ac_only` masters and the D33 Order suppliers. The
route-honest reading adds the funded AC chain, as D-10 requires and as
`[T155]` section 8 did for its own extended seed.

**Coverage.** The definition spans cover 7,581 of the shared part's 8,757
lines. The gap is imports, OPTIONS headers and module header lines. Those
stay with the master in any split, so they never move. `[T155]` recorded
the same exclusion.

**Direction of error.** A name collision adds a consumer edge that is not
real. That inflates the AC count and deflates the GCH-only count. My
conclusion is "almost nothing is GCH-only", so this is the unsafe
direction, and I say so. Section 3 therefore reports the consumer-only
reading beside the route-honest one. The consumer-only reading is the
upper bound, and it is 245 GCH-only lines.

## 8. What contradicts the brief. My reading wins where I say so.

**1. The 2,100 to 2,700 figure is stale, and by a large factor.** The brief
takes it from `[T155]` and D36. Three things changed since `[T155]` ran.
`L.Rud.HF` (608 movable) and `L.Rud.Finite` (598 movable) are archived
(commit `43ca411`). `L.Rud.StepGraph` (473 movable) is now `ac_only` and
holds 2,152 lines. `L.Rud.BelowLim` did not exist and now holds 1,815. The
correct figure at today's tree, under the same D-10 discipline `[T155]`
itself applied in its section 8, is **216 lines**, plus the 329 that
section 6 reaches by other means.

**2. The brief's question 1 assumes declarations remain available. They do
not.** Every legal target is used. This is not a defect in the mechanism.
It is what happens when the tree's only neither-closure masters are the
funded AC chain.

**3. The brief's question 3 has an answer the brief does not anticipate.**
The brief asks whether a natural split happens to follow the closure
boundary. At today's tree the closure boundary does not fall INSIDE any
file in a way that could carry a subject. It falls at file edges
(`Coding/Base`, `Absoluteness`, `Recursion`), where no split is needed at
all, or it dissolves into dust.

**4. I confirm the orchestrator's expectation and I strengthen its
reason.** The brief expects few files to pass because shared content is
"one subject serving two consumers". My measurement says something
sharper. In 24 shared masters the GCH side reads 22 lines that the AC side
does not. The shared part is not shared content. It is AC content that the
wing reads a few corollaries from.

**5. One thing I could not settle, and I name it (D-10).** My scan is a
text-level measurement, like `[T155]`'s. It is not Agda-verified. A name
collision inflates the AC count. I ran no Agda, as the brief requires, so I
cannot close that doubt. The doubt runs against my conclusion, so I report
the consumer-only upper bound of 245 GCH-only lines beside the
route-honest 22.

**6. A note for the size discipline.** Every figure here is a MEASUREMENT
of today's tree. None is a projection. The 329-line saving in section 6 is
naive and calibrated alike, because it removes existing lines rather than
predicting new ones (D26).
