## 1. The probe proved the right thing

the same carrier generality, with the `sub` hypothesis in the same
position.

- My own typecheck: `GHCRTS=-M8g agda src/ProbeT175.agda`, one process,
  2.07 s wall, 1.69 s user, exit 0, no warnings, no holes, no postulates.
  The probe's 4.2 s cold figure is consistent with my warm run.
- Statement strength. Probe `F1-defSet≡ : L.defSet (pinned op1) ≡ F1 a b`
  (ProbeT175.agda:139-149) against delivered `Describe.F1-defSet≡ :
  DefA.defSet Φ₁ ≡ F1 a b` (Describe.lagda.md:499-509). Same carrier,

  → Σ[ Φ ∈ Formula ⟪ C ⟫ 1 ] (Ds.Defu.defSet Φ ≡ W)`
  (Bridge.lagda.md:170-172). The probe's generic equation `Fof-defSet≡ :
  (i : Op16) (sub : (v : S) → ⟨ v ∈ˢ Fof i a b ⟩ → ⟨ v ∈ˢ C ⟩) →
  L.defSet (pinned i) ≡ Fof i a b` (ProbeT175.agda:131-138) is the same
  shape. `L.defSet` is `DefOf.defSet` (LevelKit.lagda.md:86-89 re-exports
  `DefOf`'s `defSet`), so the codomain is literally Bridge's.
- The `sub` position. Bridge's `Arm` takes the subset certificate as an
  argument; the probe's `Fof-defSet≡` does the same and performs no per-op
  membership analysis. The closed `F1-defSet≡` mirrors the delivered

  (Bridge.lagda.md:263-265 passes a certificate into `D.F8-defSet≡`).
- The fiber parameters. The frame derives `mₐ` and `m_b` from `a∈` and
  `b∈` via `∈-asFiber`, exactly as every delivered `FkDesc` does
