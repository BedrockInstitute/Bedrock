## 1. The exact obligation

The surviving consumers of StepInL's two theorems are exactly two call sites in `p4`'s successor clause (Bridge.lagda.md:780-819), wired through `Reduce`'s hypothesis telescope (Bridge.lagda.md:697-706) and instantiated once at the Landing (SatTable.lagda.md:427). StepInL's importers are SatTable, CodePred (retires), and Everything.

**O1 — value-members at four-up** (the inclusion half's supplier; Bridge.lagda.md:811):
`stepSub = Lstep⊆ (Sset δ) ζ₄ A∈₄ u∈₄ (values∈L (Sset δ) ζ u∈)`, where `Lstep⊆` (Bridge.lagda.md:620-633) turns `ValuesInU u ζ` into "members of `step u` land in `Lset (sucV ζ)`". `ValuesInU` (Bridge.lagda.md:616-618) is:
```
ValuesInU u ζ = (i : Op16) (a b : S)
  → (⟨ a ∈ˢ u ⟩ ⊎ (a ≡ u)) → (⟨ b ∈ˢ u ⟩ ⊎ (b ≡ u))
  → (v : S) → ⟨ v ∈ˢ Fof i a b ⟩ → ⟨ v ∈ˢ Lset ζ ⟩
```
Minimal statement: **every member of every one-step value over a set that already lives in a constructible stage lives four stages higher** — `(u ζ : S) → ⟨ u ∈ˢ Lset ζ ⟩ → ValuesInU u (suc⁴ ζ)` (StepInL.lagda.md:370). Generality: fully general in `u`, `ζ`; the consumer instantiates `u = Sset δ`, `ζ` = the IH index. The four is uniform, not tight: members of the sixteen values land at most two stages up (pairs one up, nested pairs two up), so `suc⁴` carries two stages of slack; the statement need not change, only the proof (via a mono lift from the 2-up stage).

**O2 — step definability** (the membership half's supplier; Bridge.lagda.md:820):
`stepSet∈L (Sset δ) ζ₅ u∈₅ (up₁ ζ₄ A A∈₄) stepSub : Sset (sucV δ) ∈ Lset ζ₆` (via `Sset-suc`), where (StepInL.lagda.md:2419-2422):
```
stepSet∈L u ζ u∈ A∈ sub = 𝒟ₒ⊆Lsuc ζ (step u) (𝒟ₒ-intro (Lset ζ) (step u) ∣ St.stepForm , St.stepSet-desc ∣₁)
```
Minimal statement: **one rud step over a set that already lives in a stage, whose every added value also lives in that stage, is a definable subset of the stage, hence a member of the next stage** — `(u ζ : S) → ⟨ u ∈ˢ Lset ζ ⟩ → ⟨ A ∈ˢ Lset ζ ⟩ → ((v : S) → ⟨ v ∈ˢ step u ⟩ → ⟨ v ∈ˢ Lset ζ ⟩) → ⟨ step u ∈ˢ Lset (sucV ζ) ⟩`. The relativization slot `A` is the one side condition (the formula's slot; `slot∈L` supplies it at the limit, Bridge.lagda.md:706, discharged by `∅∈Lset`, StepInL.lagda.md:2406-2412). Generality: fully general in `u`, `ζ`, `A`.

Nothing else from StepInL survives. Both obligations are S-into-L (J-tower levels land in L-stages); neither is a Q obligation (T4 §1 is sound on that), and neither has a delivered alternative supplier (L4's mirror is dead, l3.31-r2lever-report.md:17). The membership half **cannot** be had without a whole-step formula: `Lset (sucV ζ) = 𝒟ₒ (Lset ζ)` (`Lsuc≡Def`, DefInJ.lagda.md:85), whose members are exactly the definable subsets of the stage, and the step quantifies over all argument pairs, so no finite constant disjunction replaces the bound-variable sixteen-graph formula. That is the honest reason `stepSet∈L`'s core ports rather than deletes.
