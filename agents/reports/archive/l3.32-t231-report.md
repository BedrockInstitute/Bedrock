# [L3.32-T231] Adversarial review: is the induction hypothesis really vacuous at a₀?

## 1. The site list

The claim says that the STEP's membership proof consumes the fact only at
limit members through the induction hypothesis. It says that every other
site is reached by the successor climb. The walk below lists every site
where the proof needs the membership fact at a smaller index. The sites
live in the Step module (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:780-1787`) and in
its Assembly frame (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:120-175`).

## 2. The induction hypothesis type in the telescope

The IH type is quoted from the tree. It is identical in the Assembly
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:121-122`) and in the T222 probe
(`src/ProbeT222.agda:52`).

```
(β : S) → β ∈ᵗ γ → ⟨ isLimit β ⟩ → ⟨ Sset β ∈ˢ Lset (sucV β) ⟩
```

The notation `x ∈ᵗ y` is the underlying type of membership
(`src/FOL/ZFStructure.lagda.md:94-95`). So the IH quantifies over the
members `β` of `γ`.

Every site in section 1 reaches the fact through this type. The memberships
row needs `Sset β ∈ˢ Lset γ` at a limit `β`. The derivation is the shape
of `below-lim-old` (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:152-155`):

```
Lset-mono {α = γ} {β = sucV β} (limit-succ-mem γ β limγ β∈γ)
  (IH β β∈γ limβ)
```

The inputs `β ∈ γ`, `limγ`, and `limβ` are all in scope in the STEP. The
Assembly's `topPair` needs the exact type, with no upgrade. No site needs
an index that the type does not reach. The telescope is sufficient.

The full telescope is as T221 states. It has `γ`, `limγ`, the IH, the nine
clause parameters at C (`stepSub`, `graphOf`, `graph-out`, `graph-in`,
`bigOr`, `bigOr-in`, `bigOr-out`, `eqFrame`, `eqFrame-ok`,
`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:781-803`), and the γ-indexed geometry
(`Tgeom`, `segGeom`, `:804-805`, with `a₀` replaced by `γ`). The T222
probe checks a slice of this telescope at a variable `γ` and exits 0
(`_build/l3.32-t222-report.md:18-24`, `:40`).

## 3. The a₀ discharge

There are two routes. The Assembly route needs no explicit discharge. The
induction supplies the IH at `a₀` as its own hypothesis
(`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:135-138`):

```
indStep a₀ IH a₀-lim = stepHyp a₀ a₀-lim (λ β β∈a₀ limβ → IH β β∈a₀ limβ)
```

The STEP at `a₀` never applies this term. No member of `a₀` is a limit
(`member-a₀-not-limit`, `src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:1714-1723`). The
word "vacuous" is exact. `wall₁ = below-lim a₀ a₀-lim` (`:159-160`) closes.

The standalone route instantiates the STEP module at `a₀`. It supplies the
IH with the term

```
ih₀ : (β : S) → β ∈ᵗ a₀ → ⟨ isLimit β ⟩ → ⟨ Sset β ∈ˢ Lset (sucV β) ⟩
ih₀ β β∈a₀ limβ = Empty.rec (member-a₀-not-limit β β∈a₀ limβ)
```

The term typechecks. `β ∈ᵗ a₀` is definitionally `⟨ β ∈ˢ a₀ ⟩`
(`src/FOL/ZFStructure.lagda.md:94-95`). The motive is the proposition
`⟨ Sset β ∈ˢ Lset (sucV β) ⟩`. `member-a₀-not-limit` supplies the
refutation at `:1714-1723`. The discharge is immediate, as the claim says.

## 4. What I tried to break and could not

### The successor of a limit

At `a₀` no such member exists. The a₀ instance never exercises the case.
At `γ` the case works. For a limit `β ∈ γ`, the IH and `Lset-mono` give
`Sset β ∈ˢ Lset γ`. Then one `sucStep` application gives
`Sset (sucV β) ∈ˢ Lset γ`. The climb step has the same body at `a₀` and
at `γ`. Compare `sucStep` (`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:830-862`) with
Bridge's successor case (`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:1120-1147`). The two
are the same shape, with `a₀` replaced by `γ`.

### Zero

`memberAt zero` uses `Sset-zero` and `∅∈La₀` (`:815-816`, `:865`). The
generic base is `∅∈Lset γ limγ ∅∈γ` (`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:1110-1114`;
`src/ProbeT222.agda:62-63`). It covers 0.

### Numerals in a general limit

`nk∈a₀` uses `St.a₀≡ω` (`:819-820`). At `γ` the analogous fact follows by
recursion on `n`. The base is `∅∈γ`. The step is `limit-succ-mem`. This is
support content, about 5 lines. It is not an IH site.

### The a₀ equals ω shortcut

`seg∈L` reduces members to numerals through `St.a₀≡ω` (`:869-876`). At a
general `γ` this reduction is impossible. The memberships row must use
trichotomy on the member. The limit case is the IH. The successor case is
the climb. The zero case is `∅∈Lset`. The generic row already exists in
Bridge as `rstep` and `rudBelow` (`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:1097-1170`).
Its limit case calls the `BelowLim` residue at `:1150-1153`, which the
Assembly derives from the theorem (`:152-155`). The claim's sentence about
the climb covers all members of `γ` only with the claim's preceding
sentence about the IH. Together they cover every member.

### The R-40 cost shape

The per-step body of the climb is the same at `a₀` and `γ`. The depth for
numeral `# n` is `n` at both. At `γ` a member `sucV^k β` above a limit
`β` costs `k` climb steps above one IH application. The IH application is
a parameter. Its normalization cost belongs to the caller, the `∈-induction`
closure. The a₀ measurement (T182) does not cover depth above a limit.
T222 measured the parameterized slice at 0.085 s per line
(`_build/l3.32-t222-report.md:5`, `:49`). T223's stop-line re-measures at
`γ` (P-l). This is a cost caveat. It is not a type error.

### The general chain

The decode at `γ` reads first components `ξ ∈ γ`, including limits. It must
force `x ≡ Sset ξ`. The a₀ chain `chainValueW` (`:958-1013`) is
numerals-only. The general chain needs the six-clause story and a recursion

### The inl case
