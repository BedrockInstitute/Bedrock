The hand-written 6-clause decode costs 7.5 s. The bundle-alias 6-clause
decode costs 22.0 s. The ratio is 2.9. The shape is the difference. The
clause count is not. The limit clause adds 0.5 s to the whole file
(src/ProbeT242b.agda against src/ProbeT222.agda).

The brief's candidates, tested:

1. Neutral vs computed carrier. Both probes use Lset of a module
   parameter. The computed carrier is not the differentiator. T242
   reproduces T222's story-decode cost at Lset γ.
2. Clause count. T222 proves five clauses. T240 proves six. The sixth
   clause costs +0.5 s. It is not the 3.5x driver.
3. Nesting. T240's heavy context makes the same alias content 2.5x
   dearer. The content costs 55,000 ms at T240's site. It costs 22,011 ms
   at T242's site. The site effect is measured. The mechanism is
   unproven.
4. Rename. T222 has no rename family. The rename is part of the
   pair-family content. It is absent from the slice.
