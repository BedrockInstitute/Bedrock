# [L3.32-T18] The GCH scope gate: the internal sentence needs bijection and initial-ordinal vocabulary; the surjection form cannot serve the direction

**Verdict in one paragraph.** The answer to the orchestrator's scope question is
**NO**: the direction of Devlin's GCH-in-L (5.5-5.6) is not servable by the
surjection form alone, so the bijection and initial-ordinal theory does **not**
defer to the geology era. Part of the vocabulary is forced by the STATEMENT,
before any proof is priced: "κ is a cardinal" and "κ+ is the successor
cardinal of κ" are initial-ordinal notions by definition (a cardinal is an
ordinal with no smaller ordinal equinumerous to it; κ+ is the least cardinal
above κ), and "equinumerous" is a bijection, so the internal sentence `L ⊨
GCH` cannot even be written in surjection-only vocabulary. The proof then
forces the rest: 1.1(vii)'s successor case is injection-shaped (the
formula-count, T9's T1), its limit case is the ordinal-pairing theorem (T9's
T2, an injection `λ × λ ↪ λ`), 5.4's `|M| = max(|X|,ω)` needs bijections
(concrete CSB), 5.5's "γ < κ" step needs κ's initial-ordinal property against
the collapse bijection `γ ↔ L_γ ↔ M ↔ L_α ↔ α`, and 5.6's conclusion needs
Cantor plus successor-cardinal minimality. What DOES defer is not the
bijection/initial-ordinal layer but only its functional form: the cardinality
function `|·|`, the aleph sequence, general CSB as a packaged theorem, and
general cardinal arithmetic beyond the pairing core. Band consequence: T9's
red branch stands, no W7 row shrinks, and the confirmed definitional layer
raises chapter C's upper edge by a bounded amount (anchored bracket below);
W7 stays at roughly 1.30-2.45k naive / 3.90-7.35k calibrated.

## 1. THE EXACT INTERNAL SENTENCE (Q1)

### How a sentence is stated in this development

The constructible universe is a structure: `𝒮ʟ = 𝒮ᵥ ↾ isL`
([L/Constructible.lagda.md:410-411](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Constructible.lagda.md)),
whose carrier is the constructible sets (`isL x = ⋁ S (λ α → IsOrd α ⊓ x ∈ˢ
Lset α)`, [Constructible.lagda.md:376-377](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Constructible.lagda.md)).
The internal language is `Formula K n` with the carrier as constant domain
([FOL/Syntax.lagda.md:94-100](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Syntax.lagda.md)),
satisfaction is the model's own `γ ⊨ φ`
([FOL/Semantics.lagda.md:91](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Semantics.lagda.md)),
and a sentence is `Formula S 0` evaluated at the empty environment. The ZF
axioms were packaged as a record ([L/Model.lagda.md:99-105](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Model.lagda.md));
GCH is a theorem about the same model, so its packaging is an actual
object-language sentence plus a satisfaction proof:

```
L⊨GCH : ⟨ [] ⊨ GCHFo ⟩          -- GCHFo : Formula S 0, at 𝒮ʟ
```

Devlin 5.7's alternative packaging, `ZF ⊢ (V=L → GCH)`
([dev2.txt:1394-1399](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt)),
is the internal implication route and still needs the same internal sentence,
as T5 recorded ([l3.32-t5-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t5-report.md) §1,
step 5).

### The sentence, in this development's vocabulary

Devlin's GCH is stated at the conclusion of 5.6: for every infinite cardinal
κ, `|P(κ)| = κ+` (the proof text at
[dev2.txt:1386-1389](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt);
the `2^ω = ω₁` notation at [dev2.txt:17](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt)).
The internal rendering is schematic below (de Bruijn plumbing and the
relabelling moves, which the delivered `mapFo` supplies,
[FOL/Manipulation/Relabelling.lagda.md:54](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Manipulation/Relabelling.lagda.md),
are elided; this is the statement's shape, not a typechecking term):

```
GCHFo = ∀κ ( Card(κ) ∧̇ Inf(κ)  ⇒̇
              ∃p ( Pow(p,κ) ∧̇ ∃s ( SuccCard(s,κ) ∧̇ Eq(p,s) ) ) )
```

The predicates, each a reified `Formula S n` with a certificate that its
satisfaction at 𝒮ʟ equals the intended host notion:

- `Ord(v)`: "v is an ordinal", already delivered as `φ-ord : Formula K 1` with
  its Δ₀ certificate ([L/Ordinal/Stages.lagda.md:294-302](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Ordinal/Stages.lagda.md)),
  matching the meta `IsOrd` (transitive set of transitive sets,
  [Constructible.lagda.md:141-142](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Constructible.lagda.md)).
- `Eq(x,y)`: "x and y are equinumerous", `∃f (Bij(f,x,y))`, where `Bij` is the
  internal bijection predicate: f is a set of Kuratowski ordered pairs, a
  relation, single-valued and total on x, injective, and surjective onto y.
  This is the biggest new expressibility piece: the tree has no internal
  ordered-pair/function/bijection formulas (grep: `Kuratowski|isFunction|
  bijectionFo|pairFo|single-valued` over `src/` masters hits only the meta-
  level coding machinery, e.g. [L/Coding/Environment.lagda.md:99-106](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Coding/Environment.lagda.md),
  which is about code graphs, not a reified internal function predicate).
- `Pow(p,x)`: "p is the power set of x", `∀z (z ∈̇ p ↔ z ⊆̇ x)`; the inclusion
  half `_⊆̇_` is already expressible as `subFo`
  ([L/Axioms/Power.lagda.md:99-103](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Axioms/Power.lagda.md)).
- `Card(v)`: "v is a cardinal", `Ord(v) ∧̇ ∀̇∈ v (¬̇ Eq(var, v))`, an ordinal
  with no smaller ordinal equinumerous to it, i.e. an initial ordinal.
- `Inf(v)`: "v is infinite", `∀̇∈ ωʟ (var ∈̇ v)`, ω ⊆ v, using the delivered
  constant `ωʟ` ([L/Axioms/Infinity.lagda.md:66-69](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Axioms/Infinity.lagda.md)).
- `SuccCard(s,κ)`: "s is the successor cardinal of κ",
  `Card(s) ∧̇ κ ∈̇ s ∧̇ ∀λ (Card(λ) ⇒̇ κ ∈̇ λ ⇒̇ (s ∈̇ λ ∨̇ s ≐ λ))`, s the least
  cardinal above κ; ordinal comparison is membership (ordinals are transitive
  and trichotomous, `ord-tri`, [L/Ordinal/Linear.lagda.md:136](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Ordinal/Linear.lagda.md)).

**What the STATEMENT needs (expressibility).** Existing: `φ-ord`, `subFo`,
the `ωʟ` constant, the language itself. New: `Eq` with the function substrate,
`Pow`, `Card`, `Inf`, `SuccCard`, each with a certificate. Explicitly NOT
needed for the statement: the cardinality function `|·|`, the aleph sequence,
cardinal arithmetic, or CSB. The statement is a `Formula S 0` of moderate
reification depth, and the initial-ordinal content (`Card`, `SuccCard`) is
forced by the sentence itself, not merely by its proof: "κ+ is the least
cardinal above κ" is a statement about initial ordinals.

One formulation note: the equivalent GCH shape "no cardinal λ lies strictly
between κ and |P(κ)|" avoids the `SuccCard` relation as a separate symbol, but
it still quantifies over cardinals, so it still needs `Card` and `Eq`; no
statement shape of GCH avoids the initial-ordinal predicate, which is the
load-bearing point for the scope question.

**What the PROOF needs (beyond the statement).** The meta-level cardinality
theory over the tree's own ordinals and small indexes: the formula-count (T9's
T1, measured 448 code lines, [l3.32-t9-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t9-report.md) §1),
the product bound (T9's T2, the ordinal-pairing theorem, 250-400 lines,
[t9-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t9-report.md) §3), the shape-count
(T9's T3 extra piece, [t9-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t9-report.md) §4),
the tower induction 1.1(vii), 5.4, condensation (W5), the initial-ordinal
facts (`|κ| = κ`, "|γ| = |α| < κ implies γ < κ", κ+ leastness), Cantor, and
the certificates linking the internal predicates to these meta notions.

## 5. THE HONEST RISK (Q5)

**The lean choice does not force a rebuild, provided it is the relation layer
built generally.** The minimal vocabulary of §3 is the classical substrate
that both later eras consume, not a throwaway:

- Fine structure's `|J_γ| = |γ|` is the same tower-induction shape as 1.1(vii),
  consuming the same count + pairing core; its Σ₁ enumeration is genuinely
  surjection-flavored (the Σ₁ surjection `α ↠ J_α^A`,
  [dev/literature/j-hierarchy.md:110-113](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/dev/literature/j-hierarchy.md)),
  and its "cardinal in M" / Σ₁-cardinal notion
  ([dev/literature/fine-structure.md:53](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/dev/literature/fine-structure.md))
  is a surjection-shaped strengthening of the same initial-ordinal `Card`
  predicate. The minimal layer is its base, not its replacement.
- Geology needs internal cardinality at a class carrier (`|a|^W < δ` for
  δ-approximation/δ-cover), which is currently not even stateable
  ([l3.31-glprobe-report.md:272](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-glprobe-report.md), 0.3-0.7k).
  That is BEYOND the minimal GCH layer (it needs the internal `|·|` function
  at a class carrier), so geology pays for it on top either way; the minimal
  layer's `Eq`/`Card`/initial ordinals are its substrate. Choosing the layer
  now is what lets geology's `|·|` be built as "the least ordinal equinumerous
  to a" on top of it.

**What actually defers, and what does not.** Deferrable: the aleph sequence,
the `|·|` function, general CSB, general cardinal arithmetic, cofinality. Not
deferrable: the pairing core (T16), the count (T1), the initial-ordinal
relations `Card`/`κ+` in both statement and proof, and the bijection-shaped
equalities (1.1(vii), 5.4, the collapse chain). The owner's option-B ruling
(build the pairing chapter now, [dev/PLAN.md:1124](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/dev/PLAN.md)) is
confirmed by this gate, not merely by T9: the pairing theorem is consumed by
1.1(vii)'s limit case at κ+ and will be consumed again by fine structure's
`|J_λ| = |λ|`.

**The trap the surjection-only hope would have created.** A surjection-only or
raw-injection "cardinality" would force a rebuild precisely because the
collapse chain `γ ↔ L_γ ↔ M ↔ L_α ↔ α` and the "γ < κ" step are
bijection/initial-ordinal-shaped, and fine structure's `|J_γ| = |γ|` and
geology's `|a|^W` are the same shapes: every consumer would re-derive the
bijection and the initial-ordinal facts from scratch. Two formulation rulings
avoid that:

1. Define `Eq` as "there is a bijection", not as "injection each way": the
   latter makes every equality a per-consumer CSB obligation (5.4, 1.1(vii),
   5.5's composition) and forces the general CSB theorem into W7 anyway.
2. Build the predicates generally (`Eq : Formula S 2`, `Card : Formula S 1`,
   `SuccCard : Formula S 2`) with certificates, not as GCH-specific shims:
   fine structure's Σ₁-cardinal and geology's internal `|·|` are extensions
   of these predicates, so the layer is reused, not rewritten.
