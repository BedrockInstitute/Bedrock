check records the reason (`_build/l3.32-t24-report.md`, section 1). The
comparison atom is not an atom of the inner world. The level formulas have

`Describe` does not build the producer order. T24 grep-verified the absence

`Jset-order` is the W3 chapter.

T19 ran W3's gate RED (`_build/l3.32-t19-report.md`). T22 re-priced W3 at
1.1 to 2.7k naive and 3.2 to 7.8k calibrated
(`_build/l3.32-t22-report.md`). So only W3-class content discharges `phi`.

The verdict is (c). The AC route carries a W3 slice. The slice is the order
formula over the level plus its two adequacies. T24 names this slice W3's
elimination half (section 5). The description side is AC-forced through the
J-to-L direction. Reading B holds.
