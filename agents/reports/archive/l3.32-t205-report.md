not a measurement. Its origin is the L3.28 compression survey of

the levers at minus 755 conservative to minus 1,486 optimistic. It

(`dev/memos/L3.28-ac-route.md:66`). No delivered tree reached the floor.
The internalization route delivered 17,496, reproduced from `main` this
session. That measurement passed 1,496 lines above the floor.

## 3. Caliber comparison

The checkable claim: the delivered figure reproduces. `main`'s `src/`
counts 17,496 non-blank lines inside ` ```agda ` fences by the ledger
caliber. I verified this with `git ls-tree` and `git show`, this
session. The survey's recorded baseline does not reproduce at its own
commit. Both `017b2b2`, the candidate-(3) deletion, and `ac226a2`, the
survey commit, count 17,496 by the same convention. The recorded 17,492
is four lines lower. The cause is not in the record.

The counting convention matches. The survey counted non-blank lines
inside ` ```agda ` fences. The D36 cap uses the same convention through
`ledger.py --trophy-split` (`dev/PLAN.md:194`).

The object differs. The survey counted the internalization route's whole
tree, 75 masters. Everything in that tree served `L ⊨ AC`. The cap
counts the ruled route's AC closure. The closure is base plus ac-only
plus shared, surviving tree. Same ruler, different objects.

The 16,000 was a projection of what compression could remove from the
old tree. It was never a measured state of any tree. T174 records a
content mismatch on the same bar. The old tree had no counting
machinery, and `FOL.Count` did not exist
(`_build/l3.32-t174-strategy.md:128-131`).

## 5. The honest bar

If the floor is unsupported, the evidence supports these numbers:

- 17,496, in both calibers. This is the internalization route's
  delivered whole-tree size. It is a measurement, reproduced from `main`
  this session. A measurement carries no calibration multiplier.
- The ruled route's own honest projection, from
  `_build/l3.32-t201-decision.md:6-8`: 17,810 to 19,430 naive; 19,105 to
  22,774 calibrated. The tool reads 19,100 to 20,720 naive and 21,180 to
  24,849 calibrated (`scripts/ledger.py --budget`, this session). The
  measured ac-total today is 15,061 (`scripts/deletion-test.py
  --shadow`, this session). That is a partial tree, not a landing.

For 16,000 to be the bar the evidence supports, all of these must hold:

- The survey's optimistic removal of 1,486 lines lands at full value.
  It never executed. The project's rule prices a survey term at times
  three, not at face (the T205 brief; `dev/LESSONS.md:1143`, D-6).
- The ruled route's landing comes in 1,810 to 3,430 naive below the
  T201 projection. That requires unidentified compression of that size.
  The measured record on internalization-content rewrites is zero
  compression (T132, T140, T142, recorded at `dev/PLAN.md:189`, D32).
- The calibrated projection falls 3,105 to 6,774 lines below 16,000.

The D36 cell records the same gap in the owner's own posture: "after
every lever was measured and the cap sat 1.4-3.7k beyond the evidence:
continue the work and bet on unknown compression appearing"
(`dev/PLAN.md:194`). This report adds the provenance. The floor itself
was a survey projection.

No advocacy. The owner ruled the cap and may keep it.
