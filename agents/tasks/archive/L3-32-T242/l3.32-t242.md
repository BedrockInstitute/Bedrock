# [L3.32-T242] Two decode shapes, both "bound-variable", 3.5x apart. Why?

tier: codex (default), `--agda`. **A probe.** Explain a measured
discrepancy, and price the escape if one exists.

## THE DISCREPANCY

Two probes measured decode content at a variable carrier. Both are in
the tree. They disagree by 3.5x and nobody has explained it.

| probe | lines | cold | rate | carrier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `[T222]` | 126 | 10.74 s | **0.085** | the witness stage at a variable limit γ |
| `[T240]` | 589 | 174.62 s | **0.297** | `Wξ = Lset ξ`, nested, ξ a variable limit |

`[T222]`'s own words: "the cost appears at the bound-variable carrier
after all, with 70 percent of the check in two named, written-type
decode definitions, which is P-n's signature at a neutral carrier and
about 2.6 times cheaper than the instantiation rate."

`[T241]` profiled `[T240]` and found the same signature, P-n, at 0.297.
**So both are P-n content at a variable carrier, and one is 3.5 times
dearer per line.** One of two things is true, and this task says which:

1. **A sampling artifact.** `[T222]` is 126 lines and `[T240]` is 589.
   `[T222]`'s slice may simply not contain the expensive kind. Then the
   0.085 figure has been over-applied across this campaign and the
   ledger should say so.
2. **A structural difference that is exploitable.** If `[T222]`'s shape
   is genuinely cheaper for the same work, bringing `[T240]`'s content
   to it is worth **3.5x on 589 lines, about 125 s.**

**That is the whole remaining question on the seconds threshold.** The
gate needs 293 s that the base does not have (the full cold profile,
2026-08-09, `_build/l3.32-coldprofile-2026-08-09.txt`). 125 s is not
293, but it is the only unpriced quantity left, and if 1 is the answer
then several standing projections are wrong in the other direction.
