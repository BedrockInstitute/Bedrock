continuation, and one is dearer.** Priced from zero on today's experience, the
three architectures land at **naive 16.0-21.3k (R-ideal), 17.7-23.6k

and +2.3 to +6.2k for G — the Gödel route's ideal form is *dearer* than its
continuation**, because its sunk stack is worth more than the discipline it

### 3.2 What the ideal form buys, and what it does not

the Levy/crossing line, at **1.00-2.55k naive / 3.00-7.65k calibrated**
([l3.31-ivprobe-report.md:482-487,:547](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ivprobe-report.md)),

0.30-0.80k. In the ideal form:

- the **equivalence row vanishes** (0.30-0.80k): there is no separately
  delivered monomorphic description to reconcile the generic reading with;
- the **certificate row is written at birth** rather than retrofitted
  (0.30-1.00k → 0.20-0.60k, row I-6f), on the φ-ord precedent;
- the **σ-form row survives unchanged** (row I-6a). It is new statement work
  in every architecture and genericity does not conjure it.

Net: **1.00-2.55k → 0.60-1.35k naive.** That is the whole of the ideal
internalization form's structural advantage, and it is worth **0.4-1.2k
naive / 1.2-3.6k calibrated**.

## 9. What this report does not claim

It does not rank the routes. On the naive caliber R-ideal leads by ~2k of
center; on the calibrated caliber I-ideal leads by ~4k of center; the two
calibers disagree, and the disagreement is structural (§5.3), not noise. It
does not claim any band is achievable — every one of them breaches or grazes
the 21.5k tripwire somewhere. And it does not claim the ideal-form exercise
found a cheaper route: **it found that all three ideal forms sit within ~3k
of their own continuations, which means the ideal-form question, honestly
priced, does not move the fork.** The measurements in §8.5 do.
