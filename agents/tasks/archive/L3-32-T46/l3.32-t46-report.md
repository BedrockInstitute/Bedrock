## 3. Every call site of the square law: ordinal and goal type

There is exactly one kind of call site, with one exact ordinal parameter,
and it is the datum the parallel scope check needs.

**Call site (the counting bound).** `Bound.count-bound`, and through it
`Bound.formula-bound` and `AtHull.hull-bound`, invoke the square law at the
infinite bound ordinal `β` (`CardinalCount.lagda.md:155`, `:204`). The goal
type is

```
Σ[ f ∈ (⟪ β ⟫ × ⟪ β ⟫ → ⟪ β ⟫) ] ((x y : ⟪ β ⟫ × ⟪ β ⟫) → f x ≡ f y → x ≡ y)
```

the exact shape `L.Ordinal.SquareLaw.Core`'s `ih` hypothesis and the
successor step's `sqγ` take. **The goal is not proposition-valued**: the
first component is a function `⟪ β ⟫ × ⟪ β ⟫ → ⟪ β ⟫`, and only the second
component (injectivity) is a proposition. The obstruction is therefore
exactly the extraction risk T31 recorded: the least-of cardinal search in
`SquareLaw.Card` returns `∥ ⟪ α ⟫ ≃ ⟪ κ ⟫ ∥₁` (truncated), and a truncated
witness cannot be eliminated into a Σ over a function type; the law at `β`
must come from a delivered construction, not from the least-of reduction.

**Where the law is delivered.** At `β = ω`: `SquareLaw.CoreAtω` discharges
all four hypotheses and `Core.bound`/`bound-inj` has the consumer's exact
shape; per T31 the in-file instantiation heap-exhausted under the 8 GB cap
and was removed, so the `ω` base is one module application away (the
orchestrator can attempt it sealed or under a raised cap), not in-file. At
`β = sucV γ` from the law at `γ`: `SquareLaw.pair-suc`/`pair-suc-inj` is
delivered in-file, so the law at `ω` plus its finite successors is available
once the base is instantiated. The hull-bound instantiation at `β := ω`
(X's index injects into `⟪ ω ⟫`) is exactly that pending base.

**Where the law is open.** At every other infinite ordinal: the non-initial
limit ordinals (`ω + ω`, `ω · ω`, ...), the initial ordinals beyond `ω`
(`ω₁`, `ω₂`, ...), and every infinite ordinal whose members contain one.
There the call site is open with the goal type above, and the chapter does
not postulate it, does not work around it, and names it here.

**A second, non-square-law residue for the hull's `β`.** The hull statement
takes the bounding injection `⟪ X ⟫ ↪ ⟪ β ⟫` as a hypothesis, matching
Devlin's `max(|X|, ω)` in injection form. The natural stage-side choice
`β := α` would additionally need 1.1(vii)'s `|Lset α| ≤ |α|` (an injection
`⟪ Lset α ⟫ ↪ ⟪ α ⟫`), the tower induction of the cardinal chapter, which is
out of this chapter's scope and is not a square-law call site.

## 7. New terms

- **Composed count** (复合计数): the composition of `FOL.Count`'s two
  injections into the formula-count `Formula K 1 ↪ Σ[ k ] (Formula ⊥* k × ℕ
  × Vec K k)`, injective with the shape kept in the image.
- **Index reduction** (索引归约): the injection of any
  propositionally-witnessed index into the formula family, by the
  propositionality of the witness component.
- **Counting bound** (计数界): the injection of the count's image into an
  infinite ordinal `β` carrying the square law, via the numerals and the
  iterated pairing.
- **Call site of the square law** (平方律调用点): the named place where the
  bound consumes the law at `β`, with its exact ordinal and goal type.
- **Arity-transport stability** (元数搬运稳定性): the commutation of a
  recursor (`code`, the tuple growth) with transport along an arity path,
  proved by `J`.

None are added to `dev/glossary.toml`; they are named here for the owner's
ruling.
