## 5. The options, ranked

The ranking order is honesty first, then cost, then risk to the endpoint.

**Rank 1. Continue as ruled and re-judge at the landing.** The D36
posture is the owner's word: continue the work and bet on unknown
compression appearing; the decision re-opens at the tripwire, not before
(`dev/PLAN.md:194`). The work is funded under D37 until the endpoint is
proved inside both thresholds (`dev/PLAN.md:193`). This option is the
most honest because it makes no claim that outruns a measurement. Its
cost is the build itself, which is funded anyway. Its risk is that the
landing arrives RED at 17.8-19.4k naive, and the re-judgment then has no
time budget left. It is the recommendation, with one condition: the
landing must run the real deletion test and the naturalness review
together, exactly as D36's amendment orders.

**Rank 2. Re-price the cap on a principled bar.** The internalization
route delivered L models AC at 17,496 lines (`dev/PLAN.md:194`). A cap
set at 17,496, or at the reconciled low end 17,810, beats the delivered
route but not the old route's probed floor of 16,000. The owner's two
reasons were both of those numbers (`_build/l3.32-t174-strategy.md:273-288`).
Re-pricing to the delivered bar is honest only if the owner accepts that
the second reason is spent. It is the cheapest option and it removes the
endpoint risk, but it changes a threshold the owner ruled hard. It
should be a documented D26 overage with the corrected projection, not a
silent change.

**Rank 3. A named exemption for the residual.** The residual after the
measured and gated adjustments is 1,810-3,430 naive. That is not a
single row. The D30(B) precedent exempted exactly one row, bridge-landing,
with a mechanical record and a checker-enforced status
(`dev/PLAN.md:187`; `dev/ledger.toml:880-889`). An exemption for 1.8-3.4k
is a different object: it removes the brake at the largest spend of the
campaign. Honest only as a shadow-metered exemption with the deletion
test reported at every commit, which T174's Rank 1 already described
(`_build/l3.32-t174-strategy.md:273-288`).

**Rank 4. A further compression campaign.** The named candidates are the
F5 order-story, the S18 double-encoding collapse, and the naturalness
splits. The measured record is against this class: T132, T140 and T142
each measured compression at zero on internalization content
(`dev/ledger.toml:441-450,487-510`). F5's own fork is wide: story
comparables price it at 1.0-1.8k, and the convergence pattern prices it
at port size, a wash (`_build/l3.32-t174-strategy.md:184-207`). Expected
yield is small and uncertain, and the cost is build time that D37 has
already spent on the critical path.

**Recommendation.** Rank 1, with Rank 2 prepared as the documented
fallback. The honest landing is 17.8-19.4k naive. The cap as ruled will
be RED at the landing unless the survey terms collapse or new
compression appears. The owner should see the deletion test's own number
before re-ruling the bar. That is the D36 posture, and it is the honest
order: measure the landing, then decide.
