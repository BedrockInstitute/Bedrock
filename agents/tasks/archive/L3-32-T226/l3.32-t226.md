## WHY THIS IS A SECONDS LEVER

`[T222]` measured a generic STEP at **0.085 s per line**, and its
profile put **70 percent of the check in the two decode definitions**.
The story assembly's decode is exactly that shape. Removing a hand
assembly removes decode lines, and decode lines are the expensive ones.
T213 prices C4 at net 50-120 lines; at 0.085 that is **4-10 s** of gate
margin, and the tree currently straddles the gate by 9 s.
