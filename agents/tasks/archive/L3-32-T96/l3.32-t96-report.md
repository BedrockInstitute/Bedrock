## 1. Statement-bound or consumer-bound?

**Neither Bridge's consumer pattern nor SquareLaw's statement pattern: the
cost is in the BODIES of the two assembly definitions, which elaborate
delivered machinery at the concrete formula `LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero)`.**
The decisive experiment (the `[T88]` shape, adapted): the hottest definition,
`ambientOnly-from`, has its body replaced by a one-line reference to a named
lemma carrying the same concrete application. Its row collapses from 128.5 s
to 0 ms, and the cost moves to the named lemma (131.1 s); the module total is
unchanged (205.1 s vs 201.9 s, +3.1 s noise). A statement-bound cost would
survive the gutted body (as `h₀-inj`'s 190 s did in `[T88]`); here it does
not. `crossOut-from`'s 54.4 s is the same shape: its body is the one-place
instantiation of `Transport`'s `amb-agree` at the concrete formula.

## 4. The fix, measured

**No cheap export-preserving fix exists. The 203 s is the price of the two
concrete assembly applications the crossing section delivers; the removal
price is a statement-level restructure of the exported obligations, which is
outside this dispatch's scope (not export-preserving) and beyond the 60-line
seal stop-line.** The decomposition, all measured in scratch copies
(`GHCRTS=-M8g agda --profile=definitions -i /tmp/t96 -i src
/tmp/t96/ProbeT96*.lagda.md`, one process):

| probe | what changed | row(s) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| control | byte-identical body, renamed module | `ambientOnly-from` 128.5 s, `crossOut-from` 54.4 s, Total 201.9 s | baseline |
| gut | `ambientOnly-from` body = one-line reference to a named lemma with the same concrete application | `ambientOnly-from` 0 ms, `loLset` 131.1 s, `crossOut-from` 54.6 s, Total 205.1 s | the cost follows the concrete application, not the assembly statement |
| decomp | same, plus `loLset3` (delivered `Lset-only` application alone, inner reading) and `loLset4` (both readings as free premises) | `loLset` 131.5 s, `loLset3` 54.1 s, `loLset4` 54.7 s, `crossOut-from` 56.2 s, Total 315.5 s | the `Lset-only` application itself is ~54 s; the extra ~77 s is the `tl` threading |
| where | same, plus `loLset13` (constructibility witnesses as free variables, `tl` threading kept) | `loLset` 130.4 s, `loLset13` 129.6 s | the where-block's `ord-isL`/`vl` applications are not the cost; the ~77 s is the env conversion in `tl vL bL h` |

The measured bill of materials for the 182.9 s of hot rows:

- **~77 s**: the conversion between `AmbientOnly`'s premise
  `⟨ (v ∷ b ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ φ ⟩` (unprojected `S`-environment) and
  `TransferL`'s premise `⟨ (fst vL ∷ fst bL ∷ []) AbsL.⊨ᵛ φ ⟩` (projected
  `Sʟ`-environment). The two are definitionally equal (the projections
  unfold), but the conversion walks the whole satisfaction tree of the
  concrete `LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero)`. Removing it requires restating
  one of the two exported obligations at the other's environment form, which
  changes the exported statement.
- **~54 s**: the delivered `Lset-only` application at the concrete
  environment, `Lset-only zero (suc zero) (vL ∷ bL ∷ [])`. This is the
  chapter's content (the reduction of the ambient form to the delivered
  inner form); no cheaper body of the same type exists.
- **~56 s**: the `Transport`/`amb-agree` instantiation at the concrete
  `LsetGraphAt {2} zero (suc zero)` inside `Crossing.crossOut-from`.
  Removing it requires parameterizing `Crossing` on the formula, a module
  signature change (no tree consumer exists today, but the exported
  interface changes).

**The Bridge fix does not apply.** There is no read lemma stopped one layer
early that consumers re-normalize: each hot definition performs its own
concrete application once, and there are no re-using consumers. The opaque
seal at birth (the exact Bridge shape) is measured separately; see the wall
table. The SquareLaw-style statement restatement does not apply either: the
statements are cheap (the type synonyms have no profile rows) and the gutted
body collapses the row.

What the 203 seconds would cost to remove, priced:

| piece | seconds | removal lever | export impact | lines |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| env conversion in `ambientOnly-from` | ~77 | restate `TransferL` at `S`-binders with constructibility witnesses, or `AmbientOnly` at `Sʟ`-binders | changes an exported obligation statement | ~25-30 |

| `Lset-only` application | ~54 | avoid the delivered concrete lemma (restate the obligation at variable indices, which removes the delivered reduction) | content restructure, retirement of delivered content | priced not built |

No export-preserving combination exists: every lever touches an exported
statement. The measured floor if the two hot rows vanished is about 19 s (the
rest of the module, dominated by the two `level-abs₀` rows at 2.9 s each).

**Both dimensions.** Seconds: 201.9 s profile / 201.4 s wall before, 201.9 s
after the only body-preserving change that was measured (the opaque seal,

between rows without changing the total. Lines: no fix landed, so the module
is unchanged at 551 non-blank in-fence lines (the probes were scratch, +13 to
+28 lines each under `/tmp/t96`, not in `src/`). The refusal is therefore a
full deliverable: the removable fraction is measured and priced, and no
export-preserving change within the 60-line stop-line exists.
