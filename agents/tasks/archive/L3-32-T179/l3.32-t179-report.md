## 3. The two T161 walls

**Wall 1, the inexpressible stage clause, dissolves.** The successor
clause states no "x is born at stage δ" predicate. Its domain is the
carrier's members (`SM`), and the membership it characterizes is the
order's own pair set. The birth-stage membership belongs to the order
story's domain clause, which F3's mechanism reads from the carried tower
story `f`: with `pr δ (Sset δ) ∈ f`, "x is born by stage δ" is the
bounded formula "∃ w, pr δ w ∈ f ∧ x ∈ w", exactly T48's face reading
(`_build/l3.32-t48-report.md:93-98`). The probe never needs `Sset δ` as a
term, so the D-10/`L.OrderFormula` record (no internalized level
predicate, T44's B1) does not fire here. The wall was a wall of the
delivered formulation, not of the obligation.

**Wall 2, the unbounded argument descent, dissolves.** The argument
comparisons `p ≺ r` of the producer lex (`src/L/Rud/Order.lagda.md:329-332`)
descend through producer trees without bound. The successor clause
replaces each comparison with one membership read from the previous
order: `pr u u' ∈ r` and `pr v v' ∈ r` (`codeLex`,
`src/ProbeT179.agda:85-91`). The recursion is carried by the story, one
level per read, exactly the sequence-witness shape T48 priced
(`_build/l3.32-t48-report.md:104-112`) and SZ's `r_{γ+1} = clause(r_γ)`.
The probe measures the clause over the values lex's reading; the reading
itself (the code reads via the sixteen-op structure and the argument
reads) is the delivered supplier's composition.
