## THE STANDARD

Judge as a READER of the finished book, not as an accountant. For each:

1. **The chapter test**: state the chapter's story in one sentence. If
   you cannot, that is the finding.
2. **The seam test**: read the prose at the cut. Does it explain the
   content on its own terms, or does it explain the move? Prose that
   says "moved out of X" is a seam and must be rewritten.
3. **The reading-order test**: does `src/Everything.lagda.md`'s
   catalog still read as a sequence a learner could follow? The
   catalog's blurbs are the book's table of contents.
4. **The re-merge question**: name any cut that should be merged back
   before the landing, and say what it should merge into.
