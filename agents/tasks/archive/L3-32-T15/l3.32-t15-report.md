## 4. Hypothesis ledger (nothing consumed silently)

1. **The σ-form and the two readings (`Face`, `:120-151`): zero hypotheses.**
   `σ-in` is the truncation intro; `σ-out` is the definitional identity of §3.
   No LEM, no transitivity, no absoluteness, no `BoundedFo`. The three object
   clauses `Ap`/`Cl`/`Rg` are module parameters (tower content), not
   hypotheses consumed by the readings.
2. **The adequacy (`Adequacy`, `:184-230`): exactly FOUR telescope entries.**
   Two are inherited from the probe, two are new:
   - `Approx : V ℓ → V ℓ → Type (ℓ-suc ℓ)`, the meta-level approximation read.
     **Why unavoidable:** the approximation clause's meta-level meaning is the
     tower story itself (functionhood, the zero step, the limit step, the
     domain bound), and at an arbitrary carrier there is no tower for the face
     to point at. The probe's two concrete reads (`DefStep`, `_∈ran_`) are
     simple membership and coding statements the face can own; the
     approximation is not of that kind. Defining it here would mean importing
     the delivered tower (ordinals, `Lset`, `HierOf`) into the face, which is
     the retiring cone's shape and exactly what the fresh-generic ruling
     deletes ([l3.31-r2lever-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-r2lever-report.md),
     fact 4). So the read is a named telescope entry, and the consumers supply
     it as their tower means it. This is the one deliberate relaxation of the
     probe's "meta-level reads are concrete" discipline, and it is the whole
     point of the approximation being consumer content.
   - `a-ok`, the approximation clause's adequacy: `Ap`-satisfaction ⟷
     `Approx u f`. **Why unavoidable:** the payload and both adequacy
     directions carry the approximation conjunct; without `a-ok` neither
     direction can be produced (negative control 3, §5).
   - `c-ok`, the Def-step clause's adequacy (the probe's hypothesis): `Cl`-
     satisfaction ⟷ `DefStep u f`. Unavoidable as in the probe: the successor
     step's meaning is tower content.
   - `r-ok`, the range read's adequacy (the probe's hypothesis): `Rg`-
     satisfaction ⟷ `(⟪ u ⟫↪ x) ∈ran (fst f)`. Unavoidable as in the probe.
   Everything else in the adequacy is hypothesis-free: the two directions are
   `defSet-mem` (delivered) + the readings + the three parameter adequacies,
   and nothing more (no transitivity, no rud closure, no LEM).
3. **`Perturb` (`:252-270`): zero hypotheses** beyond the garbage formulas.
4. **No private export needed.** The face consumes only public `DefOf` exports
   (`SM`, `ι`, `_⊨ᵐ_`, `defSet`, `defSet-mem`) and public properties
   (`⟪_⟫`, `⟪_⟫↪`), `V.Coding.pr`, `V.Hierarchy.𝒮ᵥ`. Nothing had to be
   un-privated; no fiber fact was needed, so the new `V.Presentation` kit was
   not required either (nothing re-derived, §7).
5. **LEM: none.** The probe's `lem` was arm plumbing (only the `L.Rud.Step`
   import for ARM B consumed it, [l3.32-t2-report.md:207-208](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.32-t2-report.md)).
   With the arms out of batch 1, the chapter takes no `lem` parameter and the
   core is LEM-free; consumers in the classical cone supply `lem` at their
   instantiation site. This is a finding, not a loophole: the face's statement
   layer never needed the principle.

Every hypothesis is an explicit module parameter at its consumption site; the
DELIVERED claim does not ride on any uninstantiable assumption.
