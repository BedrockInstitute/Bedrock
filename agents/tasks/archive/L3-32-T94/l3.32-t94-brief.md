## THE CAVEAT THAT DECIDES WHETHER YOUR ANSWER IS USEFUL OR CATASTROPHIC

**This route is mid-campaign, so UNCONSUMED IS NOT UNNEEDED.** `Bridge`, `HF`
and `Finite` have no external consumer *yet* because their consumer is the

So the deliverable set you design against is **not** the 38 names. It is:

1. the 38 names actually imported today, plus

**Establishing (2) accurately is most of this dispatch's value.** Read the

## THE FOUR QUESTIONS

**1. What must the route deliver?** The union of (1) and (2) above, stated as
a list of results with `file:line` where they exist today. This is the
specification everything else is measured against.

**2. How much of the 3,230 is genuinely distinct mathematics?** This is the
D29 question and the one with the most money in it. How many obligations are
**instances of one pattern written repeatedly**, which could be written once at
a generic carrier and instantiated? `[T79]` already measured **700 to 850
standing lines written twice**, and `[T80]`'s `L.LevelKit` delivered exactly
this move once: the level story's shared clauses written once generic cut
`LevelFormula` 613 to 258 and `LevelSigma` 788 to 456, **net -321 lines**. Look
for the same shape here. Name the patterns, count their instances, and say what
a generic form would cost against what the instances cost now.

**3. Which modules exist for a route that has since changed?** This project has
changed route more than once, and code outlives the reason it was written. A
module whose content served a superseded approach is not a rewrite candidate,
it is a **retirement** candidate, and that is a different and cheaper answer.
Check each of the five unconsumed modules against the register: was its
consumer retired, frozen, or never built?

**4. The ideal decomposition.** Given (1), (2) and (3): what modules SHOULD
this route have? Say how many, what each holds, and why the cut falls there.
Design it **backward from the deliverables**, not forward from what exists.
Then price it: total obligations, total lines, and the expected s/obligation
given that the design is written to the discipline in question 5.

## THE DISCIPLINE THE REWRITE MUST BE WRITTEN TO, MEASURED NOT GUESSED

`[T93]` read the cheapest tree in this repository before its archival and found
what makes it cheap. `L.Godel.Closure` (3,490 lines, 102 s) and
`L.Godel.Levels` (2,775 lines, 60 s) contain **zero `opaque`, zero
`postulate`**, and still run at a fraction of the hot modules' rate, because:

- everything lives in a module-parameterized block at an abstract carrier,
  `module _ (A : V ℓ) where` (`src/L/Godel/Closure.lagda.md:135`,
  `src/L/Godel/Levels.lagda.md:133`);
- statements are at **variable indices** over that carrier;
- every `subst` runs along a locally proved equality at that same abstract
  carrier, so **no conversion ever normalizes a concrete presentation**.

And the counter-example that proves sealing is not a substitute: `Bridge` WAS
sealed, 939 s to 199 s, and **plateaued at 0.61 s/obligation, ten times the
0.063 benchmark**. Sealing captured 80 percent and could not reach the rest.

**So your decomposition must be one this discipline can be written in.** If a
proposed module cannot state its content at an abstract carrier, say so and say
why, because that is where the seconds come back.
