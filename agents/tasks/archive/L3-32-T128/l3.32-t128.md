# [L3.32-T128] The limit-clause gate: is the sixth clause expressible, and what does it cost?

tier: codex (default; a D-1 gate probe with a written stop-line)

## GOAL

`[L3.32-T127]` proved that the general below-lim route needs a **sixth story
clause**: at a limit index, the value is the union of the values below. Decide
two things about it, in this order:

1. **Is it expressible** as an object formula over the carrier, in the kit's
   language, with a two-way decode? **This is a real question with a live
   precedent for NO.**
2. **If yes, what does it cost**, in lines and seconds, at the first limit
   where it has content.

Green funds the general below-lim build. **Red closes the route as recorded
and is the more valuable answer**, because the campaign would otherwise fund a
term that cannot be written.

## WHY THIS EXISTS, AND WHY THE ANSWER IS NOT OBVIOUS

`[T127]` gated B4 and found that at general `β` the recorded O7 and O8 are
FALSE: the delivered one-way story carries no limit clause (the kit's Def-step
clause is `⊤̇`, `src/L/LevelKit.lagda.md:189`; `LevelSigma.aSt1` is five
clauses without one, `src/L/Rud/LevelSigma.lagda.md:727-728`). Below a general
limit there ARE limit ordinals, so a witness may put an **arbitrary** value at
a limit index without violating pairhood, single-valuedness, the zero clause,
the exact domain, or the one-way successor clause, which constrains successor
indices only. The chain does not force `x = Sset a` there and the carve
overshoots.

`[T127]`'s first limit is green precisely because `ω` has no limit ordinals
below it, so **the sixth clause has no content at `ω` and cannot be gated
there**. Your gate has to reach a carrier where it does: the first place a
limit index sits strictly below the carrier's index, `β = ω·2`, or whatever
the tree's delivered ordinal arithmetic makes cheapest. **Say which you chose
and why.**

## THE LIVE PRECEDENT FOR RED, AND YOUR D-10 OBLIGATION

**`[L3.32-T84]` refuted a structurally similar clause.** The
`B-condensation-story` lever proposed folding the condensation level story
into the face, and T84 stopped it at the D-10 check before any build: **the
kit does not serve that consumer's Levy obligations, and its clause layer is
not Delta-0 certifiable** (`dev/ledger.toml`, the `B-condensation-story`
lever row). That is the same layer you are being asked to add a clause to.

So the FIRST thing you do, before writing a line of the clause, is price the
truth and the expressibility:

- A union over an unbounded-below-the-limit family is the obvious hazard. The
  clause quantifies over all `ξ < a` for a limit `a`; if that quantifier
  cannot be bounded inside the carrier, the clause is not Delta-0 and the
  route is red **for the same reason T84 was**.
- `dev/LESSONS.md` **R-35** is the standing warning about union
  representations: index or path extraction from the representation of a union
  of an open term leaves unsolved metas or churns the presentation machinery,
  and the cure is to state memberships at the SMALL INDEX.
- **D-24** says a transitive tower converts block absorption into a uniform
  bound, and that a plan of the form "the members sit at unbounded-but-finite
  offsets and the block absorbs that" is always false for membership. Check
  whether your clause is that plan in disguise.

**Report the expressibility verdict before the price.** If it is red, stop
there: the price of an inexpressible clause is not a number anyone needs.
