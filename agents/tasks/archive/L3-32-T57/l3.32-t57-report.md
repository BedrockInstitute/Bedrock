## 1. Q1: Is the subtraction right?

The derivation (T55 report, question 3, row 7) subtracts from T18's W7 band
the measured deliveries inside W7: A+B at 204 lines and C content at 1,153
(`_build/l3.32-t55-report.md:305-312`), leaving D+E (0.20-0.40k) plus the
C-row remainder (C book minus 1,153, "at most about 0.30k"), hence
0.20-0.70k.

**Row A+B (elementarity + criterion, definable hull), delivered 204, booked
0.35-0.75k: discharged.** T40 measured row A at 112 and row B at 92, summing
to 204, inside T5's 0.35-0.75k band for the two rows (`_build/l3.32-t40-report.md:77-82`);
the deliverable `src/L/Hull.lagda.md` measures 241 in-fence lines today, of
which 37 are the import block, consistent with T40's split. T40's two
standing 5.3 consequences (the full elementary reading at hull parameters and
smallestness) were priced by T40 "and left standing" as the order formula's
residual (`_build/l3.32-t40-report.md:12-18`), which is W3 content, not a
row-A/B cost. The subtraction of 204 from A+B is sound.

**Row C (cardinal chapter), booked 0.80-1.45k, delivered 1,153: the
arithmetic holds and the premise does not.** Today's measured deliveries:
`src/L/CardinalPredicates.lagda.md` 399, `src/FOL/Count.lagda.md` 588,
`src/L/CardinalCount.lagda.md` 166, summing to 1,153 (measured this run,
same basis). T18's C row is 0.80-1.45k naive
(`_build/l3.32-t18-report.md:206`), and the register's C booking covers the
whole cardinal chapter: T5's row C names "|·|, Card, κ+, max, formula-count,
1.1(vii) incl. the limit case, Cantor" (`_build/l3.32-t5-report.md:348`), and
T18's proof-minimal list names the tower induction, 5.4, the initial-ordinal
facts, Cantor and the certificates inside the cardinal row's book
(`_build/l3.32-t18-report.md:121-128`). So 0.80-1.45k minus 1,153 does give a
remainder of at most about 0.30k. **But that remainder is a subtraction
between two survey numbers, not a price for what the residue is.** The
residue items were never individually priced: T46 says explicitly that
1.1(vii), the 5.4 equality half and Cantor are "the row's remaining
sub-items", NOT this chapter (`_build/l3.32-t46-report.md:62-65, :177-178`),
and T49 records them as "always booked W7 remainder" with no standalone band
(`_build/l3.32-t49-report.md:33`). Itemizing them against delivered
comparables (section 3) prices the C remainder at about 0.53-1.02k, which is
0.23-0.72k above the subtraction's "at most 0.30k". The subtraction is
therefore **right as band arithmetic and wrong as an estimate**: it does not
overstate the residue (no booked row covers content the campaign dropped; the
items are live and named in four reports), it understates it, because the C
book held two things, the delivered 1,153 and the residue items, inside one
survey band that measurement has now separated.

The arithmetic of the understatement, stated plainly. If the residue items
are really 0.53-1.02k (section 3's C-remainder rows: tower induction, 5.4
half, Cantor, 𝒮ʟ certificates), then the C row's true content is delivered
1,153 plus residue 530-1,020 = 1,683-2,173 lines, which exceeds the C
booking's upper edge 1,450 by 233-723 lines. The booking's "at most about
0.30k remainder" is a band-maximum artifact (1,450 minus 1,153); it is not a
measurement, and it cannot coexist with the itemized residue unless the
delivered 1,153 was under-booked. Either reading says the same thing: the
derived row is not a price.

**Rows D and E, 0.20-0.40k: live, and E holds an item the seven named items
omit.** Row D (bounded subsets, 5.5) and row E (GCH assembly, 5.6) stay in

0` plus certificates" (`_build/l3.32-t18-report.md:208`), and T18's statement

## 4. Q4: The widest term, and its gate

**The widest term is the 1.1(vii) tower induction**, 0.30-0.55k naive /
0.90-1.65k calibrated. It is widest on three grounds: it carries the largest
band in the table (0.25k naive spread, 0.75k calibrated), it is the least
anchored (no delivered comparable exists for a tower-level cardinality
induction; the other items either ride delivered objects directly or have a
measured anchor), and it is the one item whose price compounds the other
items' content (its successor step consumes the count and the square law, its
limit step consumes the square law, and the 5.4/5.5/5.6 chain consumes it).

**The gate (one dispatch, D-1 probe, stop-line 250 non-blank in-fence
lines).** Concrete instance: the tower at `ω` (a limit ordinal, initial, with
the square law delivered at it), two target statements plus one negative
control:

1. **Successor case at `ω`**: an injection
   `⟪ Lset (sucV ω) ⟫ ↪ ⟪ ω ⟫`, composed as
   `index-reduction → FOL.Count count → CardinalCount.Bound` at `β = ω`
   with `SquareLaw.Initial ω iω`'s `bound`/`bound-inj`, over
   `Lset-suc` (`src/L/Axioms/Basic.lagda.md:196`), `𝒟ₒ A = sett
   (Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1) defSet` (`src/L/Definability.lagda.md:110-114`). This is
   exactly T5's T3 payoff shape at the one ordinal where every ingredient is
   delivered (`_build/l3.32-t5-report.md:386-409`).
2. **Limit case at `ω`**: `Lset ω = ⋃_{n < ω} Lset n` read at the index level,
   then the union injects into `ω` through `SquareLaw.Initial`'s truncated
   square law at `ω` (`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:1242-1277`).
3. **Negative control**: the lower injection `⟪ ω ⟫ ↪ ⟪ Lset ω ⟫` (`α ⊆ Lset α`
   at `α = ω`), which should be near-free via the delivered tower facts
   (`Lset-mono`, `Lset-in`/`Lset-out`, `src/L/Axioms/Basic.lagda.md:52-54`);
   its purpose is to catch a presentation wall of T31's class
   (`_build/l3.32-t31-report.md:115`) early, at the cheapest arm.

Green means all three arms typecheck inside the stop-line, one Agda process,
`GHCRTS=-M8g`, no postulate/hole/`TERMINATING`; the tower induction item
moves from x3 to x1.3 and the row's calibrated band narrows from about
1.70-3.62k to about 1.40-2.90k (naive unchanged at 0.65-1.32k). Red names
the defeated obligation and the next technique: if the union arm fails, the
limit case is a real wall and the tower induction funds at its top or above;
if the successor arm fails at `ω` where every piece is delivered, the count's
composition itself is the wall, which would re-open T5's T3 verdict.

**What the probe does NOT establish.** It measures the mechanism at `ω`, not
the full induction over every `α ≥ ω`; it does not price the transfer at
non-initial ordinals (a separate, already-measured item); it does not
establish the 5.4, Cantor, 𝒮ʟ or `Pow` rows; and a green at `ω` does not
guarantee the induction skeleton is cheap at arbitrary ordinals (that is why
the item keeps a band rather than collapsing to the probe's line count).
