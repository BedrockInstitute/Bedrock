# [L3.32-T228] The endgame status memo, written for a reader who was not here

tier: codex (default). A read-only synthesis. It runs no Agda.

## GOAL

Today the campaign moved from "the line cap is the problem" to "the
wall is the problem" to "the wall turns on block A's line count", and
the evidence for that arc now lives in twenty reports. **Write
`dev/memos/L3.32-endgame-status.md`: the single document a reader who
was not here could use to understand where `L ⊨ AC` stands and what
decides it.**

This is not a plan (that is `dev/memos/L3.32-ac-endgame.md`) and not a
decision brief (that is `_build/l3.32-t201-decision.md`). It is the
STATE: what is proved, what is measured, what is projected, and what is
open, each with its evidence.

## WHAT IT MUST CARRY

1. **What is proved today.** The theorem closes at the first limit in a
   real master (`[T195]`); the general STEP is a parameter. Say exactly
   what that means and what it does not.
2. **The two thresholds, with their measured state.** Lines: the
   endpoint projects 20.1-21.8k against 16,000, and two independent
   max-effort campaigns (`[T210]`, `[T213]`) say it does not close.
   Seconds: the tree is 395.2 s measured, the uncured landing is dead
   at 775-842, the reshaped landing straddles at 489-522 against 498.
3. **The reshape**, because it is the live plan: `[T221]` and
   `[T223]`, minus 898-933 lines, and why block A's line count is now
   the deciding quantity at 0.085 s per line (`[T222]`).
4. **The measured laws this endgame produced**: P-m, P-n, D-26, R-40,
   C-23, and what each one forbids or permits. A reader should be able
   to see why the campaign stopped funding cures.
5. **The open questions**, ranked, each with the measurement that would
   close it. Distinguish what is open because nobody measured it from
   what is open because the owner must rule.
6. **The honest summary in one paragraph**: what it would take for
   `L ⊨ AC` to land inside both thresholds, and what the evidence says
   about whether that is reachable.

## EVIDENCE

**Every claim carries `file:line`.** A number takes the line of the row
or the report section that measured it: `dev/ledger.toml:NNN` for a
ledger figure, `_build/l3.32-tNNN-report.md:NN` for a finding,
`src/L/Rud/BelowLim.lagda.md:NN` for a statement in the master, and
`dev/LESSONS.md:NNNN` for a law. A report that cannot be checked can
only be believed. Where you state a figure with no line to cite, say
"UNCITED" beside it: that is a finding, not a formatting problem.

## THE STANDARD

**No advocacy, and no bucket arithmetic.** Every number carries its
source and its status: measured, gated, projected, or survey. Where two
reports disagree, say which is right and why. Where the campaign was
wrong today, say so plainly: the record of this week is that a document
was wrong more often than a measurement was.
