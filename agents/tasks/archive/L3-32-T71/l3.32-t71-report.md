## 2. The target statements, and the D-10 correction on the 5.4 half

**Cantor.** Target: `(a : S) → (Σ[ f ∈ (⟪ a ⟫ → ⟪ Pow.𝒫V a ⟫) ]
((y : ⟪ Pow.𝒫V a ⟫) → ∥ Σ[ x ∈ ⟪ a ⟫ ] (f x ≡ y) ∥₁) → Empty.⊥)` — no
surjection from the members of `a` onto the members of its power set. This
is T18's "one genuinely surjection-flavored fact" in the honest index shape
the counting side works in. Checked true at the intended generality; the
proof is the diagonal subset carved out of `a`, with `Pow.power-spec`
(`src/V/Model.lagda.md:327`) supplying `D ∈ 𝒫V a`. The `¬ HostEq a (𝒫V a)`
carrier form follows from this via CSB and the singleton injection; it is a
2-line corollary left to the assembly (section 7).

**The 5.4 equality half.** The item's own text (T57: "lower bound `X ⊆ M`,
`ω ⊆ M`, then CSB to the bijection form") decomposes into:

- `X ⊆ M`: delivered (`src/L/Hull.lagda.md:393`), restated in the
  report's exports through `AtHull`'s use of `A.HullX.X⊆M`.
- `ω ⊆ M` (new): every numeral `# k` is the least witness of its defining
  formula, the empty-set formula at zero and the successor relation at the
  step, so each numeral is a member of the hull. The statement is
  `ω⊆M : ⟨ ω ∈ˢ Lset α ⟩ → (x : S) → ⟨ x ∈ˢ ω ⟩ → ⟨ x ∈ˢ Hull ⟩`; the
  hypothesis `ω ∈ˢ Lset α` is the honest generality (the numerals must live
  in the stage; Devlin's "α a limit ordinal" is the sufficient case). This
  is the D-10 correction recorded beside the original: the lower bound is
  stated relative to the stage, not absolutely.
- CSB: `(a b : S) (f : ⟪ a ⟫ ↪ ⟪ b ⟫) (g : ⟪ b ⟫ ↪ ⟪ a ⟫) →` an injective
  and surjective `h : ⟪ a ⟫ → ⟪ b ⟫`, plus the carrier lift
  `csb-eq : ... → HostEq a b` (the graph of `h` as a set of Kuratowski
  pairs, the five `HostBij` clauses).
- The bijection form: `HostEq M β` from an injection `⟪ Hull ⟫ ↪ ⟪ β ⟫` and
  one back. The honest correction recorded here: the counting side's
  delivered upper bound lives at the hull's **raw** index
  (`Index ↪ ⟪ β ⟫`, `src/L/CardinalCount.lagda.md:306-337`), and the raw
  index does **not** descend to the presentation index `⟪ Hull ⟫`, because
  `hullVal` is not injective (two formulas can share a least witness). The
  carrier form therefore takes the presentation-index injection as a
  hypothesis; the descent is a named piece for the assembly (section 7).

The literal "|M| = max(|X|, ω)" equality is not asserted, because the tree
has no cardinality function `|·|` with an honest bijection (SquareLaw's
`Card` search returns the least ordinal only as a truncation,
`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:561-575`). What is delivered is exactly
the item's own decomposition, and the max statement is recorded as the
assembly's remaining piece.

   `fiber` operation is transparent and its projection in a `cong₂ pr`
   argument normalized the whole presentation; the fix was to carry the
   fiber's path and use path composition instead of `subst` over
   presentation-index types.
4. **New wall, no LESSONS entry covers it**: in Agda 2.8.0, a `with` clause
   on **any** expression whose type is `⟪ a ⟫` (a defined function applied
   to a module or function parameter) hangs the checker indefinitely (tested
   down to `test a x with x` on `x : ⟪ a ⟫`, vs 2.3 s for the same shape at
   `x : S`). The cure is structural: thread the LEM decision as an explicit
   `_⊎_` argument and pattern-match on it (`h`, `h-inj`, `h-surj`, `h-cons`),
   with the final function `ĥ` closing over the decisions. Proposed as a new
   entry (I-series) for the owner; not minted here.
