## 1. D-10 first: T63's claim verifies at file:line

T63's claim decomposes into four parts: (a) `graphOf`/`graph-out`/`graph-in`
are two-way, (b) they cover all sixteen operations, (c) they are at arbitrary
arguments rather than at pairs only, and (d) the sequence-witness step clause
does feed non-pairs to the four tuple operations, which is what makes the
arbitrary-argument strength load-bearing. All four verify on today's tree.

**(a) Two-way.** `graphOf : Op16 → Formula ⟪ C ⟫ 3` with `graphOf i = eqFrame
f2 (memOf i)` ([StepInL.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/StepInL.lagda.md:2139-2140)).
`graph-out` (the decode direction) states, for every `i : Op16`, every
`b a y : S` with membership in the carrier `C`, and every `sub : (v : S) → ⟨ v
∈ˢ Fof i a b ⟩ → ⟨ v ∈ˢ C ⟩`, that `⟨ (pt b b∈ ∷ pt a a∈ ∷ pt y y∈ ∷ [])
DefC.⊨ᵐ graphOf i ⟩ → y ≡ Fof i a b` (:2142-2150). `graph-in` (the encode
direction) states the converse under the same hypotheses (:2151-2160). The two
directions are separate theorems, composed through `eqFrame-out`/`eqFrame-in`
(:713-736). No direction is conditional on pairhood.

**(b) All sixteen.** `Op16` has exactly the sixteen constructors `op0`-`op15`
([Step.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Step.lagda.md:184-200)),
and `Fof : Op16 → V ℓ → V ℓ → V ℓ` maps every one of them
(:230-246), with `Fof op11 a b = F11 a b` through `Fof op14 a b = F14 a b`
(:242-245). The dispatch `memOf : Op16 → Formula ⟪ C ⟫ 4` covers all sixteen
([StepInL.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/StepInL.lagda.md:2079-2095)),
and `memOut`/`memIn` cover all sixteen in both directions (:2097-2136),
including `memOut op11 = mem11-out` (:2112-2115) and `memIn op14 = mem14-in`
(:2133-2136). Since `graphOf i = eqFrame f2 (memOf i)`, the graph inherits
every op, op11-op14 included.

**(c) Arbitrary arguments, not pairs only.** The graph statements quantify
`b a y : S` with carrier-membership witnesses and carry no pairhood hypothesis
on `b` or `a` (:2142-2160). The same holds at the membership level: `mem11`-
`mem14` (:1166-1169) have two-way adequacy at arbitrary `z b a y` in the
carrier (`mem11-out`/`mem11-in` :1822-1897, `mem12` :1899-1974, `mem13`
:1976-2016, `mem14` :2018-2050). The tuple frame `tupleOut`/`tupleIn`
(:1770-1860) binds the two projections to the second argument through
`leftEqF f1 f3` and `rightEqF f0 f3`, and the junk readings that make the
non-pair behaviour content, `right-nonpair` and the `left-⋂` collapse/empty
facts ([Images.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Images.lagda.md:533-534,
:571-572, :584-585)), are encoded in `rightEqF`'s classical case split
([StepInL.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/StepInL.lagda.md:1037-1112)).
So the delivered graphs characterize `y = Fof 11 a b` etc. at non-pair `b`,
which is exactly the strength W3 needs.

**(d) Non-pairs are really fed to op11-op14.** The step clause's image formula
is `imgForm = ∃̇ (∃̇ (inU f1 ∧̇ (inU f0 ∧̇ bigOr)))`
([StepInL.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/StepInL.lagda.md:2317-2318)),
where `inU(t)` is `t ∈ u ∨ t = u` (:2297-2298) and `bigOr` is the sixteen-fold
disjunction of the per-op graphs, `graphOf op11`-`op14` included (:2182-2184,
with the `bigOr-in` arms for op11-op14 at :2206-2218). The parameter domain is
`u' u = u ∪ {u}` ([Step.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Step.lagda.md:93-94)),
which always contains the level itself (`u-self-in` :102-103) and, at any
non-zero stage, `∅` (`∅-in-u'` :760); at the S-tower the carrier is a level
`Lset ζ` whose empty set is a member (`∅∈Lset`,
[StepInL.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/StepInL.lagda.md:2411-2417)).
The empty set is not a Kuratowski pair (`∅` has no members while a pair has
them, so `∅-empty` and the pairing facts refute any `b ≡ pr p q`), so non-pair
parameters are always quantified, and the both-new arm of the canonical order
quantifies `x = F_i(u_x, v_x)` over the whole parameter square
`16 × S^A_{β̄} × S^A_{β̄}`
([j-hierarchy.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/dev/literature/j-hierarchy.md:122-133)),
so a graph complete only at pairs would leave the F11-F14 arms' non-pair
clauses undecodable. T63's finding that the delivered graphs are complete at
all arguments is verified: the graph statements themselves carry no pairhood
condition, and the `mem11`-`mem14` adequacy closes at every carrier value.

Two corrections to T63's citations, both immaterial to the claim: T63 cites
the graph span as `:2139-2160` and the membership span as `:1822-2050`, both
verified; and T63's statement that the W3-side remaining price is "consumer
wiring rather than new machinery" is confirmed in section 5, with the op-order
as the one piece of formula content T50 priced but StepInL does not carry
(section 3).
