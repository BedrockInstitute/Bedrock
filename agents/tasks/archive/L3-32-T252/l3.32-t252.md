## WHAT TO JUDGE, per master

1. **Does the file read as ONE chapter?** State its subject in one
   sentence without using "and". If you cannot, that is the finding.
2. **Does each section answer the file's charter**, or is it there
   because it needed a home? Name the section and the charter line.
3. **Would a reader learning this material find the content here?**
   That is the pedagogical half of the condition and it is not the same
   question as the architectural half.
4. **Is any cut threshold-driven?** A cut made to move lines across a
   boundary, rather than because the content belongs, is exactly what
   D36 says must be regularized. `[T226]`'s assembly is the one to
   examine hardest: it was KEPT for naturalness after measuring +72
   lines and +0.85 s, so naturalness is the whole of its
   justification. **If it is not natural, it has no case at all.**
5. **The catalog blurbs.** `src/Everything.lagda.md` describes each
   master. Do the blurbs still describe what the files now hold?
   `[T192]` found five stale blurbs; today's four masters were not
   re-blurbed.

## THE STANDARD

**Judge the content, not the diff.** The question is not whether each
move was reasonable when made. It is whether the RESULT reads as
something a person would design. A sequence of individually sensible
moves can arrive somewhere nobody would have chosen.

**A finding of NOT NATURAL is the valuable return.** Everything landed
green and passes every gate; this is the one condition no checker
enforces, so a clean bill from you is worth only as much as the doubt
you brought. Name what you would move, where, and why, and say what it
would cost.

## EVIDENCE

judging a section against. Where you propose a move, name the target
