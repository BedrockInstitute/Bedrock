## 6. Walls by LESSONS class, and the proposed rule

**No performance or conversion wall.** The kit checks cold in 2.6 s, the two
consumers in 7.5 s and 3.1 s, the consumer probe in under 2 s; P-h was
applied as the law (module-parameterized, carrier abstract, nothing unfolds),
which is the first funded application of D29 and the mechanism this lever was
priced on. No P/R/I-class wall was hit; no re-formulation was needed.

**One craft friction event, resolved by probe, proposed as a C-14 extension.**
The kit's design initially re-exported its opened names with a plain
`open U using (...)` inside `LevelKit`, expecting `open Kit public` in the
consumers to carry `SM`, `ι`, `_⊨ᵐ_`, `defSet` across. Agda does not do that:
a plain `open` (or `open import`) inside a module is not re-exported through
`open M public`; only `public` opens and module aliases are. The probe
(`/private/tmp/reexport-test/A.agda`, `B.agda`, three variants, under a
second each) isolated it: the plain-open variant fails with `NotInScope`, the
`open ... public` variant checks. The fix was one line at the definition site
(`open U using ( SM; ι; _⊨ᵐ_; defSet ) public`, `LevelKit.lagda.md:89`), which
also made the consumers' seams two lines each. **Proposed as an extension of
the existing C-14 law [B]** ("re-export what a consumer's proof needs"),
rather than a new ID, per the Adding-an-entry rule: a kit's opened names that
a consumer needs must be opened `public` at the definition site; privacy is a
name wall, and a consumer that cannot see the kit's opened names either
re-opens the inner module or re-derives the names. Measurement: the probe
above, and the two consumer seams at `LevelFormula.lagda.md:102-103` and
`LevelSigma.lagda.md:124-125` that depend on it. Enforcement point: the
definition-site `public` on the kit's `open U` line, checked by review when a
parameterized kit chapter is added (the lint cannot see module re-export
semantics).
