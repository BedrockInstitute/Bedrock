## 1. THE MINIMAL CLASSICAL PATH (Q1)

The corpus's only full derivation of GCH in L is Devlin II.5, "The Condensation
Lemma. The GCH in L" ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1066-1394),
already pinned as the GCH-critical path by the projection recon
([gch-projection-recon.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/gch-projection-recon.md):29:
W1+W2+W3+W5+W7, no Skolem functions, no acceptability). The consumer end, from
"condensation holds" to "L satisfies GCH", is the following chain, every lemma
named with its hypotheses:

### The chain

**Step 1 — elementarity, defined, and the Tarski-Vaught criterion (Devlin 5.1).**
"Recall from 1.10 the definitions of the notions of elementary substructure,
Σn-elementary substructure, elementary embedding" ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1067-1069);
5.1 states the equivalence: N ≤n M iff every non-empty Σn+1-definable subset
of M meets N ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1071-1074), the
witness-closure (Tarski-Vaught) form. For this development the needed instance
is the full-elementary version at a **set** carrier: for a transitive set
M ⊆ L_α and every formula φ(x, ȳ) with parameters p̄ ∈ M,
`L_α ⊨ ∃x φ(x,p̄) → ∃q ∈ M, L_α ⊨ φ(q,p̄)`. Hypotheses: M a transitive set
(so the inner reading at carrier M is one line via `Single` at `∈ˢ M`,
probed GO, [l3.31-ivprobe-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ivprobe-report.md):70-71);
nothing else. This is greenfield: the notion `≺` does not exist anywhere in
`src/` (grep, §2).

**Step 2 — the definable hull and its elementarity (Devlin 5.3).** For α a
limit ordinal and X ⊆ L_α, M = "the set of all elements of L_α which are
definable in L_α from elements of X" ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1329-1332);
then M ≺ L_α (full) and M is the smallest elementary substructure containing X
([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1333-1335). The proof is
Tarski's criterion ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1347) applied
to the least-`<_L`-witness formula ψ(v0) = φ(v0) ∧ ∀v1 (v1 <L v0 → ¬φ(v1))
([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1349-1351): if L_α ⊨ ∃x φ(x)
then the <_L-least witness satisfies ψ, and ψ defines it from elements of X, so
it lies in M. Hypotheses: α a limit ordinal; a well-order <_L of L_α that is
available to the argument (either as a formula, W3's σ_<, or as the delivered
meta `SWO (Mem (Lset α))` — formulation fork below); definability/satisfaction
at the carrier L_α (delivered: `DefOf` + `Single`'s inner satisfaction, §2);
the set of formulas with parameters from X as a small index (`Formula ⟪X⟫ 1`,
small because ⟪X⟫ is small, [FOL/Syntax.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Syntax.lagda.md):94).

**Step 3 — the smallest substructure and its size (Devlin 5.4).** For any
X ⊆ L_α there is a unique smallest M ≺ L_α with X ⊆ M, and
|M| = max(|X|, ω) ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1357-1360).
The cardinal half is literally one sentence in Devlin: "Since ℒ∈X has
max(|X|, ω) many formulas, we clearly have |M| = max(|X|, ω)"
([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1360). Hypotheses: Step 2's hull
plus the formula-count |Formula ⟪X⟫ 1| = max(|X|, ω) — the cardinal chapter.

**Step 4 — bounded subsets of κ lie in L_κ (Devlin 5.5).** Assume V = L. Let κ
be a cardinal; if x ⊆ L_α for some α < κ then x ∈ L_κ
([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1373-1375). Proof: pick a limit
λ ≥ κ with x ∈ L_λ; by 5.4 take M ≺ L_λ minimal with L_α ∪ {x} ⊆ M and
|M| = |L_α|; by condensation π: M ≅ L_γ; since L_α ∪ {x} is a transitive
subset of M, π fixes it pointwise (5.2(ii)), so π(x) = x and x ∈ L_γ; by
1.1(vii), |L_α| = |α| and |L_γ| = |γ|, hence |γ| = |α| < κ, so γ < κ (κ a
cardinal), and x ∈ L_κ ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1376-1385).
Hypotheses: 5.4; condensation 5.2 with its transitive-fixing clause (W5, whose
collapse half is probed GO and whose crossing is the W1′ obligation,
[l3.31-ivprobe-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ivprobe-report.md):165-168,
§4.1-4.2); 1.1(vii); the "γ < κ" step (κ a cardinal — cardinal chapter);
ordinal arithmetic to choose λ (boundingOrd machinery, delivered).

**Step 5 — GCH (Devlin 5.6).** V = L implies GCH: by 5.5, P(κ) ⊆ L_{κ+} for
all infinite cardinals κ; by 1.1(vii), |L_{κ+}| = |κ+| = κ+; hence
|P(κ)| ≤ κ+; the reverse inequality is Cantor (|P(κ)| > κ, and κ+ is the least
cardinal above κ) ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1386-1394).
Hypotheses: 5.5; successor cardinals κ+ exist and are cardinals (cardinal
chapter); Cantor's theorem (cardinal chapter); 1.1(vii) at κ+. The packaging
"L ⊨ GCH" additionally needs the internalized predicates Card, κ+, and
|P(κ)| = κ+ written in the object language and the sentence's satisfaction
proved at the model — the GCH-assembly chapter, comparable in shape to the
`L ⊨ ZF` assembly ([L/Model.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Model.lagda.md):99-105),
with Devlin 5.7's "ZF ⊢ (V=L → GCH)" route ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1394-1401)
as the alternative packaging that still needs the internal sentence.

**Step 0, the hidden load — Devlin 1.1(vii): |L_γ| = |γ| for γ ≥ ω.**
([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):113, proof :217-230.) Steps 4 and 5
consume it twice (at α and at κ+), and its proof is a tower induction whose
successor case is the formula-count — "the set of formulas of ℒ∈A is easily
seen to have cardinality |A|" ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):217) —
and whose limit case is |⋃_{β<λ} L_β| ≤ |λ| ([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):219-226),
i.e. product/sum cardinal arithmetic (κ·κ = κ). **This is the cardinality
theory the brief asks me to price separately, and nobody has costed it:**
`|·|`, initial ordinals, κ+, max(|X|,ω), the formula-count, Cantor, and the
tower induction are all absent from `src/` (grep, §2).

### Where the delivered reflection machinery's shape forces a different formulation

The task names the finding; here is its exact reach. The reflection package is
`L.Reflect` + `L.ReflectFo` (not `src/FOL/` — the machinery lives at
[L/Reflect.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Reflect.lagda.md):44 and
[L/ReflectFo.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/ReflectFo.lagda.md):39; FOL supplies the
syntax, semantics, Levy hierarchy, and relativization substrate underneath).
Its shapes: `ClosedFor β ψ` is witness-closure for **one** matrix
([Reflect.lagda.md:253-254](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Reflect.lagda.md)),
`Ladder` closes **one** matrix along an ω-chain ([Reflect.lagda.md:256](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Reflect.lagda.md)),
`mkReflect` reflects **one whole formula** with φ **outside** the quantifier:
`(γ ⊨ φ) ≡ (γ ⊨ relativize (LsetS β oβ) φ)`
([ReflectFo.lagda.md:525-530](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/ReflectFo.lagda.md)), and closure is
**not inherited by larger stages** — the chapter's own prose: "closure is not
inherited by larger stages: enlarging the stage admits more environments to
answer for" ([ReflectFo.lagda.md:7-10](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/ReflectFo.lagda.md)).

The consequence is exactly the ivprobe's: this shape "cannot be iterated into a
hull without a new simultaneous-in-a-finite-set form"
([l3.31-ivprobe-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ivprobe-report.md):384-385).
Concretely, the natural textbook alternative to Devlin 5.3 — build the hull as
an iterated Skolem closure M₀ = X, M_{n+1} = M_n plus witnesses, M = ⋃ M_n —
would need, at each round, witness-closure for **every** formula at **every**
tuple from the growing hull. No delivered piece supplies that: `ClosedFor` is
per-matrix, `Single`'s ladder is per-matrix, `ReflectFo`'s joint ladder is per-
**formula** (all matrices of one fixed formula at once), and even that closure
cannot be reused at a larger stage. The direct definable-hull formulation
(Devlin 5.3) avoids the iteration entirely — elementarity is *proved of the
defined M* via Tarski's criterion, not *constructed into it* — so the honest
route is to follow Devlin 5.3 verbatim and to prove 5.1's criterion as its own
formula induction. That is the one formulation change the reflection shape
forces: **do not reach for the reflection package in the hull chapter at all;
it is the wrong instrument, and the right instrument is the least-witness trick
plus the criterion induction.**

A second, smaller formulation fork is the role of <_L in ψ. The textbook's ψ
names "v1 <L v0" as a formula of the extended language
([dev2.txt](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt):1349). The internal order
**formula** σ_< at the set carrier is W3's unprobed residual (the Rud-side
chapter explicitly disclaims internality: "Nothing internal is claimed… the
internal reading… belongs to a later chapter",
[L/Rud/Order.lagda.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Rud/Order.lagda.md):1016-1019; W3 band
0.30-0.70k, [l3.31-ivprobe-report.md](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ivprobe-report.md):500-510).
But the meta well-order at the exact carrier exists: `orderAt α oα :
SWO (Mem (Lset α))` at every ordinal
([L/Choice/Order.lagda.md:690-693](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Choice/Order.lagda.md),
via [L/Choice/Table.lagda.md:121](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Choice/Table.lagda.md)), and
`leastOf` ([L/WellOrder/Base.lagda.md:165](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/WellOrder/Base.lagda.md))
extracts least elements. So there are two honest formulations of Step 2:
(i) Devlin's exact ψ-formula, which consumes W3's σ_<; or (ii) a meta-pick
formulation — M = { the `leastOf`-least L_α-witness of φ | φ ∈ Formula ⟪X⟫ 1 },
with the elementarity proof still running through Tarski's criterion — which
consumes only the delivered `orderAt`/`leastOf` and changes nothing in Steps
3-5, since everything downstream is meta-level. Option (ii) is cheaper and
removes a W7→W3 dependency; it should be settled by a statement probe (gate B
below), not by assumption.

### The gate: the successor clause of |L_γ| = |γ|, in surjection form

The probe attacks the single clause whose truth and cost decide the cardinal
chapter's size: Devlin 1.1(vii)'s successor case ([dev2.txt:217-218](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/literature/dev2.txt)),
i.e. |L_{α+1}| = |𝒟ₒ L_α| ≤ |L_α|·ω = |α| for infinite α. It is stated in pure
surjection form so it needs **no** new cardinal vocabulary — the targets are
staterable today.

**Target statements** (module `ProbeCard`, over `L.Constructible`'s tower, one
fixed infinite ordinal parameter `α` with `⟨ ω ∈ˢ α ⟩`):

- **T1 (the count, syntax-bound):** an injection
  `Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1 ↪ Σ[ k ∈ ℕ ] (Formula (⊥*) k × (⟪ A ⟫ ^ k))`
  for any set `A` — every formula over the constant type ⟪A⟫ is a constant-free
  shape plus a finite tuple of constants. Pure syntax; uses
  [FOL/Syntax.lagda.md:94](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/FOL/Syntax.lagda.md). (Do **not** count
  through `Name`: its environment is function-valued,
  [L/Choice/Name.lagda.md:334](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Choice/Name.lagda.md), so |Name| is
  not max(|A|,ω); `defSet`'s surjection onto `𝒟ₒ A` is free via `Def-spec`
  refl, [Definability.lagda.md:137](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Definability.lagda.md).)
- **T2 (the product bound):** for infinite `α`, an injection
  `Σ[ k ∈ ℕ ] (⟪ α ⟫ ^ k) ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` — finite tuples over an infinite ordinal are
  bounded by the ordinal itself. This is the κ·ω = κ core; the honest work is
  the ordinal-pairing theorem (α×α ≃ α), which has no in-tree precedent.
- **T3 (the payoff, one clause of 1.1(vii)):** given the induction hypothesis as
  a bijection between `⟪ α ⟫` and `⟪ Lset α ⟫` (i.e. an injection each way),
  construct an injection `⟪ 𝒟ₒ (Lset α) ⟫ ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` — note `⟪ 𝒟ₒ A ⟫ =
  Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1` definitionally, since `𝒟ₒ A = sett (Formula ⟪ A ⟫ 1) defSet`
  ([Definability.lagda.md:114](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Definability.lagda.md)) — hence, by
  `Lset-suc` ([L/Axioms/Basic.lagda.md:196](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/L/Axioms/Basic.lagda.md)),
  `⟪ Lset (sucV α) ⟫ ↪ ⟪ α ⟫` (and, under the standing LEM, the matching
  surjection `⟪ α ⟫ ↠ ⟪ Lset (sucV α) ⟫`). Composition of T1+T2 with the IH.

**Stop-line: 400 code lines** (the standing D-1 budget), one agda process at a
time, `GHCRTS="-A64m -I0 -M12g"` (the wave's memory discipline), no postulate,
no hole, no `TERMINATING`.
