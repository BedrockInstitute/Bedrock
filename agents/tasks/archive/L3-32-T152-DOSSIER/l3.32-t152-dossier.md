## 1. frame (框架，フレーム)

A reusable formula shape in the internal language. `eqFrame` is the frame every
later graph applies once.

Current usage:

- zh 框架：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:15` (等词框架)，`:78` (框架体)；`src/L/TowerGraph.lagda.md:22` (内化框架)，`:790`；`src/L/Rud/Describe.lagda.md:1613` (对值框架)；`src/L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md:75` (等词框架).
- ja フレーム：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:17`，`:84` (等号フレーム).
- Code: `eqFrame` at `src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:111`.

Literature: Chinese modal logic renders Kripke frame as 框架 (克里普克框架).
Sources: the PKU modal logic notes
(http://wangyanjing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ML.pdf) and the zh Wikipedia
article on relational semantics
(https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%85%B3%E7%B3%BB%E8%AF%AD%E4%B9%89). Japanese
modal logic uses フレーム the same way; see the Kripke semantics article
(https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%97%E3%82%AD%E3%83%BB%E3%83%A2%E3%83%87%E3%83%AB)
and the TPP2024 slides on modal logic in Lean
(https://www.math.nagoya-u.ac.jp/~garrigue/tpp2024/slides/noguchi.pdf).

Assessment: the literature attests 框架 / フレーム for a different "frame", the
Kripke frame. This book has no modal logic, so the collision is cross-field and
weak. The metaphor matches: a skeleton that instances fill. The tree already uses
框架 in three chapters.

RECOMMENDATION: keep 框架 / フレーム；the renderings are attested for logic's
"frame", the Kripke collision is out of scope, and three chapters already use them.

## 2. graph layer (图层，グラフ層)

The layer of operation-graph formulas, assembled at a transitive carrier.

Current usage:

- zh 图层：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:15`，`:74`，`:76`；`src/L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md:18`，`:75`，`:457`.
- ja グラフ層：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:17`，`:80`，`:82`.
- Code: `module Layer` at `src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:88`.

Decomposition: the head "layer" is ruled，层 / 層 (`dev/glossary.toml:828`). The
modifier "graph" is established: the graph of a function is 所有有序对组成的集合，named 图形 or 图像 or 图
(https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E5%87%BD%E6%95%B0%E5%9B%BE%E5%BD%A2); Japanese
uses グラフ. So the compound question is composition only, and both sides compose
the ruled heads correctly.

Collisions, for the owner to weigh:

- zh 图层 is the lexicalized image-editing word, a Photoshop layer
  (https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%9B%BE%E5%B1%82/1494948). A reader can misread
  the compound as that single word. Context recovers the reading: the chapters
  say 诸图，十六运算图，图读式 throughout.
- ja 層 alone also names a sheaf, so グラフ層 can read as "sheaf of graphs" to a
  mathematician. The alternative グラフの層 reads more like a sheaf, not less.
  This collision belongs to the ruled head 層，not to this compound.

RECOMMENDATION: keep 图层 / グラフ層；both compose ruled heads, six sites already
use the zh form, and no alternative removes the collisions without new cost.

## 3. membership formula (隶属公式，所属式)

A formula that characterizes membership in an operation's value.

Current usage:

- zh 隶属公式：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:15`，and 隶属 alone at `:177`, `:252`,
  `:539`; the wider tree uses 隶属 for the ∈ relation, for example
  `src/Everything.lagda.md:171` and `src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:893` (成员隶属).
- ja 所属式：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:17`，`:541`；所属 alone at `:82`.
- Code: `mem0` to `mem15` at `src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:576`.

Literature, zh: Chinese texts call the element-set relation 隶属关系，read 属于.
Sources: the Tianjin University discrete mathematics notes
(https://cic.tju.edu.cn/faculty/hyh/discretemathematics/01.pdf) and a standard
set-theory primer that opens with 集合与元素间的关系是隶属关系
(https://blog.csdn.net/Shao_yihao/article/details/120767776). So 隶属 is attested
for exactly this relation, and 隶属公式 composes it with the ruled 公式.

Literature, ja: Japanese names the relation 属する，with the noun forms 所属 and
帰属；the element article uses 帰属関係
(https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%85%83_(%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6)), and school
materials use 所属 freely
(https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%9B%86%E5%90%88%E9%96%93%E3%81%AE%E9%96%A2%E4%BF%82%E3%82%92%E8%A1%A8%E3%81%99%E8%A8%98%E5%8F%B7).
Both are attested; neither is wrong.

Composition note: the glossary rules formula = 論理式 on the ja side, so strict
composition gives 所属論理式. Japanese compounds do drop 論理 into 式 freely
(関係式，帰属式 pattern), and 所属式 stays transparent. The owner should rule
whether the short form is acceptable, because the chapter also writes 式 alone for
"formula" in ja prose (`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:543`).

RECOMMENDATION: keep 隶属公式 / 所属式；zh is attested composition, ja is a
transparent short form, with 所属論理式 as the strict-composition alternative.

## 4. equality frame (等词框架，等号フレーム)

The frame for internal equality; the single place the layer argues extensionality.

Current usage:

- zh 等词框架：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:15`，`:78`；`src/L/Rud/StepStory.lagda.md:75`.
- ja 等号フレーム：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:17`，`:84`.
- Code: `eqFrame` at `src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:111`.

Decomposition: the head "frame" is term 1. The modifier is the equality symbol of
first-order logic. Chinese logic names it 等词，as in 带等词的一阶逻辑. Sources:
the zh Wikipedia first-order logic article
(https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E4%B8%80%E9%98%B6%E9%80%BB%E8%BE%91) and
textbook notes that introduce 等词 as the special binary predicate
(https://www.cnblogs.com/qixingzhi/p/18984928). Japanese names the symbol 等号.
The zh 等词 versus ja 等号 split follows the glossary's deliberate-divergence
pattern (满足/充足，一致性/無矛盾性).

RECOMMENDATION: keep 等词框架 / 等号フレーム；both modifiers are the attested
names of the equality symbol, and the compound follows term 1.

Current usage:

- ja 論理和：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:17`，`:613`.
- Code: `bigOr` at `src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:640`.

Literature, zh：析取 is the standard rendering; the zh Wikipedia article names the
operation 逻辑或，also 析取
(https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E9%80%BB%E8%BE%91%E6%88%96), and the term's
etymology is discussed as settled vocabulary
(https://www.zhihu.com/question/41880537). Established; no candidate needed.

Literature, ja: three synonyms are attested：論理和，選言，離接. The ja Wikipedia
article title is 論理和
(https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%AB%96%E7%90%86%E5%92%8C)；選言 is the
philosophical-logic tradition
(https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%AB%96%E7%90%86%E5%92%8C-10127). The in-use 論理和 is
the Wikipedia headword and the common mathematics form. 選言 stays available if
the owner prefers the logic tradition; both are correct.

RECOMMENDATION: keep 析取 / 論理和；both are the established headwords, with 選言
as the attested ja alternative if the owner prefers the logic tradition.

  `src/L/Rud/Images.lagda.md:460`.
- zh 切片 (15 sites, 5 files): `src/L/Rud/Describe.lagda.md:1117`, `:1192` (the

- `src/L/Rud/Images.lagda.md` uses both words for the same object：切片 at `:338`
  and 像片 at `:460`. That is drift inside one file.
- ja スライス：`src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:17`，`:543`.
- Code: `sliceMem` at `src/L/Rud/StepGraph.lagda.md:567`.

Literature, zh: the literature contradicts 像片.

- 像片 means photograph. The zh Wikipedia article 像片 covers only photographs
  (https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%83%8F%E7%89%87), and 全国科学技术名词审定委员会
  fixed 像片 as the standard word form for "photograph" in 2001
  (https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%83%8F%E7%89%87/4947749). No mathematical use
  exists.
- 切片 is the established rendering of "slice" in category theory: slice category
  = 切片范畴 (https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/364284965).
- 截口 is the established rendering for the section of a set in a product, in real
  analysis and descriptive set theory: E_x = {y : (x,y) ∈ E} 的截口，the Fubini
  vocabulary (https://math.fandom.com/zh/wiki/Fubini_%E5%AE%9A%E7%90%86). This is
  the mathematically closest concept to F10.

Literature, ja：スライス is attested, slice category = スライス圏
(https://mathlog.info/articles/PlzBr3dfrELVW9okCX6g). The ja word 切片 is spent
twice over: Japanese mathematics uses it for the intercept of a line, and the
glossary already spends it on segment in 始切片 (`dev/glossary.toml:884`). So
スライス is the safe ja rendering.

Candidates, zh:

- 切片：attested (category theory) and the in-tree majority by 15 sites to 3.
- 截口：attested (analysis, the closest concept), but zero in-tree sites; adopting
  it would move five files instead of two.

RECOMMENDATION: change zh 像片 to 切片；像片 means photograph, the literature and
the in-tree majority both say 切片. Keep ja スライス，which avoids the ja 切片
collisions. The two 像片 files (`StepGraph`, `Images:460`) are the whole repair.

## 10. master (主章，主章)

Project vocabulary: the canonical trilingual source file of a chapter
(`AGENTS.md`, one master `.lagda.md` per module).

Current usage:

- zh 主章：`src/L/TowerGraph.lagda.md:22` (退役主章)，`:790`. StepGraph itself
  does not use the word; the uses live in the same build family.
- ja 主章：zero in-tree sites. TowerGraph has no ja section yet.

Assessment: zh 主章 is a coinage, "the chief chapter text", and it reads
correctly next to 退役 and 章. It collides with nothing; the publishing word for a
master copy，母本，stays available but adds no clarity here. ja 主章 is not an
attested Japanese word; a Japanese reader parses it as kanji composition, "main

alternatives：原本 (the original of a document) or マスター (the loanword). Both
are ordinary；原本 collides with nothing in the glossary.

RECOMMENDATION: keep zh 主章；for ja the owner should choose between 主章
(symmetry, a coinage) and 原本 (natural Japanese), and the dossier leans 主章 for
symmetry since the word is project jargon on both sides.
