## 1. The shape of the cost

**One family, and it is NOT Bridge's family.** Four near-duplicate concrete
pairing-injectivity proofs carry 88 percent of SquareLaw's own time. In the
clean control run the four top rows sum to 711,383 ms of the 810,704 ms
profile Total (87.8%); every other definition is under 39 s. The four are two
byte-mirror pairs, exactly as T47/T86 described:

- `h₀-inj` twice (`Core` and `InitialCore`, byte-identical bodies): the
  injectivity of the pair fibering at `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫ × ⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫`;
- `pair-eq` twice (`FiniteBase.no-inj-finite`'s `g-inj` and
  `CoreAtω.no-inj-finite-ω`'s `g-inj`): the pair-path equality
  `f Xx ≡ f Xy` inside the counting chase, at `⟪ α ⟫` and `⟪ ω ⟫` shapes.

The shape is stable across every run: T86's profile gave the same four rows at
761.5 s of 862 s, my baseline gave 783.0 s of 886 s (under a concurrent second
process), and the clean control gave 711.4 s of 810.7 s.

## 3. Whether T86's diagnosis held

**Held in shape, and the mechanism is now pinned by measurement.** T86 said
the cost is "states memberships at concrete tower positions rather than at
variable indices", the I-4/I-5 class. The four hot definitions confirm the
concrete-position reading at the STATEMENT level, not the body level. The
`h₀-inj` statement is
`h₀ p r pr ≡ h₀ p r' pr' → r ≡ r'` with
`h₀ : (p : PairA) → (r : PairA) → r ≺' p → ⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫ × ⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫`
(`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:372-375`), where
`γp p = ⟪ α ⟫↪ (max' (fst p) (snd p))` is a stuck concrete tower and
`⟪ a ⟫` is the member type `Σ[ x ∈ S ] (x ∈ₛ a)`
(`src/V/Presentation.lagda.md:31-38`), so `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫` normalizes the
`sucV` union tower. The `pair-eq` context is the same class:
`Xx : ⟪ ω ⟫ = fiber ω (...)` and `Xx : ⟪ α ⟫ = fiber α (...)`
(`src/L/Ordinal/SquareLaw.lagda.md:166-169` and `:835-838`), where `fiber ω`
feeds the `ω` union tower.

The one correction to T86's wording: `pair-eq` is not itself an injectivity
proof but the pair-path equality inside the injectivity chase
(`f Xx ≡ f Xy` from the two coordinates). The family is still one: both
`pair-eq` rows and both `h₀-inj` rows are the same theorem at two carrier
shapes.

## 4. The fix, measured

**SquareLaw does NOT respond like Bridge. The Bridge-shaped read-lemma
restatement is neutral at best for `h₀-inj` and actively harmful when sealed,
and the generic-carrier restatement for `pair-eq` heap-exhausts at `-M8g`.
The honest answer: this 856 seconds is expensive to remove; no cheap fix
exists within the 60-line seal stop-line.** Every claim below is a measured
run, all with `GHCRTS=-M8g agda --profile=definitions -i /tmp/t88 -i src
/tmp/t88/ProbeT88*.lagda.md` against scratch copies of the module (renamed,
body identical apart from the stated change).

### 4a. `h₀-inj`: the Bridge restatement (T86's cheapest probe), measured

The probe adds a generic lemma stated at a VARIABLE carrier
(`FiberPairInj.fiber-pair-inj : (B : S) {a b a' b' : ⟪ α ⟫} → (qa : ⟨ ⟪ α ⟫↪ a ∈ˢ B ⟩) → ... → ((fiber B qa .fst , fiber B qb .fst) ≡ (fiber B qa' .fst , fiber B qb' .fst)) → (a ≡ a') × (b ≡ b')`, +15 lines, the exact analogue of Bridge's "state the read lemma where consumers use it and absorb the normalization once"), and restates both `h₀-inj` bodies as one-line instantiations of it (net zero lines there). Two variants:

| variant | wall | profile Total | `h₀-inj` rows | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| control (unmodified) | 13:32.84 | 810,704 ms | 189.2 + 171.4 s | baseline |
| probe 1c: helper TRANSPARENT | 13:35.98 | 813,693 ms | 190.5 + 171.8 s | neutral, exit 0 |
| probe 1: helper sealed `opaque` (the exact Bridge shape) | >26:06 clean, interrupted (exit 130) | none | none | at least 1.9x slower |

The transparent variant is decisive: with the body reduced to a one-line
application of a generic lemma whose own profile row is below 10 ms (it is
essentially free at a variable carrier), `h₀-inj` STILL pays its ~190 s. The
cost therefore sits in elaborating the statement itself, whose type mentions
the concrete tower `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫`, not in per-site body normalization by
consumers. Bridge's fix worked because its expensive normalization sat in
consumers re-using a read lemma; here the normalization sits in the
statement, which the restatement leaves untouched. The opaque variant shows
the seal interaction is worse: even under clean conditions the sealed run
was interrupted at 26:06 with no profile rows (raw log
`/tmp/t88-squarelaw-probe1-clean.log`), so the P-c/R-36 seal pattern that
cures Bridge actively hurts this statement shape.

### 4b. `pair-eq`: the generic-carrier restatement (R-35 shape), measured

The probe moves the entire counting chase into a generic module stated at a
VARIABLE carrier (`NoInj (A : Type ℓ) (into : (m : ℕ) → ⟪ # m ⟫ → A)
(into-inj : ...)`, body = the current `no-inj-finite` chase with `into` for
`numeral-into-α`/`numeral-into-ω`), and reduces `no-inj-finite` and
`no-inj-finite-ω` to one-line instantiations. Two runs:

| variant | result |
|---|---|
| probe 2: generic module + both instantiations | **Heap exhausted at `-M8g`** after 10:26.15 wall (621 s user), raw log `/tmp/t88-squarelaw-probe2.log` |
| probe 2a: generic module ALONE, both bodies original | **Heap exhausted at `-M8g`** after 26:10.80 wall (1563 s user), raw log `/tmp/t88-squarelaw-probe2a.log` |

The generic module's own elaboration is heap-poisoned, so the variable-carrier
form is not cheap for THIS theorem (T59/T85's 12.8 s probe measured a
different theorem, the lower injection; it does not transfer to the counting
chase with `toFin-inj`/`fromFin-inj`/`factor-inj`). Per C-12/P-i both exits
are wall events and are reported, not rerun.

### 4c. What the 856 seconds would cost to remove

Both remaining levers are builds, not seals, and both are priced only:

- **Statement reshaping (R-35 small-index) of the `h₀`/`comp₀` chain**: restate
  `h₀` and its consumers at variable index so no statement carries
  `⟪ sucV (γp p) ⟫`. The generic lemma shows the variable form is free, but
  the chain (`h₀`, `h₀-inj`, `comp₀`, `comp₀-inj`, `exclude`) would all move,
  an internal-interface restructure far over the 60-line seal stop-line. It
  does not change the exported `bound`/`bound-inj`/`Initial` shapes, but it
  was not built here.
- **Deduplication of the byte-mirror pairs**: `Core`/`InitialCore` cannot
  share `h₀-inj` directly (the modules' hypothesis lists differ and
  InitialCore lacks Core's `ih`/`no-inj-down`), and the pair-eq pair's share
  is exactly the heap-poisoned generic module. Not a cheap lever.

The measured floor if the four rows disappeared is about 100 s for the
module (810.7 minus 711.4); the removal itself is not funded by any measured
cheap change.
