## 2. The tower and its two facts

The tower is one recursion, exactly the brief's closed form:
`γ α = +ω (⋃_{δ<α} γ δ)`, sealed opaque at birth with `γ-compute` as the
only unfolding (P-c/R-38, the Constructible `Lset` pattern). One equation
covers zero (`⋃` over the empty index is `∅`, so `γ ∅ = +ω ∅ = ω`, proved as
`U-zero` and checked by the control `ctl-base`), successors (the running sup
collapses to `γ β`, so `γ (sucV β) = +ω (γ β)`, the next ω-block) and limits
(the brief's `γ(λ) := +ω (⋃_{β<λ} γ β)` verbatim).

**The two facts the induction needs of it** (`γ-lim`, `γ-suc`):

1. `γ-lim : (α : S) → ⟨ isLimit (γ α) ⟩` is **unconditional**: every value is
   `+ω` of the running sup, the sup is an ordinal because every family member
   is by the recursion (`γ-ord`, also unconditional), and the delivered
   `+ω-limit` does the rest. This is what makes `Sset (γ β)` a J-level
   (`Jset (γ β) (γ-lim β) ≡ Sset (γ β)`, `Hierarchy:545`), so BlockPowLim and
   the full switch apply at the tower's own index.
2. `γ-suc : (β : S) → IsOrd β → γ (sucV β) ≡ +ω (γ β)`: the union over
   `sucV β` collapses to `γ β`: below by `γ-mono` (the tower is monotone in
   the index), at the top by the union's own family membership. This is what
   turns BlockPowLim's `+ω`-step into the tower's next value.

The recon's tower band (0.10–0.35k) did **not** price the monotonicity kit,
and the kit is the probe's largest surprise: see the D-8 wall, §6. With the
kit, the tower and both facts are 141 code lines, inside the naive band and
at its lower-middle.
