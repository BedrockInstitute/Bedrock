`[T127]` is green at `ω` precisely because `ω` has no limit ordinals below it,
so its segments never met one. At a general `β` they do, and two things are
unmeasured:

1. **The honest segment at a limit index**: the segment's value there must be

   induction, which does not reach past `ω`. What replaces it.
