**Verdict, compressed.** R5, re-found on `isJ`, prices at a **naive 2.0-3.7k**
trophy spend (re-proving the Def-specific proof content over the delivered J
tower, re-pointing the tower-generic consumers, re-homing choice, and the

naive band sits **fully under the 21.5k tripwire** (margin ≈ 1.0k at the top);

R2′'s probe-priced 2.25-4.3k), it is cheaper on its *endpoint*, because it
retires the 5,970-line bridge cluster and the 26.5k-line old trees instead of

caveat is that the naive low end assumes the delivered engine (switch,
