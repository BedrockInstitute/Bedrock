## THE QUESTION, and it is a truth question first

**D-10 binds hardest here.** Before pricing the principle, ask whether
it is TRUE at the generality the four sites need:

1. State the general principle as a type: what exactly must hold at
   zero, what must transfer at successors, and what carries at limits?
2. **Check each site against it.** The carried objects differ. Does the
   principle abstract over the carried object, or does each site need
   a different carrying condition? A principle that fits three sites
   and not the fourth is worth knowing precisely.
3. If it unifies: price it, and say what each site pays to instantiate.
4. **If it does not unify, say so and close C5.** That is the useful
   answer at this point in the campaign, and it is worth as much as a
   saving: an open option nobody can act on costs attention every time
   a build is planned.
