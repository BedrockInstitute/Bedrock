## 1. D-10 first: the two targets, truth-checked at the intended
generality

### Residue 1: `Q-lim`, the reshaped induction's limit clause

```
Q-lim-statement = (l : S) → IsOrd l → ⟨ isLimit l ⟩
  → ((δ : S) → ⟨ δ ∈ˢ l ⟩ → ⟨ Lset δ ∈ˢ Sset (γ δ) ⟩)
  → ⟨ Lset l ∈ˢ Sset (γ l) ⟩
```

**Target, at the intended generality:** at a limit `l`, the tower
hypothesis (every stage is a member at its own tower index) forces the
limit stage `Lset l` to be a member of the rud level at the tower's limit
value `γ l = +ω (U l)`, `U l = ⋃_{δ<l} γ δ`.

**Truth check: TRUE.** This is Devlin VI.2.3's limit step read at the
closed form (dev6.txt:1655-1670, the initial-segment formula with
`dom(f) ∈ α` at 1659-1662): `L_λ = {x ∈ J_λ | ∃f ∈ J_λ, f an L-tower
initial segment with dom f ∈ λ and x ∈ ⋃ ran f}`, so `L_λ` is
Σ₁-definable over `J_λ`, hence `L_λ ∈ Def(J_λ) ⊆ J_{λ+1}`; at the closed
form `γ l = +ω (U l)` plays `J_{λ+1} = S_{ω(λ+1)}`. Instance check
`l = ω`: the claim is `Lset ω ∈ Sset (γ ω) = Sset (+ω (U ω)) =
Sset (ω² + ω)`; classically `L_ω = J_1 ∈ J_{ω+1} = S_{ω²+ω}`, and the
finite stages `Lset n ∈ Sset (γ n)` are exactly the tower's own successor
clause. No correction to the statement.

**Route check (this is where D-10 has teeth again): the recorded route is
incomplete.** T35 recorded the proof route as "the initial-segment face at
the rud carrier + `full-switch-⊇` + a sup-is-a-limit fact + `Lset`'s own
union structure". Walking that route against the delivered objects shows
the composition `Lset l ≡ defSet (Sset (U l)) σ` cannot be built from the
delivered `LevelSigma` face:

1. **The range read is the wrong read for the stage.** The delivered
   `read-off` decodes `Rg` to `x ∈ran f` (`x` is a *second component* of a
   pair in `f`), so `Elem x` says "x is a value of an initial segment".
   Membership in `Lset l` needs `x ∈ ⋃ ran f` (x lies in a value), and
   even the honest sequence's values are the stages, not their members.
   With the delivered read, `defSet u σ` is a set of *stages*, i.e. the
   family `{Lset δ : δ < l}`, not `Lset l`.
2. **The approximation story is the three-clause fragment.** The delivered
   `Approx` at the rud carrier is pairhood + single-valuedness + the zero
   clause only; the limit clause and the ordinal domain bound are the
   pieces T32 measured as omitted (T32 §4: "the limit clause and the
   ordinal domain bound ... priced in the report"). Without the domain
   bound (`dom f ∈ l`) the out direction fails: `x ∈ ran f` for an
   arbitrary three-clause `f ∈ K` forces only `x ∈ Sset (U l)` (by
   transitivity), never `x = Lset δ` for `δ < l`. A concrete
   counter-instance to the *weak* form: with `K = u`, the two-pair set
   `{pr ∅ ∅, pr (Lset δ₀) (Lset δ₀)}` satisfies all three clauses and
   `DefStep` for any carrier member `Lset δ₀`, yet its range is a single
   stage, and nothing forces a general `x ∈ ran f` to be a stage below
   `l`.
3. **The honest-sequence alternative needs the carried sequence in the
   level.** Choosing `K` as the set of honest initial segments makes the
   out direction work (a value of an honest segment of length `< l` is a
   stage below `l`), but the *in* direction then needs the honest segment
   `⟨Lset δ | δ < ν⟩` to be a member of the carrier `Sset (U l)` for every
   `ν < l`, which is exactly the carried-sequence fact; and the carrier
   cannot name the bound `l` (`l ∉ Sset (U l)` in general, e.g. `l = ω²`
   with `U l = ω³`), so the domain bound cannot be written into the
   formula at all.

The corrected route is therefore: the tower story at the rud carrier must
be extended by the limit clause and the ordinal domain bound (T32's
omitted pieces, priced at the real tower by T36 at 168 and 148 in-fence
lines respectively), the range read must be the union-of-range read, and
the honest initial segments must be shown to lie in the carrier (the
carried-sequence fact). All three are undelivered; the price is measured
in §3.

### Residue 2: `below-lim`, the surviving direction's limit membership

```
BelowLim = (γ : S) → (limγ : ⟨ isLimit γ ⟩) → (β : S) → IsOrd β
         → ⟨ isLimit β ⟩ → ⟨ β ∈ˢ γ ⟩ → ⟨ Sset β ∈ˢ Lset γ ⟩
```

**Target, at the intended generality:** for limit `β ∈ γ` with `γ` a
limit ordinal, the rud level at `β` is a member of the constructible stage
at `γ`.

**Truth check: TRUE.** Classically `S_β = J_{β/ω}`-in-scale and
`J_α ∈ J_{α+1} ⊆ L_{ω(α+1)} ⊆ L_γ` when `ω(α+1) ≤ γ`; at the same index
`S_β ∈ L_γ` holds because the rud level is Σ₁-definable over its own
stage and `β+1 ≤ γ`. Instance checks: `β = ω` (`S_ω = L_ω ∈ L_{ω+1} ⊆
L_{ω·2}`); `β = ω·2` (`S_{ω·2} = J_2 ∈ J_3 = S_{ω·3} ⊆ L_{ω·3}`); the
finite pattern `S_2 = L_3` and `S_3 ∈ L_5` shows the statement has room
past the naive "one level up" form (which is false: `S_3 ∉ L_4`, a
16-element level against a 21+-element rud stage). No correction to the
statement.

**Route check.** T35 recorded the route as "the S-hierarchy's sequence as
a member of the right constructible stage, Devlin's carried sequence
`⟨S_τ | τ ≤ ν⟩ ∈ L_{ωα}`". That route is correct in direction but the
object is not delivered: the limit step of an induction for `below-lim`
collects `W = {Sset (sucV δ) : δ < β}`, every member of `W` lies in
`Lset β` (the `Below` successor memberships, each requiring only the
smaller `below-lim` instances the induction supplies), and
`Sset β = ⋃ W`; but `W ∈ Lset γ` requires the family as an element of a
constructible stage, which is the carried-S-sequence fact, and the
cofinality of the stages in `γ` blocks any common-stage shortcut. The
same wall blocks `Sset β = defSet (Lset β) φ` (the definability route),
whose formula would need the bound `β` (`β ∉ Lset β`).
