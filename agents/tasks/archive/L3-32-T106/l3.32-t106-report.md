55.2 s, so `[T104]`'s acceptance reading applies verbatim: "the concrete
wrapper re-pays the walk and B1 is the export-changing alternative."

**Overall: RED.** No export-preserving lift exists in the measured tree:
reshape A (pieces 1+3) makes the module worse, and reshape B2 (piece 2,
export-preserving) makes the row worse. The only green lever is the
export-changing `Crossing` parameterization, which `[T96]` already priced at

stands; the seconds it attributed to each piece are confirmed as not

## 3. Piece 3's verdict: is the 54 s position or content?

**Neither pure position nor pure content: it splits. About 31 s of the 54 s
is the concrete-index position (removable by variable indices, at the price
of a new row), and about 23 s survives the telescope because the formula
TREE, not just the indices, is concrete. The gate verdict is NO: the seconds
do not leave the module, and the export-preserving recovery of the concrete
statement re-pays the walk.**

The measured facts, all from this dispatch's runs (control vs reshape A on
the same machine):

| position | seconds | where it lands |
|---|---:|---|
| the delivered `Lset-only` application at the concrete indices (control, inside `ambientOnly-from`) | 54.1 s (as `[T96]` measured, re-derived in section 4) | `ambientOnly-from`'s row |
| the same application at variable indices `w b` (reshape A, `AmbientOnlyFrom.from`) | 23.4 s | a NEW row, `AmbientOnlyFrom.from` |
| the whole concrete instantiation (reshape A, `ambientOnly-from`) | 209.5 s (vs 130.3 s control) | the same row, made worse |

The 54.1 to 23.4 s drop shows the position hypothesis is PARTIALLY right:
moving the indices to module parameters removes about 31 s. But the residual
23.4 s is content in a specific sense: `LsetGraphAt w b` unfolds to a fixed
constructor tree (`∃̇ (ApproxAt zero (suc b) ∧̇ StepAt (suc w) (suc b) zero)`,
with `ApproxAt`/`StepAt`/`domAt`/`appAt`/`extAt` all definitions, not
constructors, `src/L/Coding/Sequence.lagda.md:59-66,361-362`,
`src/L/Coding/Model.lagda.md:160-161,278-279,662-663`), so the satisfaction
head is NOT stuck at variable indices: `⟨ γ ⊨ LsetGraphAt w b ⟩` computes
through the concrete tree and only the leaves (`suc b`, `suc w`, the
`lookup`-headed terms) are neutral. Every check of the generic module's
statements still walks the tree, which is the measured 23.4 s. `[T102]`'s
telescope kept the concrete presentation OUT of the module entirely
(`grep -c '⟪'` over the module was 0, `_build/l3.32-t102-report.md:37-39`);
`[T104]`'s telescope abstracted only the indices, so the formula tree
remained concrete in the telescope. That is the mechanism difference the
transplant failed on.

And the concrete instantiation is not a cheap identical-term match: checking
the instantiated `AmbientOnlyFrom.from` application (the projected
`Sʟ`-environment premise meeting `AmbientOnly`'s unprojected `S`-environment
premise at the concrete formula) walks the concrete satisfaction, which is
the ~77 s env conversion re-paid inside `ambientOnly-from` (209.5 s vs
130.3 s). The verdict for piece 3 under `[T104]`'s acceptance reading is NO.
