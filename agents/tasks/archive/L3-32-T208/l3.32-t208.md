## GOAL

The owner's instruction: **pin down the original optimistic 16,000
estimate, because the answer may teach this campaign something about
where it stands now.**

`[T205]` established the provenance: the floor is the optimistic end of
the L3.28 compression survey of 2026-07-31. The survey measured the
baseline at **17,492 non-blank Agda lines over 75 masters** and priced

**Measure them now.** The delivered tree still exists on `main`
(17,496 lines, reproduced this session), so the survey's own laboratory

## WHAT TO DO, per lever

1. **Recover the seven levers verbatim** from the L3.28 record. Quote
   each at `file:line` with the number the survey assigned it.
2. **Locate its content on `main`.** Use `git show main:<path>` and
   `git ls-tree`; the branch is read-only to you and you never check it
   out. For each lever: does the content it names still exist there?
3. **Measure what the lever would ACTUALLY yield**, at the project's
   caliber (non-blank lines inside ` ```agda ` fences). Count the
   content the lever names; check whether its consumers would survive
   the removal (a `rg` over `main`'s tree via `git grep main --`).
   Where the lever says "compress" rather than "remove", say what the
   compressed form would have to be and price it from a delivered
   comparable, marking it survey if none exists.
4. **The ratio.** For each lever: the survey's optimistic figure
   against your measured figure. **The set of ratios is the point of
   this task**: it calibrates how far this project's survey-optimistic
   numbers sat from measurement, in the one case where the answer can
   be checked.
5. **The transfer.** For each lever CLASS that over- or under-promised,
   say whether the same class appears in TODAY's open survey terms (the
   `w3-ac-slice` carve and correctness terms, the Q-lim residue's two
   unmeasured terms, the `choice-rehome` row). **P-l binds: a measured
   ratio elsewhere is a hypothesis here.** Say that plainly and mark
   every transfer as a hypothesis, not a price.
