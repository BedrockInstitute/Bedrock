## 1. What was asked, and what was answered

The brief: *does `LsetGraph` localize from `𝒮ʟ` to the restricted carrier `⟪ Sset γ ⟫`, i.e. is
`hierL λ` a `DefOf.defSet` over `Sset γ`?*

The statement types (P1, `ProbeLocalize:46-49`):

```agda
Localizes : (γ lam : V ℓ) → ⟨ isL lam ⟩ → IsOrd lam → Type (ℓ-suc ℓ)
Localizes γ lam hl ol =
  Σ[ φ ∈ Formula ⟪ Sset γ ⟫ 1 ]
    (DefOf.defSet (Sset γ) φ ≡ fst (hierL lam hl ol))
```

Both sides fit without friction, and that is the first (good) news: `𝒮ʟ` and `⟪ Sset γ ⟫` are
*both* instances of one generic machine. `𝒮ʟ` is `FOL.Absoluteness.Single 𝒮ᵥ isL isL-trans`
(`Coding/Sequence:61`), and `DefOf A` is `InnerSmall (∈ˢ A) ⟪ A ⟫ e ι` (`Definability:78-92`).
Same `Inner` apparatus, two restriction classes. **The architecture is already half-generic**;
what is not generic is everything built on top of the `𝒮ʟ` instance.

## 2. The two halves that are free (P2, P3)

### 2.1 Containment: `hierL λ ⊆ Sset γ` — 14 lines

`hierL-spec` unfolds a member of the internal hierarchy to `Recorded lam z`, i.e.
`z = pr β (Lset β)` for some `β ∈ λ`; `InLevel.pr∈J` (`L/Rud/CodeSet:285`, which is `F9` plus
`Jset-rud`) closes the level under pairing. Fourteen lines, zero walls, no re-derivation.

### 2.2 The assembly: crossing in, `Localizes` out — 48 lines

`ProbeLocalize:105-156` proves, **green**:

> given `φ : Formula ⟪ Sset γ ⟫ 1` and the two crossing readings, `defSet φ ≡ fst (hierL λ)`.

Both inclusions go through `defSet-mem`/`defSet⊆A`/`∈-asFiber` on the `DefOf` side and
`hierL-spec` on the `L.Hierarchy` side; the only new lemma needed was `recL` (13 lines): every
set the internal hierarchy records is constructible, since it is a pair of an ordinal with the
tower's value and the model has pairing (`prʟ`, `prʟ-fst`).

**This is the probe's positive deliverable.** R2′'s limit case is now a two-line residue.

## 3. The crossing, measured

The residue is exactly:

```agda
cross-out : (m : ⟪ Sset γ ⟫) → Sat1 γ φ m → ⟨ Recorded lam (⟪ Sset γ ⟫↪ m) ⟩
cross-in  : (m : ⟪ Sset γ ⟫) → ⟨ Recorded lam (⟪ Sset γ ⟫↪ m) ⟩ → Sat1 γ φ m
```

where `Sat1 γ φ m = ⟨ DefOf._⊨ᵐ_ (Sset γ) (DefOf.ι (Sset γ) m ∷ []) φ ⟩` is the inner
satisfaction at the rud carrier. The dossier's plan is: take `φ` to be `LsetGraph` transported,
and discharge the crossing by absoluteness. Four measurements, all machine-checked in the probe
(`ProbeLocalize:158-249`), price that plan.

`abs₀` chain at the bound `InL`; re-instantiating it at the bound `∈ˢ Sset γ` is free. That is
the *only* free part.

## 5. The hypothesis set consumed (D-10 ledger)

Consumed by the green reduction (P2 + P3):

1. `limγ : ⟨ isLimit γ ⟩` — the rud index is a limit. *(spent by `pr∈J`.)*
2. `h2a : (β : V ℓ) → ⟨ β ∈ˢ lam ⟩ → ⟨ β ∈ˢ Sset γ ⟩` — every ordinal below λ is a member of
   the level.
3. `h2b : (β : V ℓ) → ⟨ β ∈ˢ lam ⟩ → ⟨ Lset β ∈ˢ Sset γ ⟩` — every L-level below λ is a member
   of the level. **This is exactly R2′'s induction hypothesis `Q(β)` for all `β < λ`**, so it
   is supplyable; the probe consumes nothing R2′ cannot give.
4. `hl : ⟨ isL lam ⟩`, `ol : IsOrd lam`.

*Not* consumed by the reduction, and therefore still free to be spent by the crossing:

5. `hierL β ∈ˢ Sset γ` for `β ∈ λ` (the brief's other half of (h2)). The containment and the
   assembly do not need it. It is what `cross-in` will need, and D-21 applies: the witness the
   graph's existential wants *is* the approximation `hierL β`, present in the carrier by this
   hypothesis. That part of the dossier's optimism is confirmed.

**A hypothesis the dossier does not mention, and it is not free.** `φ` may name constants from
`⟪ Sset γ ⟫`, so the natural formula names `λ` and says "β ∈ λ". But under the dossier's index
tower (`γ(0)=ω`, `γ(β+1)=+ω γ(β)`, `γ(λ)=⋃`) we have `γ(λ) = ω·λ`, and `ω·λ = λ` at cofinally
many limits (`λ = ω^ω` is the first). At those λ, `λ ∉ Sset γ(λ)` and **the formula cannot name
λ**. The repair exists and is complementary: at a fixed point the carrier's ordinals are
exactly `λ`, so "every ordinal of the carrier" is the right bound; strictly below a fixed point
`λ ∈ Sset γ(λ)` holds and can be named. But that is a **case split R2′ must carry**, it needs a
`Sset γ ∩ Ord = γ` lemma **which the tree does not have** (no such statement exists under
`src/L/Rud/`), and it is unpriced in the dossier. Flagged as unverified: I did not confirm the
exact ordinal content of `Sset γ` in this tree, only that no lemma states it.

## 9. Lesson candidates

- **(candidate) A formula's Levy class is a machine-measurable gate; measure it before pricing
  any localization.** Four twelve-line counting functions and one `refl` against a wrong
  numeral turned "it looks discharge-able" into "no delivered transfer theorem applies", in
  under ten minutes and three agda runs. The `sz`/`nc`/`nEx`/`nAll` + wrong-numeral trick reads
  the true shape of any closed syntactic object out of the error message.
- **(candidate, extends D-16)** D-16 says to write readers at an abstract transitive carrier.
  `L/Coding` did not, and the bill arrives here as a 6,219-line, 134-reading cone that is
  monomorphic in `𝒮ʟ` for no mathematical reason: `FOL.Absoluteness.Single` and
  `L.Definability.DefOf` are already two instances of one machine. **The cost of skipping D-16
  is not paid in the chapter that skips it; it is paid by the first chapter that needs a second
  carrier.**
- **(candidate, extends D-21)** D-21 held: the witness `cross-in` wants *is* `hierL β`, present
  by hypothesis. The dossier was right about that and it is still not enough, because the
  witness being present does not make the *description* readable at the smaller carrier.
  "The approximation's target is the witness" prices the witness, not the reading.
