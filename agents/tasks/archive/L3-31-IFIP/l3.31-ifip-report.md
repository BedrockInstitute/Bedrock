## 6. Extrapolation: I-6a / I-6f and the ×3 → ×1.3 question

The two rows are I-6a (σ-form packaging, 400-750 naive) and I-6f (Levy
certification at birth, 200-600 naive), together **600-1,350 naive**, class U
at ×3 ([l3.31-ifrecon.md:326-331](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/_build/l3.31-ifrecon.md)).
The full move to ×1.3 would cut the calibrated band by
(3−1.3)×600-1,350 = **1.0-2.3k** (ifrecon §8.5's 1.0-2.6k, close).

What this probe measures: the **reading/instantiation layer** of the generic
cone. The clause is carrier-generic at ≤ ×1.36 (amortizing toward ×1.0),
hypothesis-free, and the second-carrier instantiation is one line given
`Single`. That layer is the clause-scale core of what I-6a's "packaging"
describes, and it is now probed: **it moves from ×3 to ×1.3**.

What this probe does NOT measure: (i) the σ-form statement content itself
(the K(u)-binding set and adequacy, new statement work in every architecture)
and (ii) I-6f's certificate content (quantifier-structure certification of
the cone's combinators). D-16 is supported at clause scale — the reading
carries no Levy obligation, so I-6f's certificates are due only where
satisfactions cross carriers (W1′'s other half), not in the reading layer —
but the certificate lemmas themselves remain unprobed.

**Verdict on the band: partial move.** The reading/instantiation sub-layer of
I-6a/I-6f is measured and re-classifies to ×1.3; the certificate sub-layer
keeps ×3 until a birth-certificate probe (φ-ord precedent, one combinator)
is run. If the reading layer is roughly a third to a half of the 600-1,350
band, the defensible calibrated cut is ≈ **0.3-1.1k**, not the full 1.0-2.3k;
the remaining ×3 on certificates is untouched by this probe.
