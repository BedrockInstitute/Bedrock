## 4. The flattening question

**Yes: reading the argument comparison as membership in the earlier
entry removes the unbounded descent, and the descent does not reappear in
the statement.** The goal that settles it is
`Flatten.flatten`:
[ProbeW3Seq.agda](/Users/alsg/Agentic/Bedrock/src/ProbeW3Seq.agda:263-269),

```agda
flatten : (a c : V ℓ)
        → (sa : ⟨ a ∈ˢ Sset γ ⟩ ⊎ (a ≡ Sset γ))
        → (sc : ⟨ c ∈ˢ Sset γ ⟩ ⊎ (c ≡ Sset γ))
        → (parKey a sa ≺ parKey c sc) ⟷ argcmp a c sa sc
```

The right-hand `argcmp` (the flattened read) is: `Sset-below γ` for two
old parameters (the pair-membership content of `pr a c ∈ r₁`, where
`r₁` is the order at γ), `Unit*` for an old parameter against the level
itself, and `⊥` in the other two boundary cases. It carries **no
producer recursion at all**: the recursive descent of `p ≺ q` into the
argument producers is replaced by the atomic read. The four cases are
the pullback that *defines* `Sset-below` (old-old, definitional), the
stage-membership of an old key (`memberStage-in`), and the two
self-boundary refutations (an ordinal is not a member of a stage inside
it).

## 5. Walls

1. **The Step 0 claim is false on its literal reading (D-10).** The
   level membership at `sucV (sucV ∅)` is a 5202-slot enumeration, not
   17; 17 is the previous level. Both are finite, so the gate survives
   with the correction recorded in section 2. Not one of T22's six; a
   truth check that did its job.
2. **The op-index encoding of the two-image clause (the formula's
   wall).** The clause needs "i < j" and "¬∃ k < i" over the 16 ops as
   *formula* content. The carrier `u = Sset (sucV β)` is only three Rud
   steps deep: the ordinals 0..2 are its only transitive members, and
   the 17 slots of `Sset (sucV ∅)` collapse to about three distinct
   values (`Fof op1 ∅ ∅ ≡ ∅ ≡ Fof op15 ∅ ∅`, measured, section 2), so
   no 16-element ordered structure exists inside it cheaply. The honest
   encodings are (a) the numerals in a limit carrier (16 membership
   proofs + the set 16; then "i < j" is the single formula `i ∈̇ j`) or
   (b) an explicit 16×16 disjunction over hand-built distinct
   representatives (~120 lines). Both blow the stop-line on top of the
   op-graph bodies. **New wall, LESSONS candidate**: the finite op-order
   of a producer lex is not embeddable in a small concrete level; the
   variable-form op-graph/order frame must precede the sequence-witness
   formula. This is exactly T48's "operation-graph formulas" row

3. **The key-shape case analysis (T22's wall, cured).** `keyDecomp`
