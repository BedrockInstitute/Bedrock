## 4. Proof that the AC-and-GCH column matches ledger.py --brief

`--trophy-matrix` prints the identity field `both-equals-brief yes`. The
field is printed only after `trophy_matrix` in `scripts/ledger.py` asserts
that the AC-and-GCH column equals standing plus every remaining row, which
is exactly the endpoint that `--brief` prints.

The test suite re-checks the identity against the live repo. The test
compares the matrix's both column to the `--brief` line, and it passes:

```
standing 19,085 | endpoint 27.39-33.95k naive, 34.62-50.58k calibrated
trophy matrix | ... | both-naive 27,390-33,953 | both-calibrated 34,615-50,580 | both-equals-brief yes
```

The both-naive band 27,390 to 33,953 rounds to 27.39 to 33.95k. The
both-calibrated band 34,615 to 50,580 rounds to 34.62 to 50.58k.

AC-and-GCH standing and 18,964 for GCH standing. HEAD now measures 19,085

naive and 34,610 to 50,570 calibrated. The current `--brief` prints 27.39
to 33.95k and 34.62 to 50.58k. The band moved with the standing figure,
and the board and the test use the live figures.
