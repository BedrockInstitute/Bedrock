## THE METHOD, and it is cheap on purpose

**You are not building these masters.** For each term, assemble the
smallest file that carries its CONTENT in master shape (the real
imports, the real telescope, the real statements, the delivered proofs
where they exist and module parameters where they do not), and time it
cold. A master's cost differs from a probe's because of its imports
and its telescope, and that difference is exactly what `[T218]` is
missing.

Where a term's content does not exist yet, say so and time the closest
standing analogue, naming the analogy and marking the figure as a
projection rather than a measurement (P-l).
