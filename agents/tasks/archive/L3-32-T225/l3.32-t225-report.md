## 3. The shared home and the moved names

The shared home is `L.LevelKit`, argued by content. The kit's charter is the
tower-story clause kit at a generic transitive carrier (LevelKit.lagda.md:1-12
and 101-108): every clause is written once, at the meta level and as an
object-language formula, with two-way decodes, and the consumers instantiate
the kit at their own carrier. The bounded successor atom is exactly the class
of content the kit already hosts (`isOrdAt`, `exactDomForm`, `pair∈`); the
one-way successor clause is a tower-story clause, now shared. The alternative,
a new module, would add wiring and a top-level addition for content the kit
already names; LevelKit is imported by both consumers today. The naturalness
condition (D36) holds: the kit reads as one story, "the shared tower-story
clauses", and the successor clause is one of those clauses.

   consume it, including `clauseOk succValClause1 succValForm1 succVal1-ok`

   typechecks with them. The line counts above exclude the sibling's 66 kit

   snapshot includes their work; all checks in this report ran on that shared
   state.

   a content regression; the report records it because it masqueraded as a
   heap wall.

   LevelSigma.lagda.md:258-278 and 304-319, StepStory.lagda.md:106-112 and

   no hole, no `{-# TERMINATING #-}` was added.
