## 5. The `Lsetω⊆Ssetω` avoidance

REFUTED as stated. The claimed eight-line transitivity does not close
the landing. The landing is the membership
`⟨ Sset ω ∈ˢ Lset (sucV ω) ⟩`. Transitivity closes only the subset
`Sset ω ⊆ Lset (sucV ω)`. Feeding the membership into transitivity
needs `⟨ Sset ω ∈ˢ Lset ω ⟩`. That premise is self-membership of
`Sset ω`, which regularity forbids (`∈-irrefl`,
`src/V/Hierarchy.lagda.md:155`).

both readings under `GHCRTS=-M8g`. It uses the memberships' inclusion

(`src/L/Rud/Bridge.lagda.md:100`) and `Ltr`
(`src/L/TowerKit.lagda.md:90`). The subset closes. The membership
leaves the self-membership premise unsolved.

identification `Sset ω ≡ Lset ω` (for example
`src/ProbeT159.agda:525-526`). The identification consumes
`Lsetω⊆Ssetω` (`src/L/Rud/HF.lagda.md:654-661`). T182's gate text
confirms the first instance's top pair needs
