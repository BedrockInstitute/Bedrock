   `PinFrame` (whose 166 lines live in StepGraph, block 1's scope) and the

   P-h (the walk is module-parameterized in StepGraph; Bridge only
   instantiates), P-k (the consumers read `Fof-defSet≡` at the shape they
   need), P-l (the statements stay at the abstract carrier `C`; the tower
   positions at the opaque `suc⁴`), R-35/R-40/I-4/I-5 (no new
   representation machinery, no deep successor chains, no implicit
   inversion, no truncation branches entered). Measurement wins.
2. **`∅∈C` lands at `Lval` only, not "one line each at `Lval` and
   `Lpair`".** T177 edge 2 priced the F0 arm as needing `∅∈C` because the
   old Bridge `Lpair` could not instantiate `Layer`. Block 1 discharged
   that half: `L.TowerKit.F0Arm` is Layer-free and needs no `∅∈C`, and
   `PinFrame.F0-defSet≡` re-exposes it. The rebuilt `Arms` takes `∅∈C` in
   its telescope (as the brief requires), and the one remaining
   instantiation site, `Lval`, supplies it in one line,
   `∅∈Lset-sg A ζ hA` (StepGraph's `∅∈Lset`, any member gives the empty
   set; Bridge's own `∅∈Lset` needs a limit index, so the StepGraph lemma
   is imported under the rename `∅∈Lset-sg`). The goal's parenthetical is
   satisfied in substance: the edge's two sites collapsed to one after
   block 1's extraction.
3. **Prose:** the one affected block (the "sixteen values over a
   transitive carrier" section, en+zh) now points the description story at
   StepGraph's pin frame and sixteen defSet equations. The file's standing
   language set is en+zh (no `<!--ja-->` marker in the file); both
   languages were updated together and cross-checked for drift. Both
   linters pass.
4. **The open arms use the generic `Fof-defSet≡`, the closed arms the
   delivered closed equations, and op0 the Layer-free F0 arm.** The
